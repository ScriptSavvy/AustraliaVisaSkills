## Start
> Use student-500-preparation to help me prepare my Australian Student visa documents. Ask one question at a time and wait for my answer. Ask only what changes my checklist. Do not submit anything or send documents to additional services.

## Tailored prompts
- **Checklist:** “Use student-500-preparation to create a conditional checklist for my student or family role. Distinguish requirements at application from later checks. Ask only the next relevant question.”
- **Review:** “Use student-500-preparation to review these redacted documents. Compare course, insurance and welfare dates. Identify the exact pages checked and any unreadable pages.”
- **Department request:** “Use student-500-preparation to organise a response to this redacted Department document request. Extract each requested item and the stated deadline/timezone. Match my evidence and identify gaps. Do not send the response.”
- **Unavailable record:** “Use student-500-preparation. The civil registry cannot issue this record. Organise my truthful explanation, attempts and alternative evidence; do not assume a waiver.”
- **Newborn:** “Use student-500-preparation. A child was born while my application was pending. Identify the chronology, records and current-rule questions before assuming applicant addition or a fresh paid application.”

If you prefer to provide facts together, useful details include your role, application location and intended date, citizenship, provider/course, age, relevant family members and document list. This is optional, not a required questionnaire. Proposed combined family and existing family or historical subsequent-entry circumstances must not be assumed to use the same route.

## Student-specific scope and limits
A family member may have different requirements from the student. From2October2026, onshore applications, family inclusion and further-onshore course packages have distinct gates; new subsequent-entry applications are not authorised. The16November2026 targeted refresh adds exact exemption conditions and newer-Regulations/later-amendment coverage, not a whole-visa legal audit. Each person's role, current visa, location and relevant course/family facts still matter. Some qualifying existing-family default/completion cases can be assessed independently of the onshore transition branch; this is not a blanket family exception. Public package guidance includes PhD, but the progression class separately limits further enrolment. Unclear CoE dates, course definitions, package and individual newborn/status interactions remain visible. See [official-source verification and limits](references/verification.md).

Do not ask for invented financial evidence, hidden contradictions or fabricated Genuine Student answers. Keep truthful facts. Statutory bars/waivers, disputed custody, health/character interpretation or refusal/cancellation issues may need a registered migration agent or Australian legal practitioner. Ask for a specific referral question, not a guessed conclusion.
