## Identity and application arrangements
Compare inspected identity, course, insurance and family evidence; ask for supported name-change explanations. Home Affairs requests current passport biodata/issue/expiry pages and a valid passport for lodgement. PIC4021's unreasonable-to-require-passport alternative is a decision criterion in the retrieved historical text, not a demonstrated current lodgement waiver.[1][8]

Record application date/location and current visa subclass, primary/dependant role, expiry and conditions. From 2 October 2026, Home Affairs says most applications must be offshore; an onshore applicant needs an eligible visa **and** the applicable exemption. A student dependant seeking a primary student visa must apply and be granted offshore under the new public guidance, with no exemption to that branch. Current-primary-student exceptions include specified course completion (up to 12 additional months, same provider/main course), sponsorship, PhD, school study, defined progression and provider default; inspect the exact category, not just its label. This is a screening/referral gate, not an eligibility decision.[1][4]

Do not infer permission from absence on an old prohibited-visa list or treat 28 days as a general grace period. Verify the law applying on the applicant's date, statutory bars, approved form/location, charges and exemptions. No fixed fee is provided here: the retrieved public fee placeholder was blank. Do not lodge/pay or recommend travel as a workaround without resolving status consequences.[1][6]

## Course evidence
For applications covered by LIN24/109 (from 1 January 2025), s4 requires an uncancelled/unrevoked CoE number for **each** intended course in the relevant form field, or the specified alternatives: Foreign Affairs/Defence ministerial support letter, approved secondary exchange AASES, or a provider letter requiring the applicant to remain in Australia for postgraduate thesis marking. An ordinary offer letter is not among those alternatives. The instrument's thesis wording is broader than the public page's “postgraduate research” summary; verify the legal branch rather than erase it.[12][1]

Inspect provider/course, dates, completion and actual enrolment status. Do not infer a system label's meaning or claim access to departmental systems: seek provider confirmation. Decision-stage continuing enrolment/support/thesis marking is distinct from application evidence. Home Affairs says a cancelled or completed-course CoE is no longer valid at decision; a doubtful expired-CoE/offer-only case is not ready merely because a letter exists.[1][7]

Inspect every allowed packaged course. From 2 October 2026, the public page says current Student visa holders applying for a further visa in Australia cannot package courses except school study or provider default. Verify permission before budgeting a package. For allowed packages inspect progression and gaps; public guidance describes gaps under two calendar months with academic-year exceptions, not an automatic statutory refusal finding. For English rules, LIN25/090 defines principal course by highest AQF level; the public page describes the last course as main course. If these differ for the actual package, resolve the scope rather than silently choose one.[1][9]

## Under18 welfare and consent
Ask age at application, decision and every intended Australian stay. The official welfare page specifies parent/guardian consent (form1229 or stated statutory-declaration route; sole-custody evidence if consent from both cannot be supplied) separately from welfare. Welfare routes include provider CAAW, approved exchange AASES, minister-approved Foreign Affairs/Defence arrangements, or nominated guardian using form157N. A qualifying relative must be in the stated relationship category and at least21; inspect nomination, relationship, character and lawful-stay evidence, rather than assuming any adult qualifies. Guardian subclass590 eligibility is outside scope.[3]

If turning18 before arrival, that page expressly instructs signed form157N questions1–8 and still completed form1229; no welfare arrangements for that post-birthday stay. This does not prove that decision-age criteria or an earlier under18 Australian stay disappear. Historical clauses500.217(2) and PIC4012A/4017/4018 distinguish welfare, consent and best interests for a minor at decision; verify currently applicable law before a compliance conclusion. Unknown decision age remains Needs verification.[3][7][8]

Compare continuity across providers, approved start with actual arrival, and end dates. Public welfare guidance says no arrival before welfare commencement and gives CoE period plus7 days or until18; historical PIC4012A(b) states minimum enrolment plus7 days without that express age cap. Read the relevant branch and current law if the difference matters. School students' historical clause500.216/public guidance distinguishes age6 at application and commencement limits for years9–12; calculate age with a tool and check exchange exceptions, not a blanket university age cap.[3][1][7]

## Genuine Student preparation
Public guidance applies GS to applications lodged on/after23 March2024, GTE before that. Organise truthful circumstances, study/employment, course/provider choice and understanding, future value and immigration/compliance history; inspect evidence for statements. The fetched GS page specifies English responses,150 words per response and an additional question for previous Student holders or onshore non-student applicants; refresh the actual form before enforcing its limits. Evidence examples are not a universal mandatory list. Future permanent-residence intentions do not count against an applicant under that page. Do not invent facts, score genuineness or declare that a career change/gap alone passes or fails. Ministerial Direction/current legal applicability remains to be checked for a consequential assessment.[2][1]

## English evidence — primary students
Establish an evidenced exemption and documentary trigger from a dated official Checklist Tool outcome or actual request. No applicant-specific outcome is supplied by this release. Historical clause500.213 permits a request at any time; do not assume a streamlined outcome removes every later request. Do not demand a primary English test from every dependant.[1][7][11]

For applications covered by LIN25/090 (on/after7 August2025), read ss6–9 and the actual tables. Section7 includes citizenship **and valid passport** for specified countries; the course branch requires the listed course to be the **only** visa-purpose course, not simply the principal course of a package. Its study branches have exact place/language/completion/time/student-visa conditions; “substantial component” has no numerical percentage in that provision. If recognition/substantial completion is uncertain, seek evidence and mark Needs verification.[9]

Distinguish pre-commencement tests (s8/Schedule3) from later tests (s6/Schedule1); currency depends on evidence at application versus later decision. Read exact test/metric/score and lower-score ELICOS timing/duration or named foundation/pathway conditions. C1 Advanced lower-score branches are not accepted under Schedule1; OET's newer overall-score rows differ from older B-each-component rows. No invented equivalence or combining unrelated sittings. Delivery/registration matters: the public page rejects wholly online/at-home tests and gives date-specific TOEFL/C1 and LANGUAGECERT-document instructions. Refresh those details for the actual test. Public “principal course” exemptions and evidence-level timing summaries differ from the instrument's exact conditions; use applicable law, and explain a material conflict rather than infer an exemption.[9][1]

## Funds and genuine access
Historical clauses500.214/500.313 distinguish genuine access for the stay from requested specified evidence; public guidance also distinguishes checklist/request documentary evidence and genuine access. Inspect holder/relationship, source, availability, restrictions and scholarship/loan scope. A balance alone does not establish access. LIN19/198 s10 lists deposits with a financial institution, financial-institution/government loans and scholarship/financial support; property valuation alone is not one of those forms. Do not invent a universal holding period.[7][1][10]

Refresh exact amounts and transitions. Sections6–8 distinguish primary/combined from subsequent-entry financial routes; the latter is relevant to saved/historically applicable applications or resolving the applicable-date question, **not permission to make a new subsequent-entry application**. Income alternatives require the prescribed primary student's parent/spouse/de facto and official government documents issued within12 months before application; not any sponsor's salary. Sponsored letters count only within their statutory coverage. Read exact role/time provisions.[10]

Use a tool for calculations, showing currency, roster, period, verified costs and prepayments. Section11 uses annual amount/365 × intended days for applicable pro rata branches, not a monthly shortcut. Tuition deductions require actual paid amounts/evidence; do not invent allocation or private-rent deductions. If a boundary (including exactly12 months) or cost allocation is unclear, show it as unresolved rather than choose a favourable rounding/branch.[10][1]

For a historically applicable subsequent entrant, s8(2) includes relevant student/existing-family living, tuition and school costs; travel is for new applicants, not automatically existing holders. School-cost nil under s6(2)(c)(iv)(C) requires the qualifying primary status **and actual government-school fee waiver**. School enrolment/PhD/scholarship alone is insufficient. Verify local school fees separately. Do not assume a funding instrument overrides current family admission restrictions.[10][1]

## Insurance
The public page specifies OSHC for the intended stay and each covered person unless a stated exception applies, starting at actual offshore arrival, not merely course start. Onshore cover continuity matters if previous visa required health cover. Compare actual dates/people, not predicted guaranteed grant dates. Ordinary travel insurance/Medicare does not establish an exception.[1]

For provider-arranged cover inspect provider information, CoE and cover dates; the public page distinguishes application policy-number requirements where applicant/agent arranged versus provider arranged. Its Gather documents section asks proof of paid adequate insurance: do not announce a universal certificate waiver or conflate application information with evidence of cover. Swedish exception requires stated Kammarkollegiet cover, not citizenship alone; Norwegian/Belgian alternatives also require exact category evidence. Special sponsorship cover for dependants is not established just by a support letter: verify actual adequate insurance arrangements. Do not infer all scholarships exempt insurance.[1]

## Family and school-age dependants
**Check date/permission before assembling a new family application.** Current public guidance says from2 October2026 most students cannot include family unless an exemption applies and family cannot apply as subsequent entrants in any circumstance; applications lodged before that date use the earlier rules according to the page. Other sections still describe subsequent-entry documents. Treat them as potentially historical/unreconciled, not a live invitation to apply.[1]

For a new combined application, read the exact exemption and same-time/location/eligible-visa conditions: PhD (not Master's by Research), DFAT/Defence, fully funded foreign-government scholarship, specified Pacific/ASEAN nationality with separate onshore conditions, or specified existing-family/course-completion/default/progression categories. “Sponsored”, “research” or a country's broad region alone is insufficient; do not treat fee concessions as family exemptions. Verify applicable post-change law and saved applications; reform commencement and application saving are verified, but exact additional classes under item1222(5)(c)/(d) and their applicant-specific applicability remain unresolved.[1][5]

Collect exact relationship/age, original declarations, family-formation dates, primary visa and included persons. The public definition covers partner and unmarried dependent child under18; historical regulation1.12(6) and2.07AF distinguish family definition/declaration/notification from adding an applicant. A newborn or newly formed spouse requires the applicable current/saved branch, not an automatic addition promise. Missing prior declarations, sibling-combination assumptions and disputed custody need precise verification/professional questions, not concealment or automatic relodgement advice.[1][6]

Secondary genuineness/funds/insurance are separate from primary criteria. For a historically applicable school-age dependant, clause500.315 triggers schooling evidence for proposed stay over3 months; s1.03 defines school-age as5 to under18. The public page asks schooling evidence including a school/education-department letter. Verify current law, jurisdiction, age/stay and fees; a primary CoE rule does not automatically govern a dependant's school placement. Detailed conditional Defence, catchment-residence, home-school and compulsory-age branches are retained in `operational-knowledge.md`; no blanket exemption or verified current acceptance is implied.[7][6][1]

## Other criteria and later obligations
Follow actual applicable health/character/biometrics requests and current official guidance; do not mandate every police certificate/exam for every person or certify clearance. Historical subclass500 provisions list public-interest/special-return criteria, but current detailed bars, waivers, health/character/security and critical-technology assessment are not resolved here. Insurance is not a health clearance.[1][7]

Post-grant work/course-change/waiver advice is outside scope. Suggest reading actual grant/VEVO conditions, not generic promises; any further task needs independently refreshed rules, especially after the recent changes.[1]
