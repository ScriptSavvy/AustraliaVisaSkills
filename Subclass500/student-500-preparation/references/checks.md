# Preparation checks

Use these as branching instructions, not as a universal list of attachments. Refresh applicable official requirements before a compliance conclusion. Legal anchors below help the executing agent trace rules without burdening an applicant with a research register. Applicable historical provisions and exceptions may differ.

## Identity and application arrangements
Compare inspected passport/identity details with course, insurance and family evidence. Ask about supported name changes. Valid passport and any exception need verification; do not assume waiver entitlement. For applicants in Australia, verify visa subclass/stream, conditions and expiry against current/historical onshore restrictions; being absent from an old prohibited-visa list is not proof of ability to lodge. A 28-day rule is not a general grace period. Verify fees/exemptions, approved forms and location rules; do not lodge or pay.
Legal anchors: Migration Regulations 1994 item1222, subclass500 criteria, PIC4021.

## Course evidence
Usually inspect a Confirmation of Enrolment (CoE) for each intended course, including a package's principal course. Confirm course/provider, dates and actual status; do not equate an 'expired' system label with cancellation. Separate evidence at application from continuing enrolment at decision. Verify special alternatives for approved secondary exchange, DFAT/Defence and onshore postgraduate thesis marking. An ordinary offer letter is not automatically an acceptable substitute for a new application; see verification.md. Course changes/gaps may need factual explanation; policy examples are not automatic statutory refusal thresholds.
Legal anchors: item1222, LIN24/109 or applicable successor/predecessor, clause500.211.

## Under18 welfare and consent
Ask age at application, decision and arrival, not just current age. Verify the applicable branch: nominated parent/custodian/qualifying relative (form157N), education-provider-approved accommodation/welfare (CAAW), approved exchange AASES or DFAT/Defence arrangements. Do not assume any adult relative qualifies. Review continuity of dates, guardian evidence and consent/custody as distinct checks.
Turning18 before intended arrival does not automatically remove all minor-related requirements. Under the foundational guidance, a student applying while17 and first arriving after18 supplies signed form157N questions1–8 without welfare arrangements for that post-birthday stay; decision-age criteria and consent remain separate. Verify current rules and any earlier under18 Australian stay. If age at decision is unknown, do not mark its criteria Not applicable. School students have additional age/entry-level requirements; verify them separately. Subclass590 eligibility is outside scope.
Legal anchors: item1222, PIC4012A/4017/4018, clause500.216.

## Genuine Student preparation
Use the regime applicable to the actual application date. Prepare truthful facts and supporting evidence about circumstances, study/employment history, course/provider choice, knowledge of study/living arrangements, future value, immigration compliance and significant changes. Verify current official form questions/language/limits; do not impose remembered word counts. Supporting employment/tax/study examples are not mandatory for every person. Don't invent family ties, salary prospects or history. Career change, study gaps or future residence aspirations alone do not establish failure. Do not score or decide genuineness.
Legal anchors: clause500.212; applicable ministerial direction; official Genuine Student guidance.

## English evidence — primary students
First establish an evidenced exemption, then whether formal documentary evidence is required using the dated official Checklist Tool outcome or actual request. Do not require a primary student's English test from every dependant. Exemption conditions matter: a package ending in research or a course named 'English' is not automatically exempt.
If testing applies, verify exact test name, date, evidence-submission timing, applicable instrument, score/metric, delivery mode and qualifying course package. Read the exact statutory score row; don't approximate IELTS equivalents or combine highest components across unrelated sittings. Lower-score branches depend on exact ELICOS duration, commencement conditions or recognised foundation/pathway course. OET and C1 Advanced treatment differs by test date; older general summaries cannot override the applicable table. 'Substantial completed study' is not automatically a fixed percentage of course duration. Uncertain exemption/course recognition/test mode: Needs verification.
Legal anchors: clause500.213; LIN25/090 sections6–9/Schedules1–3 or the applicable instrument.

## Funds and genuine access
Separate genuine access to support for the entire stay from specified first-year documentary calculations. Streamlined paperwork does not mean funds are unnecessary. Establish funding route, holder/relationship, source, actual access/restrictions and sponsor/loan/scholarship coverage. A balance alone proves neither access nor sufficient support. A property valuation alone is not interchangeable with permitted funds evidence; a qualifying evidenced loan is different. No invented universal holding period.
Refresh living-cost, school-cost and annual-income figures. For an income alternative verify prescribed person, government documentation and timing; any sponsor's salary does not automatically qualify. For funds, verify living, travel, applicable tuition less evidenced prepayments and school costs by role and period. Use a tool for calculations; show currency, verified inputs and relevant dates. If the applicable instrument uses annual amount/365 times days for a shorter stay, use that rule, not an invented monthly shortcut. Do not invent prepaid-fee allocations or deduct private rent by assumption.
Combined applications and subsequent entrants have distinct calculations. Subsequent entrants may need to cover relevant living/tuition/school costs of existing student/family holders, while the applicable travel branch concerns new applicants; verify the roster and exact provision. A PhD/scholarship or government-school enrolment alone does not prove a nil school-cost branch: establish actual applicable fee waiver. Verify state/territory school fees separately.
Legal anchors: clauses500.214/500.313; LIN19/198 sections6–8,10–13 or applicable version.

## Insurance
Usually review Overseas Student Health Cover (OSHC) for relevant people over the intended stay, including actual arrival, not only course start/end. Do not predict a guaranteed visa end date. Provider-arranged cover may have a supported declaration/confirmation route; do not demand a separate certificate as a universal requirement. Ordinary travel insurance or Medicare is not automatically a substitute. Verify any alternative arrangements and their conditions.
Swedish citizenship alone does not establish qualifying alternative coverage; obtain actual cover evidence. DFAT support does not automatically cover dependants' insurance; Defence support depends on actual sponsorship scope. Do not assume all scholarships confer an exception. Proposed arrival before cover start is a confirmed planning gap if dates are inspected, even if legal compliance still needs current verification.
Legal anchors: clauses500.215/500.314; applicable official OSHC guidance and condition8501.

## Family and school-age dependants
Establish exact relationship/age, original family declarations, dates of family formation and primary visa. Do not assume adult dependent children or other relatives qualify under subclass500's family definition. Existing non-accompanying family declarations and new family notifications differ from adding an applicant. Newborn provisions do not automatically allow adding a new spouse to an already lodged application. Missing prior declarations require verification/professional review, not concealment or an automatic relodgement recommendation. Sibling subsequent entrants without a parent must not be assumed eligible for a combined sibling application.
Assess secondary genuineness/funds/insurance separately from primary checks. Where schooling arrangements apply, check child age, proposed stay, intended state/territory and acceptable enrolment/placement arrangements; fees and schooling evidence are separate. A school offer for a dependant can have different rules from a primary student's CoE. Verify sponsor/catchment/home-school exceptions rather than demand one document in all cases.
Legal anchors: regulation1.12(6), regulation2.07AF, clauses500.311–500.318; schooling clause500.315.

## Other criteria and later obligations
Health/character/biometrics and other requests depend on applicable rules/circumstances. Follow actual official requests; do not mandate police certificates/exams for everyone, certify security/medical clearance or confuse insurance with the health requirement. Flag material professional questions. Detailed referenced instructions are not bundled. Post-grant work rights, course changes and no-further-stay waivers are outside this skill: suggest reading the actual grant/VEVO record, not generic promises.
Legal anchors: subclass500 primary/secondary criteria; relevant Schedule4/5/8 provisions and actual official requests.
