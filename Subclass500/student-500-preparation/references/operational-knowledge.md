# Operational knowledge — Student visa preparation

Read this file in full with `checks.md` and `verification.md` before selecting branches. It preserves substantive preparation guidance, not just webpage summaries. Distinguish **stored policy guidance**, **verified instrument requirements** and **applicant-specific compliance**. Missing accessible corroboration is not a reason to discard a preparation branch. It is also not permission to invent a citation, certify current law or ignore a conflicting applicable provision. Show only relevant uncertainties to the applicant. Never use the material below to promise visa eligibility or acceptance.

## Application dates, authority and uncertainty gates

For each branch identify role, stage, application/test/evidence/decision dates, exact trigger, exceptions and evidence. Guidance may inform what to gather while compliance remains Needs verification. Where an applicable legislative provision contradicts a stored policy summary, use the provision, preserve the conflict and do not revive the contradicted claim.

**2 October 2026 changes are real, but not every exception is fully verified here.** The decoded official Student page describes onshore, family and packaging changes. The reform makes secondary applications combined with the primary application through item1222(3)(e) and replaces the subsequent-entry decision criterion. Do not restore a general new subsequent-entry route. Pre-commencement applications remain under their applicable earlier rules by clause16601. ## Identity, role, lodgement and charges

**Lodgement versus decision.** Inspect passport identity, validity, date/name variations and explained changes. The unreasonable-to-require-a-passport alternative in PIC4021 concerns the decision criterion; it does not establish a general lodgement waiver. Do not infer identity fraud or authentication from an inconsistency. Primary and secondary roles have distinct criteria.

Normally applications use the approved online form157A; an alternative process needs actual authorisation, not a workaround invented by the agent. Verify approved form, charge, location, course evidence, minor welfare and combined-application provisions applying on the date. The skill does not submit or pay. Location must not be immigration clearance. Onshore checks need actual visa subclass/stream, primary/secondary status, conditions and expiry, not just a visa label.

**Current onshore screen.** Item1222(3)(aa) requires a current Student holder seeking primary criteria to be offshore unless a primary holder falls in a specified exemption; a secondary Student holder becoming primary has no exemption in that branch. Item1222(4) separately addresses substantive visa/precluded-person classes and non-substantive alternatives. LIN26/102 s6 covers all streams of400/407/408/417/457/462/482/485/600/601/602/651/771/988, only403 Domestic Worker (Diplomatic or Consular), and995 granted under Division995.2. Do not generalise all403 or all995 holders. Verify both application and grant location: new500.411 requires offshore grant where application was required offshore by1222(3)(aa) or failure of1222(4).

**Non-substantive visa cases.** There is no universal28-day grace period. The reform removes the ordinary last-student-visa branch; residual special-purpose/dependent-diplomatic branches need all applicable prior-visa/timing/previous-grant conditions. New1222(4B) requires no substantive visa; last substantive visa a student visa; cancellation; ART setting aside/substituting cancellation or non-revocation after the earlier visa ceased; application within28days of the legally specified notification; and no previous grant from a non-substantive-visa application. Seek professional review of unlawful status, statutory bars, no-further-stay conditions and uncertain notification deadlines. Do not advise deliberate expiry or travel to circumvent a rule.

**Fees and exemptions.** Refresh fee, subsequent temporary application charge and exact instrument. Stored preparation categories include Foreign Affairs/Defence sponsorship, approved Commonwealth training schemes (including evidenced partial funding), approved exchange withAASES, provider default and research-family combined applications; historical emergency-related concessions are date-limited. A sponsor label alone is insufficient. Combined versus subsequent applications can have different fees, and reform items1–6 remove specified subsequent-family nil-charge branches. Collect scholarship/default/category evidence before asking the official estimator or professional to settle a claim; do not hard-code a fee or assume a concession.

### Current public exemption screening — not a verified permission finding

Keep the decoded official page's exact categories, rather than a broad label. Additional delegated classes/current individual applicability still need law verification. The six current-primary onshore categories are: (1) up to12 additional months from original main-course end, same provider as the current grant, main course if packaged; a changed course must be same or higherAQF; (2) Foreign Affairs/Defence sponsorship; (3) applying forDoctoralPhD, notMaster's byResearch; (4) primary/secondary school study; (5) completed original main course or a substituted same/higherAQF course and progression to higherAQF, with higher-education to higher-education-provider restriction; alternatively completed nonAQFmain course toAQFcourse, or completed school to nonAQFcourse; (6) inability to continue due to provider default. Completion is not just intention to complete; ELICOS-to-ELICOS is not progression under the listed nonAQFbranch.

For family, the page asks same-time and same-location application with the primary and an eligible onshore visa. Its categories are: PhD (notMaster's byResearch); Foreign Affairs/Defence; foreign-government scholarship **fully** funding Australian study; specified Pacific/ASEAN nationality; or specified existing-family exceptions. PhD/ministry categories allow inside/outside applications with eligible/student visa conditions for onshore family. Fully-funded scholarship and specified nationality allow offshore inclusion; onshore they require up-to12-month main-course completion, completed-main-course higherAQFprogression with higher-education-provider restriction, or provider default. Do not replace the exact list with a region or fee-concession category; verify passport/statutory nationality wording.

Existing-family page categories require family currently on the student's visa: completion up-to12additional months or provider default can be inside/outside; progression category is onshore and needs completed original/same-or-higherAQFcourse then higherAQF, with the higher-education-provider restriction, or the exact nonAQF-toAQF/school-to-nonAQF alternatives. The page also says held or applied for a Student visa before2October2026. New1222(3)(da) independently specifies **secondary applicant in Australia** plus at-commencement-held/pre-commencement-applied-and-later-granted status, or the express Australia-born child/date/primary-status branches. An existing-family website suggestion outside Australia must not silently override these statutory location gates; obtain the applicable delegated class/clarification if needed. Preserve both positions, not a merged or blanket permission.

## Each intended course, course status and packages

**Application evidence.** For applications covered by LIN24/109 from1January2025, provide uncancelled/unrevoked CoE numbers for every intended course, or the exact Foreign Affairs/Defence ministerial letters, approved exchangeAASES, or provider letter requiring remaining in Australia for postgraduate thesis marking. That thesis branch is not confined in the instrument to research degrees. It is not an independent exemption from onshore application rules. Ordinary offer letters alone are not an alternative under this instrument; the earlier onshore offer-only branch must not be carried forward to new applications.

**Application status versus decision status.** A cancelled/revoked CoE cannot be used for the prescribed application branch. An administrative expired label is not automatically cancellation. Ask the provider to confirm actual approved/studying/completed/cancelled status and dates; do not claim system access. Visa refusal alone does not establish cancellation. The decision-stage enrolment, required thesis-marking or minister-support branch is separate: refresh after course changes/completion rather than rely only on lodgement evidence. Conflicting expired-CoE offer-letter wording is not positive permission to use an offer alone. A completed course ordinarily needs new valid course evidence unless an evidenced alternative applies.

**Packages.** Inspect every CoE, AQF level, dates, relationship/progression and principal-course evidence. The English instrument defines principal by highestAQF; a web description of last/main course may serve a different context. Resolve mismatched definitions for the actual task. A preliminary-course CoE does not establish readiness for an unevidenced entire package; do not assume the next onshore application will be available.

Stored course-gap policy expects no more than two calendar months in the teaching year and four outside it, with individual assessment beyond these periods: satisfactory attendance/progress, good academic record and whether requiring another course in the gap would be unreasonable matter. Between-visa gaps can warrant flexibility because a student may remain within a lawful post-course visa period. Current public guidance says **less than** two months with academic-year exceptions; do not silently merge that wording with the stored **no more than** boundary. Record exact dates, seek clarification where the boundary matters and never turn the expectations into automatic statutory refusal cut-offs. Unrelated packages or weak progression need truthful GS explanations, not fabricated progression.

Current public guidance restricts packages for current Students applying for a further visa onshore to school studies/provider-default exceptions. Keep this screening gate; exact operative exemption instrument/package conditions remain to be checked. It does not erase historical package expertise or authorise offshore/onshore lodging by itself.

## Under18 welfare, consent and school-student age

**Separate stages.** Obtain age at application, decision and each intended Australian stay, calculated with a tool. Under18-in-Australia lodgement welfare is distinct from minor-at-decision public-interest criteria. Consent/custody/best interests are separate from accommodation/support/welfare. Do not infer that turning18 before arrival removes decision-age criteria.

If under18 in Australia, preparation alternatives are completed157N for parent/custodian/nominated qualifying relative; providerCAAW; Foreign Affairs/Defence welfare approval; or approved exchangeAASES. If turning18 after application but before arrival with no earlier minor stay, official guidance requires signed157N questions1–8 and1229, but no welfare arrangements for the post-birthday stay. Turning18 after arrival but before course start still requires welfare for the minor stay. An unknown decision date leaves decision-age applicability Needs verification.

**Guardian.** A parent/custodian or eligible nominated relative is not just any adult. The relative must be nominated in writing, at least21, of good character and have the required lawful-stay/residence evidence. Verify actual legal relationship; a cousin or cultural auntie title alone is insufficient. Guardian590 eligibility is a separate task. Disputed parental authority needs professional review; a form does not adjudicate custody. Consent may use1229 or the specified statutory declaration, with sole-custody evidence where both consents cannot be obtained.

**Provider welfare.** Obtain dates and continuous cover across providers; no arrival before approved welfare starts. Stored policy uses enrolment plus seven days; current website adds an until18 branch while historical PIC4012A(b) has no express cap. Preserve that duration conflict where material and obtain applicable law/provider clarification. Do not treat a recommended one-week pre-course start as permission for earlier arrival.

**School students.** For non-exchange students, stored/current guidance uses at least6 at application and under17 at year9 commencement, under18 year10, under19 year11 and under20 year12. Further completion visas can differ where studies began before the age limit: establish original commencement and exact applicable500.216 interpretation before rejecting the branch. There is no blanket university age limit.

## Genuine Student factual preparation

Applications on/after23March2024 use GS; earlier applications need the applicable GTE/Direction framework, not retrospective replacement. Refresh the applicable Direction and actual form. Current page specifies English responses/150words per response and an extra question for previous Student holders or onshore non-student applicants; no portal has been tested by this skill.

Organise the applicant's own truthful current circumstances, study/employment record, Australia/provider/course choice, actual course knowledge and living arrangements, future value, immigration/compliance history and course-change/inconsistency explanations. Supporting study, employment and tax examples are not a universal mandatory attachment list. Streamlined funds/English evidence does not waive GS. GS is qualitative, not a points checklist.

Permanent-residence aspirations, lower-level study, a reasonable career change, study gaps, age or a relative's immigration history do not alone establish failure; they also do not guarantee success. Explore the relevant facts and evidence rather than stereotype. Do not invent ties, career returns, salary prospects, reasons or a finding of genuineness.

## Documentary framework and English

Citizenship/provider/principal-course inputs guide the official evidence tool, not an invented public risk score. Obtain a dated relevant outcome; missing inputs/outcome leave documentary trigger Needs verification. Stored framework generally uses application-time evidence level and describes favourable movement to streamlined English treatment. A current tool result cannot prove historical applicability or justify a harsher retroactive demand. Preserve the timing question, actual request and deadline. Officers may still request evidence; streamlined does not exempt genuine financial access or GS.

**Exemptions first, primary applicants only.** For applications under LIN25/090 on/after7August2025, s7 includes: citizen **and valid passport holder** of Canada/NewZealand/Republic of Ireland/UK/USA; only enrolled visa-purpose course a registered non-English-delivery course,ELICOS,school or postgraduate research; Foreign Affairs/Defence/approved exchange; completed Australian English senior-secondary requirements within two years; or a substantial successful component of Australian EnglishAQFCertificateIV+ study within two years while holding a student visa; or five successfully completed English-study years in Australia/Canada/NewZealand/SouthAfrica/Republic of Ireland/UK/USA. Do not waive testing for a package merely ending in research or infer anELICOS sector from its name.

**Substantial component.** No fixed50% or duration-only test. Look at passed subjects, assessment/course structure, credits and provider evidence. Time studying longer than normal does not prove successful completion; failed subjects do not count as passed. Assess the actual language of delivery as well as completion. Keep difficult interpretation Needs verification, but preserve these operational checks instead of reducing the branch to a bare question.

**Scores and timing.** Use exact application instrument/test date, test name/metric, evidence-submission timing and course conditions. For tests on/after7August2025, Schedule1 baseline/qualifying-column3/column4 values are C1Advanced overall161/not accepted/not accepted; CELPIPGeneral overall7/6/5; IELTSAcademic and IELTSGeneralTraining average6.0/5.5/5.0; LANGUAGECERTAcademic overall61/54/46; MET overall53/49/44; OET overall1210/1090/1020; PTEAcademic overall47/39/31; TOEFLiBT total67/51/37. Column3 requires exact s6 conditions for at least10weeks accompanyingELICOS intended to start after application, specified foundation or listedCRICOSpathway; column4 requires at least20weeks accompanyingELICOS and that principal course intended to commence after the visa application was made. Never substitute a similarly named pathway.

Pre7August2025 tests governed by the new instrument use s8/Schedule3: CAE169/162/154; IELTSoverall6.0/5.5/5.0; OET at leastB in every component for every column; PTE50/42/36; TOEFL64/46/35, with exact s8 course conditions. Earlier applications need their earlier instrument. Currency is two years before application if evidence is provided then; otherwise before decision. Unknown decision date means prospective review, not guaranteed validity.

**Separate sittings.** Stored assessment policy does not allow selecting highest components across separate tests into a super-score; at least one qualifying test must satisfy requirements including currency. Components of one test taken on different days are different from combining separate tests. Preserve current provider/retake/reporting verification gates; no synthetic combined score or blanket rejection of every multi-day test. ## Funds, access, amounts and calculations

**Entire stay versus documentary amount.** Every applicant needs genuine access to sufficient support for the intended stay; a first12month prescribed evidence calculation is not a whole-stay budget or exemption. Inspect holder/relationship, source, availability/restrictions, previous support, operating business evidence, scholarships and loan disbursement/terms. A balance alone is not proof. Property valuation alone is not a prescribed funds form; a qualifying evidenced loan against property is different. No universal six-month holding period or invented buffer.

**Routes.** LIN19/198 permits qualifying deposits with financial institutions, financial-institution/government loans, scholarships/financial support; specified parent/spouse/de facto annual personal income evidenced by official government documents issued in12months before application; and exactAASES/ministerial support branches. Stored income policy permits combining parents' incomes, but not parents-in-law; do not treat any sponsor salary as the income alternative. Check relationship, relevant period, authenticity limits and continuing spouse employment if accompanying. Current aggregation/application interpretation stays subject to the applicable provision; collect evidence rather than assume it.

For applications under10May2024 amendments, stored annualAUDvalues are student29,710; partner10,394; child4,449; school-age schooling13,502; income87,856 without secondary applicant/102,500 with one. Refresh amounts/transitions every task. Older applications need older values. Travel and insurance are separate from living costs.

Use a tool; show roster, currency/exchange source/date, periods, paid fees and assumptions. Applicable short-stay pro rata uses annual/365×intended days, not monthly×months. Unclear inclusivity or exactly12month wording remains unresolved. Tuition covers prescribed first-year/remaining study less evidenced prepayments, not an invented uniform allocation; use provider fees/receipts and separate boarding from tuition to avoid double counting.

**Formal provider boarding/homestay.** Stored policy permits evidenced prepayment of formal arrangements with the education provider to reduce the living-cost component, supported by provider contract/authorised receipt. It excludes privately arranged boarding/homestay and private rent. Preserve this conditional route; verify current acceptance/amount and relevant law before compliance-ready subtraction. Show gross amount and proposed deduction separately if unresolved, not automatic full credit or erasure of the alternative.

**Travel.** Stored policy benchmark isAUD2,000 for offshore applicants/AUD1,000 onshore, with higher-cost-country exceptions. These are policy planning figures, not universal legal fixed charges; refresh actual applicable guidance. Combined applications include each included applicant's travel. Subsequent-entry s8 travel covers the new applicants, not automatically the existing student/family; its living/tuition/school roster still includes relevant existing members. This branch is for saved/historically applicable applications, not permission for new subsequent entry. Inspect sponsored s8 coverage separately if support excludes family expenses.

**School-cost nil.** Qualifying doctoral/Foreign Affairs/Defence/Commonwealth-sponsored status **and an actual government-school fee waiver** are required in the retrieved provision. Status/enrolment alone does not establish nil. Verify jurisdiction charging and waiver letter; fee waiver does not automatically remove schooling-arrangement evidence.

## Insurance arrangements, evidence and dates

UsuallyOSHC for each relevant person and intended stay, not only teaching dates. Ordinary foreign travel insurance is not equivalent. Compare arrival, current coverage continuity and planned visa-period coverage; actual grant may differ from planning. Onshore non-student applicants need cover by grant under stored guidance; prior visa cover obligations remain independently relevant.

**Provider-arranged evidence.** Stored policy accepts the application declaration that the provider arrangedOSHC without a separate certificate in every such case. Preserve that branch; inspect provider/CoE confirmation, insurer and dates. Current Gather documents guidance asks proof of paid adequate cover and distinguishes whether a policy number is required when applicant/agent versus provider arranged. This evidence-process tension remains unresolved where material: declaration alone does not prove payment/coverage and no universal certificate waiver should be asserted as verified current law.

**Special arrangements.** An Education Department letter for an EndeavourAward saying insurance is organised for the stay can support adequate arrangements under stored policy. ADFATsupport letter can establish arrangements for the sponsored student, not automatically accompanying family; Defence-supported arrangements can extend to sponsored family if the letter actually establishes that scope. Verify persons, duration and current acceptance; sponsorship alone is not exemption. Norwegian National Insurance, Belgian reciprocal arrangements and SwedishKammarkollegiet require exact scheme evidence. Swedish nationality alone, general reciprocal-agreement nationality or temporaryMedicare does not removeOSHC. Do not convert general scholarship support into health insurance.

Offshore cover must start at actual arrival, even when before course start; a one-week recommendation is not a substitute. Stored end-date planning: if cover reaches the end of all study but not the otherwise contemplated visa end, policy limits the grant to the cover end; inadequate cover for the full study period offshore may lead to refusal, while onshore policy can contemplate a shorter grant to cover end and another application. Neither branch guarantees a grant, a further application route or acceptance of inadequate cover. Existing SwedishCSN cover in Australia is a historical continuity branch, not a new-policy option; verify actual cover/current acceptance. Defence Cooperation Program (DCP) arrangements and Fee For Service (FFS)/home-country insurance may differ; the DCP/FFS distinction requires checking adequate arrangements and exact letter scope rather than force the same product on every sponsored person. Obtain actual insurer/provider dates; never guarantee visa end dates or advise a coverage gap.

## Family definition, declarations, additions and schooling

Student family ordinarily means spouse/de facto or unmarried dependent child under18 of student/partner, not every relative or adult dependent child. Relationship and original declarations matter, including non-accompanying family. Notify changes after lodgement and before decision. Family formed after grant has a different historical branch from undeclared pre-existing family; neither establishes a general post-reform subsequent-entry right.

A newborn after application has a specific combined-application rule under regulation2.08; a new spouse cannot simply be added as an applicant by notifying a marriage. Post-reform family/newborn eligibility has express date/location/status gates in1222(3)(da); check these and current guidance, including Australia-born versus offshore-born distinctions. Do not erase the newborn mechanism or promise every newborn addition automatically. Siblings are not members of each other's student family unit: under stored combined-subsequent policy, siblings applying without a parent's combined secondary application cannot assume all are validly combined (only base-charge payer was treated as valid). Preserve this historical warning and verify exact law/fees before any saved-case conclusion. No advice to omit family or automatically relodge.

Secondary genuineness, funds and insurance differ from primary criteria; do not demand a dependant's primary English test by analogy. Foreign Affairs/Defence family require the relevant minister's support in their branch.

**School-age dependant decision branch.** Age5to under18, intended stay over3months, adequate education arrangements under applicable500.315. State compulsory education and visa definition are not identical. A child aged5but below local compulsory age may need evidence of future commencement when compulsory age is reached. A child under5at decision ordinarily has no grant-stage schooling-evidence requirement under stored policy, but future condition compliance still matters. A17year-old beyond local compulsory age may not require enrolment evidence if the jurisdiction's applicable education obligations are otherwise met; verify actual state/training/employment rules, not a blanket exemption for all17year-olds.

School/provider or education-department evidence with enrolment/paid-fee details is usual. Where noCoEissues before arrival/grant, a placement offer conditional on arrival may suffice under stored policy; it is not the primary student's offer-only course branch. If neitherCoEnor offer issues until catchment residence, preserve case-by-case assessment: evidence of all reasonable enrolment efforts in intended area, understanding of prompt enrolment after arrival and intent to comply. Obtain school/authority confirmation; not an automatic waiver.

Defence students' school-age dependants may use the stored decision-stage exception to pre-arrival enrolment evidence because Defence arranges placement/accommodation and later schooling; verify sponsorship/arrangements and current acceptance. Fees remain separate. Home education requires actual localState/Territoryregistration/approval and is a stored option only for applicants already in Australia, not a promise that offshore intentions suffice. Unusual educational/cultural/location proposals need case-specific professional/authority clarification, not invented approval. Fee-exempt scholarship evidence does not alone remove schooling evidence or prove jurisdiction waiver.
