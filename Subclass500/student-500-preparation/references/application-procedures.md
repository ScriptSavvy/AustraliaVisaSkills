# Application procedures for this subclass

Read with [terms](terms.md), [document handling](document-handling.md), operational knowledge and the existing verification reference. This file contains substantive stored procedural preparation knowledge, supplemented by targeted official checks. It is not a validity assessment, complete procedural code, legal-response service or promise of a Department opportunity to correct an application. Source age and exceptions matter; use current approved arrangements and applicable law for consequential findings.

## 1. Application-stage and record checks

Keep making the application, processing requests, decision criteria, circumstances at grant, visa effect and post-grant conditions separate. Schedules 1 and 2 are not an exhaustive catalogue of every potentially relevant provision. Identify actual applicants, disclosed non-applicant relatives, hosts/sponsors and authorised recipients as separate roles.

For a form/submission review request only the relevant redacted saved application, acknowledgement, payment receipt and actual defect notices. Check the exact approved form/channel, directions, declarations, fields, relevant signature/assistance and each person's physical location. A generic statement about where an internet application is legally made does not locate the applicant. No generic email/fax route overrides approved subclass arrangements or a Department-written alternative.

Check residential address separately from correspondence/email and recipient details. If disclosure creates a safety/protection concern, record the concern and request confirmation of an authorised protected-disclosure method; historical separate-document handling is not permission to invent a secure channel or use an agent's office as the residence.

Record draft creation, payment and submission dates separately. A receipt alone does not prove the application date, validity or successful submission. Shortfall, failed payment, omitted particulars and later remediation can affect timing. Historical substantial-compliance/quick-remedy policy is conditional, not guaranteed cure or preservation of an earlier date. The stored partly-completed-form branch excludes serious information deficiency, an absent residential address, and a required personal identifier not provided. Preserve those specific exclusions when explaining that historical branch, but verify current requirements rather than declare the application invalid or promise a cure. Do not copy historical response periods or phone-attempt counts as rights. Near age limits, visa cessation, travel or a legislative change, preserve the official timestamps/timezone and refer disputed legal timing.

Onshore statutory bars, No Further Stay, prior refusal/cancellation and uncertain substantive/bridging status need actual grants/conditions/notices and a precise professional question. A waiver request is not a granted waiver; expiry or travel does not automatically remove a bar. Protection-specific restrictions must not be generalised to every Student or Visitor case. Do not recommend deliberate expiry or departure as a workaround.

## 2. Communication and current-application linkage

Use the processing office/channel or replacement address actually notified, and the current request/portal directions. Drafts should identify the correct applicant/application, request reference and attachments, without exposing full identifiers in routine reports. Inventory supplied transmission/receipt evidence; preparing a draft is not sending it.

Current ImmiAccount guidance tells applicants to follow the application form's attachment instructions, not email documents by default; it also says documents cannot be attached to finalised applications. A generic older electronic-communication discussion does not authorise an alternate submission route. Current portal receipt/upload indicators are mechanical evidence, not document acceptance or visa validity.

If substantive information was given by telephone, record only what was actually said and consider a truthful written confirmation through the instructed channel. A phone discussion is not automatically invalid or a promise of outcome.

Keep residential address, nominated document recipient, electronic-consent arrangements and professional-assistance appointment distinct. Verify appointment/change records when receipt and deadlines matter. Under the Act text read for this release, section 52(3B) uses a proposed residence change of **14 days or more**; do not substitute “more than 14 days”. Current contact-update guidance encourages use of ImmiAccount. This is not a universal deadline or a post-grant condition rule; check relevant dates, grant conditions and current instructions.

Do not assume records in an earlier visa application must be retrieved for this one. Identify which evidence was actually attached, given or incorporated under current instructions. A prior assessment, sponsor assurance, agency referral or progress update does not bind the decision maker or prove grant/waiver.

## 3. Organise an official request without inventing a deadline

First classify the supplied communication: applicant-initiated additional evidence, a Department information request, an adverse-information comment invitation, interview, or a third-party assessment/result. Do not turn every missing attachment into a promised request. Applicant-initiated evidence before decision does not compel postponement merely because more has been promised; sections 54–56 of the Act text read distinguish those matters.

Collect the complete redacted request, issue and actual applicant/recipient receipt facts, response method, exact stated deadline/timezone, requested items and extension correspondence. Build a concise request-item-to-evidence matrix. Ask for missing facts; do not calculate a deemed-receipt legal deadline from an email date alone. No generic seven-, 28- or 70-day default applies in this skill.

If more time is needed, record reason, attempts, expected availability and the actual request/grant of more time. **Requesting an extension is not an extension.** Preserve the original deadline unless authoritative correspondence changes it. Do not promise that the Department will wait or that expired periods can be extended. The Act's invitation/refusal-timing provisions and prescribed periods require applicable-version review before legal advice; do not assert that every decision, including grant, is prohibited until the deadline.

A third-party health/qualification/English assessment may involve evidence that the applicant has initiated action rather than the final result. Follow the actual request; this distinction does not create a blanket deadline exemption. Late evidence may remain relevant if actually received before decision, but that is not permission to miss a deadline or proof the application remains undecided.

An adverse-information invitation is consequential. The read Act's section 57 distinguishes potentially refusal-relevant, person-specific information not supplied by the applicant for this application, with a non-disclosable-information qualification. Do not promise disclosure of every concern or infer a procedural-fairness breach. Organise the supplied notice/evidence and refer disputed rights, confidentiality and legal response strategy with the exact question. No refusal strategy or representation is added to this preparation skill.

## 4. Changes, newborns and applicant inclusion

A changed relationship declaration is not automatically addition of an applicant. Historically distinct mechanisms concern a child born after an application, requests to add certain applicants to certain permanent visas, and dependent children for listed temporary classes. Do not import a permanent-visa spouse route or a listed-temporary-visa child route without exact coverage, undecided-stage, date, location and transition checks.

For a child born **after a parent's application but before its grant/refusal**, preserve the potential deemed-application-at-birth/combined-application mechanism rather than assume a fresh paid application. Inventory birth date/place, parentage evidence, both parents' relevant pending applications and their status at birth, citizenship question, notification, child/application acknowledgements and any health/insurance/welfare/sponsorship evidence actually required. Late notification does not change the chronology. Adoption is not birth under that mechanism. Birth after decision, a child of a visa holder and citizenship by birth are different questions.

If both parents had relevant pending applications, possible multiple child application records need clarification; do not delete one by assumption. Under the dated newborn mechanism, all applicable time-of-decision criteria remain relevant, and any prescribed time-of-application requirement for sponsorship, nomination or proposal is satisfied at decision. Stored guidance calls for new sponsorship or equivalent evidence for the child only, not redoing sponsorship for the already-sponsored family. This is conditional on the actual child/subclass sponsorship requirement: do not demand a sponsorship form from every newborn or promise current acceptance. Verify the applicable regulation, subclass provisions and transitions before a compliance finding. The dated Regulations body read for this release supports regulation 2.08 but its displayed compilation interval ends before the check date; current coverage/transitions remain a verification gate. The official newborn page retrieved exposed a questionnaire but not enough scenario-result text to settle this applicant's pathway. Do not present that retrieval as verified newborn outcome guidance.

**Student-specific gate:** distinguish regulation 2.08 birth during a pending application from a fresh family application concerning an Australia-born child, a newly formed spouse and a saved subsequent-entry application. Preserve the existing 2 October 2026 reform/status/location/application-saving questions. Neither a generic family notification nor the dated newborn mechanism establishes permission to lodge a new subsequent-entry application. Keep insurance, welfare, relationship and school-age checks distinct from permission to apply.

Notify relevant changes using the current authorised process. Include contact/passport, relationship, birth and relevant study/welfare changes only when they apply. No instruction here authorises actual notification, submission or contact.

## 5. Withdrawal, decision and existing-visa boundaries

Not travelling, failing to respond, making another application or removing an application from an account is not automatically withdrawal. Inventory any actual written notice, affected persons, minor-signatory authority and Department acknowledgement. One person's withdrawal is not automatically everyone's withdrawal. The read Act distinguishes written withdrawal from refusal; current online guidance says removing an account item does not withdraw the application. This skill does not choose or execute withdrawal, promise a refund or calculate bridging cessation.

Death of an applicant/sponsor can affect remaining applications but does not automatically dispose of every person's application. Handle sensitively; organise the relevant records for qualified review rather than determine outcomes/refunds.

No notification is not proof that a decision has not been made. A pre-decision evidence organisation task cannot be described as reopening a recorded decision. Decision notification, grant conditions/effect and existing visas need their own records. A new substantive visa can affect an existing visa subject to applicable exceptions; do not adopt a blanket “every new grant cancels every previous visa”. Refer status/travel/withdrawal consequences. Refusal-invitation reapplication routes, review, cancellation, jurisdictional error and officer internal systems remain outside this skill.
