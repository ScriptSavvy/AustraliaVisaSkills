# Using this skill

At first activation in a new task/session, the agent shows the disclaimer and a brief CC BY-NC 4.0 non-commercial licence notice. Each intake question shows progress, such as **Question 1 of 10** for a known set or **Question 1 of about 10** for an adaptive estimate. If the total is not yet known, it says so; if answers change the total, it briefly explains why. It still asks one question at a time and does not ask extra questions just to reach the total.

For a focused question, expect a direct answer, the specific supported problem and a concrete next action—not a full intake or report by default. If the answer is blocked, the agent explains the precise missing fact or unresolved requirement and asks only the next material question. For a requested or necessary broad review, main findings and priority actions come before scope notes and detailed tables. Required notices and material uncertainty remain visible.

First-use prompt:
> Use gsm-points-preparation to help me prepare my GSM points claims. Ask one question at a time. Do not decide my visa eligibility.

The agent displays the notice and answers the actual question from supplied facts. When a material fact is missing, it asks one relevant fact per turn; it does not require a full points interview for a focused factor question. Unsure answers remain uncertain, not Not applicable. The agent can continue other checks and show a conditional lower–upper points range with explicit assumptions, separately from the evidence-supported subtotal. Dependencies and caps still apply; if defensible bounds cannot be calculated, it explains the partial range or missing information instead. Tell the agent if you prefer grouped questions.

You can ask: “Some answers remain unsure. Show my conditional points range, the assumptions at each endpoint, and what I should verify first. Do not treat assumptions as facts.”

Examples:
- “I have a 190 invitation. Compare my EOI points with my redacted evidence.”
- “I am planning an EOI. Use an explicitly hypothetical invitation date.”
- “Check only my skilled-employment points; do not assess other visa criteria.”
- “Review this official request. Ask about its deadline first.”

Supply only relevant redacted records. The skill itself needs no external document service, but your agent/host may process uploaded files remotely. Ask about processing and retention before sharing sensitive records. External OCR or third-party transmission requires permission.

For a full estimate, expect main findings and priority actions first, followed by a provisional breakdown, supported subtotal, unresolved claims and evidence gaps. For a focused question, expect a direct scoped answer instead; relevant date checks, dependencies, caps and uncertainty still apply. Ready means ready for the specified preparation check, not eligible or likely approved. Missing means evidence absent; Needs correction means a confirmed problem; Needs verification means facts, contents or rules are unresolved; Not applicable requires known facts.

When sources are unavailable, the agent may still organise evidence and provide explicitly conditional estimates; it must not label unverified rules legally current. A formal departmental assessed score is not the same as this estimate.
