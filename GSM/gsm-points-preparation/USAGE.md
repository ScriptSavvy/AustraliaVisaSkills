## Start
> Use gsm-points-preparation to help me prepare my GSM points claims. Ask one question at a time. Do not decide my visa eligibility.

This is a points-claim and evidence preparation module, not a full visa assessment. A focused factor question does not require a full points interview.

## Tailored prompts
- “Use gsm-points-preparation. I have a 190 invitation. Compare my EOI points with my redacted evidence.”
- “Use gsm-points-preparation. I am planning an EOI. Use an explicitly hypothetical invitation date.”
- “Use gsm-points-preparation. Check only my skilled-employment points; do not assess other visa criteria.”
- “Use gsm-points-preparation. Some answers remain unsure. Show my conditional points range, the assumptions at each endpoint, and what I should verify first. Do not treat assumptions as facts.”
- “Use gsm-points-preparation. Review this official request. Ask about its deadline first.”

## Points-specific results and limits
A full estimate separates the evidence-supported subtotal from any conditional lower–upper range. Dependencies and caps still apply. If defensible bounds cannot be calculated, the agent explains the partial range or missing information instead. Uncertain answers remain unresolved, not zero or Not applicable.

When sources are unavailable, evidence organisation and explicitly conditional estimates can continue, but unverified rules must not be labelled legally current. A formal departmental assessed score is not the same as this estimate. See [source refresh](references/source-refresh.md), [material conflicts and coverage limits](references/verification-notes.md) and [scenario ranges](references/scenario-ranges.md).
