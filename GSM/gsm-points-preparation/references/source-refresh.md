# Applicable-source refresh

Stored knowledge is preparation guidance, not a declaration that all rules are current. Before a compliance conclusion, retrieve actual official source bodies and match their scope and effective period to the applicant.

1. Identify actual invitation, application and known formal points-assessment dates. Planning uses an explicit hypothetical date. Do not replace an unknown formal assessment date with a claimed legal finding.
2. Read the applicable Migration Act 1958, especially sections 92–96, and Migration Regulations 1994 Schedule 6D, regulation 2.26AC and related definitions/restrictions. Match the compilation's displayed coverage, not merely a URL labelled latest. Check amendments/transitional provisions if dates fall outside coverage.
3. Follow factor-specific instruments for approved English tests/scores (including the change from 7 August 2025 and transitional results), occupation lists/assessing authorities, Professional Year, specialist fields, community language, regional areas and qualifying score. Check test sitting date as well as invitation timing. Generic English pages may describe an application window; do not override a Schedule 6D invitation requirement without reading the applicable provision.
4. Official public points tables help corroborate point bands but do not replace legislative definitions or detailed operational guidance. Neither a calculator result nor search snippet is a verified legal conclusion.
5. For each adopted rule record title, authority type, URL, provision, version/publication/effective dates (unknown if absent), date checked, relevant scope, and relationship to stored guidance: confirms, supplements, changes, conflicts or unresolved. Put a compact relevant subset in the applicant report; keep full research separately.
6. If retrieval fails, retry official alternative formats or rendered/embedded content. If still unavailable, identify the failed source and affected conclusion. Continue independent evidence inventory and conditional estimates; do not invent scores/test thresholds or treat an access failure as rule repeal.
7. For unresolved conflicts list what each source says, references, missing fact/authority, effect on points and evidence needed. Do not select the most convenient answer. Applicable legislation and valid later changes govern over historical operational guidance; retain unaffected rules and explain any actual amendment.

- [Proficient English](https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/help-support/meeting-our-requirements/english-language/proficient-english)
- [Superior English](https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/help-support/meeting-our-requirements/english-language/superior-english)

These are lookup links, not a claim of complete verified coverage. Use the corresponding subclass's official table for nomination/sponsorship claims. Historical 489, invitations before 1 July 2018, COVID-era study concessions, disputed skilled-employment periods, unusual qualifications and relationship changes require their own applicable-source checks. Never infer a current student work-hours limit from a historical example.
