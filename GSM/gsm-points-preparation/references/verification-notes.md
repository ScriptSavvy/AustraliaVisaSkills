## Supplementary operational details from the checked versions
- Schedule 6D age, English, employment, education, additional-factor and partner amounts corroborate the baseline tables. Employment thresholds are **12/36/60/96 months in Australia** and **36/60/96 months overseas**, not rounded CV years. More than 20 combined employment points is recalculated to 20; it is not added as another bonus.
- Item 6D61 specifies a completed Australian Professional Year in the nominated/closely related occupation, **at least 12 months in the 48 months before invitation**. Official [190 points guidance](https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/visas/getting-a-visa/visa-listing/skilled-nominated-190/points-table) clarifies completion within that window; commencement can precede it. Approved-program status remains a separate instrument check.
- Regulation 1.15F requires completed Australian degree(s), diploma(s) or trade qualification(s) from registered courses, at least **16 calendar months**, at least **two academic years' study**, instruction in English, undertaken in Australia with study-authorising visa permission. “Completed” means academic requirements met, not necessarily formal conferral. The academic-year instrument and official GSM credit guidance were separately checked in the Australian-study supplement below; conflicting RPL interpretations and concessions remain conditional. Read [Australian study assessment](australian-study.md) rather than treating this core summary as a complete edge-case assessment.
- Regulations 2.27C/D specify substantive, Bridging A (010) or Bridging B (020) visas, permission and condition compliance for counted work/study. Do not generalise to every bridging visa.
- Nomination/sponsorship amounts are corroborated by the [190 table](https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/visas/getting-a-visa/visa-listing/skilled-nominated-190/points-table) and [491 table](https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/visas/getting-a-visa/visa-listing/skilled-work-regional-provisional-491/points-table). These pages also distinguish partner-status assessment from invitation timing.

## Australian study supplement — checked 9 October 2026
- [LIN 19/085](https://www.legislation.gov.au/F2019L00508/latest/text), Migration (LIN 19/085: Academic Year) Instrument 2019, was labelled in force. Section 6 defines an academic year as at least 46 weeks of registered course duration; two academic years are 92 weeks. Registered study volume remains separate from 16 calendar months. Its as-made version was registered 2 April 2019 and commenced the next day. Match historical applicability separately.
- Retrieved Schedule 6D item 6D81 requires the Australian study requirement **at invitation** and does not impose a general six-month recency rule. The retrieved 485 provisions expressly impose a six-month application window; do not transplant that into GSM points. The instrument explanatory statement distinguishes those visa contexts.
- Official [GSM Australian study guidance](https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/visas/getting-a-visa/visa-listing/skilled-nominated-190/australian-study-requirement) confirms 92 weeks, non-duplicated eligible credits and completion evidence. Ordinary calculations exclude overseas/non-CRICOS and previously counted credit. Stored RPL treatment can permit gross registered-course calculations under a different interpretation. Where that conflict decides the result, show both calculations and leave the possible 5 points unresolved pending authoritative reconciliation; a webpage alone does not settle the legal interpretation.
- [CRICOS course search](https://cricos.education.gov.au/course/coursesearch.aspx) permits exact course-code lookup. Present registration and duration do not establish historical status throughout an applicant’s study.
- The 2007 National Code used a historical 25% online/distance limit. The [2018 National Code](https://www.legislation.gov.au/F2017L01182/latest/text), Standard 8.19, uses one-third of units or equivalent for higher education/VET with separate delivery conditions. These are provider standards, not automatic GSM entitlements. Its published compilation F2026C00148 covers 21 January–1 October 2026; later commenced amendments were not available as a compilation, so the post-1-October Code also needs refresh. Do not apply either percentage as an evergreen immigration rule.
- Offshore COVID study concessions in the stored guidance are specific to affected 485 cases. They do not establish a blanket GSM exception; historical savings and any claimed GSM concession require their own authority.

## English tests: use dated rules, not memorised scores
[LIN 25/016](https://www.legislation.gov.au/F2025L00905/latest/text), Migration (Specification of Language Tests, Test Scores and Passports) Instrument 2025, commenced **7 August 2025** and repealed the earlier 2015 instrument. Check actual component tables, test sitting date, test version/mode, approval exclusions, retakes and transitional provisions. [Proficient](https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/help-support/meeting-our-requirements/english-language/proficient-english) and [superior](https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/help-support/meeting-our-requirements/english-language/superior-english) guidance separate post-change and older tests. Their 6 August 2028 legacy-use statement is qualified by visa validity rules, not a universal extension.

**Unresolved timing issue:** regulations 1.15D/1.15EA use three years before **invitation**; the saved-test provision in LIN 25/016 section 10 uses 36 months before **application**. Do not replace one date with the other or assume every old test valid at invitation remains saved at application. A result near either boundary needs provision-specific clarification; report the affected English claim Needs verification rather than awarding it unconditionally.

## Other precise limits and conflicts
- **Diploma:** the checked regulation 2.26AC(6) uses the historical tertiary register as at **1 July 1999**, with “associate diploma, or a diploma”; advanced diploma is included separately under the AQF limb. Use the applicable legislative definition rather than an inconsistent historical summary.
- **Specialist education:** the stored operational description uses two academic years **in Australia**; regulation 2.26AC(5A) says **at the institution**. Physical-location applicability for offshore or unusual study is unresolved. Preserve both positions, verify the field instrument and obtain authoritative interpretation before excluding or supporting such a claim. Do not equate ordinary Australian study and specialist education tests.
- **Doctorate:** public guidance's “PhD” shorthand must not silently exclude a qualifying professional doctorate where the actual rule says doctorate. Check the degree definition/recognised standard and actual research level; professional titles alone are insufficient.
- **Partner occupation list:** public shorthand “same list” and the statutory specified-occupation test need the applicable occupation instrument. Do not infer list applicability from code/title alone.
- **Student work hours:** the checked condition 8105 substitutes 48 hours for relevant Student/associated bridging cases, with exceptions. This is not an evergreen universal cap. Use visa conditions/law at each actual employment date, never a historical 40-hour example as current proof of breach.
- **Remaining instrument/policy checks:** qualifying score, approved PY programs, specialist fields, NAATI standards, regional postcodes, recognised-standard body, current operational discretion and historical savings require their own checks. This bounded review does not certify them all.

If any issue changes the points result, include the specific source positions, missing fact/authority, effect and next action in the applicant report. No private provenance is needed to execute this skill.
