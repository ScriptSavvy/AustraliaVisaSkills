# Evidence checks

These are practical evidence suggestions unless an applicable rule or official request requires a particular document. Request only what a triggered claim needs.

| Factor | Useful evidence and checks |
|---|---|
| Age | Redacted birth/passport date; calculate completed years at actual invitation, check inconsistent birth dates. |
| English | Full test report with provider, test type, sitting date and every component; applicable test-date instrument, validity and invitation window. No points for a passport alone. |
| Overseas/Australian work | Employment letters with dates, duties, hours and pay; contracts, payslips/tax/payment records; skills assessment opinion; occupation classification and career progression; actual work location; visas/conditions for Australian periods. These are examples, not a universal mandatory bundle. |
| Professional Year | Completion dates, provider, program duration and relation to occupation; approved-course instrument. Identify internship separately from other paid skilled employment. |
| Education | Completion letter, award, transcript, institution and recognition opinion when relevant. Completion of requirements and award date may differ. Do not infer degree equivalence from title alone. |
| Specialist education | Research mode, Australian study duration, institution and field evidence. Coursework masters is not research masters. |
| Australian study | Registered-course details, credits, completion, instruction language, physical location/duration, visa permission; assess actual regulation 1.15F. |
| Community language | Credential type, issue/validity dates, NAATI evidence and applicable instrument/transitions. Language fluency alone is not the credential. |
| Regional study | Campus and residence throughout the course, study mode, transcript/completion and postcode instruments; rental/utilities are suggestions and alternatives may exist. |
| Partner | Actual relationship and change chronology, citizenship/PR evidence; same-subclass inclusion; age, English, occupation and assessment as applicable. Australian birth alone does not establish citizenship. |
| Nomination/sponsorship | Relevant subclass, invitation, nomination and withdrawal status or accepted sponsorship; intention to apply for nomination is not nomination. |

## Employment chronology
Record each period's start/end, country/work location, duties, paid status, weekly hours or evidenced roster, full-pay leave, unpaid leave, visa permission and overlaps. Clip to the ten years immediately before invitation. Do not count employment after invitation. Merge overlapping eligible periods within each location rather than double-count concurrent jobs. Investigate Australian/overseas overlap rather than assume physical presence in both. Do not approximate threshold years using 365 days or rounded CV years; exact dates, leap years, excluded leave and uncertain start/end conventions need review. Mechanical interval work does not decide whether employment was legally qualifying.

## Six dimensions
For each reviewed document record: presence; readability including all pages; completeness including required fields; consistency with other evidence/EOI; currency under the applicable rule; compliance with a specifically verified requirement. Use unknown where a dimension was not checked. Do not use one overall Ready status to hide an unreadable page or unresolved legal-version gap.

When documents conflict, identify the exact differing field and ask for the smallest clarification. Do not erase a contradictory record or fabricate a replacement. Possible integrity issues are not a proven finding of fraud. Escalate consequential discrepancies for appropriate professional advice.
