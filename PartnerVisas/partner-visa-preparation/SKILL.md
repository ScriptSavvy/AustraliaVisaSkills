## Purpose, activation and boundaries

Use for a tailored document checklist, content/consistency review, evidence summary, official-request preparation or next-action plan for **Prospective Marriage (300), initial Partner (820/801 or 309/100), and permanent Partner (801 or 100)** preparation, including related child and relationship-change evidence. This is ONE self-contained skill. Share evidence work while keeping subclass, role, application/grant location, stage, dates and exceptions distinct. A prospective marriage is not identical to an existing spouse/de facto relationship.

Do not decide visa eligibility, application validity, authenticity, legal evidence sufficiency, lawful stay, cancellation/review rights or approval prospects. Do not choose a consequential legal strategy, recommend visa expiry, promise a waiver or treat separation as automatic loss of every pathway. Organise the supported evidence and identify the exact question requiring qualified judgment. There is no submission, payment, withdrawal, account operation, third-party communication or commercial-service regulatory clearance under this skill.

Use only facts material to the requested output: task; known route/stage and role; relevant application/receipt/grant/entry/event dates; visa and grant basis; relationship basis/status; actual Department request; available documents and applicant language. - Before adaptive intake, explain once: “I’ll ask one question at a time. Your answers help me decide what to ask next, so you won’t need to answer questions that don’t apply to you.” Do not introduce intake if the answer is independently supported.
- Label every interactive fact/document request with the next rendered number, such as **Question 3**, in the chosen language. Use **Question 3 of 8** only for a genuinely known complete applicable set. No approximate totals, literal placeholders, percentage progress or unsupported “almost done” claims. Topic labels describe the actual question, not a progress estimate.
- Maintain a question ledger across clarifications, skipped answers, replacement pages and resumed intake. Do not count volunteered facts or irrelevant branches. Unknown/skipped answers remain unresolved, not Not applicable. If a known set changes, explain briefly and keep a total only if still known. Stop when enough is known for the task.
- A request to share a clearer page here is intake even if imperative: explain processing/redaction, ask it as the single numbered request, and wait. A report action to obtain a readable copy for later review is not an immediate upload demand.
- Prioritise a real urgent request, safety issue or possible deadline risk before routine intake. Do not demand violence details, perpetrator contact, shared passwords or unsafe joint statements. Do not delay useful cautions to finish a questionnaire.

## Workflow and completion checks

1. **Set the requested check.** Display required notices. Identify the task and records actually available. If stage matters but is unknown, ask one stage question; do not first demand location, visa, relationship and dates together. An already granted permanent visa is not a pending stage-two case. Completion: scope, role/stage where material, and unknowns recorded.
2. **Establish the chronology and branch.** Keep application location, decision/grant location, original combined date, temporary/provisional grant, entry, marriage, cessation/death, Department receipt and remittal separate. Check ordinary versus exceptional grant basis and child role before applying a checklist. Completion: branch supported or exact applicability question unresolved; no guessed conversion or grant.
3. **Apply source and uncertainty gates.** Use verification.md and the relevant operative provisions/public directions for the person's dates. Classify each item as mandatory legal, conditional with trigger, official evidence direction, useful example or practical suggestion. Retain alternatives, discretion and material conflicts. Completion: consequential conclusions have a source/knowledge basis and scope/date limits.
4. **Build the relevant evidence inventory.** Use the operational matrix and only triggered modules. Whole-stage reviews cover identity, chronology, relationship evidence collectively, role-specific sponsor/witness material, applicable children/checks, relevant changes and correspondence. Filename-only items can be listed but contents stay unverified. Completion: every applicable item has purpose, stage, evidence status and next action; unknown applicability remains visible.
5. **Inspect and compare.** Review actual pages and extraction confidence. Compare names, dates, addresses, roles, declared relationships, residence periods, prior records and genuine changes. Separate presence, readability, completeness, consistency, currency and verified compliance. Do not silently repair OCR or select a convenient date. Completion: confirmed problems, possible concerns and unreadable/uninspected evidence are distinguished.
6. **Deliver the preparation result.** Answer focused questions directly. For a broad review, use the report template, with a concise combined findings table and ordered actions. Show only case-relevant uncertainty and short official-source notes; do not dump the library. When blocked, explain what can be prepared now and the exact missing fact/authority or professional question. Completion: actionable output, no invented acceptance or readiness score, all material unresolved gates retained.

|---|---|
| Ready for the stated preparation check | Evidence supports the expressly named checks; not overall visa readiness, validity, authenticity or official acceptance. |
