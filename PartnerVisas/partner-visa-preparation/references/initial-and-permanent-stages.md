# Initial and permanent Partner stages

Use this reference to distinguish ordinary combined 309/100 and 820/801 applications, the existing permanent application, and exceptional separate applications. It provides preparation knowledge, not an application-validity decision, legal advice or a guaranteed immigration outcome. Read [verification](verification.md) for the applicable-date and source limits; do not describe stored guidance as comprehensive current law.

Use [operational knowledge](operational-knowledge.md) for the common evidence review, [relationship and sponsorship](relationship-and-sponsorship.md) for substantive relationship and sponsor rules, [children and checks](children-and-checks.md) for secondary roles and health/character preparation, and [application procedures](application-procedures.md), [document handling](document-handling.md) and [terms](terms.md) for the procedural distinctions. Load [prospective marriage](prospective-marriage.md) or [exception pathways](exception-pathways.md) whenever relevant or not yet excluded.

## Route, role and existing application

Ordinary offshore Partner preparation starts with one combined **309/100** application: the provisional and permanent applications are made together. Ordinary onshore preparation starts with one combined **820/801** application. This pairing is different from combining a child's application with the primary applicant's application. Do not merge visa stage, applicant role and family grouping into a single field.

Record the actual original submission and acknowledgement, visa class/subclass, primary or secondary role, linked sponsor, relevant family inclusion, first-stage grant and basis, existing permanent application status and current requests. A payment, draft form, transaction reference or portal label alone does not prove that a valid application was made. When 2.08E or an ART order supplies a deemed application date, retain that legal date rather than substituting the original 300 application or marriage date.

| Stage | Ordinary primary relationship basis | Relationship to the permanent stage |
|---|---|---|
| 309 | Spouse or de facto partner of an Australian citizen, permanent resident or eligible New Zealand citizen; a distinct intended-marriage application basis also exists. | Ordinary primary 100 criteria are 309-based, with separately prescribed ministerial, death, cessation and remittal alternatives. |
| 820 | The applicable 820.211 application-time limb, including ordinary spouse/de facto or the specified current/former-300 branches. | Ordinary primary 801 criteria are 820-based, with distinct exceptional grant-basis and other alternatives. |
| 100 or 801 ordinary Stage 2 | Fresh assessment of the relevant existing permanent application, not a new ordinary initial paid Partner application. | Initial grant does not establish every permanent decision criterion or automatically produce permanent residence. |
| 445 child leading to permanent Partner | A child may satisfy primary criteria for 445 and then secondary criteria for 100 or 801. | Do not demand that the child prove the primary applicant's spouse/de facto relationship. Actual visa, inclusion/addition and linked permanent grant are separate. |

A 309 holder does not use the ordinary primary 801 route, and an 820 holder does not use the ordinary primary 100 route. A subclass number alone is not an eligibility finding. Refusal, cancellation, ministerial intervention, historical grants or a child whose parent has already been granted permanent residence require their own specified alternative, not a forced ordinary checklist.

## Application location is not grant location

The following identifies the named location rules, not every validity requirement:

| Application | Location at application | Location at grant under the inspected current provisions |
|---|---|---|
| Ordinary 300 | Outside Australia under item 1215(3)(b). | In or outside Australia, but not in immigration clearance, under 300.412. |
| Ordinary combined 309/100 | 309 outside Australia under 1220A(3)(b), at the same time and place as Class BC under (c). Class BC ordinarily outside Australia under 1129(3)(c). | Both in or outside Australia, but not in immigration clearance, under 309.412 and 100.411. |
| Ordinary combined 820/801 | In Australia, but not in immigration clearance; 820 at the same time and place as Class BS under 1214C(3)(a),(c), with Class BS location under 1124B(3)(b). | Both in or outside Australia, but not in immigration clearance, under 820.411 and 801.411. |
| Specified separate Class BC application | Item 1129(3)(d) permits the specified 445 holders and specified ministerially granted provisional-visa holders to apply in or outside Australia, but not in immigration clearance. | Do not use this application-location exception to rewrite another route's requirements. |
| 445 holder seeking Class BS/801 | In Australia, but not in immigration clearance, under 1124B(3)(b). | The broader permanent grant-location rule does not change the addition/application whereabouts requirement. |

From **25 November 2023**, the 309 and 820 grant-location reforms apply to grants on or after commencement regardless of application date. From **1 July 2024**, the 300 grant-location reform applies to applications made before commencement but not finally determined, and applications made on or after commencement. The current 100 and 801 grant clauses also permit in/outside grant. Do not instruct travel merely to meet a superseded offshore or onshore grant summary.

These rules do not give permission to enter Australia, remain lawfully, work, travel on a bridging visa, disregard first-entry requirements or lodge an ordinary onshore application offshore. Read the actual visa grant, travel facility and conditions separately. Status and travel decisions are outside a document-preparation finding.

### Historical location and cessation reforms

Use the rule applicable to the relevant historical grant or decision, not the latest text retrospectively:

- The **27 February 2021** 300/309 location concession required grant after 26 February 2021, application before the concession period ended, presence in Australia at some time during the concession period, and presence in Australia but not immigration clearance at grant. The initial concession period began on **1 February 2020**; its specified ending day was **25 November 2023**. Do not confuse that ending day with either fixed 300 extension expiry in [prospective marriage](prospective-marriage.md).
- The **20 August 2022** amendments introduced detailed 309 cessation alternatives and the then 100.221(4B)/(4C) links, applying to decisions on or after commencement regardless of application date. Do not attribute that complete exception structure solely to the February 2021 concession.
- The **25 November 2023** grant-location reform removed the concession condition from 309.221(1)(b), but the introductory requirement that the applicant be in Australia remained until the July 2024 reform. Broader grant location alone did not yet make that relationship-cessation exception exercisable offshore.
- The **1 July 2024** reform removed that remaining 309 present-location restriction, changed the entry condition for the 309/100 family-violence branches, separated the 100 child-rights branch, removed the named death-route close-ties conditions and removed the specified current/former-300 marriage prerequisites. The relevant regulations 1.23/1.25 and Schedule 2 amendments cover applications made before commencement but not finally determined, or made on or after commencement. They do not automatically reopen finally determined cases. The special application provision for regulation 4.02 is separate, not a global retrospective rule.

The complete conditional pathways are in [exception pathways](exception-pathways.md). Do not retain repealed 100.221(4B)/(4C) as current automatic permanent-grant alternatives or treat a later unrelated family-violence amendment as changing every Partner subclass.

## Validity, application-time criteria and decision criteria

Keep three separate findings:

1. **Validity:** Schedule 1, applicable approved form/place/manner instruments, payment and statutory application bars. It is not established by having strong relationship evidence.
2. **Application-time Schedule 2 criteria:** for example the selected 820.211 limb or 309.211 basis and relevant secondary criteria. A later event does not automatically cure a requirement fixed at application.
3. **Decision-time criteria:** continued or exceptional relationship basis, sponsorship where required, holding/grant-basis conditions, health/character and other public-interest or return criteria, and grant location. An application can be valid without meeting grant criteria.

The primary 100 and 801 Schedule 2 criteria are decision-time criteria, but this does not abolish Schedule 1 validity requirements or secondary application-time criteria. Nor does it create a new ordinary permanent application date.

For onshore preparation, trigger a specific check where there is no substantive visa, refusal/cancellation since entry, a section 48 issue, an imposed application-restricting condition, specified regional-visa history, a transit visa, or an unusual application/remittal history. A substantive visa excludes bridging, criminal justice and enforcement visas. Do not treat a bridging visa as a substantive visa merely because the person may be lawfully present.

The ordinary 820.211(2)(d) no-substantive-visa branch has its own alternatives: the specified diplomatic/special-purpose entry and Status of Forces Agreement circumstances with criterion 3002, or criteria 3001, 3003 and 3004 unless the Minister is satisfied there are compelling reasons not to apply them. This is not a universal Schedule 3 requirement appended to every current/former-300 limb. The secondary 820.312 status criterion has its own wording and must not be assumed waived by the primary's route.

For a possible compelling-reasons issue, prepare truthful evidence of why status was lost, circumstances outside the person's control, illness/incapacity where relevant, time without status, compliance history, steps taken to regularise status and material family circumstances through decision. A genuine relationship, ordinary separation hardship or an Australian citizen child is not an automatic waiver. Do not coach deliberate non-compliance, advise the applicant to wait for expiry, or determine Schedule 3 sufficiency. Complete current companion guidance and the actual applicable criteria require qualified review.

Specified current/last regional visas can raise **both** a Schedule 1 holding-period restriction and separate Schedule 2 substantial-compliance criteria. Stored rules distinguish two-year holding for the specified older regional classes and three-year holding for 491/494, with substantial-compliance clauses 820.212 and 820.313 covering their specified visa lists. Retain exact grant and holding history and evidence of conditions; refresh the applicable lists and periods before a validity or criteria conclusion. Do not infer that satisfying one of these requirements satisfies the other.

Section 48 has particular initial onshore requirements, including its specified sponsorship and two qualifying third-party statutory declarations with declaration timing, status and same-time/place predicates. Those are not routine Stage 2 witness requirements. The recent Partner-refusal restriction is another initial validity question, not an instruction to lodge serial applications or rely on bridging travel to reset history. Use [application procedures](application-procedures.md) for the exact records and review gate; this reference does not decide an applicant's bar, deadline or ability to lodge.

**Unmarried exceptional 820 and continuation gates:** the removed marriage prerequisites in 820.211(7), (8) and (9) do not resolve their interaction with the marriage-based exception and general sponsorship-approval wording in **820.221A**. Keep that question **Needs verification** for an affected unmarried case; do not invent an exemption, require unsafe sponsor cooperation or declare the reformed route unavailable. Application-time holding under 820.211 and decision-time continuation under 820.221 also remain separate. Do not insert a hidden 8515/8519-compliance limb into (6), or promise that a later cancellation has no effect. Preserve actual grants, conditions, entry, marriage/event, application, cancellation and notification records for the precise sponsorship/status/continuation assessment in [exception pathways](exception-pathways.md).

## Intended-marriage 309 applications

Subclass 300 is not the only possibility before marriage. Clause 309.211(3) provides a distinct application basis for an intended marriage to the qualifying person, where the intended marriage would be valid for the Act. No 300 holding is required by that limb. Prepare genuine marriage arrangements and the relevant intended-spouse sponsorship; a helpful proposed date is not proof of every criterion.

The ordinary 309.224(a) decision requirement is that the marriage has occurred and the applicant remains the spouse of the intended person. Do not promise ordinary 309 grant before marriage. Stored preparation knowledge distinguishes freedom to marry at application from the ability to complete a legally valid marriage by decision: unresolved divorce or recognition facts need the actual documents and a workable truthful chronology, not a promised processing window.

The exceptional 309.224(b) alternative requires both the applicable 309.221(1)(b) exception **and that the applicant was the intended person's spouse or de facto partner before death or relationship cessation**. Pure engagement does not satisfy that safeguard. A change to the relationship basis is not a free substitution of a new partner, a deemed 820/801 application or a reason to conceal changes. Pending-300 notice-based deeming is a different mechanism in [prospective marriage](prospective-marriage.md).

## Forms, channels and charge architecture

Before any proposed lodgement or addition, refresh the exact approved form, place/manner instrument, official channel, current Department instructions and charge category for the relevant date. Stored forms identify the online Partner application commonly known as 47SP (Internet); a paper or other alternative requires the actual written authorisation, directions and specified date. General email policy is not authority to lodge through any mailbox.

Keep these charge mechanisms separate; do not hard-code an amount:

| Mechanism | Preparation consequence |
|---|---|
| Ordinary initial 309/100 | The nil UF charge under item 1220A does not remove the BC charge under item 1129. Combined initial preparation is not universally free. |
| Ordinary initial 820/801 | The nil UK charge under item 1214C does not remove the BS charge under item 1124B. Current/former-300 concession categories have their own predicates. |
| Routine permanent-stage evidence | Usually already paid for with the combined temporary/provisional and permanent applications; it is not a second ordinary initial charge merely because a Stage 2 portal starts with “New application”. |
| Permanent Partner application by a 445 holder | Specific nil treatment under 1129/1124B, including the stated combined-with-that-person limb. This is not the charge for obtaining 445 itself, which has its own Class TK framework. |
| Newborn combination under 2.08 | The first-instalment components are excluded under 2.12C(2)(d). This is not every later-child addition. |
| Later addition under 2.08A or 2.08B | Applicable charge payments, if any, form part of the addition's requirements and deemed date. Neither universal payment nor universal exemption is supported. |
| Marriage-based deemed Partner applications | Regulation 2.08E(3) treats the TO first-instalment payment as BC payment; actual ordinary/review facts and child additions remain separate. |
| Exceptional separate permanent application | A ministerial grant without an existing combined application may require a prescribed application and the applicable charge. Do not assume either universal nil or universal repayment. |

From **1 July 2026**, official public directions introduce a lower-cost category for eligible specified Pacific Island/Timor-Leste passport holders, with family treatment linked to the primary applicant or visa holder's specified valid passport. This is a fee-refresh trigger, not a conclusion based on residence, ethnicity or regional description. Check actual route, date, valid passport, composition and applicable charge rules. Dynamic blank price fields are missing data, not nil amounts. Medical, police, biometrics and other costs are separate from the visa application charge. Do not infer refunds from a change of route, refusal, withdrawal or a nil component.

## Permanent-stage clocks and early assessment

For ordinary primary 100 and 801 cases, the usual two-year interval runs from the **original combined application date**, not from 309/820 grant, first entry, a later upload, today's review or a new portal label. Identify a legally deemed application date where applicable. Calculate calendar dates with a reliable tool; do not use a fixed 730-day shortcut. Record ambiguous dates or leap-day issues rather than inventing the legally operative anniversary.

Specified ministerial cases under 100.221(2A) or 801.221(2A) use the **Minister's decision to grant the provisional/temporary visa** as their clock, subject to the applicable waiting exceptions. The sponsoring-partner definition in those cases is linked to the partner at grant; formal sponsorship treatment differs. Current inspected provisions refer to section 351 or 501J, or repealed section 417. Do not copy old section 345 wording as an available current intervention power or advise how to obtain intervention.

| Early-assessment basis | Conditions retained | What it changes |
|---|---|---|
| Long-term partner relationship, both permanent routes | **At application**, spouses/de facto partners for at least three years, or at least two years where there is a dependent child of both, other than a step-child. Establish the relevant relationship, not merely dating. | 100.221(5) or 801.221(6A) removes the specified waiting limb. Later processing time does not retroactively make the relationship long-term at application. Other criteria and holding remain. |
| Permanent humanitarian sponsor, 100 only | Sponsor is or was a permanent-humanitarian-visa holder; married/de facto relationship existed before that grant; Immigration was informed of it before the grant. Evidence may include the earlier application, interview or recorded notification. | 100.221(6) removes the specified waiting limb. No corresponding humanitarian-specific 801 waiting exception was identified. This is different from a humanitarian applicant's de facto duration exception. |
| Death or qualifying cessation before permanent decision | Exact death, family-violence or child-rights limb and holding predicates; 309/100 and 820/801 conditions differ. | 100.221(7)(b) or 801.221(7)(d) permits grant within two years where its named exception applies. It does not promise timing or approval. |
| Particular exceptional 820 grant bases | Actual qualifying 820 grant and current holding, with the precise 801.221(3), (4), (6AA) or (6AB) link. | These are separate alternatives without the ordinary waiting limb, not a universal post-separation exemption. |

The two-year rule is not immunity from earlier refusal: the relevant permanent provisions expressly allow refusal within that period. Stored practice supports considering a qualifying long-term permanent case promptly after first-stage grant, but neither a waiting exception nor an internal priority practice guarantees simultaneous grant, a processing deadline or an applicant's right to immediate finalisation.

### Prepare early, but distinguish a reminder from upload authority

Stored permanent-stage reminder practice is around **22 months** after the combined application and invites preparation/upload. Current public 100/801 Gather/Apply directions and the Partner FAQ say to submit routine Stage 2 material **after two years**. Preserve that practical timing difference. Prepare and review early, but do not turn the reminder into universal permission to upload early. Inspect an actual request, any applicable early-assessment exception and the current official submission direction. Do not ignore a specific request solely because two years have not passed, or promise that every applicant will receive a reminder.

## Permanent-stage substantive preparation

For an ordinary continuing-relationship case, the relationship is assessed afresh on the evidence available at permanent decision. Preserve identity and original relationship continuity; the same sponsoring relationship must support the ordinary permanent route. Do not substitute a later partner into the existing permanent application. A marriage certificate alone is not the continuing relationship; being apart physically is not automatically permanent separation.

Build a factual timeline from original application through the review date, covering relevant residence, work, travel, separations/reunions, marriage and family changes. Update actual financial, household, social and commitment evidence rather than merely re-uploading old files. Separate-account arrangements, temporary separations and different cultural practices need truthful explanation, not manufactured joint records. Use [relationship and sponsorship](relationship-and-sponsorship.md) and [operational knowledge](operational-knowledge.md) for the full evidence treatment.

For the ordinary route, current public permanent-stage preparation includes a sponsor Commonwealth statutory declaration about the actual relationship and **two supporting statements from people aged 18 or over who know both parties and the relationship**. Form 888 can be used; it is not automatically the only method. Do not impose the section 48 nationality, six-week or statutory-declaration rules on every Stage 2 witness, or confuse the sponsor's declaration with a witness supporting statement or a family-violence declaration. Execution, identity/status evidence and translations have their own instructions. If the relationship changed, do not automatically request an ongoing-relationship declaration from a deceased, unsafe or former sponsor.

For 100, clause 100.226 contains a conditional nomination requirement: its trigger includes the elapsed two years and excludes the specified ministerial, death, family-violence and child-rights limbs. It is not a universal condition for every 100 exception. The ordinary 801 continued-sponsorship wording is separately expressed. Use the selected branch, not one common sponsor form demanded regardless of circumstances.

Health, character, integrity, passport, debts, values and family-linked criteria do not disappear because the first stage was granted. Prior permanent health clearance or a prior waiver can limit repeat examinations under stored policy, subject to changed facts, suspected nondisclosure/fraud, cessation history and actual requests. Foreign police lookback for permanent applicants is tied to the relevant 309/820 grant, not the sponsor's separate request-date lookback. Repeat AFP directions are conditional, not an instruction for every applicant to buy another certificate. Load [children and checks](children-and-checks.md) for the actual role/stage/country/request matrix. An examination attendance record or certificate inventory is not a health or character decision.

Secondary application/addition-time relationship criteria, 445's continued dependency at its own decision, and permanent secondary grant-basis criteria differ. Do not impose a universal new dependency test at permanent decision. Nor should a primary permanent grant be treated as guaranteeing the child's grant. Newborn citizenship, section 78 visa effects, deemed applications, ordinary additions and actual grants remain separate mechanisms.

## Visa effect, conditions and adverse events

Keep the grant notice's travel period, first-entry date, conditions and actual visa effect separate from the date a permanent application can be considered. The 309.511 period ends at the **end of the relevant notification or withdrawal day**; the 820.511 wording must be read separately with the actual permanent-decision/withdrawal event. Do not invent an instantaneous cessation timestamp or generalise one subclass's exact period to another. The permanent 100/801 travel facility is ordinarily five years from grant; it is not a statement that permanent stay expires after five years. A return after the facility ends needs its own check.

For 309 and an offshore-granted 100, inspect the specified first-entry date and any imposed conditions. Stored policy commonly uses a 12-month first-entry date, but the actual notice governs preparation. A missed first-entry date does not automatically mean the visa ceased; possible cancellation is a different question. Discretionary conditions such as 8502, or secondary 8515, must be read as actually imposed. No 820 conditions may be imposed under its conditions subdivision; that does not settle earlier visa breaches, cancellation, lawful status or bridging travel.

On refusal, cancellation, withdrawal or remittal, preserve each family member's exact decision, notification and application record. Temporary/provisional and permanent refusals are separate decisions; one review result does not establish every person's status or permanent entitlement. Use [exception pathways](exception-pathways.md) for primary versus secondary remittal distinctions. Do not calculate review access/deadlines, advise withdrawal, promise deferral, or infer lawful stay or visa restoration from stored summaries.

## Official sources for the selected question

The principal official reference is the **Migration Regulations 1994**: Schedule 1 items 1124B, 1129, 1214C, 1215 and 1220A; regulations 1.03, 2.08A/B/E, 2.12C and 4.15; and the relevant Schedule 2 subclass criteria. The **Migration Amendment (Location Requirements for Grant of Visa) Regulations 2023** and **Migration Amendment (Family Violence Provisions for Partner Visa Applicants) Regulations 2024** supply the named reforms and application provisions. Home Affairs' 309, 820, 100 and 801 pages and **Partner Visa Frequently Asked Questions** give preparation directions. Resolve an actual notice, historical transition or missing instrument through the focused process in [verification](verification.md), not an assertion that a whole visa family was reverified.
