## Runtime verification procedure

1. Identify the exact conclusion and its material facts: subclass, role, stage, relevant dates, location, grant basis and trigger. Do not ask for a full history when a single fact controls the check.
2. Read the authoritative substantive body, relevant provision/form/public section and surrounding conditions. Check issuer, title, URL, actual checked date, version/effective interval and any amendment/transition. A large successful response or loading page is not verification. Follow official links to the correct volume rather than inventing a dated body URL.
3. Classify law, official applicant evidence direction, stored policy/practice, illustrative supporting evidence or practical suggestion. Determine whether the new source supplements, clarifies, amends or replaces a **specific** rule. Later supply alone does not supersede all previous knowledge. Preserve exceptions, alternatives and discretion.
4. For legal conclusions, use applicable legislation and interacting instruments; for practical preparation, also inspect the correct public applicant/sponsor section and the actual request. Do not use public silence to declare an internal policy repealed, or generic upload directions to settle every branch-specific format.
5. Bind verification to the facts/conclusion it supports, not merely a URL/date. Maintain a case-specific source note outside reusable skill files. Give the applicant only short relevant source titles/sections/check dates and unresolved effects.
6. Refresh changing fees, passport concessions, form/manner rules, country certificate instructions, current portal limits and real deadlines when material. An extracted blank price is an evidence gap, not zero. Do not rewrite the installed skill automatically from runtime findings.

## Retrieval failure and privacy

If a source fails, try a materially different official retrieval method where available, inspecting real content and preserving the failure. Embedded official page data can be read where legitimately accessible; that is not account/portal testing. Do not defeat access controls, invent source text or transmit applicant data for research. Secondary summaries can be leads, not silent overrides.

If still unavailable, state the exact question, competing positions, missing body/fact/date, blocked conclusion and next useful check. Use Needs verification for affected compliance, not Missing or ineligible. Keep usable preparation work moving and do not describe offline knowledge, a recent export, a model answer or a structural check as comprehensive current-law verification.
