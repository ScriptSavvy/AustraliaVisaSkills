# Operational knowledge and evidence workflow

Read with the entry point and [verification gates](verification.md). This is preparation knowledge, not a legal eligibility engine. Load the substantive pathway references before applying their rules; a shared evidence method does not make subclass criteria interchangeable.

## 1. Decide what is actually being reviewed

A document list supports inventory only. A content review requires readable pages. A narrow date comparison is not a whole-application review. Record the applicant's task, route/stage and role only to the extent material, the review date, records/pages inspected, unavailable capabilities and exact unresolved facts. Do not make the applicant repeat volunteered information.

Separate these events in a factual chronology:
- Initial application/draft/payment/acknowledgement and any deemed or separate application.
- Application location and current location; neither establishes grant location or permission to travel.
- Temporary/provisional grant, actual grant basis, conditions, entry and visa cessation if material.
- Meeting, relationship/de facto commencement, marriage, living arrangements, periods apart, reconciliation, child changes, death/cessation and other relevant events.
- Department notice/receipt/request, response/extension outcome and any review/remittal.

Use actual records, not a visa subclass label alone. Original combined 820/801 and 309/100 applications normally continue to permanent assessment; a portal “New application” button is not proof of a new legal visa application or another initial charge. Ordinary two-year permanent-stage clocks run from combined lodgement, not temporary/provisional grant; exceptions and ministerial cases differ. Use [initial and permanent stages](initial-and-permanent-stages.md), not an inferred clock.

Calculate dates using reliable calendar/date tools. Do not replace calendar years with 730 days or invent a legally operative leap-day rule. Record ambiguous formats and missing timezones. A deadline calculation requires the actual notice and applicable receipt rules; see [application procedures](application-procedures.md).

## 2. Evidence areas, not document quotas

For an existing spouse/de facto relationship, review the total evidence of mutual commitment to a shared life to the exclusion of others, genuineness/continuity, living together or not permanently apart and relevant legal definitions. A marriage certificate alone does not prove every relationship element. Dating, a de facto relationship and intended marriage are not interchangeable.

| Area | Useful evidence examples and review method |
|---|---|
| Financial arrangements | Actual contributions and shared expenses, transfers, liabilities/assets, mortgage/lease/loan records, bank statements and bills. Separate accounts are not automatically a failure: record genuine arrangements and available evidence. No mandatory joint account. |
| Household | Actual addresses/living arrangements, domestic responsibilities, mail, bills and relevant shared care of children. A period apart or different issue dates may need explanation rather than a fraud finding. No requirement to have a child. |
| Social recognition | People with first-hand knowledge, invitations, events, travel, family/friends and relationship status disclosed to relevant organisations. Photos and social media are examples, not proof of authenticity or a fixed numerical quota. |
| Commitment | Knowledge of each other's circumstances, ongoing contact during separation, combined personal affairs and genuine plans. Wills may help where relevant, but are not universally required. |

Assess collectively, not by requiring one predetermined document in each box. Gaps can call for truthful explanations or alternatives, not invented records. Use [relationship and sponsorship](relationship-and-sponsorship.md) for the detailed definitions, additional de facto conditions and sponsor evidence. For 300, prospective marriage and shared-life intentions have their own test; use [prospective marriage](prospective-marriage.md).

For permanent-stage updates, cover genuine developments since the earlier application/temporary or provisional stage and recent continuation, rather than merely re-uploading old records. No universal monthly evidence count or fixed recent-document period is established here. Preserve previous application records to compare new statements truthfully.

## 3. Whole-stage evidence inventory

For a requested whole-stage review, consider every area below. For a focused question, include only the requested item and material dependencies. Unknown applicability is a verification task, not a missing mandatory document.

| Area | Trigger, purpose and evidence to consider |
|---|---|
| Identity and civil status | Current passport's relevant identity/signature/issue/expiry pages, genuine name changes, birth/civil records and relevant prior marriage termination. Collect only necessary fields/pages. Biometrics, photos and specific forms follow actual directions, not a universal copied packet. |
| Application and visa chronology | Acknowledgement, combined/deemed/separate application dates, first-stage grant/conditions/entry, prior exceptional grant basis, pending or already-granted permanent stage. Payment, draft and valid lodgement are distinct. |
| Relationship basis and changes | Spouse/de facto/prospective-spouse evidence, four areas collectively, living apart, actual relevant dates and changes. Ask safely about material complications; no demand for a trauma narrative or continuing relationship where an exception is being prepared. |
| Sponsor | Relevant status/identity, actual sponsoring role and prior sponsor history only when triggered; initial sponsorship and permanent-stage relationship declarations differ. Full sponsor restrictions exceed the criminal-history regime alone. |
| Declarations and witnesses | Ongoing permanent-stage public directions call for a sponsor Commonwealth statutory declaration and two qualifying witness statements; Form 888 is an option, not a universal substitute for every declaration. A truthful applicant narrative can help but is not silently another mandatory declaration. Exceptions may make current sponsor cooperation inappropriate. |
| Children and family | Actual applicants versus disclosed non-applicants, parentage/birth/status, prior inclusion, ordinary additions or newborn/citizenship mechanisms, custody/consent and relevant changes. A 445 child is not an ordinary primary Partner applicant. |
| Police/character | Actor, stage, age, country-by-country cumulative periods, request date or temporary/provisional grant date as applicable, certificate type/issue/attachment and new concerns. No automatic fresh AFP purchase or reuse guarantee. |
| Health and other checks | Prior clearance/waiver/request history, changes, new family members, relevant non-applying family and surviving integrity/passport/values/child-interest criteria. Attendance is not legal health compliance. |
| Requests and safe communication | Actual full notice, authorised recipient, deadline/receipt uncertainties, channel, attempts to obtain evidence, response receipts and written extension outcome. Updated safe contacts and changes use the applicable channel. |
| Charge/form/manner | Only for an application/addition/deeming or specific charge question. Separate ordinary combined charges, specific nil/no-component/deemed-payment mechanisms and other service costs. No hard-coded price or nil inferred from a blank field. |

Load [children and checks](children-and-checks.md) for all triggered child/health/character/integrity matters and a whole-stage review. Load [exception pathways](exception-pathways.md) where the relationship, prior grant basis, long-term exception or status is changed/uncertain. Do not mechanically request a deceased/unsafe/former sponsor's ongoing-relationship declaration.

## 4. Inspect six dimensions separately

For each relevant item record:
1. **Presence:** supplied, list-only, absent from review or claimed available. Do not equate “not supplied” with “does not exist”.
2. **Readability:** pages/fields actually legible, extraction method and uncertainty. Unreadable is not evidence of failure of the underlying claim.
3. **Completeness:** all relevant pages/backs/attachments, signatures, dates, periods and fields needed for the selected check.
4. **Consistency:** names/aliases, dates, addresses, roles, relationship and family details across actual available records. Distinguish a confirmed contradiction from a possible innocent explanation.
5. **Currency:** document issue/validity/request dates and source applicability. No arbitrary expiry invented for every document type.
6. **Compliance:** only against a verified applicable requirement, with actor/stage/date/conditions. Unverified law or applicability is Needs verification, even if the document is readable.

Use the five entry-point statuses and explain the check to which each applies. “Ready for the stated preparation check” is never an overall approval or validity finding. “Not checked” can describe a dimension not examined. A statutory declaration draft without required execution is not a completed declaration. Do not authenticate documents or treat page parsing as official acceptance.

## 5. Output and follow-up

Answer the applicant's actual question first after required notices. Give the concrete action that resolves a confirmed preparation problem. If a legal conclusion is blocked, identify the exact question, missing facts/authority and useful work still possible; avoid a generic referral replacing the answer.

For a broad review use [the report template](../templates/report.md): main findings/actions, brief scope and one canonical detailed findings table with evidence dimensions, then only material uncertainties and concise sources. Do not repeat each issue under several headings or dump all hypothetical branches. Keep any material conflict beside its effect and next action.

Interactive follow-up requests remain one numbered material question at a time. A list of preparation actions for later review is not permission to solicit all sensitive records now. Preserve the question ledger and do not repeat complete notices on short same-task follow-ups.
