# Application and correspondence preparation

## Stages and records

Separate Schedule 1 application validity, application-time grant criteria, decision-time criteria, later evidence updates, grant location and visa effect. A subclass with no primary Schedule 2 application-time criteria still has validity rules; secondary applicants can have their own application-time criteria. Use [initial and permanent stages](initial-and-permanent-stages.md) for route-specific location and combined application rules. A grant possible in/outside Australia is not permission to lodge an onshore application offshore or travel on a bridging visa.

An ordinary combined 820/801 or 309/100 application usually continues to permanent assessment without another initial legal application/charge. Ministerial/no-prior-application, child additions and deemed applications need their specific rules. Keep payment receipts, submitted forms, acknowledgement and application identifiers distinct; payment or acknowledgement does not establish legal validity, cure, backdating or grant.

Follow the applicable approved form, place and manner and actual Department directions. General email/fax or older written-process knowledge is not authority to lodge outside the prescribed channel. A written alternative needs its actual authorisation, date and conditions. Do not treat every sponsorship form as a universal validity prerequisite, or erase conditional initial validity requirements.

## Initial validity complications

Where refusal/cancellation since entry, section 48, substantive-visa status, specified regional visa history or recent Partner refusal is relevant, prepare the exact records for a provision-specific validity check. Do not choose a new application strategy or pronounce a waiver/valid application from a generic checklist.

Stored section-48 preparation includes a signed sponsorship form, two specified third-party statutory declarations with particular status/timing requirements, dependent-child rules and a six-week declaration window. These are **not** the ordinary permanent-stage witness statement rules. Verify current applicable legislation/instruments and the actual entry/refusal/cancellation/application history before applying them. Bridging travel and a 21-day recent-refusal restriction have distinct legal predicates; do not convert them into universal bans or deadlines. Inventory useful documents without presenting the historical details as a complete current validity audit.

## Requests and responses

Read the full actual notice, attachments and recipient details before preparing a response. Distinguish an evidence invitation, adverse-information response, interview, third-party check and unsolicited update. Record the notice date, stated deadline/timezone, actual/deemed receipt uncertainty, appointed recipient, requested items, channel and evidence of response.

Give an urgent caution when the supplied facts show a possible deadline risk. Do not invent seven/28/70-day periods, assume opening the email starts the legal clock, or calculate elapsed time mentally. If interpreting receipt or review law is necessary, explain the precise question and records needing qualified review. A dated document or screenshot is evidence of its contents, not a complete legal service finding.

An extension **request** is not an extension **grant**. Preserve the request and written outcome; while pending, plan around the existing stated deadline and seek appropriate urgent clarification. Evidence of attempting to obtain a certificate may help explain a delay but is not automatically the requested final evidence or an extension. Do not promise another invitation, a pause on every decision or an officer's discretionary deferral. A late response does not prove refusal occurred; non-response is not withdrawal. Refusal-timing restrictions must be read in their exact applicable provision, not extrapolated to every decision.

Use the notified channel and necessary identifiers only in an authorised private draft. A draft, sent email or upload is not proof it reached the correct application record or was accepted. Confirm substantive telephone information truthfully in writing through the appropriate channel, preserving copies/receipts. This preparation skill does not send, submit or contact anyone.

## Changes and safe contact

Organise material changes in passport/name, residence/correspondence addresses, contact details, relationship, children and relevant circumstances. Residence, mailing address, sponsor, authorised recipient and professional representative are distinct. Use the applicable current change procedure; do not assume every change uses the same form.

Where safety is relevant, avoid disclosure to an unsafe sponsor/recipient or use of shared credentials. Current public family-violence guidance discusses safe email/account arrangements and confidential communication. Organise the appropriate official private channel without operating accounts or promising privacy outcomes. Removing an application from one ImmiAccount is not withdrawing the visa application. Notification does not guarantee refusal/cancellation stops or every deadline extends.

## Withdrawal, review and visa effect

Withdrawal is a consequential authorised action, not file deletion, failure to respond or a new application. Family applicants' consent/guardian authority and both subclasses of a combined application require their own check. Pending 300 marriage directions request withdrawal material, but legal deeming and withdrawal are separate; use [prospective marriage](prospective-marriage.md).

Death, withdrawal, refusal, cancellation, grant and remittal have different consequences. Preserve the actual decision, notification, visa and review/order records. Do not infer refund rights, review standing/time limits, visa restoration, bridging status, continued lawful stay or travel permission. The [exception pathways](exception-pathways.md) retain preparation routes and the exact questions requiring qualified judgment; do useful evidence work without a universal status or outcome prediction.
