## 1. Start with the person, event and stage

A child disclosed in a family history is not necessarily a visa applicant. Citizenship, a visa already held, an application made by operation of law, addition on request and a later visa grant are different matters. Establish which one is relevant before recommending a form or payment.

Use volunteered facts first. During intake, ask one numbered question at a time and continue the question numbering already in use. Parentage and a parent's status at birth are separate facts, not a compound yes/no question. Unknown facts remain **Needs verification**, not Not applicable. For a narrow question, collect only what changes the answer.

For each relevant child, keep a concise record of:

- Birth date and country, legally recognised parentage, and any adoption, step-parent or guardianship issue.
- Each parent's citizenship and actual visa status at the child's birth, supported by the relevant records rather than present-day status alone.
- The parent's application class, lodgement or deemed-application date, temporary/provisional grant date, permanent-stage status and any refusal, cessation or review history.
- Whether the child was originally included, subsequently added, born during a pending application, or is only a disclosed non-applying family member.
- The child's own application and grant notices, visa class, primary or secondary role, whereabouts when addition is sought, and the relevant sponsor.
- Any parental-responsibility, custody, removal, consent or best-interests issue and material change since earlier assessment.

A child can be a primary applicant for subclass 445 but a secondary applicant for permanent Partner. Do not require that child to prove a spouse or de facto relationship with a sponsoring partner. Ordinary primary subclass 100 criteria are based on subclass 309 and specified exceptions, not a general alternative allowing every 445 holder to apply as a primary Partner.

This reference retains preparation and policy guidance alongside provision-specific rules. A stored policy practice is not a verified current exemption, an individual legal finding or permission to disregard an official request. Use [verification](verification.md) to select the rule applicable to the actual application, birth, grant and decision dates. Read [operational knowledge](operational-knowledge.md) and [initial and permanent stages](initial-and-permanent-stages.md) for the wider pathway.

## 2. Citizenship comes before a visa route

### Birth in Australia

Section 12 of the Australian Citizenship Act distinguishes birth in Australia with an Australian citizen or permanent-resident parent **at birth** from the separate limb concerning ordinary residence throughout the first ten years of life. It also contains a narrow enemy-alien/enemy-occupation exception. Do not turn birthplace alone, a parent's later permanent grant or current sponsorship eligibility into a finding of citizenship at birth.

Obtain the birth record and the parent's citizenship or permanent-residence evidence for the birth date. If the child is already an Australian citizen, do not prepare an unnecessary visa application for that child, but still disclose the family change appropriately. An Australian passport or citizenship evidence may be needed to document status and travel; the agent does not issue or conclusively determine it.

### Birth outside Australia

An Australian citizen parent may support a citizenship-by-descent application; citizenship is **not automatically acquired merely because that parent is Australian**. Section 16 has distinct conditions, including additional residence or statelessness alternatives where the parent acquired citizenship by descent and an adult good-character requirement where applicable. Under sections 19 and 19A, approval and the surviving statutory parentage condition matter to when citizenship begins. Record whether citizenship was actually approved, not merely whether a family thinks the child qualifies.

Do not assume an Australian permanent-resident parent can pass citizenship by descent. Do not equate an eligible New Zealand sponsor, a Special Category visa holder, permanent residence for migration purposes and permanent residence for citizenship purposes. New Zealand-related deeming rules, historical dates, disputed parentage, adoption and surrogacy need a targeted applicable-rule check. Until resolved, organise the records but leave the citizenship conclusion open; do not infer an answer from a questionnaire's questions alone.

## 3. Separate the newborn mechanisms

| Mechanism | When it may apply | What it does not establish |
|---|---|---|
| Migration Act section 78 | A **non-citizen born in Australia**, with the specified parental visa circumstances at birth. | Citizenship, a permanent visa not yet held by a parent, or a visa effect for an overseas birth. |
| Regulation 2.08 | A child born **after a non-citizen parent applied and before that application was decided**, subject to the contributory-parent newborn exclusion. Birth can be inside or outside Australia. | Automatic satisfaction of decision criteria, automatic permanent grant or removal of applicable sponsorship requirements. |
| Regulations 2.08A and 2.08B | A qualifying **written request** to add a person to an undecided application, with the relevant relationship, whereabouts, payment and secondary-criteria conditions. | A birth-time application date or an automatic grant simply because a relative was mentioned. |

### Visa held at birth under section 78

Section 78(1) addresses a parent holding a visa other than a special purpose visa where the other parent is included in that visa under section 83 or does not hold a visa other than a special purpose visa. Section 78(2) addresses both parents holding visas other than special purpose visas and can result in the child being taken to hold the visas of both parents. The kind, class, terms and conditions follow the actual parental visas. Verify both parents' records: do not simply copy whichever visa is more favourable.

For example, an Australian-born non-citizen may have a temporary/provisional visa effect under section 78 while separately having a pending permanent application under regulation 2.08. Neither mechanism by itself grants the pending permanent Partner visa. If neither parent's circumstances fit section 78, do not invent a visa at birth; flag the actual status question promptly.

### Pending applications under regulation 2.08

The child is taken to have applied for the **same class** at birth, with the child's application combined with the parent's. Identify each actual pending application. A birth before the ordinary 820/309 decision can affect both applications in the combined 820/801 or 309/100 process; a birth after first-stage grant but before the permanent decision concerns the still-pending permanent application. Do not tell every newborn to obtain 445 without first checking citizenship, section 78 and regulation 2.08.

The newborn must still meet the relevant decision-time criteria and any applicable application-time sponsorship, nomination or proposal criterion at decision. Notification is necessary preparation, not proof of final inclusion in Department records or a permanent grant.

### Notify and preserve evidence

Prepare Form 1022, a clear colour birth-certificate scan and, if the child has a passport, colour scans showing photograph, personal details, issue and expiry dates. Follow the current route's instructions: upload through the relevant ImmiAccount application or send to the processing office where that application is not in ImmiAccount. Partner instructions may also require notification through the Partner Processing Enquiry Form. Keep the transmission and receipt evidence and check the child's recorded details and affected application stages.

Do not delay notification solely because a passport has not yet been issued. Seek the specific outstanding identity/health instructions instead of marking everything complete. Apply [application procedures](application-procedures.md) and [document handling](document-handling.md); do not submit or change an account without separate authority.

## 4. Requested additions before the first-stage decision

Regulation 2.08B covers dependent-child additions to listed temporary/provisional classes, including **TO (300), UF (309), UK (820) and TK (445)**. The original applicant must make a written request after lodgement but before decision, stating that the child is their dependent child. Relevant whereabouts requirements must be met when the request and any required payments are in place. The deemed application date is the latest of receipt of the request and payment of the additional applicant charge and subsequent temporary application charge, **if those charges apply**. It is not necessarily the day the request was drafted or uploaded.

Regulation 2.08A separately governs qualifying permanent additions, including the relevant **BC (100) and BS (801)** applications. The written request, relationship claim, undecided parent application, applicable additional applicant charge and application whereabouts must be checked. The deemed date is the later of receipt and applicable payment. Application-time secondary criteria apply at that deemed date; decision-time secondary criteria remain to be met.

The general permanent-addition regulation mentions a spouse or de facto partner as well as a dependent child, but the Partner subclass secondary criteria do not permit indiscriminately adding a new spouse or every family member. Prepare a child addition for each affected stage; do not assume an update to one application proves combination with the other.

Original 300/309 secondary applications are combined family applications, not separate later applications of the same kind after the primary grant. While the relevant initial application is undecided, newborn inclusion and requested child addition are distinct routes. The 820 pathway also distinguishes dependent children from family members of a current/former 300 holder. Any non-substantive-visa, Schedule 3 or specified regional-visa condition issue must be routed to [exception pathways](exception-pathways.md), not treated as an ordinary missing-document problem.

A form such as Form 1436 may be the instructed way to prepare a pre-decision addition; verify the actual current form and channel. This is not a replacement for the different Form 1002 process for a 445 holder's permanent addition.

## 5. Later children and the subclass 445 route

After the parent has received 309 or 820, the **ordinary later-child route** is to obtain 445 and then arrange addition to the still-undecided permanent application. This is not a universal instruction for citizens, newborns, children already holding the relevant 309/820 or specified exceptional cases.

For the ordinary 445 route, check dependency and the qualifying parent's visa at application **and at 445 decision**. Clauses 445.221 and 445.222 expressly require continued qualifying parental visa holding and continued dependency. An intervening permanent grant to the parent is therefore not harmless to every undecided 445 application. Do not promise that lodging 445 alone protects the child's eventual permanent route or lawful stay.

The statutory qualifying-parent list includes 309, 310, 445, 820 and 826 with the relevant historical labels. Do not treat this as permission to use any parent's temporary visa. Sponsorship ordinarily follows the parent's sponsor/nominator. Clauses 445.223 and 445.224 contain specific alternatives following sponsor death or relationship cessation with a parental request for family-violence or child-parental-arrangement consideration; the special case of a child of a 445 holder has its own sponsor alternative. Establish the exact branch and continuing approval rather than assuming a deceased or unsafe former sponsor must cooperate. See [relationship and sponsorship](relationship-and-sponsorship.md) and [exception pathways](exception-pathways.md).

### Add the 445 holder before the permanent decision

A 445 grant is not itself an application for permanent Partner. Prepare the addition **before the parent is granted or refused the permanent visa**. Do not wait until after the parent's permanent grant.

There are two preparation alternatives retained for a 445 holder: the parent's written addition request under regulation 2.08A, or Form 1002. An uploaded or emailed Form 1002 can itself serve as the written request. Do not say the regulation invariably requires that particular form. Current public instructions use Form 1002 uploaded to the parent's application in ImmiAccount, followed by the Partner Processing Enquiry Form; where ImmiAccount upload is unavailable, they provide for uploading the completed form to that enquiry form. Do not rely on an old email practice as confirmation of today's approved channel. Full form/place/manner requirements and receipt remain a verification gate.

| Permanent application being joined | Where the 445 holder must be when applying/being added |
|---|---|
| **100, Class BC** | In or outside Australia, but not in immigration clearance: Schedule 1 item 1129(3)(d). |
| **801, Class BS** | In Australia, but not in immigration clearance: Schedule 1 item 1124B(3)(b). |

These are **application** requirements, not grant-location rules. The general ability to grant 100 or 801 in or outside Australia does not remove the 801 addition's onshore requirement or confer travel/entry rights.

## 6. Dependency, role and permanent grant sequencing

Do not apply a universal “under 23” rule. Regulation 1.03's **dependent child** definition excludes a child or stepchild who is engaged, married or has a de facto partner; it distinguishes under 18 from an adult who is dependent or incapacitated for work in the specified way. Regulation 1.05A's ordinary dependency test concerns substantial-period reliance for basic food, clothing and shelter and the relative source of support, with a separate incapacity limb. Regulation 1.12's **member of the family unit** definition has its own under-18, 18-to-under-23 and 23-plus incapacity/dependency branches, subject to its later provisions. Use the term actually used by the selected clause and the law applicable to the relevant date. A birthday, study enrolment or remittance receipt alone does not settle it. See [terms](terms.md).

Temporary-stage continuation tests differ from permanent-stage historical visa/grant-basis alternatives. Ordinary 300/309 secondary decision criteria require continued family membership; 820 has dependency/family-membership and a specific review alternative. Preserve those tests and 445's express decision-time dependency rule.

By contrast, do **not** impose a universal renewed financial-dependency or family-membership test at the **100/801 permanent decision** merely because the child has become older. The relevant permanent criteria use application/addition-time facts and specified visa and grant-history alternatives. Continue to disclose births, deaths, relationship changes and other material changes; a false original dependency claim or later visa cancellation is a separate issue, not cured by this distinction.

- **100:** clause 100.321 distinguishes the child's qualifying 309 grant basis, the 445 grant based on a parent holding 309/445, and specified personal ministerial-grant alternatives. The linked person must already have been granted 100. Do not import 801's differently drafted ceased-visa alternative into 100.
- **801:** clause 801.321 distinguishes current 445/820 holding, specified 445/820 cessation following notification of refusal of 801 to the linked person, and specified personal ministerial-grant circumstances with the relevant historical family/visa facts. The linked person must already have been granted 801.

Primary grant first is a required sequence, not a promise that the child's other criteria are met. A review/remittal does not automatically restore a ceased visa, confer bridging status or guarantee either grant. Preserve the actual grant basis, refusal and notification records and review orders. A specific 309/820 secondary review branch can address a family-membership refusal after the primary has received both stages; do not generalise it to every late addition. Use [exception pathways](exception-pathways.md) for the particular review/status question.

## 7. Children when a pending 300 application changes after marriage

The primary applicant's regulation 2.08E deeming is **not automatic transfer of all 300 secondary applicants**. Prepare each child's independently applicable newborn route or requested additions under 2.08A for permanent BC and 2.08B for provisional UF, with relationship, whereabouts, secondary criteria and charges if any.

For ordinary 2.08E, the primary applied for Class TO, then married the specified prospective spouse before decision under a marriage recognised as valid; BC/UF applications are deemed on the day Immigration receives marriage notice, not marriage day or later data entry. This is not automatic 820/801 conversion. From **14 October 2024**, the review mechanism formerly in 2.08E(2A)/(2B) is located in regulation **4.15(4)**. Its marriage, ART-notification and recognised-validity conditions include **ART deciding to remit**, with the additional applications dated on remittal day. Do not reproduce a universal mandatory-remittal claim or treat every judicial-review marriage as this route.

Read [prospective marriage](prospective-marriage.md) for the full route and historical transition. Preserve current public directions to provide marriage evidence, a request for 309/100 consideration, the requested 300 withdrawal statement, the ImmiAccount update and enquiry notification. A withdrawal statement is not itself a legal predicate in 2.08E and this agent must not withdraw an application. Establish the actual marriage/receipt/review records before any consequential advice.

Regional residence alone is not the test. Check the current specified-country list and applicable Schedule 1 category for the actual route, including 445 where relevant. Police, health, translations and biometrics costs are separate from visa application charges. Preserve receipts without treating payment alone as proof of submission or validity.

## 9. Minor children, parental responsibility and non-applying family

For a combined applicant who has **not turned 18**, keep both the primary-linked PIC 4015/4016 requirements and the secondary applicant's PIC 4017/4018 requirements visible. These concern lawful removal/parental responsibility and best interests; a child's inclusion can also affect the primary applicant's grant criteria. Do not confuse the decision-time minor check with the application-time values threshold.

The parental-responsibility provisions contain alternatives: the law of the home country permits removal; each person lawfully able to decide where the child lives consents; **or** grant is consistent with an Australian child order in force. Do not universally demand both a court order and the other parent's consent. Prepare the actual birth/parentage evidence, orders or consent and any relevant adoption, death or guardianship evidence; no single document is declared legally sufficient here. A parenting plan may help explain arrangements without necessarily being the order required by a particular limb.

Stored permanent-stage policy permits reliance on appropriate earlier custody/best-interests assessments, but doubt, changed custody, new adverse information or a newly included child can require fresh evidence. Notify changed custody or guardianship facts. Do not assume prior assessment is conclusive or demand new orders routinely.

A **non-applying member of the family unit** is not automatically outside health, character, security or debt-related assessment. The relevant primary-linked provisions have distinct applicant and non-applicant limbs. The non-applicant health limb includes a Ministerial unreasonable-to-require-assessment qualification; that is not this agent's power to exempt the person. A newly disclosed non-applicant dependent may need assessment even at the permanent stage. Conversely, not every relative disclosed anywhere is a member of the family unit under the applicable definition. Identify the relationship and clause before demanding sensitive records.

Child-safety-related sponsor checks can still arise at the permanent stage even though the specific initial-stage sponsor criminal-record regime does not apply to associated 100/801 sponsorship. Do not make unsafe sponsor contact or decide custody rights, child best interests or sponsorship approval from the records alone.

## 10. Police checks: keep actor and stage separate

Build a dated country-residence ledger, counting cumulative periods within each relevant country rather than requiring one uninterrupted stay or adding all foreign travel together. Record age, stage, grant dates, certificate scope/issue date, prior attachment, new relevant circumstances and the exact Department request. Use [relationship and sponsorship](relationship-and-sponsorship.md) for sponsorship assessment, consent and safe handling.

| Person and stage | Preparation trigger and lookback |
|---|---|
| Applicant at ordinary 300/309/820 stage | Public instructions call for an Australian certificate for a total of at least 12 months in Australia within the last ten years since turning 16, and certificates from each country with at least 12 months in that window. Include instructed Form 80 and military service/discharge records where applicable. Do not transplant the sponsor's no-Australian-residence-threshold rule. |
| Applicant at 100 stage | Foreign-country total of at least 12 months **since 309 grant**, including home country, with relevant age and country instructions. Stored policy also addresses certain 445 cases. Australian certificate directions are conditional, not a fresh universal residence test or mandatory repeat purchase. |
| Applicant at 801 stage | Foreign-country total of at least 12 months **since 820 grant**, including home country, with relevant age and country instructions. Certain 445 and age-triggered family cases require their own record. |
| Family applicant reaching checking age | Permanent public directions identify applicable police documents for dependants **16 or older**. A person turning 16 during processing is not excluded merely because younger at the first-stage grant. This threshold is not the values-statement threshold of 18. |
| Initial-stage 300/309/820 sponsor | Public instructions call for AFP and qualifying foreign certificates. Under regulation 1.20KC(5), a specified foreign country's cumulative 12 months is counted from the **later of ten years before the request or turning 16**. The Australian request power has no statutory 12-month residence threshold. |
| 445 child and child-safety sponsor checks | The 445 public page has its own character/Form 80 directions and Code 33 AFP instructions. Where the child will be under 18 at decision, its sponsor guidance requires an Australian check for any Australian residence since 16 and qualifying foreign checks; the sponsor's spouse/de facto partner must also provide the specified documents. Check the exact request and age wording for that route. |
| Sponsor at permanent stage | Stored policy supports checks for certain sponsorships involving an under-18 applicant in relation to child best interests, with possible reliance on earlier 309/820 checks unless adverse child-interest information exists. This is not automatically a fresh 1.20KC exercise or an exemption from all checking. |

Regulations 1.20KC/KD apply to the stated **TO/UF/UK applications made on or after 18 November 2016**, not merely decisions or remittals after that date, and not associated BC/BS applications. A later deemed application may have a different date from the original 300 application. Use the actual chronology, not an inconsistent historical table or a blanket grandfathering assumption. Other sponsorship and child-protection rules remain separate.

### Certificate scope, currentness and repetition

Use **complete-disclosure Australian Federal Police National Police Certificates** for the ordinary Partner directions, not standard-disclosure or state/territory police substitutes. Check the instructed purpose/code, names and aliases, country process and translations. The 445 page specifies Code 33, Immigration/Citizenship. A certificate or Form 80 is evidence, not a complete character/security finding.

Public instructions describe police certificates as valid for immigration purposes for **12 months from issue**. Plan timing against the actual request and current processing information, not a promised processing date. Permanent Partner instructions usually call for an AFP certificate if none was attached at 309/820 stage and may require a new certificate if the old one expired. They do not require every applicant to purchase a fresh certificate automatically and do not guarantee reuse of an old upload. The 820 sponsor instructions also say a previous 300 sponsor may not need to resubmit; the Department will say if required.

Retained sponsor policy guidance describes possible use of expired checks where delay was outside the applicant's/sponsor's control **and** no relevant offences were shown, and limits subsequent requests to sufficient reason about intervening relevant convictions or outstanding charges. It also describes an Australian check for any Australian presence since 16, including at ages 16–17, with a no-presence practice where there has been none since 16. These are **stored policy practices, not verified current entitlements**. Public general sponsor instructions do not expressly confirm the no-presence exception. No present sex-based exemption is established. Do not use sex, expiry, previous upload or a lack of residence to disregard a specific request.

### Unavailable documents and official requests

Identify whether the request is to the applicant or sponsor. Sponsor checks under 1.20KC are requested from the sponsor; its foreign lookback is request-date based. The regulation uses **reasonable time**, not a universal legislated 28-day deadline. Stored policy's 28-day guide, including evidence of requesting a certificate and possible longer time, is not an extension already granted. Preserve the actual request, receipt and response/extension evidence; do not transplant ordinary applicant notification rules.

If a certificate cannot be obtained, retain current country directions, applications, receipts, correspondence and a truthful account of efforts. Regulation 2.03AA has an applicant penal-document/Form 80 framework and a limited country-statement waiver where provision is not reasonable; do not turn it into sponsor-check waiver machinery. Stored sponsor guidance uses a standard Commonwealth statutory declaration describing inability, efforts and convictions/no convictions rather than the applicant-specific character declaration. A declaration is not automatically accepted or a granted waiver. Seek the particular Department direction; do not diagnose character, classify offences conclusively or promise approval. Keep any criminal or child-safety material in a restricted, necessary evidence inventory.

## 11. Health examinations and the permanent stage

First-stage 300/309/820 instructions ordinarily require health examinations. Timing and any health undertaking follow the actual HAP/request and route instructions; initial public directions describe a 12-month assessment validity period and a six-month undertaking where requested. These are not a universal rule requiring another medical at permanent stage.

**Retained permanent-stage policy:** a person who received a permanent health clearance at 309/820 or the relevant 445 stage, or had a health waiver exercised, is not ordinarily required to repeat health screening merely because permanent assessment has begun. The policy describes an exemption from additional screening, not simply reuse of a still-current examination. It identifies these exceptions:

- Suspected nondisclosure of a health condition or fraud at the earlier stage.
- Department information that health has changed since the relevant 309/820/445 grant.
- The temporary/provisional visa ceased **more than 12 months before the delegate commenced permanent assessment**.

Current permanent public instructions say the Department will advise whether repeat or additional checks are needed. Preserve the earlier HAP, examinations, clearance/waiver evidence, cessation dates, any changed facts and the new request. Do not order repeats solely because an old examination is over 12 months old; do not ignore a current request because the earlier examination was completed.

A child born, adopted or newly added after first-stage grant who never completed the required examinations may need them at the permanent stage. Newly disclosed non-applying family also needs its own assessment question. Earlier 445 assessment can support the relevant family-linked checks under stored policy, but it is not a blanket exemption from every surviving secondary criterion. Separate **test requested**, **test attended**, **result/clearance known** and **legal criterion established**.

### The limited health discretion is not a general waiver

PIC 4007 includes assessment/request and undertaking obligations, tuberculosis and public-health/community-danger requirements, and a distinct cost/access limb. Its limited Ministerial waiver concerns **paragraph (1)(c)** and requires the other visa criteria plus the specified undue-cost/undue-prejudice assessment. It is not a power for this agent to waive tuberculosis, public-health requirements, examinations or all health criteria.

The inspected cost/access wording also has a specific exception for a **minor born in Australia and ordinarily resident in Australia**. Do not inflate that exception into a waiver of all health requirements for every child, every newborn or every Australian resident. Organise the clinical/Department records necessary for the actual question without diagnosing, predicting a medical opinion or declaring a waiver approved.

## 12. Integrity, values and narrow public-interest exceptions

Health and police documents do not exhaust the public-interest criteria. Preserve applicable character/security, Commonwealth-debt, health, settlement-related, identity/integrity, passport and child-related checks. PIC 4019 values requirements are generally triggered by having turned **18 at the relevant application time** in the specified secondary/Partner provisions; this is distinct from police checking at 16 and from being a minor at decision. Primary 300 has its own values requirement and adult application criteria.

Keep the ordinary applicant criteria distinct before checking an exception:

|---|---|
| 300 primary, clause 300.223 | 4001, 4002, 4003, 4004, 4007, 4009, **4010**, 4019, 4020 and 4021. |
| 300 secondary, clause 300.323 | 4001, 4002, 4003, 4004, 4007, 4009, **4010**, 4020 and 4021; 4019 if 18 at application. |
| 309 primary/secondary, clauses 309.225/309.323 | 4001, 4002, 4003, 4004, 4007, 4009, 4020 and 4021; 4019 if 18 at application. |

| 801 primary/secondary, clauses 801.223/801.323 | The ordinary 4001, 4002, 4003, 4004, 4007 and 4009 limb is subject to the precise exceptions below; 4019 if 18 at application. Clauses 801.226/801.325 separately require 4020 and 4021. |

The primary-linked family provisions are additional and differently worded, not simply a copy of every primary applicant criterion for every relative. For 300/309, previous presence in Australia can also trigger **special return criteria 5001 and 5002** for primary and secondary applicants, with corresponding family-linked checks. Record previous visa/refusal/cancellation and travel history where relevant; do not decide an exclusion period or exemption without the applicable provision and records. These special return criteria are not interchangeable with a police certificate or a health clearance.

PIC 4020 concerns bogus documents, materially false/misleading information and identity, with distinct refusal-history limbs, limited under-18 exceptions and limited waiver provisions. Do not label a discrepancy fraudulent without evidence or assume correcting it automatically removes all consequences. PIC 4021 concerns a valid qualifying official passport or its specified unreasonable-to-require alternative; a missing passport is not an agent-granted exemption. Use [document handling](document-handling.md) for accurate correction and translation, and refer a consequential integrity question with the actual document and notice.

### Precisely limited former-300 branches

Do not treat all former 300 holders, all 445 holders or everyone previously medically examined as exempt. The surviving 820.211(5) branch requires, among other things, no substantive visa, **last entry on 300**, marriage to the intended Australian citizen/permanent-resident/eligible New Zealand citizen, cessation of a substantive visa **after marriage**, the required spouse relationship and sponsorship. It is different from current-300 and other relationship-change branches. Do not advise someone to let a visa expire to fit it.

- **Primary 820:** 820.223(2) disapplies only 820.223(1)(a) for the specified 820.211(5) applicant. Application-age values under (1)(b) and integrity/passport under **820.226** survive.
- **820 family-linked and secondary:** 820.224(2) and 820.323(2) require the specified dependent-child relationship and **entry holding a visa of the same class as the relevant primary applicant**. The family-linked exception removes only the named 820.224(1)(a)/(1A)(a) limbs; the non-applicant health limb is not generally removed by it. Triggered values and integrity remain, and **820.326** separately preserves secondary integrity/passport checks.
- **Primary 801:** 801.223(2) depends on holding an 820 granted on the basis of **820.211(3), (4) or (5)**. The first two are historical repealed bases, not new present-day choices.
