## Start with your task

Use your preferred language and only the information needed for the task. These are synthetic prompt examples, not applicant findings:

- **Focused question:** “Use partner-visa-preparation. Compare these two redacted date fields only. Identify any mismatch and the next preparation action; do not begin a full visa review.”
- **Prospective marriage:** “Use partner-visa-preparation to help organise my subclass 300 evidence. Ask one question at a time and separate proposed marriage evidence from an existing spouse/de facto claim.”
- **Initial Partner application:** “Use partner-visa-preparation to create a provisional document checklist for my initial Partner application. Keep application location and grant location separate, and ask only the next material question.”
- **Permanent stage:** “Use partner-visa-preparation to review my permanent-stage evidence. I hold a temporary/provisional Partner visa. Keep my original combined application date separate from grant date and show any upload-timing uncertainty.”
- **Changed relationship:** “Use partner-visa-preparation to organise the documents already available after a relationship change. Do not assume every pathway has ended or ask me to contact an unsafe sponsor. Start with one necessary question.”
- **Child or newborn:** “Use partner-visa-preparation to identify the evidence needed for my child's situation. Do not assume citizenship, automatic permanent grant or that every newborn needs subclass 445.”
- **Department request:** “Use partner-visa-preparation to turn this redacted notice into preparation tasks. Preserve the stated deadline, recipient and channel, and distinguish an extension request from a written grant. Do not send anything.”

You may volunteer relevant details together; the agent should use them without redundant questions. You do not need to share a complete history by default. If a permanent visa has already been granted, say so: a pending stage-two checklist may not be the right task.

## What the agent should do

After displaying the notices for a new task, answer an independently supported question directly. If intake is necessary it should explain once that questions adapt to your answers, then ask one material fact at a time with a numeric label such as **Question 1**. Unknown/skipped answers remain unresolved. Question numbering continues through follow-ups and clearer-page requests; an exact total appears only for a known complete question set. You can explicitly request grouped questions.

Expect the particular preparation finding, evidence, next action and material limits, not a generic referral after every item. A full review should show findings first and separate the six evidence dimensions. It should not require a joint account, arbitrary photo quota, automatic repeat medical/police check, new paid initial application at ordinary permanent assessment or unsafe sponsor cooperation without the applicable basis.

## Pathway-specific cautions

- One family skill does not make the legal criteria identical. Initial onshore/offshore location, decision location, original sponsor, visa holding, entry and event dates can change the required evidence.
- Marriage during a pending 300 application, marriage after 300 grant and intended-marriage 309 are distinct. A notice/deemed application is not automatic grant or authorisation to withdraw another application.
- Ordinary permanent-stage clocks use original combined lodgement, with specific exceptions. Prepare early, but do not assume a reminder alone authorises early upload; actual requests and verified exceptions matter.
- Relationship cessation/death/violence and child-rights provisions have different conditions. The agent can organise truthful evidence but must not promise legal sufficiency, force an ongoing-relationship declaration, require perpetrator contact or advise remaining in danger.
- Children require separate checks of parentage, birth/status, pending applications, actual visas and role. A disclosed family member is not necessarily an applicant. Citizenship, application addition and visa grant are separate.
- Fees, current forms, country check directions, portal limits and consequential dates require the applicable official source. Dynamic blanks are not nil prices; a source marked latest can still be outside its displayed period.

Read the [material verification limits](references/verification.md) when they affect your task. Unsupported legal/status/specialist conclusions remain Needs verification while useful evidence-quality work continues.

## Sharing and follow-up

Use relevant redacted pages or summaries when sufficient. Do not give passwords, payment credentials or unnecessary identifiers. Explain redactions that hide a field being compared. Additional external processing needs separate permission; the skill cannot enforce your host's retention/deletion policies.

Useful follow-ups:
- “Show only the confirmed corrections and next actions.”
- “Which missing fact changes this document's applicability?”
- “Which pages did you actually read?”
- “Show the official source, provision and applicable date.”
- “What exact question and records need qualified professional review?”

Preparation does not authorise submission, payments, withdrawal or contact. Installed copies do not automatically update. At the coordinated replacement, use only the intended active family edition rather than competing old and new Partner instructions; preserve personal customisations and case records before any replacement.
