## Purpose and scope
Prepare a tailored document checklist, review supplied evidence, or organise evidence for an actual Department request for Australian Student Guardian visa (subclass 590). Cover ordinary under-18 guardians, exceptional adult-student cases, bilateral-benefit preparation and permitted accompanying-child evidence. These are preparation branches, not permission-to-apply findings. Check preparation, not eligibility, application validity, authenticity, official acceptance or likely approval. Do not import subclass 500 Genuine Student rules or family permissions.

No submission, payment, third-party contact, account access, refusal strategy, cancellation advice, representation or waiver determination. Personalised immigration assistance can raise professional/regulatory obligations; a disclaimer alone does not resolve them.

## Workflow
1. **Define the check.** Record roles, stage and dates. Keep lodgement, processing, decision and post-grant conditions separate. Apply the relevant-date/onshore/child-inclusion gates in verification.md before assuming a route. Unknown facts support a conditional checklist, not a full Ready result.
2. **Refresh requirements.** Read actual official source bodies, not search snippets or loading pages. Check applicable law, effective intervals, directions, forms, fees and financial amounts for the applicant's date. Record title, URL, section, version/effective dates and actual check date. A stored check date is not today's verification. Preserve unresolved conflicts and continue independently supported work.
3. **Select evidence rows.** Classify each as mandatory, conditional (with trigger), useful supporting evidence or practical suggestion. Keep alternatives, exceptions and discretion. Policy examples are not universal obligations. Do not turn a general public summary into a rule overriding a verified exception.
4. **Inspect evidence.** Assign neutral document IDs and identify actual pages read. Filenames/statements do not prove contents. Compare identities, relationship/consent, nominations, student grants, intended residence, insurance periods, family welfare and financial allocations. Confirm a mismatch before calling it an error. Do not allege fraud from a name variation or an unexplained deposit.
5. **Assess each dimension.** Separately assess presence, readability, completeness, consistency, currency and compliance with a verified applicable requirement. Unreadable pages are evidence gaps, not proof missing content. Use deterministic date/calculation tools for all arithmetic. Do not invent exchange rates, fees, deadlines or visa durations.
6. **Answer first.** After required notices, give the direct supported answer, main problem (if any) and concrete next action. For a broad report lead with main findings/priority actions, then detailed rows. Keep material uncertainty visible without dumping research logs or every untriggered branch. Give a brief official source note for material requirements.
7. **Final audit.** Every material finding has inspected evidence or a declared gap, a source/type/stage and an actionable next step. No hidden uncertainty, readiness score, approval probability or claim that documents establish eligibility. No full-application Ready if an applicable mandatory issue remains uncertain.

## Status rules
- **Ready for the stated preparation check:** the named dimension is supported; verified compliance additionally requires applicable authority. Not a visa outcome.
- **Missing:** an applicable item is known absent. If not supplied for review, say that instead of claiming the applicant lacks it.
- **Needs correction:** a confirmed mismatch or defective supplied evidence; name document/page and correction.
- **Needs verification:** unknown trigger, currency, source conflict, unread contents, doubtful extraction or professional interpretation needed.
- **Not applicable:** evidence supports a false trigger or an applicable exception; never use for unknowns.

Readability can need correction while obscured contents/compliance need verification. A readable scan proves neither authenticity nor official acceptance.
