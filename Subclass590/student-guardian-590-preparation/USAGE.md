## Guardian-specific scope and limits
Onshore permissions, relevant application dates, children approaching six, newborns and guardian travel without the student require particular checks. Do not use a draft review as authority to travel, let a visa expire or lodge through an unavailable route. For a deadline, preserve the exact request and timezone; asking for more time does not grant it.

A GTE statement must reflect your true circumstances. Do not ask the agent to invent ties, income, custody or reasons for travel. A broad review uses the [report template](templates/report.md); a focused question should not trigger a full questionnaire. See [verification limits](references/verification.md) if a requirement remains unclear.
