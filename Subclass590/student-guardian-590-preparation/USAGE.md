## Start with your actual task
Examples:
- “Use student-guardian-590-preparation. Help me prepare a checklist to care for my child studying in Australia.”
- “Review the redacted documents I supply for names, dates, nominations and missing pages. Do not decide eligibility.”
- “Help organise the evidence requested in this redacted Home Affairs letter. Do not submit anything.”
- “Does this financial evidence explain how I can access the money? Answer that question only.”

The agent should answer the narrow question first where possible and ask only one material question per message. Before intake it shows progress such as **Question 1 — total not yet known**. It adapts to volunteered facts; an unknown answer remains unresolved. A report may list several next actions without asking you to answer everything at once.

## Share safely
Start with synthetic facts. For real reviews remove passport/account numbers, addresses and signatures unless essential. Keep readable dates, relevant names or consistent labels, relationship information and page references. A hosted agent may process files through its provider; check its privacy settings first. No additional external OCR/upload service is required. The agent needs your separate permission before sending documents to one, contacting anyone, submitting or paying.

## Understand the result
The review separates presence, readability, completeness, consistency, currency and compliance with a verified applicable requirement. “Ready for the stated preparation check” is not visa eligibility or official acceptance. “Not supplied for review” does not mean you do not own a document. “Needs verification” can mean unreadable evidence, an unknown trigger or unresolved policy—not a confirmed failure. “Not applicable” requires evidence.

Onshore permissions, relevant application dates, children approaching six, newborns and guardian travel without the student require particular checks. Do not use a draft review as authority to travel, let a visa expire or lodge through an unavailable route. If web access fails, the agent can still inspect supplied documents but cannot call unverified requirements current. For a deadline, preserve the exact request and timezone; asking for more time does not grant it.

A GTE statement must reflect your true circumstances. Do not ask the agent to invent ties, income, custody or reasons for travel. A broad review uses the [report template](templates/report.md); a focused question should not trigger a full questionnaire. See [verification limits](references/verification.md) if a requirement remains unclear.
