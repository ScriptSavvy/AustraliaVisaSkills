# Financial evidence — subclass 590

Read [verification](verification.md) first for applicable-version gates. Do not hard-code a living-cost threshold, fee, exchange rate or travel allowance from recollection. Financial declaration, prescribed evidence and genuine access are separate checks. Review actual contents, not an account-balance filename.

## A. Select the evidentiary route
Stored guidance requires evidence in all guardian cases, not the subclass 500 streamlined/regular distinction. Under 590.216(2), whole-stay sufficiency covers the guardian and **each family-unit member who will be in Australia**, not just accompanying 590 applicants. A family member holding another visa must not be omitted from that whole-stay check. Each nominating student is also covered unless the bilateral limb applies. Keep that whole-stay group distinct from the prescribed initial documentary-calculation categories.

**Bilateral funding verification gate:** 590.216(2)(c) excepts the nominating-student costs from its whole-stay limb for a bilateral case. However, the retrieved financial instrument's section 9(2)(c) prescribed funds-evidence route expressly includes the nominating student without an express bilateral carve-out. The historical preparation summary exempts student funding more broadly. Preserve that distinction and uncertainty: do not omit the student's prescribed evidence components merely by assuming the whole-stay exception carries across. Mark bilateral financial compliance Needs verification until the applicable instrument/criterion interaction is confirmed. Continue guardian/family and access checks independently.

Two alternatives need separate treatment:
1. **Prescribed funds:** relevant travel, initial living expenses, student course fees and applicable dependent/schooling costs. Use the applicable instrument's persons, period, permitted evidence and deductions, not a generic sum. Record each nominating student's own funding and what is committed; do not recycle the same money as both guardian living costs and student support. Deduct paid fees only where the instrument permits and evidence proves payment.
2. **Annual income:** the guardian's spouse/de facto partner's income, not automatically the guardian's own former salary or a friend's income. Current public guidance specifies the supporting partner is not travelling. Verify the applicable threshold and official government income evidence/date window; a bank statement/employer letter alone is not the government income evidence specified for this route. Inspect relationship and continued access/support. An annual-income shortfall is not necessarily a failure of the alternative funds route.

Money deposits, qualifying institutional/government loans and formal scholarship/financial support can be relevant. Property valuations are not prescribed deposit/loan evidence; selling/transferring assets requires evidence of actual available funds and source, not a paper conversion. A business balance is not automatically the individual's money.

## B. Calculate transparently
Use a calculator or deterministic date/code tool. Record application date, onshore/offshore context, expected start, intended stay, persons/ages, student fee periods, fees actually paid, currency and dated exchange-rate source. Show components and assumptions; distinguish prescribed minimum evidence from practical local cost-of-living planning.

Stored initial-period guidance starts at expected arrival for offshore applications or expected grant for onshore applications, ending at the earlier of 12 months or proposed stay end. Pro rata methods, school-age timing and different cost categories must follow the applicable instrument/current guidance. Do not infer an expected grant date as fact or extrapolate a stale allowance to every country. The current public page gives a per-person travel guide that differs from the stored onshore/offshore allowance; resolve or visibly qualify, not silently merge.

Living-cost, student living-cost and course-fee rows are distinct. Public guidance may omit a component amount while saying the component is required; retrieve its applicable legislative basis rather than count the missing figure as zero. A child under six at decision can reach school age during the intended stay; do not omit relevant schooling solely on age at application. Avoid double-counting a nominating student as an accompanying secondary child.

Do not produce a verified required total when rates, date-version coverage or applicability remain unresolved. You can give an assumption-labelled preparation estimate with the unresolved legal components visible; no unsupported upper/lower precision or Ready compliance status.

## C. Genuine access: inspect substance
For each funding item record owner/provider, relationship, issuer, balance/limit, currency/date, origin, restrictions, transfers, competing commitments and how it will actually support the stay.

- **Institution:** verify the relevant regulated deposit-taking/lending definition and prudential basis. A post-office/provident fund entity does not automatically qualify. A transfer into an acceptable institution can help only with source, withdrawal and genuine-access evidence. Do not claim an unpublished internal bank list is available.
- **Deposits:** statements, source/accumulation history, large recent transfers, owner relationship, transfer controls and restrictions matter. Term/fixed deposits can be usable if released and accessible when needed; do not automatically reject or accept them. Third-party or jointly contributed funds require an explanation, not a universal legal ban. Own/spouse/parent holdings can be more readily explained but are not automatically genuine.
- **Provident funds:** distinguish a fund manager from an account at a qualifying commercial bank, and accessible funds from legally restricted withdrawals. Keep terms, withdrawal records and transfer history. Nominal account value alone is insufficient to establish accessible funds.
- **Business funds:** identify the legal business provider and persons authorised to commit it, commitment/access documents and source/transfer trail. Individual shareholding alone does not establish power to spend a company balance.
- **Loans:** a legally enforceable institutional advance/approved facility can be relevant without all money already disbursed or a fixed repayment schedule. Do not demand complete drawdown universally or say every credit-card limit qualifies. Inspect lender, borrower, enforceability, available undrawn amount, permitted purpose, collateral owner, repayments during stay and funding of repayments without assumed Australian work. Older loans, student-committed loans and third-party facilities need availability/relationship checks. A loan obtained more than a year earlier may need evidence of undrawn balance or actual course/travel expenditure, not automatic rejection.
- **Government/provider/international support:** inspect issuer, lawful operation, duration, beneficiaries, amount, conditions and allocation. A formal national-government/commonwealth/state/international arrangement can support access, but doubt about issuer capacity or lifespan remains relevant. Provincial foreign-government support has a distinct national-support expectation in stored guidance.
- **Provider scholarship:** stored policy expectations include merit/open selection, Certificate IV or above and intake limits (no more than 10% or three students, whichever greater). Preserve these as conditional policy considerations, not universal legislation or automatic disqualification of all other support. Request the provider's actual award/selection terms if material and verify current treatment before compliance.

Genuine access remains separate from a computed balance. A recent lump sum is a question to explain, not proof of fraud. Do not suggest moving money to conceal its origin, count fictional transfers or demand arbitrary additional funds beyond the prescribed requirement as if legally mandatory. A practical budget buffer must be labelled a suggestion.

## D. Findings
Report funded components, evidence inspected, double allocations or confirmed gaps, access uncertainty and next action. Mark rate/version uncertainty separately from document presence or arithmetic. Do not conclude the funds are authentic, accepted or sufficient for visa grant merely because a mechanical total passes.
