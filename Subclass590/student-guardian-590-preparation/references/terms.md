# Terms for Student Guardian preparation

Explain only terms relevant to the task. Use applicable legal definitions before a compliance finding; familiar family words do not automatically meet a defined relationship.

| Term | Preparation meaning and boundary |
|---|---|
| Lodgement | Submitting through the approved process. Draft, payment and submission are separate events. |
| Primary guardian / secondary applicant | Distinct criterion roles. A disclosed relative is not necessarily applying; subclass 590 does not include every family member. |
| Nominating student | Student nominating the guardian on 157N; the student's own primary Student visa at decision is a separate dependency. |
| Parent / custodian / relative | Different relationship limbs; inspect evidence and applicable definitions. A cultural title alone proves no legal relationship or custody. |
| Family unit | A legally defined concept that can differ by context; distinguish family disclosure, under-six composition rules and permission to be an applicant. |
| GTE | Genuine temporary entrant: the truthful temporary-stay assessment used for guardian preparation. Do not substitute the subclass 500 Genuine Student (GS) requirement. |
| Bilateral benefit | A specific government-relationship branch, not any applicant's personal benefit or an ordinary employer sponsorship. |
| Substantive / bridging visa | Different visa categories; a pending application is not itself proof of a bridging grant or stay/travel rights. |
| Schedule 1 / Schedule 2 | Application requirements and grant criteria; a complete document folder proves neither validity nor eligibility. Other applicable law can also matter. |
| PIC / SRC | Public interest criteria / special return criteria. Legal health, integrity, character, return-history and other issues need actual applicable provisions, not a blanket packet of forms. |
| CAAW | Confirmation of Appropriate Accommodation and Welfare; relevant education-provider welfare evidence, not blanket Department travel approval. |
| OVHC / OSHC | Overseas Visitors Health Cover / Overseas Student Health Cover; do not assume student insurance covers the guardian. Other adequate insurance may be possible. |
| Policy / legislation / public guidance | Different authority and scope. A policy example is not mandatory law; a website summary may omit exceptions. |
| Grant / effect / notification | Decision, when a visa operates and communication can be different events; no notification is not proof no decision exists. |
| Authorised recipient / professional representative | Receipt of documents and professional assistance are different appointments. A disclaimer is not professional authorisation. |
