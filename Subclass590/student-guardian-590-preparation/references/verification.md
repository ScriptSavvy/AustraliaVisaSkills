# Verification, official sources and unresolved limits

## How to verify at runtime
The supplied preparation knowledge is not a declaration of comprehensive legal currency. Targeted official source bodies were checked **20 October 2026**. Do not present that as the executing agent's fresh check. Read the version applicable to the actual application/decision/test/event date, not simply a `latest` URL. Check compilation intervals, commencement and saving/application provisions. A later document delivery/export does not supersede an earlier rule by itself.

Authority order is applicable legislation, applicable ministerial directions, departmental policy and official public guidance, with practical suggestions separately labelled. Compare exact scope before claiming a conflict is resolved. Read actual body text; search snippets, empty fee templates, loading shells and inactive placeholder/sponsor content are not requirement evidence. If a source cannot be read, say what remains unverified and continue independent document checks.

For each material rule keep a working record of source title/URL/section, authority, version/publication/effective dates (unknown where absent), check date, subclass/role/stage, trigger and relationship to earlier guidance. Applicant reports need only brief relevant source notes, not the entire register. Preserve all material uncertainty in the result.

## Applicable-date and onshore gates
For applications on/after **2 October 2026**, S1–S3 replace the older onshore exclusion list and expired-student-visa summary. Under LIN 26/102, listed classes include 400, 403 **Domestic Worker (Diplomatic or Consular) only**, 407, 408, 417, 457, 462, both named 482 categories, 485, 600, 601, 602, 651, 771, 988, and 995 **primary-criteria holders only**. The table has separate 482 Skills in Demand and Temporary Skill Shortage rows. Do not treat 403/995 as blanket subclass exclusions or the table as every possible legal bar.

The substantive-visa route is not the only item 1222 route, but current **1222(4A)/(4B)** do not preserve a general 28-day grace period after any student/guardian visa expires. The former general expired-student limb was removed; the current defined-student-visa route involves specified cancellation/ART set-aside facts and further timing/no-prior-grant requirements. The regulation 1.03 student-visa definition does **not** include 590. Escalate a non-substantive/expired/ART case with the actual grants, cessation and notification records; do not compute permission from a website's simplified paragraph.

The new 1222(3)(aa) current-student-holder location restriction expressly concerns **500 primary** applications, not every 590 primary applicant. Current 590 holders are not categorically required to depart to apply for another 590. Do not certify a fresh application valid without actual form, condition and Act-level barrier checks. For applications before 2 October, retrieve the correct historical version instead of applying the new list retrospectively.

## Additional safeguards
- Student nomination is a decision-time dependency under 590.111; do not demand the student's grant before every concurrent preparation/application. “Student visa” also has historical defined subclasses; do not silently restrict the legal definition to 500 or revive a closed route.
- Part 590's parent/custodian limb for an under-18 student differs from its age-21 relative limb. S7's broad shorthand is not a substitute for the exact applicable criterion. Adult-student and bilateral branches have their own age requirements.
- Current 8534 names protection, 485 and 590 grant exceptions. This is neither 485 eligibility nor a complete validity/waiver opinion. Do not copy historical 'in practice only protection/590' as an exhaustive current rule.
- Financial threshold/evidence versions are anchored by **application date**, while Part 590 grant criteria are decision-time. There is no universal 'latest amount always applies' rule for historical applications.
- For known source defects, retain the exact original privately and use verified actual provisions publicly. Never silently repair a literal identifier as proof that the original was valid.

If sources conflict and authority/dates/scope settle the exact rule, explain that resolution with references. If not, keep the issue open. Uncertainty must not become a favourable assumption, an exemption or an unsupported negative conclusion.
