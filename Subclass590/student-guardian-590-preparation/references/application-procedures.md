# Application and request preparation — subclass 590

Read [verification](verification.md) before applying a stored rule to a current case. This is a focused preparation workflow, not a complete code of application procedures. Separate practical organisation from verified legal requirements. No account access, lodgement, payment, withdrawal or communication is authorised here.

## 1. Application records and route
Record actual applicants, disclosed family, guardian and every nominating student separately. Verify exact approved forms/channel and the applicant's physical location. Stored guidance normally uses online 157G plus completed 157N; a Department-authorised non-internet route is exceptional, not an applicant choice based on convenience. A system error calls for current official technical-support instructions, not an invented email application. Obtain actual written authorisation if claimed, preserve its directions/attachments and verify the applicable instrument and deadline before relying on it. Do not revive the expired historical pandemic form route.

The stored alternative-lodgement timetable is tightly tied to authorisation and Australian time. Do not copy it as a universal deadline, promise an authorisation or send applicants to staff-only contacts. High-profile/sensitive cases, expired passports or a tribunal event do not automatically authorise paper lodgement.

A fee receipt, payment attempt or draft form is not proof of completed submission or validity. For a submission-record review, inspect the actual saved application, acknowledgement, payment receipt and any defect notice separately. Refresh fees/concessions, additional-applicant/subsequent-temporary charges, payment arrangements and any claimed exemption for the relevant date; the public page's fee placeholder is not a verified price. Chargebacks/failed payment can raise consequential validity questions: organise the notice/payment records and seek prompt qualified clarification, not a promise of a repair opportunity or preserved date.

Onshore permission cannot be inferred from the older excluded-visa list or a generic statement that 590 can be applied for in Australia. Verify current application-date transitions, exact visa/stream, substantive/bridging status, conditions and prior applications. Do not import subclass 500 changes wholesale into 590. Non-substantive-visa/tribunal scenarios need actual cessation/notification records and applicable current law. Do not recommend deliberate expiry, travel or withdrawal as a workaround. Unknown status or bars remain Needs verification.

## 2. Family application boundaries and births
Stored ordinary secondary applications must be made with and combined with the primary application; different physical locations do not by themselves erase that requirement. There is no ordinary subsequent-entrant route in the stored material. Declaring a family member does not add that person as an applicant. Check actual combined applicants, relationship, age at decision, fees, consent and insurance.

Do not use ordinary child-addition language to resolve a newborn case. Distinguish birth during a pending application from birth after grant, birth inside/outside Australia, parentage and potential citizenship at birth. A child born in Australia to a visa holder and a deemed pending application are different legal mechanisms. Stored guidance contains broad newborn summaries that do not establish every current scenario. Organise birth/parentage evidence and both parents' visa/application status at birth; obtain official/qualified clarification of the applicable Act/regulations and actual notification/inclusion record. Do not promise automatic visa/citizenship, require a fresh paid application in every case or claim notification alone establishes status. Check actual current newborn instructions and health/insurance/welfare needs independently.

## 3. Official requests and deadlines
Classify the actual communication: document request, adverse-information invitation, interview, third-party result or applicant-initiated additional evidence. Preserve the complete redacted notice, requested items, applicant/application linkage, actual recipient/receipt facts, exact stated deadline/timezone, response channel and any extension correspondence. Do not derive a legal deemed-receipt deadline from an email date alone.

Create a request-item-to-evidence table. Record already supplied evidence and genuine missing items. A missing attachment is not a guarantee that the Department will request it. If more time is needed, prepare the truthful reason, efforts and expected availability; requesting an extension does not grant it. Keep the original deadline unless actual authoritative correspondence changes it. Do not say the Department must await every promised item or that a request automatically extends lawful stay.

Use date/time tools before saying a deadline has passed or calculating remaining time; record the current instant, actual timezone, relevant notice facts and assumptions. If legal receipt/version or timezone is unclear, mark deadline status Needs verification and advise prompt official/qualified checking. An organisational plan is not deadline advice.

Prepare response inventories/drafts using only the actual instructed current channel. Keep transmission/receipt evidence separate from content acceptance. Do not email documents by default, submit to a finalised application as if pending or assume earlier applications' documents are automatically in this one. Adverse information, disputed legal timing and response strategy warrant a specific professional question with the notice and evidence.

## 4. Changes and decisions
Prepare truthful corrections using current official instructions; preserve original answers and the change trail. Distinguish residential from correspondence address, authorised recipient from professional representative, and a pending waiver/request from approval. Record actual passport/contact/relationship/birth/welfare changes only when material. No notification has been made merely because a draft exists.

No decision letter received is not proof that an application remains undecided. Removing an application from an account, non-response, another application or not travelling is not automatically withdrawal. Do not choose withdrawal/refund/review strategy. Inspect actual acknowledgements/grants/conditions for consequential questions; a pending application alone proves no bridging, stay, work or travel right. A further grant can affect an existing visa—seek qualified review rather than promise coexistence. Stored officer contact-attempt and response-period examples are not applicant entitlements.
