# Evidence handling — subclass 590

Read with [verification](verification.md) and [application procedures](application-procedures.md). Practical quality checks do not establish authenticity, legal sufficiency or official acceptance. Never alter original records or invent missing facts.

## Inventory and inspect
Use neutral person/document IDs. Record issuer/type, language, issue/expiry dates, pages actually reviewed, relevant claim, evidence limits, translations and contradictions. Inspect relationship chains, name changes, DOBs, student/guardian nominations, consent, intended addresses, welfare periods, funding sources/commitments and insured persons/dates.

A file title is not proof of contents. A readable excerpt may be incomplete. Check reverse sides, continuation pages, seals/annotations and missing schedules relevant to the claim. If extraction fails or text/tables are garbled, request a better copy or approved local OCR, treating the contents as unknown meanwhile. Do not silently fill cells from nearby lines. Separate OCR uncertainty from an actual document inconsistency.

A signature/certification does not prove every statement true; a translation does not authenticate the original. Name variation is a matter to reconcile, not automatic fraud. Show the actual mismatch, page reference, possible innocent explanation where supported, and needed truthful correction/explanation. Do not conceal contradictory versions.

## Public preparation directions
The official subclass 590 applicant page retrieved on 20 October 2026 says:
- Translate non-English documents and include both original-language and translated copies.
- Australian translators must have NAATI accreditation; overseas translations must include the translator's full name, address/telephone and qualifications/experience in English.
- Use readable colour scans/photos; keep a multi-page document together as one file.
- Ordinary documents do not need certification under that page's preparation directions.

Follow any specific applicable request and current form directions; do not promise that an original/certified copy can never be requested. An AI translation for understanding is not certified/accredited translation evidence. Do not transmit files to a translator/OCR service without explicit permission.

## When evidence is unavailable
Name the missing record and fact it should prove, issuer, attempts/dates/responses and any genuine impediment. A useful preparation suggestion may be an issuer replacement/availability response, contemporaneous alternative record or truthful explanation. Label suggestions as such. An alternative is not a waiver or guaranteed accepted substitute. Keep known absence, not supplied for review, and unknown contents distinct. For a live request, preserve the deadline and any actual extension decision.

## Forms, consent and identifiers
Use current official forms and follow their actual signature/declaration instructions. In particular, inspect each required 157N signature and relevant parent/custodian support, and applicable minor consent/authority. Do not replace a person's consent with an agent-written assertion or assume an online declaration universally needs a wet signature. A court order or foreign-law custody issue needs qualified interpretation when material.

Health examinations, biometrics, photographs and police evidence depend on actual triggers and requests; they are not a default universal collection bundle. Do not collect biometric images or signatures in ordinary chat. Do not expose full passport/account numbers in reports, public examples or logs. Keep case records in a separate authorised task location, never in the distributed skill.
