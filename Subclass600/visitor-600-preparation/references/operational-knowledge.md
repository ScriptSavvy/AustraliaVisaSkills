# Operational preparation knowledge

## How to use this knowledge

This reference preserves detailed **stored preparation guidance**, including policy examples, discretionary pathways and officer-assessment considerations. Not every detail below has been independently corroborated by an accessible public source. It is not a statement that every rule is current law, nor permission to work, lodge, remain or travel. Read it with [preparation rules](preparation-rules.md) and [official checks and refresh gates](sources-and-refresh.md). Apply the applicant's facts, trigger and stage; distinguish a stored policy/evidence expectation from verified applicable legal compliance. Public sources supplement and may amend specific branches, but lack of accessible corroboration alone is not a reason to erase useful preparation knowledge.

Use this knowledge to ask targeted questions, choose evidence, identify potential issues and prepare precise review questions. Do not convert an example into a universal obligation or an officer's discretion into an applicant entitlement. Where currency, an exception, a source conflict or an individual outcome is unresolved, keep **compliance Needs verification** while continuing supported inventory, readability, completeness and consistency checks. Do not label a stored-only detail independently public-verified or attach a public citation that does not support it.

## Integrated procedural references

Read [application procedures](application-procedures.md) for form/submission, communication, requests and newborn/change distinctions; [document handling](document-handling.md) for translations, copies and unavailable-evidence alternatives; and [terms](terms.md) for role/stage language. Apply the Visitor-specific overrides; ordinary grouping is not a Student-style dependent route. Generic preparation knowledge supplements, not replaces, the subclass foundation.

## Common evidence and temporary intention (C03–C10)

- **C03 temporary intention and other visas:** consider prior compliance, intention to obey visitor conditions, purpose/duration, personal/economic circumstances and credible return plans together. A partner in Australia or a pending/permanent visa application is not, under stored guidance, by itself a reason to conclude temporary intention fails. Assess intentions **during the proposed visitor period**, not an assumption that every longer-term aspiration precludes a visit. Neither this guidance nor a partner relationship proves eligibility; retain other criteria and relevant adverse history.
- **C04/C05 funding:** accommodation/food supplied by family can change the realistic cost of a visit compared with self-funded touring. Stored evidence examples include itemised statements covering at least the last three months with source of funds, savings history and closing balance; earnings, investments, pension/retirement income; payslips; term deposits; business registration and business/personal tax records. The list is non-exhaustive and depends on circumstances, not a universal compulsory packet or minimum balance. Stored guidance counts access to credit only with demonstrated history of consistently clearing accumulated credit in full; obtain the relevant statements/payment history if relying on that pathway, but do not declare current legal sufficiency solely from this stored policy. A large short-held deposit can prompt an availability/ownership question, not an automatic fraud finding.
- **C06 support offers:** obtain a written offer from the relative/friend stating relationship, what costs/accommodation are covered and for what period. Stored guidance allows capacity evidence to be sought if necessary; current applicant-page host-funded instructions also ask for proof of funds. Keep informal support distinct from formal sponsorship. Support is not a substitute for the applicant's genuine temporary intention.
- **C07 air tickets:** stored preparation policy does not require evidence of pre-purchased flights; do not demand purchase. Plans can establish intended dates. ADS tour flight details are a different operational evidence context, not a blanket ticket-purchase rule.
- **C08 health/character:** health, character/security, Commonwealth debt, integrity/passport and return/exclusion questions must be separately considered. Stored character guidance describes police certificates at minimum where an applicant aged at least 17 would exceed 12 months cumulatively in Australia, for countries including Australia with more than 12 cumulative months in the last ten years since turning 16; concerns may trigger checking regardless of age/stay. This is stored checking guidance, not a freshly verified universal rule: refresh current character instructions and the request. Record relevant military/service records and declarations; do not infer criminal history from silence or universally demand Form80. Health examination types/exemptions and specific security/exclusion rules need their own current sources. Missing linked guidance is not an exemption.
- **C09/F02 biometrics:** ordinary collection depends on designated arrangements, exemptions and instructions. The stored Frequent Traveller process calls for collection before lodgement and no more than 12 months earlier; the read public page instead describes ABCC/email instructions and an After-you-apply section. Preserve both positions and obtain the dated arrangement/request; do not invent a merged schedule or assume older collection qualifies.
- **C10 consecutive authorisation:** the exceptional-circumstances branch concerns a proposed grant authorising more than 12 consecutive months across visitor,417,462 or bridging visas. Stored examples include extraordinary unanticipated changes beyond control, or staying to support a close family member because of death, serious illness or a serious medical condition. Prepare evidence such as a medical practitioner's description, why this applicant is needed and the specified support period. These are examples, not automatic exceptions. Consider current health/character checking for a longer stay. Extensive visitor time can still matter to temporary intention even without this consecutive-period trigger; separate cumulative visits, authorisation and physical presence, and refer uncertain legal counting.

## Tourist/onshore and sponsorship detail (T03–T06, S02–S05)

- **T03 students in Australia:** where a Student visa is held or has been held since last entry, distinguish completing a registered enrolled course from a separate graduation ceremony, examination resit or obtaining accreditation. Stored guidance recognises possible Tourist cases for the latter purposes; it does not make them automatic exceptions to600.222. Obtain enrolment/course completion, exact activity and dates, and verify the registered-course trigger. An offshore short-study purpose has different scope (see activities below).
- **T03 status/late lodgement:** capture current/last substantive visa, cessation, entry, criminal-justice/bridging status and conditions. Stored guidance treats the onshore more-than-28-days-after-last-substantive-visa case as unable to satisfy the Schedule3 timing criterion; verify applicable current law and any distinct timing provision before a case conclusion. The application-arrangement28-day language is not a grace period or lawful-stay permission. The specified403 Domestic Worker stream, No Further Stay, refusal/cancellation and employment-search scenarios need separate review. Stored guidance recommends consideration of continuing8501 insurance where relevant, subject to sponsored/financial-hardship branches; do not invent a condition from that recommendation.
- **T04 formal Tourist sponsorship:** stored policy treats Department-required sponsorship as exceptional, generally offshore and where it may be the deciding factor, not a routine applicant choice. Legal relationship/family-unit, approved/in-force sponsorship and related-relative grant requirements remain distinct. Failure of common criteria is not cured by a sponsor or bond. Inventory the actual sponsorship/security request before preparing that branch.
- **T05 hardship:** retain the conjunctive branch: changed circumstances after entry causing financial hardship; applicant/immediate family likely to become a public charge; inability to leave for reasons beyond control; compelling personal reasons to work. Relevant records can prepare a request/review question, but hardship alone does not remove8101 from an existing grant or create work permission. Actual conditions and an appropriate granted outcome control; qualified review is required.
- **T06 parent/longer visits:** stored guidance contemplates longer travel validity and stay for parents, including parent-visa-queue cases, particularly lower-risk/compliant repeat visitors; examples include three/five-year validity with12-month stays. These are policy considerations, not entitlements. Keep validity, per-entry stay, number of entries,8558 cumulative-stay restriction and8501 insurance separate; current pages/actual conditions can differ. Prepare relationship/status, purpose and full-period support evidence rather than promise a grant length.
- **S02 settled sponsors:** stored guidance uses lawful residence for a reasonable period with individual flexibility. Examples include long-term temporary residence followed by recent permanent residence; a usually resident permanent resident temporarily abroad who will be in Australia with the visitor; a citizen returning after years abroad who has purchased a home and obtained work. Prepare relevant residence/status/return evidence; no example is an automatic approval or fixed statutory two-year rule. Public guidance's usual two years/compassionate flexibility is separately recorded. Apply relative/family-unit, public-office and government-body pathways according to their own criteria.
- **S02/S03 family-unit ordering:** where an applicant is not directly related to an individual sponsor but relies on the qualifying family-unit pathway, stored guidance requires the related applicant's Sponsored Family visa to be granted before the non-relative applicant's grant. A single Form1149 can list multiple applicants, but every separate online application needs the attachment. Simultaneous applications are treated in stored guidance as simultaneous, not previous applicants merely because listed in a sequence. Do not use this to waive a genuine prior-grant restriction.
- **S03 in-force/withdrawal:** approved sponsorship must remain in force at decision. There is no stored fixed approval-to-grant validity period; a long delay can require confirmation that the sponsor remains capable and willing. Death, cessation of qualifying office/employment and withdrawal can affect it. Stored guidance contemplates another sponsorship being invited after pre-grant withdrawal; not a guaranteed opportunity. Post-grant release from undertakings is limited and needs written Department agreement; cancellation may be considered. Release from undertakings is separate from an existing security bond.
- **S03 prior sponsorship:** keep regulation1.20L's current-visa restriction and ceased-visa/non-compliance/five-years-since-grant branch separate. Family-unit/same-purpose approval is discretionary. A beyond-control/post-entry8531 overstay exception requires the relevant written determination, not self-assessment. Stored examples include serious accident/illness/unfitness to depart, death, close-family death/illness requiring support after arrival, short-notice flight cancellation without a viable alternative, unexpected unrest/serious health crisis, and relevant waiver/bond-refund circumstances. Gather timeline and evidence; none automatically lifts a bar.
- **S04 bonds:** bonds are requested, not mandatory for every sponsorship; unwillingness/inability where actually required can affect finalisation. Stored guidance treats bond lodgement independently from genuine temporary intention and other criteria. It envisages refund after compliant departure, and specified Partner/protection-grant situations on written request/verification; breaches can lead to forfeiture irrespective of cancellation. Unforeseen inability to depart may support a refund/reconsideration request with evidence; ignorance of the permitted stay is not such evidence. A repeat reconsideration needs genuinely new material under stored guidance. Do not promise refund, choose an old contact address, pay or submit; verify the actual bond/request and current authorised process.
- **S05 duration:** stored Sponsored Family policy limits the grant to the period for which the sponsor has agreed to obligations. Check the proposed sponsorship period against requested stay; do not describe this as an independently verified statutory cap or guarantee. Mandatory condition implications, including8503/8531, remain separate from policy duration/entry considerations.

## Minor consent, evidence and welfare (M01–M03, C11)

- **Both responsible parents/guardians travel:** stored guidance generally does not seek additional family-relationship proof once satisfied, especially linked family applications/prior family travel. Different names/addresses, one person's absent application or identity/integrity concerns can justify further evidence. Current applicant instructions may still request birth/identity documents; do not silently waive them using a stored usual practice.
- **One parent/guardian/relative travels, or all responsible persons not established:** prepare consent from the non-accompanying responsible persons using Form1229 or an acceptable consent statutory declaration, plus identification/signature and relationship/responsibility evidence. Court orders and lawful-removal alternatives are distinct legal pathways, not a blanket demand for both parents' signatures. Stored certified-copy examples do not override current routine no-certification guidance or a specific request.
- **Other travelling companion:** stored guidance expects consent plus a welfare undertaking. Form1257 is the preferred undertaking method, completed by the person nominated by the parent/guardian for accommodation/support/general welfare. It also recognises a written undertaking, including a statutory declaration, by a person who will be in Australia for the child's intended stay. Preserve this alternative rather than assert that only Form1257 can satisfy the undertaking; verify its applicable acceptance and wording before a compliance conclusion.
- **Travel companion and accommodation differ:** current Tourist/Business/Frequent Traveller page instructions use not staying with a relative/legal guardian for Form1257; stored companion guidance and PIC4012's compound trigger are not identical. Ask both questions. Family/ADS page omission of that instruction is not proof of repeal or blanket exemption.
- **Organised tours:** stored guidance does not require the specific undertaking declaration where a genuine organised tour has adequate maintenance/welfare/support for the whole stay. A principal's official letter describing a school group under teachers is an evidence example. Verify actual tour arrangements and applicable PIC4012; mere membership of a group does not prove adequate welfare.
- **Repeat/onshore applications:** stored guidance for an under-18 onshore application requires continued parental/guardian consent and a new relevant Form1229 and/or1257 for each application. It does not routinely re-request already-established family relationships (such as a previously supplied birth certificate) absent identity/integrity concerns. Preserve this onshore scope, not a blanket new-application rule for every stream/location; refresh current request/form requirements. Inventory dates, scope and changed arrangements.
- **Court duration/welfare:** stored guidance constrains a proposed validity/stay by the relevant custody order's permitted period; compare the requested visit to the exact order instead of promising a grant. Unresolved responsibility disputes, freely given consent, abuse allegations, vulnerable companions, caregiver character, adoption/surrogacy, citizenship/Child-visa plans and welfare concerns require qualified review. Best interests is not established from consent alone or a document checklist. Where a child turns18 before planned travel, stored guidance contemplates decision timing without demanding irrelevant under-18 documents; do not assume a delay or decide the applicable age test yourself.

## Non-standard activities (X01, B02/B03, T03)

**All rows are stored, fact-sensitive preparation guidance. They preserve qualified possibilities and restrictions, not freshly independently verified work permission.** For each row gather precise duties, Australian recipient/organisation, payment/reimbursement/prize arrangements, duration, course credit, current visa condition and actual purpose. Verify current law/related official guidance before a legal-activity conclusion. Unpaid or overseas-paid alone is not a universal safe harbour.

| Activity | Stored conditions, distinctions and preparation evidence |
|---|---|
| Domestic family support | A short family visit with non-ongoing child minding/care (for example around a daughter's childbirth) is treated as family activity rather than work. Ongoing long-term childcare/medical care needs other arrangements; ongoing care of international students is generally not a visitor purpose. Document relationship, temporary need and end date. |
| Domestic assistant accompanying overseas employer | May be considered for a short visit of three months or less with the existing overseas employer (for example their nanny), recognising continuing overseas employment. This differs from providing domestic work/services to an Australian-based family or a longer stay, including volunteering, which stored guidance does not accept. Do not generalise the accompanying-employer exception. |
| Employment vetting/skills assessment | Business Visitor may accommodate interviews/testing/in-person assessment without undertaking or commencing work. Obtain assessment invitation and duties; actual employment requires an appropriate work-rights pathway. |
| Entertainers | Paid/ticketed performance or work in entertainment/media is not the stored visitor pathway. Limited unpaid cultural/school one-off events may fit Business Visitor where no remuneration/performance payment from an Australian organisation; verify duties and event/payment facts, not merely an 'unpaid' label. Other temporary-activity/work pathways may need review. |
| Internship/work experience/student placement | Stored pathway requires credit towards the applicant's university course in their usual residence country and compliance with the maximum-three-month study limit. Healthcare placements may require additional health examinations. Obtain university credit confirmation, placement terms, duties/dates and health-setting facts; no blanket internship permission. |
| Investor | Overseas-based investors may be considered for initial establishment activity, including a requested initial stay up to six months where relevant arrangements support it. Continuing administration/operation is distinct work; no automatic investment/business-operation entitlement. |
| Journalists/media | Working media/production is generally not a visitor activity. A limited short-stay freelance photographer/writer/journalist pathway is described where no sale of work in Australia and no Australian-organisation remuneration; guest-of-government accompanying representatives are a separate context. Verify actual commercial/publication/payment arrangements and any current concession, not an automatic fee exemption. |
| Maritime/crew | Professional ongoing work within the migration zone or vessel fit-out in an Australian port is distinguished from a short under-three-month stopover for supplies/maintenance, passenger/embark/disembark travel, outside-zone shore leave and maritime-crew joining/end-of-service tourism. Proposed multiple-entry/validity practices are discretionary, not promised. Actual maritime, transit, crew and substantive-visa interactions need review. |
| Round-trip cruise | An approved Australia-origin/end round trip can count as not leaving Australia. Stored conditions include no foreign-country travel other than transit, remaining passenger/crew on that vessel outside the zone, and the prescribed outside-zone period (stored value30days; verify current period). A cruise does not necessarily reset stay; include pre/cruise/post time and actual grant. |
| Online work/influencers/digital nomads | Stored guidance generally accommodates continuing online work during short visits unless paid by an Australian organisation (for example a promotional event). It describes short-stay self-employment or overseas employment without an Australian office. These qualifications do not settle mixed local services or paid promotions; collect local links/duties/remuneration and verify8115/8101, not 'overseas payer means allowed'. |
| Pilots/airline crew | Private-plane travel/disembarking international passengers differs from ongoing flying in/out that could affect Australian employment. Commercial crew's special-purpose/crew-authority travel is not tourism permission; a separate visitor trip/visa and effect/entry interaction may need review. No universal exemption or CTA-for-holiday advice. |
| Religious activity | Short up-to-three-month events/meetings/retreats/training or part-time spiritual volunteering may fit. Full-time paid/unpaid organisational placements and conducting ministry/services are distinct and not the stored visitor pathway. Obtain role/time/payment and organisation facts; alternative activity visas may be relevant. |
| Researchers/academics | Short conferences/meetings/lectures/research without remuneration or equivalent expense reimbursement may fit Business Visitor. Overseas-course exchange study/research up to three months is a distinct pathway. Australian-institution remuneration or ongoing research beyond three months is not the stored visitor pathway; examine payment substance, not its label. |
| Service sellers | Overseas representatives negotiating service sales/agreements may request initial stay up to six months, deriving remuneration/support wholly abroad, without personally supplying services, direct public sales or work otherwise done by Australians. Relevant trade arrangements matter; no automatic six-month grant. |
| Sport | Short under-three-month unpaid/non-performance-paid amateur athlete/coach/judge/support visits, spectators and family differ from professional national-level/major-event work. Stored amateur indicators include participation without remuneration/equivalent prize money and/or primary income from other paid work. Examine payment/prize/duties and support for longer stays; 'amateur' label alone is insufficient. |
| Study/student guardians | Offshore Tourist/Business Visitor stored guidance allows study/training up to three months as a main purpose, including accredited/non-accredited study; public guidance says a Student visa might be more appropriate. The onshore Student-since-entry registered-course restriction remains separate. Extended care of a dependent student generally points to Student Guardian; short parent visits and lower-risk adult-student/guardian-partner visits are fact-sensitive policy considerations, not guaranteed periods. |
| Tour leaders/chaperones | Teachers/chaperones may provide short group support, language/organisation/administration excluding local guiding. ADS groups require their approved-agent representative leader under stored guidance; Business Visitor may be relevant only without local tour-guide work. Gather exact duties and agent/group arrangements. |
| Tournaments | Short eSports/gambling/chess and other tournaments may be considered where the applicant is not a professional receiving a performance payment. Prize/payment substance, activity and actual condition remain relevant. |
| Volunteering | Stored Tourist pathway is non-full-time, unpaid, with a not-for-profit, for less than three months; excludes roles normally remunerated for Australians and exploitation concerns. Preserve all restrictions, not blanket permission or blanket ban. |
| Business meetings | Overseas-employed/based directors/board/business representatives attending short meetings may be considered; repeat low-risk travel may attract longer multiple-entry validity under stored policy. No promised validity; Australian employment effects remain relevant. |
| One-off court attendance | Stored Tourist suitability requires a confirmed one-off date, temporary intention, support without work and all other criteria. Protracted uncertain litigation and criminal-history questions need separate review; criminal-justice pathways may be more appropriate. |
| Pacific-Australia Card | Stored guidance uses card evidence for facilitation/priority and longer multiple-entry validity considerations, with shorter validity possible for health/card-renewal concerns. Verify current programme/fee arrangements and actual validity; no guaranteed priority, fee concession or grant period. |

## Specialist touring, grants and existing visas (A01, F01/F02, X02)

- **ADS:** maintain PRC citizenship, PRC application/grant location, specified residence area, exact currently approved agent, sightseeing/related-purpose and touring statement. Stored touring details include dates, flight arrangements, group leader and whether separately funded members receive comparable accommodation/dining/sightseeing in the same city. The replacement approved-agent table is a specific verified update; the specified-area instrument remains separate. Preserve8101/8207/8503/8530 distinctions and group travel obligations; no ordinary study allowance.
- **Frequent Traveller:** expanded passport/nationality arrangements supersede the stored China-only application description specifically; do not retain it as current or assume a wholesale change to other policy. Stored standard-ten-year grant practice differs from legislation/public **up to** ten years; no guarantee. Keep per-entry three-month stay and cumulative12-in24 limits separate from travel validity. Read actual conditions and retain the unresolved biometrics sequence above.
- **Existing/pending visas:** stored guidance requires avoiding unintended loss of a more beneficial permanent/work-rights visa when another substantive visa comes into effect. Capture the existing grant/travel facility, desired status and proposed visitor effect; Act82 exceptions and specific deemed grants matter. An expired permanent travel facility normally raises Resident Return rather than routine visitor-fix planning; visitor grant may affect permanent/citizenship/residence entitlements and needs qualified review.
- **Pending onshore application and overseas return:** stored guidance requires appropriate travel/re-entry permission before departure; BVB must be granted before departure where relied on. If departed without re-entry permission, a visitor pathway can be considered under stored guidance only with genuine temporary intention for the visitor period, intended compliance, all criteria and no relevant adverse information. Some pending visa decisions require onshore presence; this is a complex interaction, not a guarantee that a visitor application restores status/bridging rights.
- **APEC/ABTC:** stored guidance describes an associated Business Visitor visa where AUS is on the card. A further ordinary visitor grant can cease that associated visa; avoid a routine replacement without checking effects. The narrow deemed-application600.513 mechanism can defer effect until another substantive visa ceases, and can mean the visitor visa never takes effect if its period ends first. It is not a general delayed-commencement rule for every visitor grant. Obtain card/associated grant and current scheme provisions; do not contact, withdraw or submit without separate authority.

## Before returning a report

For each adopted detail state whether it is an official applicant instruction, an applicable legal provision actually verified, a stored policy/evidence expectation, an illustrative possibility or a practical suggestion. Show the triggering facts and stage. Missing stored-preparation evidence is a preparation gap, not automatically unlawful lodgement or legal ineligibility. Keep genuine contradictions, unknown currency and case-specific outcome questions explicit, but do not reduce the checklist's expertise merely because public corroboration of a stored detail is unavailable.
