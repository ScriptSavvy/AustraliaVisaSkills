# Preparation rules and official-source branches

Read with [sources and refresh gates](sources-and-refresh.md). Numbered references identify official sources there; named sections below delimit their support. These are preparation instructions, not an eligibility decision. Legislative descriptions refer to the retrieved dated text: refresh the applicable version, amendments and case instructions before a current compliance finding. A document example is not automatically a mandatory document. Read the required [operational knowledge](operational-knowledge.md) for detailed stored policy guidance and exceptions alongside these official-source comparisons. Absence of accessible corroboration alone does not remove a preparation branch: distinguish stored guidance from independently verified applicable legal compliance. **Unknown applicability, current compliance and genuine unresolved conflicts remain Needs verification.**

## Tourist stream

- **T01 Purpose:** identify actual activities. Tourist → With this visa and clause 600.221 describe visiting family/friends, tourism and other purposes not related to business or medical treatment. Do not rely solely on a declared “holiday”.[3][2]
- **T02 Location:** offshore Tourist guidance requires outside Australia at application and decision; onshore guidance requires in Australia at application and decision. Clauses 600.411–412 distinguish application from grant location. Record both; travel plans can create a verification issue.[3][4][2]
- **T03 Onshore safeguards:** obtain current/last visa, conditions, last entry and cessation dates, any Student visa since last entry, and relevant refusal/cancellation facts. Clauses 600.222–223 address enrolled registered-course purposes and the Subclass 403 Domestic Worker (Diplomatic or Consular) exclusion; absence of a substantive visa engages Schedule 3 criteria 3001, 3003, 3004, 3005. Stored guidance recognises possible graduation/resit/accreditation cases; preserve their fact-sensitive distinctions in operational-knowledge.md, not automatic exceptions to the registered-course restriction. LIN section 5 provides ImmiAccount, a Department-written-notice alternative, and before/within-28-days-after substantive/criminal-justice-visa cessation timing; this is **not** a grace period, lawful-stay permission or guaranteed grant. No Further Stay, refusal/cancellation, late lodgement or Schedule 3 issues need current source and qualified review.[2][1][4]
- **T04 Hosting versus sponsorship:** a host invitation does not automatically select Sponsored Family. Clause 600.224 formal Tourist sponsorship is triggered by a Ministerial requirement and specified relationship/family-unit circumstances; approved/in-force sponsorship and a related-relative grant can then matter. Stored guidance limits formal Tourist sponsorship to exceptional, generally offshore deciding-factor use; preserve that policy distinction without attributing it to this legal citation. Do not label all family visits as sponsored or promise this pathway. Clause 600.225 security is conditional on an authorised request. Inventory that request and refer unusual sponsorship facts.[2][5]
- **T05 Conditions:** clause 600.611 distinguishes formally sponsored Tourist (8101, 8201, 8503, 8531), ordinary unsponsored Tourist (mandatory 8101/8201; discretionary 8501/8503/8558) and subclause (4)'s financial-hardship branch. That branch requires post-entry changed circumstances, likely public charge, inability to leave for reasons beyond control, and compelling personal reasons to work; hardship alone is not work permission. Explain ordinary no-work/three-month-study guidance, but use the actual grant/VEVO and applicable law, and refer exceptions rather than authorising work or a later application.[2][3][9]
- **T06 Longer visits:** offshore Tourist → Additional documents for applicants requesting a longer visa asks for reasons and relevant relationship/status evidence (for example a child's citizenship/permanent residence). Requested stay, travel validity and entries differ; the page says requested duration may not be granted. Parent longer-validity guidance also mentions insurance and a 12-month-in-18-month limit; verify the actual conditions before planning repeat visits, not a promised parent entitlement.[3]

## Sponsored Family stream

- **S01 Lodgement:** outside Australia at application and decision. Each online application needs the completed Form 1149 attachment, even if a sponsor covers several applicants. LIN section 6 makes attachment part of the application arrangement; the family applicant/sponsor steps corroborate it. A support letter is not a substitute. Flag missing attachment as an application-preparation gap; do not certify validity.[1][5]
- **S02 Sponsor:** the individual-relative pathway in 600.232(2) uses a settled Australian citizen/permanent resident aged at least 18; family-unit relationships and 600.233's prior grant to the related applicant can matter. Regulation 1.03 defines settled as lawfully resident for a reasonable period; public sponsor guidance says usually two years with compassionate flexibility—not a fixed statutory two-year rule. Review relationship chain, status and settlement evidence; cousins/in-laws are not direct relatives but family-unit alternatives must not be excluded automatically. Specified parliament/assembly/mayor and government-body pathways also exist; apply their own criteria, not every individual-relative criterion indiscriminately.[2][8][5]
- **S03 Approval/timing:** approved sponsorship must still be in force at decision (600.234). Regulation 1.20L addresses previous granted sponsorships still in effect, or ceased visas with non-compliance and fewer than five years since grant. It also permits specific family-unit/same-purpose and beyond-control/post-entry-8531 exceptions; approval is discretionary, not automatic. Public guidance allows simultaneous sponsorships; do not classify joint applications as earlier grants just because listed first. Inventory prior sponsorship/grant/cessation/compliance dates. Withdrawal, death, changed capacity/office or bar questions require verification; no checklist-based bar waiver.[2][8][5]
- **S04 Bond:** 600.235 and public sponsor Pay a security bond guidance make this conditional on a request, not universal. Record authorised request, amount, payee and deadline exactly; do not pay, hard-code a typical amount or promise refund. A bond is separate from temporary-visit/funding criteria and does not establish that they are satisfied.[2][5]
- **S05 Conditions/duration:** 600.612 prescribes 8101, 8201, 8503 and 8531. Public guidance explains no work, study up to three months, No Further Stay and leaving within the permitted stay; waivers are limited, not automatic. Use the grant/VEVO. Stored Sponsored Family policy caps the grant by the sponsor's agreed obligation period; prepare a period-consistency check from operational-knowledge.md. This is not independently established here as a statutory cap or promised entries/duration.[2][5][9]

## Business Visitor stream

- **B01 Location/evidence:** outside Australia at application and decision. Business visitor documents offers organiser invitations, registration, employer purpose, plans/contacts, qualifications, active-business records and prior contacts as relevant options. Collect actual role, dates, activities and funding. LIN section 7 provides ImmiAccount or a specific Department-written-notice Form 1415 route—not unrestricted paper lodgement.[6][1]
- **B02 Activity boundary:** regulation 1.03 defines general business/employment enquiries, investigating/negotiating/entering/reviewing contracts, official government-to-government visits, and conferences/trade fairs/seminars unless paid by the organiser for participation. It excludes work/services for an Australian-based organisation **or person**, and direct sales to the public. The public page supports the main categories but is not the full definition. Do not infer that unpaid work or an overseas payer removes these exclusions; collect activity, recipient and payment facts and refer borderline cases.[8][6]
- **B03 Employment effects/conditions:** 600.242 concerns adverse consequences for Australian citizens'/permanent residents' employment/training opportunities or employment conditions. Clause 600.613 mandates 8115 and 8201, allows 8503; Schedule 8 describes the business-activity work restriction and study limit. Public guidance allows tourism alongside business. Its general health-insurance text mentions 8501, which is not listed in retrieved 600.613: preserve that source discrepancy for verification, not a confirmed Business Visitor condition matrix.[2][9][6]

## Minors: all streams when applicant under 18

- **M01 Consent/identity:** separately identify everyone with legal parental responsibility, travelling companions, accommodation and court orders. Tourist under-18 instructions request birth/identity/guardianship evidence with alternatives, and Form 1229 **or** a consent statutory declaration plus signer's photo/signature evidence for a responsible person not travelling. One travelling parent does not establish the other's consent. PIC 4017 separately contains home-country lawful-removal, all-responsible-person consent or consistency-with-Australian-child-order alternatives; refer their interpretation, not universal both-parent signatures.[3][9]
- **M02 Welfare:** Tourist/Business/Frequent Traveller instructions ask for signed Form 1257 when not staying with a relative/legal guardian. Sponsored Family/ADS extracted under-18 sections do not state that same form instruction: do not universalise it. PIC 4012's trigger considers age, absence of either/both parents or guardians, visiting/staying with a non-relative, organised-tour status and adequate maintenance/support arrangements; it describes an undertaking, not a named form. Travelling companion and accommodation are separate facts. Stored guidance permits a written undertaking alternative and describes an organised-tour exception with adequate whole-stay arrangements; retain those detailed possibilities in operational-knowledge.md. Their applicable current acceptance remains Needs verification until law, stream/form instructions and any request establish it; do not promise a waiver or ignore welfare.[3][6][9]
- **M03 New/repeat applications and risk:** inventory consent/undertaking dates, scope and any changed arrangements or court-ordered duration. **Verification question:** does this application need renewed consent/undertakings or relationship evidence, and does the available evidence cover these dates? Stored onshore under-18 guidance requires continued consent and new relevant Form1229/1257 for each application, without routinely repeating established relationship evidence absent identity/integrity concerns. Preserve that scope in operational-knowledge.md rather than universalising it to all applications. Custody disputes, welfare concerns, adoption/surrogacy or child/citizenship interactions need qualified review; PIC 4018 best interests is not established by a checklist.[9][3]

## Specialist bounded branches

- **A01 ADS inventory only:** record PRC citizenship, PRC application/decision location, residence area, approved agent identity and written touring arrangements. The ADS page and 600.251–254/600.413 support those distinctions; the specified-area instrument is still a verification gap. LIN section 8 refers to its approved-agent Schedule 1; **use the replacement table in the 2026 amending instrument**, not the older compiled table alone. Clause 600.614 and Schedule 8 distinguish 8207 (no study/training), 8101, 8503, 8530 and tour-group obligations—not an ordinary three-month study allowance. Verify exact agent/date/area and grant conditions; no full ADS assessment or static published agent list.[10][2][12]
- **F01 Frequent Traveller inventory only:** verify passport country/nationality against LIN section 9, outside-Australia application/decision location, activity and current application route. The public page covers multiple eligible countries; do not substitute a China-only rule. Clause 600.512(2)/public How long you can stay distinguishes **up to** ten-year validity, three months after each entry, and the public 12-month-in-24-month stay limit; no promised ten years. Retrieve 600.615 and actual grant conditions for any planning; do not use generic page extension wording as permission for a later onshore visa.[1][7][2]
- **F02 Biometrics timing unresolved:** the public Frequent Traveller overview says biometrics at an ABCC and email instructions as soon as possible; its After you apply section says the Department will advise if needed and contains China collection-location information. **Verification question:** when must this applicant complete collection relative to lodgement, do earlier biometrics qualify, and which dated arrangement/request controls? Stored guidance describes pre-lodgement collection within12months; retain it as a distinct position in operational-knowledge.md, not independently verified current procedure or a merged timetable. Collection-location statements are volatile; verify the centre before booking. Inventory only until timing is settled.[7]
- **X01 Non-standard activity verification only:** remote work, volunteering, internships, domestic support, research, sport, media and tour leaders need precise duties, Australian recipients/links, remuneration, duration, course credit and visa condition. Public Tourist guidance says volunteer work might be possible; that is not blanket permission. Regulation 1.03 and work/business-condition boundaries alone do not independently corroborate all specialist examples. Read operational-knowledge.md for the detailed stored activity distinctions and use them for fact-sensitive preparation. Refer uncertain current compliance rather than pronounce either blanket approval or prohibition.[3][8][9]
- **X02 Existing visa verification:** ask for current grant/conditions, pending applications and intended travel; never recommend a visitor application as a routine fix. Act section 82(2) provides substantive-visa cessation when another substantive visa (except special purpose) comes into effect, with maritime-crew exceptions; section 82(3) concerns bridging visas. Clause 600.513 has a narrow deemed-application effect rule, not a general delayed commencement promise. Permanent-residence, travel-facility, ABTC and bridging interactions require qualified review before substantive action.[11][2]
