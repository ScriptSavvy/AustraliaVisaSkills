## Tourist stream (T)
- **T01 Purpose:** visit family/friends, tourism or other purposes not related to business or medical treatment. PI §3.9.1 / 600.221. Ask about actual activities, not merely a declared holiday.
- **T02 Outside Australia:** current public guidance requires outside Australia at application and decision. **Inside Australia:** location at grant must match onshore application. Keep location at lodgement and at decision distinct. PI §3.12; [3][4].
- **T03 Onshore safeguards:** current/last visa and conditions matter. Student visa held since last entry engages 600.222; the registered course must not be the reason for commencing/continuing/completing studies. Graduation, resit or accreditation examples are not automatic eligibility. A Subclass 403 Domestic Worker (Diplomatic or Consular) visa, lack of a substantive visa, Schedule 3, a No Further Stay condition (such as 8503), refusal/cancellation or late lodgement requires source verification and qualified review. Do not present the 28-day provision as a grace period, lawful stay or guaranteed grant. PI §§3.3.1, 3.9.1.1–3.9.1.2; LIN [1] §5.
- **T04 Hosting is not formal sponsorship:** a family invitation/support letter does not automatically mean Sponsored Family. Formal Tourist sponsorship is exceptional and Department-required under 600.224; review only if actually triggered. Bond only if requested. PI §3.9.1.3.
- **T05 Conditions:** ordinary unsponsored Tourist grants require 8101 (no work) and 8201 (no more than three months study/training); 8501/8503/8558 can be discretionary. Formally sponsored Tourist grants have additional mandatory conditions. Financial hardship under 600.611(4) is a narrow exception—not work permission from hardship alone; obtain qualified review, not permission to work. PI §3.14.1; retrieved C288 600.611.
- **T06 Longer visits:** collect the reason, support for the full stay and relevant relationship/status proof. Requested stay, travel validity and entries are different and not guaranteed. PI §§3.10.2, 3.13; [3] Additional documents for applicants requesting a longer visa.

## Sponsored Family stream (S)
- **S01 Lodgement:** outside Australia; attach completed Form 1149 to each person's ImmiAccount application, even if several are on one sponsorship form. The PI and LIN §6 treat attachment as a validity requirement. Flag absence as a lodgement-preparation problem; do not certify legal validity. PI §§3.3.1, 3.9.2.2; [1][5].
- **S02 Sponsor:** individual pathway requires a settled Australian citizen/permanent resident aged at least 18 and a qualifying relative relationship, including specified family-unit pathways. Review relationship chain, citizenship/residence and settlement evidence. Do not insist on an invented fixed two-year settlement rule. Specified MP/MLA/mayor and government-body pathways also exist; do not exclude them. PI §3.9.2.1 / 600.232. For non-direct relationships, confirm regulation 1.03 and family-unit provisions; a cousin/friend is not automatically a direct qualifying relative, but family-unit alternatives must not be discarded.
- **S03 Approval/timing:** approved sponsorship must still be in force at decision. Collect relevant prior sponsorship facts for regulation 1.20L; it includes exceptions and discretion. Joint applications are not automatically earlier sponsorships. Refer withdrawal, bar, death or sponsor-capacity issues for verification. PI §§3.9.2.2–3.9.2.6.
- **S04 Security bond:** conditional on an authorised request, not mandatory for every sponsorship. Record request, payee, amount and deadline exactly; do not pay or promise refund. A bond cannot cure failure to satisfy temporary-visit criteria. PI §3.9.2.7 / 600.235.
- **S05 Conditions:** 8101, 8201, 8503 (No Further Stay) and 8531 (leave within permitted stay) are mandatory for this stream. Explain implications before planning later in-Australia applications. The sponsor's stated period limits the grant under policy; no promised duration/entries. PI §§3.13.4, 3.14; retrieve current law before advice.

## Business Visitor stream (B)
- **B01:** outside Australia at application and grant. Collect purpose, event/meeting dates, role, organiser/invitation, employer/business details and funding as relevant. Examples are evidence options, not a requirement for every document. PI §§3.3.1, 3.9.3, 3.12; [6] Business visitor documents.
- **B02 Activity boundary:** general business/employment enquiries; investigating/negotiating/entering/reviewing contracts; government-to-government visits; conferences/trade fairs/seminars unless paid by organiser for participation. Business visitor activity excludes work for or services to an Australian-based organisation/person and direct sales to the public. Do not assume unpaid work is allowed, or that an overseas payer alone settles the issue. PI §3.9.3, regulation 1.03. If borderline, collect activity/payment facts and seek qualified review.
- **B03:** intended activities must not adversely affect employment/training opportunities or employment conditions for Australians. 8115 limits work to business visitor activity; 8201 study limit applies; 8503 may be imposed. Tourism alongside permitted business activity is not itself excluded. PI §§3.9.3, 3.14.

## Minors (M): all streams when under 18
- **M01:** ask who has parental responsibility, who travels, who provides accommodation/welfare and whether a court order limits travel. Record birth/relationship/guardianship evidence and consent. Current public guidance allows Form 1229 or a statutory declaration for a non-travelling responsible person, with identity/signature evidence. Do not assume one travelling parent proves the other's consent. PI §3.6.9.1; [3]–[7] under-18 documents.
- **M02:** PI prefers Form 1257 for welfare undertakings where applicable but permits a written undertaking (for example a statutory declaration by a person in Australia for the child's stay). It also has an organised-tour exception under PIC 4012(d), subject to adequate arrangements. Public guidance asks for Form 1257 where not staying with a relative/legal guardian. Travel companion and accommodation are separate triggers; do not collapse them. Check current PIC, forms and any request before treating an alternative as accepted. PIC 4017 lawful-removal/court-order alternatives require qualified interpretation. PI §3.6.9.1.
- **M03:** new applications require renewed relevant consent/undertaking; do not demand already-established relationship evidence again solely because it is old unless there are identity/integrity concerns. Respect court-ordered duration. Custody disputes, welfare concerns, adoption/surrogacy or citizenship/child-visa interactions need qualified review; never declare best-interests compliance from a document checklist. PI §§3.6.9.1, 3.10.1.

## Specialist bounded branches
- **A01 ADS:** identify PRC citizenship, location, specified residence area, exact approved agent and written tour arrangements (tour dates/flight details). Verify latest LIN Schedule 1 and specified-area instrument before conclusions. Maintain tour-condition/no-study distinctions (8207, not 8201). Approved agent lists change; no private/public static list. Full ADS assessment is outside this edition. PI §§3.3.1, 3.9.4, 3.12, 3.14; [1] §8/Schedule 1.
- **F01 Frequent Traveller:** do not use the PI's China-only passport/location description as a current rule. Later LIN §9 lists additional nationalities and no longer contains that PRC-location specification. Verify current passport-country/citizenship conditions, outside-Australia requirement, application route and dated arrangements. PI §3.3.1 is amended in this respect by [1] §9. No guaranteed ten-year validity: legislation allows up to ten years while the PI describes a standard ten years under policy. Keep per-entry stay distinct from overall validity. PI §§3.9.5, 3.13.6; retrieved C288 600.512(2); [7].
- **F02 Biometrics:** the PI says prior to lodgement and no more than 12 months earlier. Current public page says email notification, as soon as possible, and has an After you apply biometrics section. Timing remains unresolved; do not invent a combined process. Ask for dated Department instructions and verify current IIB-1486 if available. Inventory only until resolved.
- **X01 Non-standard activity:** remote work/digital nomads, volunteering, internships, domestic support, research, sports, media and tour leaders have qualified examples/exceptions in PI §3.11. Do not flatten them into blanket approval or blanket prohibition. Obtain precise activities, Australian links, remuneration, duration and applicable condition; refer substantive interpretation.
- **X02 Existing visa:** a new substantive visa can affect a more beneficial existing visa; permanent residence, ABTC and bridging visa interactions require review. Do not suggest a visitor application as a routine fix. PI §§3.10.4, 3.12.1.
