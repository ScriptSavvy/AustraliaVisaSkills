## Material discrepancies and limits
1. **Frequent Traveller passport/location:** PI §3.3.1 describes a PRC passport and being in the PRC. LIN [1] §9 as amended lists Brunei Darussalam, Cambodia, Indonesia, Lao People's Democratic Republic, Malaysia, People's Republic of China, Philippines, Singapore, Thailand, Timor-Leste and Viet Nam passports indicating nationality; the former PRC-place specification is no longer there. Its amendment history supports a specific legal change, also reflected in [7]. Do not silently rewrite the entire PI or apply the change to historical cases. Refresh Schedule 1 and LIN for the relevant date before a case conclusion.
2. **Biometrics timing unresolved:** PI §3.9.5 says Frequent Traveller biometrics before lodgement and within 12 months; [7] says email notification and includes After you apply guidance. The current underlying IIB-1486 and dated applicant instructions are missing. Effect: no settled scheduling/lodgement-readiness conclusion for that requirement. Needed: current official arrangement and any individual request.
3. **Current-law gap:** [2] displays a compilation period ending 1 October 2026 despite being retrieved through latest. Do not infer the cause or assume unchanged law after that date. Effect: current compliance is Needs verification unless an applicable version and amendments are obtained. Continue document presence/readability/consistency checks independently.
4. **Minors/forms/certification:** PI §3.6.9.1 permits written welfare undertakings and tour exceptions; [3]–[7] give applicant-facing form instructions and routine non-certification guidance. These concern different triggers and evidentiary preferences, not a blanket repeal. Obtain the current PIC/form/request for unusual circumstances before promising acceptance of an alternative or requiring certification universally.
5. **Ten years:** PI §3.13.6 describes a standard ten-year Frequent Traveller policy; retrieved legislation 600.512(2) permits up to ten years, and [7] also uses up to. Policy standard is not an applicant guarantee.
6. **Not covered comprehensively:** health examination matrix/exemptions, penal checking, designated biometric arrangements, Schedule 3, exclusion/return criteria, waivers, professional/regulatory compliance, specialised bilateral arrangements and all ADS details. PI references other instructions not supplied here. No fees, minimum funds or request deadlines are hard-coded. Current public pages describe fee concessions commencing 1 July 2026; verify applicable fee instruments rather than using an old PI fee example.

## Runtime refresh procedure
Before compliance checks, use official Home Affairs guidance for the selected stream plus applicable legislation. Start with [2] for Schedule 1 item 1236, Part 600, relevant PIC/SRC/conditions and regulation 1.03/1.20L, and [1] for application arrangements. For ADS, follow the specified-area instrument and current approved agents. For health/character/biometrics, follow links from the relevant current official guidance and actual written requests.

For every material adopted rule record: identity/origin, authority, URL, date checked, version/publication/effective dates (unknown if absent), relevant section, subclass/stream/circumstances/stage/date coverage, and relationship to earlier guidance (supplement/clarification/specific amendment/replacement). Most recently supplied is not necessarily controlling. Look for commencement, transitional rules and unincorporated amendments. Do not equate a website update date with legislative commencement.

If laws, PI and public guidance differ, compare authority, scope and effective dates. Explain a supported resolution and preserve references. Otherwise record each position, the unresolved question, impact and evidence needed; do not combine incompatible rules. Treat an applicant request as case-specific evidence instructions, not a policy amendment. Preserve any conflict with law for qualified review.

If browsing fails, retry another authorised official route (browser, official downloadable text or direct official page data). Record limitations; do not use a snippet, secondary summary or recollection as confirmed policy. A checklist can remain provisional. Currency/compliance and unknown conditional applicability must stay Needs verification.
