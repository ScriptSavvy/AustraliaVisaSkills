# Official sources and refresh gates

## What these sources support

The preparation rules link to accessible official legislation and Home Affairs applicant guidance. The required operational-knowledge.md separately preserves detailed stored policy/evidence guidance, not all independently corroborated by accessible public sources. Official checks supplement that expertise; absence of public support alone is not a reason to delete a preparation branch. A verified applicable legal-compliance finding is separate from using stored preparation knowledge. Official bodies were selectively checked on **2026-10-07**; this is not comprehensive current-law, professional or applicant-specific portal verification. Record the sources you actually open during a case, not this date as your own check date. Source unavailability does not permit guessing.

## Material limits and questions

1. **Dated legislation:** the retrieved Regulations compilation ends before the check date. Versions history identifies a commenced Student reform amendment; its body was read and does not rewrite the cited Part 600 branches. That targeted comparison is not an exhaustive audit of every dependent instrument or transition. Obtain the applicable text and any further amendments for the applicant's lodgement/decision dates before marking current compliance verified.[14][13][16]
2. **ADS agents:** the compiled Visitor arrangements table is not current on its own. The commenced 2026 amendment replaces that table; compare exact identity/code against the applicable replacement, never a cached generic “approved agent” description. Specified residence areas remain a separate instrument question, not answered just by the agent's province.[15][12][1]
3. **Frequent Traveller biometrics:** the official overview requires ABCC collection and email instructions; After you apply also says the Department will advise if needed. The skill does not establish collection-before-lodgement timing or a universal acceptable age of earlier biometrics. Needed: current dated collection/lodgement arrangements and the individual request. No timing/readiness conclusion until resolved.[7]
4. **Minor welfare/consent:** the legislative consent alternatives and compound welfare trigger differ from simpler form guidance. Not travelling with parents/guardians and not staying with a relative/legal guardian are separate facts. Some stream pages do not repeat the Form 1257 instruction. Needed for unusual cases: applicable public-interest criteria, current forms, exact tour/maintenance/court facts and any Department request. Alternative acceptance is not promised; neither is universal form-only compliance.[9][3][5]
5. **Business Visitor insurance:** the public page's general insurance section mentions 8501, but retrieved 600.613 lists 8115/8201 and discretionary 8503, not 8501. Do not combine these into a settled condition matrix; verify applicable provisions and actual grant before planning.[6][2]
6. **Preparation knowledge versus compliance:** detailed credit, renewed-consent, partner/other-visa, non-standard-activity, hardship and other policy branches remain in operational-knowledge.md. They inform tailored preparation; they are not all independently corroborated or individually legally decided here. Preserve genuine currency/conflict/outcome questions and refer status, custody, work and complex interpretation. No hard-coded fee, funds minimum, purchased-ticket demand or request deadline.

## Runtime refresh procedure

1. Select the stream, actor (applicant/sponsor), stage and relevant dates. Open the official stream page, then the applicable legislation and connected forms/instruments for the actual trigger. Distinguish pre-lodgement application arrangements, processing requests, criteria at decision and post-grant conditions.
2. Record title/origin/authority, exact URL, section, date checked, version/compilation/publication/effective dates (unknown if absent), circumstances and date coverage. Website last-updated dates are not legislative commencement. Read conditions, exceptions, discretion, transitions and unincorporated amendments, not just the headline.
3. Use Regulations Schedule 1 item 1236, Part 600, relevant Schedule 4/5/8 provisions, 1.03/1.20L and Visitor application arrangements as needed. For ADS retrieve both the applicable approved-agent table and specified-area instrument. For health/character/biometrics follow relevant official links and the full dated applicant request; do not infer an exemption or uniform process from a stream summary.
4. A newer page is not automatically controlling. Compare authority, scope, stage and effective period. An individual request is evidence instruction, not a legislative amendment. If a supported resolution is possible, explain it; otherwise show both positions, missing evidence, impact and concrete verification question. Do not merge incompatible positions or suppress an exception merely because the simpler page omits it.
5. If retrieval fails, retry another authorised official route (browser, official download or embedded page body). Exclude irrelevant template content. Record what could not be read. Continue inventory/readability/consistency work independently; keep uncertain applicability/currency/compliance Needs verification and the checklist provisional. No overall readiness while a material in-scope gap remains.
