# Document handling and unavailable evidence

Read before reviewing documents. Use with the subclass operational knowledge and verification reference. Distinguish stored policy/evidence expectations, current official applicant instructions, verified applicable law, useful evidence and practical suggestions. A readable or genuine-looking document does not establish authenticity, evidentiary weight or official acceptance.

## Link evidence to the claim

For each document record the claim, person, relevant date/period, neutral document ID and pages actually inspected. Explain why the document matters. Country and individual circumstances may change suitable evidence. Preserve supporting and contradictory material; ask for a truthful explanation rather than select convenient pages. A legally amended civil record can still require explanation of the change and the underlying fact. Never infer fraud from a variation or claim to conduct forensic verification.

Keep presence, readability, completeness, consistency, currency and compliance separate. A filename proves neither pages nor contents. Uncertain extraction means an evidence gap, not a confirmed mismatch. Request a clearer copy before deciding whether obscured details are missing.

## Translations, scans and certification are different checks

Pair each non-English original-language record with its English translation; compare names, dates, figures, seals/annotations and completeness. Translation is not certification or authenticity verification. Current Student and offshore Tourist public guidance says non-English documents should be translated; Australian translators need NAATI accreditation, while overseas translators need their full name, address/telephone and relevant qualifications/experience in English. An AI rendering may help comprehension but is not an accredited translation. Refresh the selected subclass/stream and actual request before a compliance finding.

Current public preparation instructions differ from older policy preferences for originals/certified photocopies. Student guidance says documents need not be certified; offshore Tourist says not unless requested. Do not impose universal certification, notarisation or wet signatures. Do not use an older statement about accepting an application without translations as permission to omit current translation instructions. Application acceptance/validity and evidence preparation are different questions.

For a relevant doubt, original inspection or certified evidence may be specifically requested. Inventory that request; do not invent it. Applicant-written correspondence and official civil/education records have different functions, not an automatic common certification rule. Retain originals securely where the applicant has them; do not send originals unless the current process actually requires it.

Use clear, legible colour scans/photos and all relevant pages, normally one file for a multi-page document under the read public guidance. Refresh portal limits and file formats. Ensure pages belong to the same document; do not merge unrelated originals into a misleading record. Successful OCR, compression or upload proves only that mechanical step. External processing requires permission.

## When a relevant document cannot be obtained

Do not end the branch at “missing” or assume a waiver. Stored preparation policy recognises genuinely difficult circumstances, including civil unrest. Ask only what helps establish:
- the exact document, fact it would prove and issuing authority;
- why it is unavailable, attempts made, dates, replies/refusal and expected availability;
- whether the issue is temporary, permanent, personal or a general issuer problem;
- alternative contemporaneous official records supporting that same fact;
- an issuer or relevant foreign mission's statement about availability, where reasonably obtainable;
- applicant-held earlier records and the truthful explanation of any differences.

These are possible evidence routes, not a universal compulsory packet. Do not demand unsafe contact or impossible repeated attempts. The Department may have other verification possibilities; this skill has no access to internal case systems or archival case records. Earlier records still need actual linkage to this application. Alternative evidence does not automatically prove the claim or waive the requirement. Record the preparation gap and proposed alternatives separately; mark acceptance/compliance **Needs verification** and formulate the exact question for the Department or a qualified professional. No contact or submission is authorised by this review.

## Personal identifiers and forms

Photographs, fingerprints and other personal identifiers are not a single universal attachment checklist. Inventory the actual form/request, collection method, completion evidence and claimed exemption. Do not infer that an identifier is universally mandatory or exempt from a generic historical example. Preserve existing subclass biometrics timing gates.

Review a form against its own current directions, language, declarations, required signatures and relevant signatory authority. Historical paper practices included each relevant adult's signature, signed/dated alterations and parent/guardian signatures for minors; a policy age guide is not a universal legal threshold. Do not impose those paper conventions on every online declaration or upload. Record assistance with completion when relevant; the applicant should review truthful answers, not disclaim them because a helper entered them.

## Subclass evidence gates

Preserve selected-stream differences, consent and welfare alternatives, sponsor attachments, funding/temporary-intention evidence and the specialist biometrics timing question. The offshore Tourist translation/certification check is not proof of identical instructions in every stream or actual request. Read preparation-rules.md and sources-and-refresh.md for stream applicability.

Read [application procedures](application-procedures.md) for records, deadlines, communication and family changes. Official entry points are in that file and the subclass verification reference. Missing stored evidence is a preparation gap, not by itself illegal lodgement.
