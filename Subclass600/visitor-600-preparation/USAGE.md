## Start
> Use visitor-600-preparation. Help me make a Subclass 600 document checklist. Ask one question at a time and wait for my answer. Ask only what changes the checklist. I prefer [language].

## Tailored prompts
- **Checklist:** “Use visitor-600-preparation. Tourist stream, outside Australia, adult, short holiday, self-funded. Prepare a provisional checklist and explain mandatory versus useful evidence.”
- **Review:** “Use visitor-600-preparation to review these redacted documents. Compare dates and funding. Tell me which pages you actually read. Do not estimate approval chances.”
- **Department request:** “Use visitor-600-preparation to organise this redacted Department request into evidence tasks. Preserve its stated deadline. Do not send a response or assume an extension.”
- **Unknown stream:** “Use visitor-600-preparation. I do not know the stream. Help me identify the missing facts and official sources. Do not decide eligibility.”
- **Unavailable record:** “Use visitor-600-preparation. The civil registry cannot issue this record. Organise my truthful explanation, attempts and alternative evidence; do not assume a waiver.”
- **Newborn:** “Use visitor-600-preparation. A child was born while my application was pending. Identify the chronology, records and current-rule questions before assuming applicant addition or a fresh paid application.”

## Stream-specific scope and limits
Tourist, Sponsored Family and Business Visitor have official-source-linked preparation branches. ADS/Frequent Traveller receive a bounded inventory and verification plan. Detailed [operational knowledge](references/operational-knowledge.md) preserves credit evidence, renewed-consent scope, welfare alternatives, activity exceptions and visa interactions as stored preparation guidance. Those details inform checklists without being represented as independently public-verified current law; legal compliance and uncertain outcomes still require verification. Complex legal-status, work, custody or refusal issues need qualified review.

A latest-labelled legislative compilation may end before your relevant date. Document presence/readability/consistency can still be checked while current compliance remains Needs verification. See [sources and refresh](references/sources-and-refresh.md).
