## First use
Ask: “Use visitor-600-preparation. Help me make a Subclass 600 document checklist. Ask only what changes the checklist. I prefer [language].”

Tell the agent your stream if known, purpose/activities, country at application, stage, approximate dates, stay length, adult/minor travellers and who pays/provides accommodation. You do not need to upload a full history or passport number for intake.

## Example prompts
- Checklist: “Tourist stream, outside Australia, adult, short holiday, self-funded. Prepare a provisional checklist and explain mandatory versus useful evidence.”
- Review: “Review these redacted documents for visitor-600-preparation. Compare dates and funding. Tell me which pages you actually read. Do not estimate approval chances.”
- Official request: “Organise this redacted Department request into evidence tasks. Preserve its stated deadline. Do not send a response or assume an extension.”
- Missing information: “I do not know the stream. Help me identify the missing facts and official sources. Do not decide eligibility.”

## Documents and privacy
Start with an inventory. Redact unnecessary passport/account/contact identifiers; preserve the dates/fields needed for the selected check. Tell the agent if redaction obscures a field. A filename does not prove contents. Explain relevant differences honestly; do not alter genuine records to remove a mismatch.

Ask the host where uploaded files are processed and retained. Some AI hosts process remotely. This skill does not enforce local-only processing or automatic deletion. External OCR or third-party uploads require your separate permission. Keep applicant reports outside the reusable skill folder.

## Understanding results
Ready means only ready for the named preparation check. Missing means known applicable evidence is absent. Needs correction means a confirmed reviewed defect. Needs verification means uncertain facts, unreadable pages, unknown applicability or unresolved sources. Not applicable needs positive support; it does not mean unknown.

Tourist, Sponsored Family and Business Visitor have preparation branches. ADS/Frequent Traveller receive a bounded inventory and verification plan. Complex legal-status, work, custody or refusal issues need qualified review. The agent must explain sources it cannot access; browsing failure is not permission to guess.
