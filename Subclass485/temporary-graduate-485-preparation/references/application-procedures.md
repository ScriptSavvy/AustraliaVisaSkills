# Application procedures — Temporary Graduate visa (subclass 485)

**Private candidate; not cleared for publication or current-law reliance.** This is preparation guidance, not a validity, eligibility, lawful-stay or legal-outcome determination. Do not lodge, pay, communicate, withdraw or change an applicant's account without express authority. Use redacted notices and minimum necessary personal information.

## Scope and source limits

This reference contains stored preparation knowledge and targeted dated legal anchors. It does not establish current legal sufficiency. Use the applicable subclass rules, instruments and actual form/request directions; see [verification and source limits](verification.md). This preparation workflow excludes officer operations, review/cancellation strategy, refunds and legal-status determinations. A dated legal anchor whose displayed interval has ended is not current-law certification. Do not transfer rules from a different subclass without checking applicability.

## 1. Making an application is not the same as paying or obtaining a visa

Identify each actual applicant, nominated stream, intended primary/secondary route, physical location and immigration status at the relevant time. Disclosing a spouse or child does not itself make that person an applicant. Where a combined application is permitted, each included person has an application and needs their own assessment; a shared form does not guarantee a shared outcome. “Primary applicant” is a form/workflow label, not proof of which legal criteria a person satisfies.

### Form, channel and directions

The stored subclass-specific instruction specifies online form **1276 (internet)** through ImmiAccount. Paper **1409** is an exception requiring an authorising Department email, not a choice created by an online error, payment, technical-support enquiry or general guidance about fax/email. The stored exception requires compliance with the email's directions, attachment of that authorising email, and submission before **11:59pm AEST or AEDT, when applicable, on the day following the date the authorising email was sent**. Check the applicable instrument and exact authorisation before reliance; an earlier visa-expiry or other statutory gate is not extended by this permission.

Possible authorisation circumstances in that guidance are examples, not entitlements: a Department-identified system problem not expected to be fixed within business hours where the person would become unlawful before the next business day; an unrectifiable system/data-matching problem where law does not bar lodgement; an expired passport; or the specified period following a Tribunal cancellation/non-revocation outcome that prevents electronic lodgement. Preserve error records and seek the actual authorised route; do not bypass a legal bar as a technical error.

Complete the actual approved form according to its directions, including declarations and assistance disclosures. Another person completing it does not remove the applicant's responsibility for its contents. Conditional paper-signature and minor-capacity practices are in [document-handling.md](document-handling.md), not a universal wet-signature requirement.

### Location, bars and validity

Physical whereabouts and where/how an application is legally made are different questions. The stored 485 rule permits an applicant claiming family-unit membership of a **person already holding a 485 granted on primary criteria** to apply in or outside Australia, but not in immigration clearance. Under inspected C289 item1229(3)(g), applicants outside (3)(f) must be in Australia, not in immigration clearance, including ordinary new-primary and regional second/subsequent PHEW applicants. The retained Replacement/family wording in (3)(f)(iii) does not reopen new-primary Replacement applications after 30 June 2024. A family application combined with a pending primary application does not acquire the already-granted-holder exception. Check each person separately and verify applicable-date law.

Check Schedule 1 item1229, the applicable instrument, correct form/channel, charge, residence, location, relevant visa/history/age/stream requirements and any application bar. The primary applicant must nominate one stream; generic class/subclass assessment discussion does not authorise changing that choice. This procedural reference does not replace the substantive stream assessment.

A no-further-application condition, refusal/cancellation history, lack of substantive visa, detention or maritime/transitory-person history may require qualified assessment. Do not treat every condition alike: stored subclass-specific guidance distinguishes **8534**, which permits a 485 application without waiver of that condition, from **8535**, which can prevent a valid 485 application unless applicable relief is established; a waiver does not independently cure the eligible-student-visa restriction for Foreign Affairs/Defence students. Requesting a waiver is not obtaining one or making a valid subsequent application. No routine “waiver pending” assurance of lawful stay.

Provide the real residential address, not merely a postal or representative's address. Stored guidance allows a separately accompanying residence document where protection/security concerns justify it; agree a secure authorised method rather than withholding residence or inventing an address.

### Payment, submission and legal timestamp

Keep draft creation, payment, submission, receipt/acknowledgement and validity as separate events. A payment receipt or draft is not proof that the application was submitted or validly made. Stored guidance locates validity when all relevant requirements are met, not simply when the first payment occurs. Part-payment, a later balance, bounced payment, or a waiver request can change the analysis. Do not backdate or promise preservation of the first attempted date. Verify current amounts, components, exemptions and each applicant; the old general “one charge” description is not a fee quotation. Stored subclass-specific guidance excludes the subsequent temporary application charge, but that is not an exemption from all charges.

Record actual submission/receipt evidence, timezone and timestamp. Stored internet guidance uses Australian Eastern Standard Time, or Eastern Summer Time when daylight saving operates in the ACT, for the legally attributed application time—not the applicant's device clock, local midnight or payment date. Verify reg2.10C and the actual route if age, visa expiry, transitional law or another deadline turns on the instant. Do not apply historical paper-office date-stamp conventions automatically to online or specially authorised applications.

Stored policy describes substantial compliance and possible quick/easy rectification, not a guaranteed cure. It expressly excludes a seriously deficient form, missing residential address or unprovided required personal identifier from the partial-form principle. Wrong place, disqualifying visa/age requirements or a statutory bar are not ordinary missing-document fixes. A possible officer invitation does not suspend status consequences or preserve an attempted date.

## 2. Communicating and receiving documents

Use the application-specific channel and processing address notified by the Department, including a later written redirection; not an arbitrary Department inbox. Give the name used in the application, date of birth and an appropriate client/file/application or payment identifier; the stored rule also identifies the processing post for an overseas application. Supply sensitive identifiers through the proper secure channel, not public chat. Keep the message, attachments, dispatch/portal receipt and evidence linking them to the correct application. Material sent incorrectly or without sufficient identifying details may not reach the decision maker in time; stored policy's attempt-to-match/forward practice is not a substitute for correct delivery.

Applicant-initiated substantive information normally belongs in writing. Stored guidance distinguishes progress enquiries and bridging-visa communications, and recognises oral applications/specific contrary instructions where legally available. A substantive phone discussion is not simply worthless: the stored policy says relevant information actually received is considered and should be recorded by officers. Confirm it truthfully in writing through the correct channel; do not invent an officer agreement or rely on a call as proof of submission, extension or grant. Department-initiated communication can be oral; preserve what was actually requested and seek clarification of uncertain instructions.

Keep residence, correspondence address, authorised recipient, assistance/representation and electronic consent separate. An authorised recipient receives application documents under the applicable arrangement; their consent, rather than the applicant's, matters for electronic delivery to them. Giving an email address alone is not consent under the stored policy. Consent might sometimes be inferred from conduct (for example, asking a question by email), but cannot readily be inferred merely from emailing a requested response. Check the actual form, appointment, revocation, recipient and delivery method.

Dated Act s52 requires the residence intended during processing and notification of a proposed change for **14 days or more**, including the proposed period. This is not the old inconsistent “more than 14” wording and is not a full account of post-grant address conditions. A recipient appointment does not remove that obligation. The same section provides a notification rule for people applying together; do not assume each person's later opening of a shared notice starts a new period.

**Receipt is not necessarily opening.** Record dispatch, actual arrival, legally attributed/deemed receipt, who was served and when, delivery method, consent and recipient appointment. Do not calculate a deadline from the applicant's personal reading date or assert invalid service from a late opening/bounce alone. Verify the actual notification provisions and notice before any consequential calculation. Department reply times do not inherit applicant response periods.

## 3. More evidence, requests, interviews and extensions

Classify the event before drafting a response:
- **Unsolicited additional material:** relevant information may be supplied before decision, but a promise to supply it does not require a delay. Material in an earlier application is not automatically evidence in this one; identify and supply the relevant material anew as directed.
- **Further-information invitation:** it can be oral or written, including potentially words used at interview. Capture the request, requested item, person affected, prescribed response method and actual period. A request is a decision-maker power, not a promise that every evidence gap will receive an invitation.
- **Adverse-information comment:** the read Act defines relevant information by all three elements: it would be a reason or part-reason for refusal; is specifically about the applicant or another person, not just a class; and was not supplied by the applicant for this application. Non-disclosable information is excluded. The Minister must provide particulars, make relevance understood as far as reasonably practicable, and invite comment where s57 applies. Do not revive the former onshore/review limitation, diagnose a fairness breach or promise disclosure of an informant's identity. Medical and confidential material can require special handling. Prepare a factual evidence response and refer consequential legal questions.
- **Interview/medical or third-party assessment:** distinguish attending/initiating the step and proving action from receipt of a final result. Stored guidance recognises third-party health, English, qualifications and other assessments may take longer than applicant response periods; that does not remove a separate deadline to show action or a 485 time-of-application criterion.

There is **no default seven-, 28- or 70-day response period** in this reference. Obtain the complete notice, date, recipient/service facts, response method, exact deadline, legal basis and applicable regulations. Where law prescribes a period, apply it only to its branch; if none is prescribed, dated s58 provides for a reasonable period. Ask one targeted question at a time and prioritise an urgent stated deadline over routine intake.

**Deadline-status restriction for this edition:** organise the request and record the deadline as stated, but do not calculate remaining time or conclude that it has passed or is still ahead. Mark temporal status Needs verification. The following late-response discussion is conditional preparation guidance, not a clock check, verification of service or determination of the legally correct deadline. Follow the entrypoint's deadline safety boundary, including on resumed conversations.

If more time is needed, request it **before the original period expires**, with the reason, evidence of attempts and realistic next step. A requested extension is not a granted one, and silence is not consent. Verify any actual extension and revised deadline. The stored policy says an expired period cannot simply be extended; do not announce a retrospective extension or guarantee a second invitation. If already late, submit what can properly be supplied promptly, preserve the chronology and seek urgent advice; do not assume the case is undecided.

After the response period, dated s62 permits a decision without further attempts to obtain the missing response; it does not compel an immediate decision. Dated s63(2)–(3) restricts **refusal** after an invitation until the first applicable event (response, stated non-provision/non-comment, or expiry); it is not a blanket ban on every grant. Relevant late information received before decision is distinct from an extension and must not simply be discarded as late; do not promise consideration of post-decision material or a delay for promised evidence.

## 4. Family changes: distinguish the mechanisms

Notify relevant changes promptly and accurately. Record each parent's application/decision chronology, the child's birth/adoption date and location, each actual application, and the intended mechanism. Family declaration or a changed relationship is not itself addition or grant.

### Child born while a parent's application is undecided

The stored guidance and dated reg2.08 treat a child born **after the parent's application but before its decision** as having applied at birth for the same class, combined with the parent's application. This is a deemed application, not a discretionary spouse/family addition. The guidance excludes adopted children, contributory-parent-newborn cases and applications precluded by the Act. Birth before decision—not the date the Department learns about it—is the critical chronology. Birth after grant/refusal is a different question, including possible child visa status, and needs separate assessment.

Notify the Department as soon as practicable and provide birth/identity/parentage evidence through its instructed process. Do not automatically demand a fresh paid application for a child within reg2.08. Stored policy allows a standard form to gather audit or child-specific information without making that form a fresh application or imposing a fresh application charge under that mechanism. The child's application still needs a decision, not an automatic grant. Check applicable decision-time criteria and, under dated reg2.08(2), any application-time sponsorship/nomination/proposal criterion at decision; child-only supporting forms may be relevant **if such a criterion actually applies**, not a generic 485 sponsor requirement.

If both parents have pending applications, the child may have more than one deemed application, even where the parents applied together. Reconcile every record and decision rather than treating one child grant/refusal as disposal of all applications. Do not infer bridging status from that possibility. Regulatory changes/deleted classes require actual saving provisions. Birth during merits review or a remittal is a specialist stage question; do not equate “not finally determined” with “no primary decision yet” or copy historic Tribunal rules.

### Other dependent child; spouse/partner; later applicant

Stored subclass-specific preparation guidance recognises a dependent-child addition under **reg2.08B** before decision. That regulation is for **dependent children of original applicants for listed temporary classes**, not generic spouse addition. The inspected **C289, 2 October 2026** text includes Skilled (Provisional) (Class VC), the class containing Subclass485. This closes the earlier class-coverage gap for the inspected regulation as checked on 16 October 2026; **dependency, request, payments, whereabouts and applicable-date rules still need case-specific verification**. Reg2.08A concerns certain permanent applications, not authority to add a spouse to an undecided temporary 485 application.

The inspected C289 reg2.08B requires the original applicant's written request in the prescribed way, a dependency claim, receipt after application and before decision, relevant charges (if any), and Schedule 1 location requirements at the relevant time. Its application time is the **latest of request receipt and the required charge payments**, not backdated to the parent's lodgement. This primary text qualifies the older general guidance's request-receipt shorthand. The child must satisfy applicable application-time secondary criteria at that deemed time and decision-time secondary criteria. Do not promise that payment after decision cures a pre-decision request.

For an eligible spouse/partner or child not using a deemed/addition mechanism, stored subclass-specific guidance recognises combined applications at the same time/place, separate secondary applications, and separate subsequent applications to join a primary-criteria 485 holder. A dependent child otherwise needs an individual application unless combined with a parent's. Verify each mechanism rather than offering generic “add family later”. Location differs for a pending-primary route and an already-granted-holder route (section1). Relationship eligibility and all secondary criteria still matter: stored family guidance includes decision-time family-unit membership, health-insurance evidence accompanying the application and continuing cover in Australia, public-interest criteria with age/minor distinctions, and relevant special-return criteria. Generic evidence flexibility does not waive an express time-of-application requirement.

## 5. Withdrawal, decisions, grant and status boundaries

An applicant's written withdrawal is distinct from deleting a person in a portal, removing an application from an account, not answering a request, or making another application. Each combined applicant's application remains separate; one person's withdrawal does not withdraw everyone else's. A parent/guardian may act for a minor where authorised. Do not purport to withdraw another competent adult's application.

Timing can be consequential. Stored withdrawal guidance distinguishes mere receipt somewhere in the Department from actual or constructive notice capable of intercepting the responsible decision process. Use the current instructed route, identify every affected application, preserve transmission and acknowledgement, and seek confirmation; do not declare effective withdrawal solely from a sent email or calculate status from it. A withdrawal request after decision is not an ordinary pre-decision withdrawal. Death is not deemed written withdrawal and does not automatically dispose of other family applications; refer eligibility/refund/status consequences sensitively rather than deciding them here.

Assessment progress, a referral, successful checks, prior officer views or an indication that documents are complete is not grant. A decision-maker must reach the necessary findings under the applicable law; preparers and external assessors do not exercise that power. Policy can guide discretion but is not an applicant entitlement to have legislation waived.

Recorded decision, notification, visa coming into effect, period of stay, travel facility and conditions are separate events. Do not assume a case remains pending until an applicant opens a grant/refusal notice, or that new documents can routinely reopen a recorded decision. Stored 485 grant guidance allows the applicant to be in or outside Australia but not immigration clearance **at grant**; that does not erase the narrower application-time location rules. Verify the actual grant record and applicable provisions.

A pending 485, technical-support enquiry, fee payment, withdrawal/extension request or preparation report does not itself prove lawful stay, work or travel rights. Another substantive visa coming into effect can affect an existing visa, subject to statutory exceptions; do not repeat the stored overbroad “every new visa cancels every old visa” shorthand. Check actual current grants/conditions and other pending applications before consequential action. Do not calculate bridging cessation using historical 28/35-day examples. Review rights, refusal/cancellation challenges, jurisdictional error, refunds and post-decision remedies require the actual notice, applicable law and qualified professional advice; preparation assistance is not those services.
