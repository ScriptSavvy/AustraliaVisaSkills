## Scope and execution

Use this reference for first-primary vocational or higher-education study checks together with [operational knowledge](operational-knowledge.md). Stored mandatory/conditional labels do not mean current-law verification. All R IDs retain source-backed knowledge; any unresolved issue affecting the case must remain visible. Do not replace the substantive tests with a single “check current rules” instruction, and do not infer present compliance from stored policy alone.

Build a row for every relied-on course/award and distinguish award eligibility, registration, academic completion, six-month application timing, registered academic load, calendar duration, English instruction, physical location and lawful study rights. Record credit origin, transfer articulation, official breaks and overlapping periods. Only the triggered complication needs detailed applicant-facing reporting. Ask one numbered material question at a time if information is missing.

**Two independent numerical gates:** at least 16 qualifying calendar months and the applicable two-academic-year registered-load threshold. Use actual dates and verified course records. Compute with a reliable date/calculation tool; do not approximate months as a fixed number of days or sum overlapping attendance. Deduplicate units/credits independently from calendar overlap. Where RPL changes the result, present supported alternative ledgers and mark the legal conclusion Needs verification (U02), not a confident pass/fail selected for convenience.

**Evidence-format conflict:** the dedicated official 485 study page still requests certified completion/transcript copies; the stream upload instructions say ordinary online documents need no certification. Preserve both positions (U05). Assemble complete colour originals and translations; do not automatically reject an uncertified scan or certify that certification can never be needed.

**Current study architecture:** use the specific 485.221 / 485.231(1B) gates with 485.111 definitions, not the broader GSM degree definition under 1.15F. The post-14-December-2024 PHEW architecture applies by application date. [Verification](verification.md) records the actual instruments and limits.

<a id="study-gates"></a>
## Study gates and separate ledgers



### R135 — Stored mandatory; application

The study requirement combines eligible completed Australian awards, registered courses, at least 16 calendar months, at least two academic years, English instruction and study in Australia on a visa authorising study.

**Limits / exceptions:** Every element must be checked; one numerical threshold does not establish the others.



### R136 — Stored practical; pre-application

Maintain separate records for registered academic load, actual calendar study time, credit origins and use, physical location and lawful study rights. Record provider, course code, campus, start/completion dates, award outcome and relied-on qualification for each component.

**Limits / exceptions:** U02: show alternative RPL calculations if needed instead of silently choosing gross registered duration or net credited duration.



### R137 — Stored policy; pre-application

For multiple courses, the next course should generally commence in the same or following academic year; other breaks should be provider-approved, such as a deferment for compassionate and compelling reasons.

**Limits / exceptions:** This general sequencing policy is separate from the more specific eligible-graduate-diploma commencement condition.


<a id="completion-date"></a>
## Academic completion and evidence



### R138 — Stored mandatory; application

Completion means meeting all academic requirements for the award; formal conferral or a graduation ceremony is not required to establish academic completion.

**Limits / exceptions:** Eligible-kind/institution award requirements remain distinct; do not assume ceremony timing decides the application window.



### R139 — Stored mandatory; application

Use the date final results or confirmation of all academic requirements became available or were published, not the date the certificate was ceremonially conferred. Record the institution's actual notification/publication evidence.

**Limits / exceptions:** A transcript saying admitted/conferred on a date does not necessarily prove completion on that date.



### R140 — Stored support; application

Completion evidence can include final-result notification by letter, online publication, newspaper or institutional bulletin board; a transcript may help, and a provider confirmation letter may be needed if the date is unclear.

**Limits / exceptions:** Do not fabricate an exact completion date from a ceremony date or undated result.



### R141 — Stored mandatory; application

Paying overdue fees/library fines or satisfying administrative release requirements later does not extend the academic completion date or restart the six-month window.

**Limits / exceptions:** Distinguish delayed access to already-available results from actual later academic completion.



### R142 — Stored mandatory; application

Submitting a thesis is not enough: obtain confirmation that the thesis is accepted, the assessing panel regards it as complete and no further changes are required, with all academic requirements met.

**Limits / exceptions:** If the thesis-completion documentation lacks an exact completion date, stored guidance permits using the letter date; verify the document actually confirms final completion.



### R143 — Checked support; application

Obtain provider completion letter and transcripts. Preserve start/end and academic completion dates, campus, distance-learning components, full/part-time status, instruction language and relied-on credit details.

**Limits / exceptions:** U05: official study-specific guidance asks for certified copies, while stream digital instructions say certification is unnecessary. Assemble clear colour originals plus translations, follow express requests, and mark affected certification Needs verification. Certified copies are a possible conservative preparation choice, not a universally established online-lodgement legal requirement.

**Source and limits:** see [verification](verification.md) and the linked topical reference. Checked 16 October 2026; limited to the identified instruments/public directions.


<a id="eligible-awards-and-packages"></a>
## Eligible awards, trade definitions and packaged study



### R144 — Stored mandatory; application and decision

An associate degree must be an AQF associate degree awarded by an authorised body. A diploma includes authorised associate diplomas and diplomas within the historical tertiary register definition, and AQF diplomas/advanced diplomas awarded by an authorised body.

**Limits / exceptions:** A course's informal title does not prove the required qualification classification.



### R145 — Stored conditional; application and decision

A trade qualification may be an Australian trade award from an indentured apprenticeship/training contract required under relevant industrial training law/award, involving formal technical-college/TAFE training and employment under the applicable award or industrial-training law.

**Limits / exceptions:** Do not classify any work-based certificate as a trade qualification without the required structure.



### R146 — Stored conditional; application and decision

Alternative trade-qualification limbs include an AQF qualification of at least Certificate III for a skilled occupation in historical ASCO Major Group IV or ANZSCO Major Group 3.

**Limits / exceptions:** A Certificate III or IV outside the qualifying trade group does not automatically qualify.



### R147 — Stored mandatory; application and decision

Only study resulting in an eligible completed qualification counts directly. If several separate courses are relied on, each must result in an eligible award, subject to genuine credited/nested-package rules.

**Limits / exceptions:** English-proficiency courses and enabling programs do not satisfy the study requirement.



### R148 — Stored conditional; application and decision

A prerequisite qualification is not accepted unless packaged with the eligible award. Certificate II, non-trade Certificate III/IV and graduate certificates are not independently sufficient eligible awards.

**Limits / exceptions:** A qualifying trade award or genuine credit/package contribution may require a different analysis; do not discard such study solely because its standalone award is ineligible.



### R149 — Stored conditional; application and decision

A PVEW packaged/nested program can contain modular certificates that individually are not eligible awards but together result in an eligible associate degree, diploma or trade qualification; the final eligible award must be completed.

**Limits / exceptions:** A partial package ending before the eligible final module is not enough.



### R150 — Stored conditional; application and decision

A PHEW package can group courses leading to one or more eligible degrees; check how the earlier components actually contribute to the eligible resulting degree.

**Limits / exceptions:** A package label does not make an ineligible standalone award an eligible PHEW degree.



### R151 — Stored support; pre-application

A four-module package awarding certificates after early modules and a diploma after the fourth illustrates why completion of the final diploma module is necessary.

**Limits / exceptions:** Do not count abandoned/uncredited modules just because they were originally marketed in a package.



### R152 — Stored support; pre-application

A natural-progression vocational hospitality package of 40-week commercial-cookery Certificate III, 40-week Certificate IV and 20-week hospitality-management diploma is a stored acceptable-combination illustration exceeding the stated 92-week baseline.

**Limits / exceptions:** Check actual course codes, distinct credited units, eligible outcomes, occupational relationship and calendar duration; do not double count overlapping modules.



### R153 — Stored mandatory; application and decision

Multiple eligible courses may combine to satisfy the study requirement; a single two-academic-year course is not required. For PVEW, every relied-on qualification still needs the close occupational relationship, including whole-career tasks.

**Limits / exceptions:** For PHEW, the same eligible degree set must meet the interconnected degree, institution and study tests.


<a id="credits-transfers-and-rpl"></a>
## Credits, transfers and unresolved RPL treatment



### R154 — Stored mandatory; application and decision

The same credits cannot be counted to satisfy more than one qualification. Trace each credited unit to its origin and eligible resulting award.

**Limits / exceptions:** Separate genuine distinct study from repeated credit labels.



### R155 — Stored conditional; application and decision

Overseas-study credits do not directly count as time studied in Australia, although a provider may recognise them as prior learning for the award.

**Limits / exceptions:** U02: preserve the RPL caveat and registered-duration interpretation issue; do not convert overseas credit into Australian physical-presence time.



### R156 — Stored conditional; application and decision

Non-award-course credit does not directly count but may be recognised by a provider as RPL. Transcripts should show how prior-learning credit contributes to the final award.

**Limits / exceptions:** U02: recognition by the provider does not alone settle every visa-study ledger.



### R157 — Stored conditional; application and decision

When changing providers in Australia, qualifying study with the first provider can count where the second provider credits it toward the completed eligible award; retain both transcripts and the credit articulation.

**Limits / exceptions:** All underlying study requirements and eligible-award restrictions still apply; a non-trade Certificate IV example must not be read as creating a new standalone eligible award.



### R158 — Stored conditional; application and decision

For a provider transfer, calendar time spent on credited units with the first provider and qualifying units with the second can count, without overlapping time twice. Credited academic load is proportionate to the relevant registered course duration.

**Limits / exceptions:** Do not count a unit again merely because both providers list it.



### R159 — Stored policy; application and decision

Stored RPL guidance says prior study recognised onshore or offshore reduces study needed for the award but does not affect the two-academic-years requirement; if it reduces qualifying study to less than 16 months the study requirement is not met.

**Limits / exceptions:** U02: this position needs reconciliation with credit-origin, lawful-study and physical-presence passages before selecting a definitive computation.



### R160 — Stored practical; pre-application

Where RPL matters, report gross registered course length, credit deducted, credit origin/location, eligible Australian study and qualifying elapsed time separately. Keep both potentially material academic-load interpretations visible until authority resolves them.

**Limits / exceptions:** Do not announce current compliance under an assumed RPL convention or treat repeated RPL caveats as a universal offshore-study concession.


<a id="registration-events"></a>
## Course registration, suspension and deregistration



### R161 — Stored mandatory; application and decision

Use study of a CRICOS-registered course delivered by a provider registered to offer that course to overseas students; confirm course as well as provider registration for relevant dates.

**Limits / exceptions:** Provider nationality or an Australian campus name alone is not course registration.



### R162 — Stored conditional; application and decision

If an enrolled registered course is later deregistered, transfer to complete the qualification with a registered provider; qualifying earlier study contributing to the award can still count under the transfer rules.

**Limits / exceptions:** Do not discard all earlier registered study automatically.



### R163 — Stored mandatory; application and decision

Study continued after deregistration ordinarily does not count toward the study requirement.

**Limits / exceptions:** A narrowly discretionary near-completion policy may be relevant; it is not an automatic exemption.



### R164 — Stored policy; application and decision

If deregistration occurs less than two months before expected completion, stored policy allows consideration of an exception where expecting a provider change would be unreasonable; preserve deregistration and scheduled-completion dates for review.

**Limits / exceptions:** No entitlement is established. A related near-completion passage uses within two months: check boundary cases rather than silently treating exactly two months as resolved.



### R165 — Stored conditional; application and decision

Suspension to new enrolments does not defeat counting where the course remains CRICOS-registered.

**Limits / exceptions:** If suspension is followed by loss of registration, study after loss of registration does not count under the ordinary rule.


<a id="calendar-time"></a>
## Calendar-time ledger: at least 16 months



### R166 — Stored mandatory; application

At least 16 calendar months are required even where summer/winter semesters or more than a full-time load compress the course. Start from study commencement, generally lectures, excluding orientation; end at academic completion.

**Limits / exceptions:** Do not use a graduation-evaluation period or administrative delay to enlarge the time.



### R167 — Stored mandatory; application

Concurrent courses do not create duplicate calendar time: overlapping periods count only once toward the 16 months. The applicant must evidence the actual study timeline.

**Limits / exceptions:** Academic-load counting is a separate ledger, with no double use of the same credits/units.



### R168 — Stored policy; application

Official course breaks can form part of the overall course period, including travel overseas during an official break such as a Christmas family visit.

**Limits / exceptions:** This is not permission to count active offshore study or all gaps between separate courses; preserve break dates and provider approval evidence.


<a id="academic-load"></a>
## Academic-load ledger: registered duration, not slow attendance



### R169 — Checked mandatory; application

LIN 19/085 section 6 defines one academic year as at least 46 weeks of course duration. The dedicated 485 study guidance expresses two academic years as 92 registered weeks; this is separate from at least 16 calendar months.

**Limits / exceptions:** Refresh the applicable instrument and actual CRICOS records; U02 credit/RPL treatment can still prevent a conclusion. Taking longer or repeating subjects does not increase eligible registered load.

**Source and limits:** see [verification](verification.md) and the linked topical reference. Checked 16 October 2026; limited to the identified instruments/public directions.


### R170 — Stored mandatory; application

Use standard full-time CRICOS duration to measure academic load. Taking two calendar years to finish a 78-week course does not turn it into 92 weeks.

**Limits / exceptions:** A longer actual timeline can satisfy calendar duration without satisfying academic load.



### R171 — Stored mandatory; application

Failed subjects and repeats do not count twice or inflate the original registered load. Only successfully completed study counts toward the requirement.

**Limits / exceptions:** Artificially extending attendance cannot create additional eligible academic study.


<a id="instruction-language"></a>
## Language of instruction and specialist language units



### R172 — Stored mandatory; application and decision

All instruction must be in English under the study requirement. An Australian qualification may ordinarily be accepted as evidence of English instruction unless foreign-language courses/units raise the issue.

**Limits / exceptions:** This is separate from the visa's English-test/passport criterion.



### R173 — Stored policy; application and decision

An incidental foreign-language component may still meet stored policy; one or two first-year foreign-language units are illustrative. Obtain transcripts; no more than ten per cent of study in another language across the two academic years is the stated acceptable measure.

**Limits / exceptions:** Do not describe this policy tolerance as a universal statutory exemption or ignore a substantial non-English component.



### R174 — Stored conditional; application and decision

For advanced interpreting/translation qualifications containing instruction in another language, provide evidence that those units teach interpreting/translating skills. Stored guidance uses NAATI Professional Level as a guide; advanced diploma, bachelor and master's awards generally fit the advanced-qualification description.

**Limits / exceptions:** Verify current accreditation terminology and the actual course; this is a specialist branch, not unrestricted non-English instruction permission.


<a id="study-location-and-lawfulness"></a>
## Lawful study, physical presence, online and overseas components



### R175 — Stored mandatory; application and decision

Ordinarily relied-on study must be undertaken physically in Australia on a visa authorising that study, with compliance with its study conditions.

**Limits / exceptions:** U02: stored text repeatedly reserves RPL; U06: historical or other concessions require separate applicable-date verification rather than being guessed.



### R176 — Stored mandatory; application and decision

Exclude study beyond the visa's permitted study rights. Where a Working Holiday holder studies for six months but the visa permits only four, only the authorised four months may count under the ordinary rule.

**Limits / exceptions:** The stored text reserves RPL; do not treat excess study as lawful merely because the provider enrolled the student.



### R177 — Stored conditional; application and decision

Online delivery does not remove the physical-Australia requirement for ordinary study counting; active online study while returning home or travelling offshore does not count as Australian study time.

**Limits / exceptions:** U02/U06: reserved RPL and any applicable historical concession need reconciliation; do not assume all COVID-era offshore study qualifies or fails without checking.



### R178 — Checked support; pre-application

Separate provider delivery compliance from visa-study counting. National Code Standard 8 uses one-third of units or equivalent and a contact-unit rule; stored 485 policy and a Department program report use a 25% statement in the visa context.

**Limits / exceptions:** U06 remains unresolved on the exact visa percentage, denominator and historical application. Do not enforce either percentage as a universal 485 counting shortcut or say one-third automatically replaced 25%.

**Source and limits:** see [verification](verification.md) and the linked topical reference. Checked 16 October 2026; limited to the identified instruments/public directions.


### R179 — Stored conditional; application and decision

For distance education, student/teacher separation by time or location does not make offshore participation count as Australian study; identify where the student actually was during the relied-on period.

**Limits / exceptions:** The stored text reserves RPL. Later-regional distance-study rules must not be transferred into the first-visa study test.



### R180 — Stored conditional; application and decision

Study at an Australian institution's overseas campus does not ordinarily count as Australian study because it occurred offshore.

**Limits / exceptions:** The stored text reserves RPL; Australian award/provider status is not physical presence.



### R181 — Stored conditional; application and decision

An offshore exchange period does not ordinarily count as Australian study.

**Limits / exceptions:** The stored text reserves RPL; separately assess the remaining eligible Australian components.



### R182 — Stored conditional; application and decision

Some offshore study does not automatically defeat the whole application: the remaining qualifying Australian study can still meet at least two academic years over no less than 16 calendar months. The study pattern is not itself decisive, even if the course finishes offshore.

**Limits / exceptions:** All other award, timing, registration and lawful-study gates remain; U02 may affect the academic-load calculation.



### R183 — Stored support; pre-application

A six-semester bachelor example has semesters 1 and 5 offshore and semesters 2, 3, 4 and 6 in Australia; stored guidance treats those four Australian semesters as two academic years.

**Limits / exceptions:** Do not infer 16 calendar months or all other requirements from a semester count alone; inspect dates and qualifying load.



### R184 — Stored conditional; application and decision

Overseas research time within a research component does not ordinarily count toward Australian study.

**Limits / exceptions:** The stored text reserves RPL; do not assume research is exempt because supervision or the awarding institution is Australian.


## Study finding format

For each material gate give: finding/status, known facts and evidence, applicable R IDs, unresolved issue/assumption, and the next preparation action. Keep a supported document-quality finding separate from a legal-compliance conclusion. Do not state “study requirement met” while a material RPL, location, completion, registration or applicable-version issue remains unresolved.

Use [U01–U10 issue gates](operational-knowledge.md#unresolved-issues), particularly U02 credits/RPL, U05 evidence format, U06 online/historical concessions and U08 changeable definitions. In a case-specific output show only material unresolved issues; do not copy the entire maintenance list.

## Material public-wording and historical-study distinctions

For a graduate diploma, 485.111 and stored guidance require **commencement** in the same/next academic year after the preceding degree’s completion. The PHEW public page says **completion** in that period. Use the law for a legal-rule comparison, preserve the discrepancy and obtain clarification if the distinction changes the result; do not reject solely from the public shorthand. Obtain both awards, entry decision, related-subject evidence and actual dates.

Public PHEW guidance preserves online study during **1 February 2020–25 November 2023**; a Department June 2024 report describes the interval as inclusive and refers to study-authorising visas. Separate ordinary onshore, ordinary offshore and claimed historical-concession units. The continuing mechanism under the dedicated 485 clauses and a case depending on the last boundary day need verification. Do not erase the concession because the ordinary rule requires presence in Australia.

Under U02 show competing registered-load calculations only where material. The public study page’s credit deductions and make-up language do not safely resolve the stored gross-duration/RPL position. No provider’s decision to award credit alone establishes visa-study compliance.
