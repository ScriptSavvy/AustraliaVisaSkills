# English evidence — Subclass 485

Read this for an English-evidence question, together with [verification](verification.md). This reference preserves application-date and test-date branches. It is not a universal accepted-test or score table. Source bodies were checked on 16 October 2026; refresh the applicable instrument and complete schedule before comparing an applicant's scores.

## 1. Decide whose requirement applies

Clause 485.212 is a primary criterion. Where it applies, the application must be accompanied by evidence of the specified passport or qualifying test/score within the specified period. Do not automatically impose a fresh primary test on secondary applicants. The exception concerns an applicant who **meets** 485.232–485.235, not everyone who merely chooses the regional/subsequent label. The retained historical Replacement exception is not a new-primary stream invitation.

The 2025 instrument commences **7 August 2025**, even if register metadata uses another date. Its explanatory statement gives an inaccurate earlier-instrument commencement and a simplified fixed earlier-test interval. Use operative ss2, 10 and 11, not those shortcuts. An old result's availability in another visa category until August 2028 does not override the 485 one-year rule.

## 3. Passport and test-product distinctions

Under LIN 25/089 s9 the alternative is a **valid passport issued by Canada, New Zealand, Republic of Ireland, United Kingdom or United States of America to a citizen of that country**. Residence, nationality without the specified passport, or an expired passport does not itself establish that alternative. Hong Kong/BNO passports select special test schedules; they are not the same as the s9 passport exemption.

C1 Advanced appears in the instrument's general specified-test list but is excluded for the new-test branch under s7 and the new schedules. It can remain relevant under the saved earlier-test schedules. Do not tell an applicant that all listed brands are accepted for every test date.

The checked PHEW public page accepts IELTS One Skill Retake and MET Single Section Retake for the new-test branch, completed before lodgement. OET reports may lack the required overall score; obtain the actual overall result. General public guidance requires a secure test centre and rejects fully online/at-home products. It instructs TOEFL registrants to select “Taking TOEFL for Australia”. Exact historical TOEFL interruptions, unusual delayed results, and retakes crossing 7 August 2025 need date-specific verification; current brand acceptance does not establish historical acceptance.

## 4. Evidence comparison workflow

1. Use the known actual/intended application date and primary/secondary/regional role. Ask only the next missing material fact, with the continuing question label.
2. Inspect the passport alternative or actual test report. Record product, test date, delivery mode, scores and any score-achievement/retake/report dates that matter. A filename or applicant statement is not inspected evidence.
3. Retrieve the applicable instrument version, application/savings provisions and **complete relevant schedule**. Check title/version, effective interval and actual source body. The approved test list alone is insufficient.
4. Copy the relevant official row into a temporary comparison: overall threshold, each listening/reading/writing/speaking threshold, exceptional branch, and timing/product restrictions. Record source section, retrieval date and application/test-date assumptions. Compare every cell; do not import competent-English, vocational-English, Student visa or provider-admission scores.
5. Calculate date comparisons with a date tool. Keep test administration, score achievement, retake and report generation separate where the text does. Do not assume a later report proves a new eligible test date.
6. Give separate presence, readability, completeness, consistency, currency and verified-requirement findings. Missing overall/component results are evidence gaps, not a zero or automatic failure. If the source is unavailable, report supported document-quality findings and mark compliance **Needs verification**. Do not invent a score table or claim current verification.
