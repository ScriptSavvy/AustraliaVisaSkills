# Verification, source hierarchy and unresolved issues

## Read before making a requirement comparison

This skill combines stored preparation knowledge with targeted official checks. Stored guidance is not automatically current law; an official webpage is not legislation; an explanatory statement is not operative text. Original source provenance is maintained separately. The portable skill does not require private documents or claim exclusively public-source development. It deliberately preserves useful knowledge that has not been independently corroborated, while limiting legal conclusions.

All targeted online checks reported here were made on **16 October 2026**. They do not establish comprehensive currency, future availability or legal review. The [source register](official-sources.md) gives actual official URLs, dates, versions and covered topics. Read [English evidence](english-requirement.md) for tests/passports; read [study requirements](study-requirement.md) for completion, qualifications and three separate study ledgers.

## Authority and refresh procedure

1. Fix the **actual or intended application date**, stage, stream/pathway, primary/secondary role and any material study/test/grant dates. Ask one missing fact at a time; do not request a full history by default.
2. Retrieve applicable legislation and exact instruments, including definitions, commencement, application/savings provisions and amendments not yet consolidated. Inspect the version history and displayed effective interval; a `latest` URL can still return a version whose interval has ended. Resolve that boundary from actual bodies rather than infer continuity.
3. Compare the actual Department stream, study and instructed form/request guidance. Use the authority responsible for a separate requirement, such as the relevant assessor or course register. Preserve their scope and dates. Secondary sources are leads, not silent replacements for authoritative material.
4. Record source title, URL, authority, version, publication/update if available, effective/application interval, check date and exact relevant section. Mark unavailable metadata **unknown**. A retrieval date, export date or webpage update is not commencement.
5. For each material finding classify mandatory, conditional (with known/unknown trigger), stored policy/evidence expectation, useful supporting evidence, or practical suggestion. Preserve actor, stage, exceptions, alternatives and discretion. If a rule is unresolved, continue independent checks and withhold only its dependent compliance finding.
6. If browsing is unavailable or the official body cannot be read, say exactly what remains unverified. Successful retrieval of a loading shell is not policy evidence. Do not claim to have browsed or convert recollection into confirmed rules.

A result may be document-ready for one named check while legal compliance remains **Needs verification**. Never convert overall preparation to an eligibility, validity, authenticity, official-acceptance or approval finding.

### Corrected location, count and regional rules

- **Location:** item1229(3)(f)/(g) controls where a person may apply. The offshore qualifying-visa limb in (4)(a)(ia) does not override it. Ordinary new-primary/regional primary applications remain onshore under the inspected text. Already-primary-holder family and retained Replacement/family wording need their own facts. Grant under 485.411 can be in or outside Australia, not immigration clearance; it does not authorise offshore lodgement.
- **Counts:** item1229(4)(aa) prospectively counts the proposed visa within no more than four total and no more than one grant based on .232–.235. Clause485.211(a)–(c) contains prior-primary exclusions; (d) separately refers to three previous PSW and two previous PHEW visas. Do not collapse these or discard secondary-held visas from numerical tests.
- **Four regional branches:** .232 and .234 use regional centres/other regional areas; .233 and .235 use the wider designated-regional category and exclude their corresponding narrower branch. Wider declarations are **.233(4)/.235(4)**; wider decision requirements are **.233(3)/.235(3)**. Keep the study, prior-visa, two-year residence/work/study, combined-family declarations and decision requirements separate. Reg1.15M(1)/(2) specifies distinct tiers.
- **Duration/conditions:** 485.513 specifies a Minister-set date, not a legislated one-/two-year or Tasmania guarantee. Stored duration and Tasmania branches are policy. Condition8610 preserves the matching tier, including related secondary grants and grant-date geographic classification while in Australia. Exact postcode history and duration settings require refresh.
- **Skills-assessment currency:** read 485.224(1) and (1A) together: suitable assessment during the last three years, and a stated validity period must not have ended. A future stated expiry does not alone displace the three-year limb. This preserves the full stored rule rather than compressing it into an either/or test.
- **Dependent child:** inspected reg2.08B includes Class VC. Its legal application time is the **latest** of request receipt and required charge payments, subject to all request, dependency, whereabouts and timing conditions. Newborn reg2.08 and this mechanism are different; neither supplies a generic spouse-addition permission.

### AFP application-time evidence

Where 485.213(1) applies, the application must be accompanied by evidence of the primary applicant's and included persons' (aged at least16) AFP applications during the 12 months immediately before the application day. The public primary-stream instruction explicitly excludes same-day AFP applications. Apply no later than the day before visa submission. A later receipt/certificate date is distinct from actual check application; preserve submission confirmation if a receipt is delayed. Do not promise a later request cures a missing application-time fact or evidence.

The 485.213(2) exception requires meeting .232–.235, not merely choosing a subsequent stream. It does not remove character assessment or later AFP requirements. The second-stream public page requires an AFP check for grant. Do not mechanically impose primary first-stream timing on every separate secondary application or mark character evidence Not applicable.
