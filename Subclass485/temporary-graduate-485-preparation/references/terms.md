# Procedural terms — Temporary Graduate visa (subclass 485)

**Private candidate, not cleared for publication.** Plain-language preparation explanations, not substitutes for applicable legal definitions or legal advice.

See [verification](verification.md) for source dates and limits. These explanations preserve role and stage distinctions; use the applicable legal definition when it changes a result.

| Term | Meaning and boundary for preparation |
|---|---|
| Law / policy / directions | Legislation and applicable instruments set legal requirements. Policy guides administration/discretion; policy flexibility is not a power for an applicant or assistant to waive legislation. Form or authorising-email directions can govern a particular lodgement route. Ministerial directions are a different, specialised source, not interchangeable with a form instruction.  |
| Class / subclass / stream | Distinct classification levels. Identify the actual subclass485 stream and applicable Schedule1 pathway; a valid application for one route is not proof of validity for every route in its class. The stored 485 primary-applicant rule requires one nominated stream.  |
| Applicant / declared relative | A person actually applying, including where a deemed mechanism applies, is not the same as a relative merely disclosed in a form. The older instruction often uses “application” as shorthand for a valid one; do not use that convention to assume validity before checking it.  |
| Primary applicant / primary criteria | The person named as primary is a workflow/form label; primary criteria are the legal grant criteria. The labels are not universally identical. For485 identify the intended primary/secondary criteria route and nominated stream instead of automatically reassigning a spouse as primary.  |
| Secondary applicant / family-unit member | A legal relationship and the applicable criteria must be met, not merely an intention to join a family. The phrase “family member” can mean disclosed relative, combined applicant or a defined category depending on context. Verify regulation1.12 and the485 criteria for the relevant person/date; do not equate every relative with a dependant.  |
| Combined applications | Separate persons' applications permitted to be made together; not one indivisible family entitlement or a universal single-fee rule. Separate evidence/findings and withdrawal consequences remain.  |
| Delegate / officer / external assessor | The authorised Minister/delegate makes the grant/refusal decision. Other staff may assist; medical, skills or other agencies may provide prescribed assessments, but a referral/check is not transfer of the visa decision or promise of grant. Earlier assessments do not necessarily bind the final delegate.  |
| Validity / eligibility / evidence sufficiency | Whether an application was validly made, whether grant requirements are met, and whether evidence proves a claim are distinct. An uploaded file, fee receipt or acknowledgement does not decide any of them.  |
| Making / payment / acknowledgement | Payment may occur before or after an attempted submission; drafts and receipts are different events. Stored guidance locates valid making when all requirements are met, subject to applicable special mechanisms. Do not substitute device time or payment date for the legally attributed application timestamp.  |
| Application-time / decision-time criterion | A criterion's required time is part of the test, not a date that evidence flexibility can erase. The newborn mechanism has specific treatment; a dependent-child addition has a distinct deemed time. Verify savings/amendments rather than treating all family routes alike.  |
| Newborn deemed application / dependent-child addition | Reg2.08 concerns birth after parental application but before decision; reg2.08B concerns dependent children of listed temporary classes and a qualifying request/payment/location mechanism. Neither is generic spouse addition; reg2.08A concerns certain permanent applications. Current485/class coverage requires verification.  |
| Residence / correspondence address | Where a person actually/intends to live is distinct from where documents are received. Dated Act s52 requires notice of proposed residence changes for14 days or more during processing. Recipient appointment does not replace that duty or define post-grant conditions.  |
| Authorised recipient / assistance / electronic consent | An appointment to receive documents differs from helping complete a form or providing professional advice. Check the appointment's scope/currentness. Consent to electronic delivery concerns the intended recipient; giving an email address alone does not establish it under stored policy.  |
| Dispatch / actual receipt / deemed receipt / opening | Different events, possibly different dates and recipients. Legal notification and deadline rules cannot be replaced by the applicant's date of opening an email. Verify the actual notice, method, consent, appointment and applicable provisions before calculation.  |
| Request / extension requested / extension granted | A request for more information can differ from unsolicited evidence or an adverse-information invitation. Asking for more time does not change the deadline; establish an actual grant and new period. No generic seven-,28- or70-day period applies in this reference.  |
| Withdrawal / deletion / non-response | Written withdrawal of a specified person's application differs from deleting an account entry, not replying or making a fresh application. Effectiveness depends on the proper process and timing, not merely pressing send. One person's withdrawal does not withdraw another person's application.  |
| Decision / grant / notification / effect | Recorded decision-making, sending/receiving notice and a visa taking effect are distinct. Period of stay, travel facility and conditions also need separate checking. Progress or “approved” shorthand is not proof of grant; “visa extension” should not obscure that a further application/grant may be required.  |
| Pending / finally determined / review | “No primary decision yet” differs from “not finally determined”. The latter is a technical legal expression potentially involving review rules. The stored source's historic Tribunal/Parts5/7 wording is not a current review-rights assessment; do not merely rename old institutions and reuse old rules.  |
| Substantive / bridging / temporary / permanent visa | These are legally defined categories, not loose synonyms for pending/granted. A bridging visa is not proof of substantive-visa eligibility or unrestricted work/travel; an application/payment/extension request is not proof that any visa is in effect. Verify actual grants/conditions and relevant law.  |
