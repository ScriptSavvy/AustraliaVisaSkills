# Document handling — Temporary Graduate visa (subclass 485)

**Private candidate, not cleared for publication.** Preparation support only; no assurance of document acceptance, legal sufficiency or grant. Do not transmit documents or access an applicant account without authority. Use redacted working copies and minimum necessary personal information.

## Applicability limits

Separate stored evidence-handling knowledge, current public document instructions, a specific Department request and verified applicable law. This is not a universal evidence packet. See [verification](verification.md) for source dates and material conflicts. Do not use another subclass's upload instructions as authority for this one.

## Link evidence to the claim

Maintain a person-specific inventory: claimed fact and criterion, relevant date/stage, document/issuer/date/language, source of copy, translation/certification state, what it proves, what it does not prove, contradictions, and next step. Distinguish a legal requirement, an actual Department request, a policy preference and a useful preparation item. Do not mark an unverified item “not applicable”. Read the actual material, rather than infer its contents from a filename or applicant description.

The decision-maker determines weight and sufficiency. A genuine document can be incomplete, irrelevant or insufficient; official alteration of a birth/identity record can require an explanation and other records even if alteration was lawful. Certification proves the copy's correspondence to an original, not the truth of every statement; translation does not authenticate the underlying document. Identify inconsistent dates, names, relationship histories and alternative inferences. Do not suppress inconvenient evidence or label a disputed document fraudulent without a proper basis. Preserve originals and the explanation; seek qualified help for integrity concerns.

Subclass-specific timing takes priority over generic flexibility. Stored subclass-specific guidance identifies, for example, secondary-applicant health-insurance evidence accompanying the application and adequate cover in Australia since application. A later explanation or alternate record is not automatically a substitute for an expressly required application-time item. Determine what the applicable criterion actually permits; keep “missing”, “unavailable” and “needs legal verification” distinct.

## Translations, scans and certification

The three official 485 stream pages read on 16 October 2026 say ordinary digital documents do not need certification. They ask for English translations plus original-language documents, clear colour scans/photos, and one file for a multi-page document. Australian translators must be NAATI-accredited; overseas translations must identify the translator's name, address/telephone and qualifications/experience in English. These are current public preparation directions, not a promise that certification/originals can never be requested. Recheck the selected stream and any individual request; preserve detailed completion-letter contents even where format instructions differ. An AI translation for understanding is not proof of accredited translation. **Study-specific conflict:** the dedicated official 485 study page still requests certified completion-letter and transcript copies. Do not convert the stream upload note into a universal no-certification finding. Mark that affected format question Needs verification; follow an express request or seek clarification. See U05 in [verification](verification.md). See [official stream sources](verification.md).

The stored source distinguishes these propositions:
- It generally preferred originals or certified true copies of official supporting records, with permitted written/electronic arrangements and special transmission arrangements for assessing bodies.
- Copies of an applicant's own communications could be acceptable; this is not identical to acceptance of an uncertified official certificate.
- Electronic or uncertified official documents were not, by that fact alone, a reason for refusal; relevant uncertainty could prompt an original/certified-copy request.
- Non-English supporting material did not, under that general guidance alone, justify refusing to accept or consider the application; officers could request English translations. This is an acceptance/consideration distinction, **not permission to ignore actual translation directions or assume evidence is sufficient**.


Keep readable, complete scans including reverse sides, seals and annotations relevant to the claim; do not alter content, merge different versions as if identical or call an incomplete extract a complete record. This is preparation quality control, not a newly asserted legislative format rule. Keep a transmission inventory and the version supplied. Send originals only where instructed and keep a copy and secure-return arrangements; the stored policy generally envisaged return of originals after examination/copying, not routine surrender of irreplaceable originals.

## When a relevant document cannot be obtained

Record the exact missing record, fact it was meant to prove, issuing authority, attempts/dates/responses, practical impediment and available alternatives. Stored policy recognises genuine difficulty, including civil unrest, and expects officers to exercise judgment and try verification; this is **not a waiver entitlement or permission for a preparer to certify the criterion as met**.

Possible preparation routes include an issuer's written availability statement or replacement procedure; official information about record availability; evidence from a relevant foreign government mission; other contemporaneous records explaining identity, dates or relationships; and an honest account of unsuccessful attempts. A Department officer may seek overseas-post advice or use authorised historical records to verify a claim. Do not claim the applicant or assistant has access to internal case systems, or ask applicants to contact staff-only units. Past migration records may assist verification but are not automatically current evidence in this application. Map every alternative to the specific fact and disclose its limitations.

If there is a live request, supply what is available by the correct channel, explain the gap, and request more time before expiry if needed. Seeking more time does not grant it, and alternative documents do not automatically satisfy a mandatory application-time requirement. See [application-procedures.md](application-procedures.md) for requests, actual/deemed receipt and late information.

## Personal identifiers and forms

Photographs, signatures, fingerprints and other personal identifiers are conditional requirements, not a universal bundle. The older source's broad photograph statement must be read with its conditional framework: requirement actually imposed for the relevant purpose, applicable exemption/waiver and compliance. Check actual form/request, person, identifier type, collection channel and completion; do not invent a biometrics demand or exemption. Do not solicit biometric images or signatures into ordinary chat.

Use the correct form/version and follow its directions. Stored paper practice envisaged signatures from each adult applicant and persons assisting with completion. For a minor primary applicant it focused on capacity to understand: approximately 16 was a policy guide, not a universal legal age threshold; an adult parent/legal guardian generally signed for younger minors and could sign for older minors who had not signed, with separate adoption/orphan practices. This does not make every online declaration require a wet signature or allow an unrelated helper to act as guardian. Verify the actual form, capacity and authority; disclose assistance truthfully.

Stored paper-amendment practice envisaged initialling/dating changes and signing/dating added sheets by each adult applicant. Preserve a legible audit trail and do not silently overwrite answers or alter original evidence. For online corrections use the actual instructed correction process, not invented paper conventions. An address omitted for a security concern requires a secure authorised alternative supplying the residence, not a false postal substitute.
