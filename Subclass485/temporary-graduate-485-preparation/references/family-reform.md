## Application-date gate

N1, section 2 and Schedule 1 item 19, inserts Schedule 13 clause **17001**. The amendment commenced **13 November 2026** and applies to visa applications made on or after commencement.

- **Application before 13 November 2026:** preserve the rules applicable to that actual application date. The reform does not simply convert every pending family application to the new rules. Verify historical versions and other applicable changes rather than treating the stored October position as the whole history.
- **Application on or after 13 November 2026:** use the new gates below. The primary's older visa grant or earlier application does not itself grandfather a new later secondary application.
- **Unknown or disputed application date:** mark dependent applicability **Needs verification**. Do not offer a separate later family route or label the new inclusion gate satisfied. Request only the next material fact or redacted submission record.
- **Deemed child application:** establish the mechanism and its own legally attributed application time. Do not automatically use the parent's date or notification date; see the child section and U11 in [verification](verification.md).

Keep legal application time separate from draft creation, payment, receipt generation and a device clock. This gate does not remove the entry point's restriction on consequential deadline-status and remaining-time calculations.

## Secondary inclusion — at least one branch

For applications within N1's new scope, Schedule 1 item **1229(3)(e)(i)–(vi)** requires at least one branch below for the secondary applicant. Branches are alternatives, not cumulative. A failure to establish one branch does not exclude another; unknown facts are not an exemption or a confirmed failure.

| Branch | Required circumstances | Evidence to check only if material |
|---|---|---|
| (i) Specified passport | The primary holds a passport from a legally defined **Pacific-regional country** or a country listed in item **1222(5A)**. | Passport issuer/type and relevant holding facts; the applicable statutory definition/list. Nationality, residence or an informal regional label alone does not establish passport holding. |
| (ii) Completed doctorate | The primary's **most recent student visa** was granted in relation to a doctoral degree that has been completed. | Relevant student grant/course basis and actual academic completion. An earlier unrelated doctorate, a research master's or a title containing “Doctor” does not by itself establish this branch. |
| (iii) Fully funded courses | The primary's **most recent student visa** was granted for one or more courses, **each fully funded by a foreign government**. | Visa/course linkage and funding terms for every relevant packaged course. Partial funding or one funded course in a partly unfunded package does not establish this branch. Do not infer that a scholarship resolves the separate eligible-student-visa restrictions. |
| (iv) Child after relevant grant | The secondary is the primary's child and was born after the primary's **most recent student visa or subclass 485 visa grant**. | Parentage, birth date and actual relevant grant chronology. Do not substitute a visa-application date or invent an age-in-days definition of “newborn”. |
| (v) Same-primary Student-secondary history | The secondary **currently holds**, **held on 2 October 2026**, or **held at any time during the six months ending immediately before the application day**, a student visa granted on secondary criteria linked to the **same primary**, who holds or held it on primary criteria. | Both people's linked grant records and the relevant holding dates. The three timing alternatives are disjunctive; the 2 October limb is not a six-month-only rule. A former dependant of a different primary does not establish this branch. |
| (vi) Same-primary 485-secondary history | The secondary **currently holds**, or **held at any time during the six months ending immediately before the application day**, a subclass 485 granted on secondary criteria linked to the **same primary**, who holds or held it on primary criteria. | Linked primary/secondary 485 history and relevant holding dates. Do not import the Student branch's special 2 October date into this branch. |

N4 supplies the definition/list: Pacific-regional countries include **Federated States of Micronesia, Fiji, Kiribati, Nauru, Palau, Papua New Guinea, Republic of the Marshall Islands, Samoa, Solomon Islands, Timor-Leste, Tonga, Tuvalu and Vanuatu**. Item 1222(5A) lists **Brunei, Cambodia, Indonesia, Laos, Malaysia, Myanmar, Philippines, Singapore, Thailand and Vietnam**. Refresh the actual lists before a consequential comparison; “Pacific” or “ASEAN” in public summaries is not a substitute for the legal passport/list test.

Meeting a branch does not waive the family-unit definition, combined-application timing, physical location, onshore status, insurance, character, other applicable requirements or the primary applicant's independent gates. Do not collect all branch evidence by default. Use known facts and ask only the next fact that changes the requested result.

## Application timing, location and onshore secondary status

N1 Schedule 1 items 7 and 13 establish independent application-time gates:

- **Primary — 1229(3)(d):** in Australia, not in immigration clearance. This includes an ordinary first or regional second/subsequent primary application.
- **Secondary — 1229(3)(f):** may be inside or outside Australia, not in immigration clearance, but must apply **at the same time as, and combined with, the primary application**. Do not impose a same-physical-place requirement. An eligible offshore family member may apply with the onshore primary.
- **No ordinary later separate route:** the inclusion exceptions above do not permit a separate subsequent-entrant application after the primary applies or is granted a visa. New 485.311 also requires the combined application at decision. Child deemed/addition mechanisms must be analysed separately, not used as a generic workaround.
- **Onshore secondary — 1229(3)(h)/(4A):** must additionally meet one of the status alternatives below. An offshore secondary is not subject to this particular onshore-status gate, but all other applicable requirements remain.

New **1229(4A)** alternatives are:

1. Hold a **substantive visa**; or
2. Hold **Bridging A (Class WA)** or **Bridging B (Class WB)** granted on the basis of a **valid visa application**; or
3. Meet the precisely worded student-visa cancellation/non-revocation ART limb: the ART set aside and substituted the Minister's decision **not to revoke the cancellation** of the applicant's student visa, with the specified oral-notification treatment under Act **368(7)** or other deemed-receipt treatment under **379C**, **not more than 28 days before the application day**.

Do not reduce alternative 3 to any favourable Tribunal decision, use a hearing date instead of the specified notification event, or promise a grace period/lawful stay. Record the actual decision and notification facts; a consequential legal-date or status assessment needs the exact authority and appropriately qualified review. Do not apply the primary's separate six-month eligible-student history requirement indiscriminately to secondaries.

New **485.311** requires family-unit membership at decision of a primary-criteria 485 holder **and a combined application with that person**. Relationship evidence alone is not proof of the combined-application requirement. ImmiAccount allowing a person to be included is not legal verification; N2 expressly warns that the portal may allow ineligible family inclusion.

## Child mechanisms — keep the chronology and uncertainty visible

1. **Child already born before the combined application:** check the applicable inclusion branch and all other triggered gates. Branch (iv) is not a separate subsequent-entrant permission.
2. **Birth after a parent's application but before decision — reg 2.08:** N4 retains the deemed application/combination mechanism; N1 does not amend that regulation. N5's current stream instructions still describe the newborn as making the same application as the parent. Obtain the birth/application/decision chronology and follow the actual notification/evidence directions. Do not automatically require a fresh paid application, declare automatic grant, or turn the general no-later-family statement into a prohibition on every newborn during processing. Check the child's applicable version/criteria where the deemed time crosses commencement.
3. **Birth in Australia to a current 485 holder:** N2 preserves automatic same-kind visa treatment and notification. Keep this distinct from a fresh subsequent-entrant application; do not make an individual legal-status determination from a filename or statement alone.
4. **Birth overseas after the parent's 485 grant:** N2 says the child cannot be added to the granted visa through a new separate subsequent-entrant application. Distinguish a later eligible **combined** application by the parent from an unavailable separate child application; do not promise that a further primary pathway exists.
5. **Other dependent-child addition — reg 2.08B / U11:** the inspected base law still lists Class VC and deems a combined application at the latest request/payment time. Its interaction with the new same-time requirement and transitional rule is **unresolved in this skill**. Preserve the request/payment/location and secondary-criteria conditions, but do not promise addition, say reg 2.08B was repealed, backdate the child's application, or treat the route as a spouse-addition mechanism. Follow the source positions, effect and exact clarification action in [U11](verification.md#material-unresolved-issues-and-operational-effect).

## Under-18 primary applicants

For applications within the new scope, N1 item 17 inserts **485.216(2A)**: if the primary applicant has not turned 18, **PIC 4017 and 4018** apply. They concern custody/parental responsibility and the child's best interests. Organise relevant truthful consent, parentage or court-order material only as supported by the actual PIC and circumstances; do not invent a universal document list.

Part 485's default is decision-time assessment unless otherwise stated. New (2A) does not reproduce the “at the time of application” age wording used for values criterion 4019 in (2). Preserve that distinction and check the actual age at the relevant stage. Do not substitute combined-family PIC 4015/4016 or the secondary applicant's own 485.313 criteria for this primary branch.

## Superseded provisions and unchanged branches

N1 items 8–16 remove the old offshore primary qualifying-visa limb **1229(4)(a)(ia)**, obsolete Replacement application provisions and the Replacement exception in **485.212(2)** for applications in the new scope. Keep the older wording only for applicable historical review. Item 12 also removes **1229(4)(a)(viii)(C)**, not the whole remaining (viii) limb. If a primary relies on retained (viii)(A)/(B), read that actual text with the independent stream, prior-primary and other gates; do not infer a new eligibility entitlement from cleanup wording or treat the whole limb as repealed. First-primary Replacement applications were already closed after 30 June 2024; this amendment does not reopen them.

Do **not** delete existing Replacement-holder regional history routes: **1229(3)(la), 1229(4)(a)(vi), 485.234 and 485.235** remain relevant where applicable. The regional English exception for applicants actually meeting 485.232–.235 remains. Do not invent changed English scores, ordinary adult age thresholds, study requirements or grant-duration settings from this family reform.

N1 items 1–6 remove separate subsequent-entrant charge limbs, not all family charges or the retained charge tables. Keep R015's applicable-date fee verification; do not offer a fee waiver or quote a new ordinary fee increase on that basis.
