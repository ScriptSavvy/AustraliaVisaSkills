## How to use the stored knowledge

This is stored preparation knowledge, not a determination of eligibility, application validity, government acceptance or likely approval. **Rules labelled Stored preserve preparation knowledge; rules labelled Checked identify targeted official verification, with its date and limits.** Stored mandatory/conditional labels describe the requirement represented in the knowledge, not independent verification that it is currently applicable. Policy describes an expectation or discretion, support gives evidence/examples, and practical describes preparation steps. A policy exception is not a waiver of law.

Use the stable R IDs in findings. Check application, test, study, grant and decision dates separately. Before marking a legal-requirement check satisfied, verify the applicable official requirement for the person's stream, stage and dates, recording the actual source body, check date and effective interval. If the source is unavailable, conflicting or outside its effective interval, mark the affected finding **Needs verification**; do not call stored policy current law. Targeted legislative and public-guidance checks are recorded in [verification](verification.md). The issue register distinguishes corrected source defects from genuinely unresolved questions; no comprehensive currency audit is claimed.

For document work assess **presence, readability, completeness, consistency, currency and verified-requirement compliance separately**. Use Ready for the stated preparation check / Missing / Needs correction / Needs verification / Not applicable, and use Not applicable only where the triggering facts support it. Unknown is not a failure, a zero or Not applicable. A clear file can still contain inconsistent dates or lack verified legal compliance. Do not authenticate a document or guarantee government acceptance.

Answer the applicant's actual question first; use only the relevant branches, not a full checklist for a narrow request. If a needed fact is missing, use the entrypoint's adaptive-intake rules: request one missing fact, show a rendered continuing label (for example `Question 2 — total not yet known:`), and wait. Do not print variable letters, append a conditional second request, or ask the applicant to select a stream they have already said is unknown. Keep numbering through clarifications, use volunteered facts and request only redacted extracts necessary for the task. Prioritise impending status/deadline risks without inventing an extension. No real applicant data is stored in these reusable references. Do not submit, pay, contact third parties or upload externally without specific authority.

## Route through the references

- First PVEW: application/visa/age gates, common evidence, vocational study and occupation relationship, skills assessment, then the complete [study reference](study-requirement.md).
- First PHEW: application/visa/age gates, common evidence, first-degree/type/institution gates, then the complete [study reference](study-requirement.md).
- Regional second/subsequent PHEW: application/visa/count gates, the matching one of four regional branches, regional evidence/physical-location/holiday/Tasmania rules, family and grant conditions. Do not reimpose first-grant English/study criteria automatically.
- Secondary: family application/location mechanism, family relationship at decision, own insurance/public-interest/return checks, any triggered AFP evidence, and grant conditions. The existing primary's stream/history can affect the family pathway.

Application location and qualifying visa are independent gates (U01). Prior-visa counts and first-grant prohibitions are independent gates (U07). Application-time facts and later decision evidence are independent gates. Keep them in separate findings.

## Authority and pathway selection



### R001 — Stored policy; all stages

Law takes priority over stored policy. Policy supports consistent decisions but is not law and must not be applied inflexibly; an individual case may justify a lawful departure.

**Limits / exceptions:** No discretionary policy departure can waive an objective statutory requirement.



### R002 — Stored practical; pre-application

Identify whether the task concerns first PVEW, first PHEW, regional second/subsequent PHEW, or a secondary family application before selecting evidence requirements. The visa supports temporary living, study and work after eligible Australian study.

**Limits / exceptions:** A subsequent regional pathway has different criteria from a first grant.



### R003 — Stored mandatory; application

A primary applicant must nominate exactly one stream when applying; the selected stream cannot be changed after lodgement.

**Limits / exceptions:** No post-lodgement stream-change mechanism is described.



### R004 — Stored support; pre-application

An employer sponsor, Australian-relative sponsor or State/Territory nomination is not required for this visa.

**Limits / exceptions:** Other eligibility and evidence requirements still apply.



### R005 — Stored conditional; pre-application

PVEW is the vocational pathway for eligible associate-degree, diploma or trade graduates who nominate an occupation on the applicable specified list.

**Limits / exceptions:** The former name Graduate Work may appear in visa records.



### R006 — Stored conditional; pre-application

First PHEW concerns recent eligible Australian higher-education degrees, regardless of field of study; a later regional PHEW pathway may be available if its distinct conditions are met.

**Limits / exceptions:** The former name Post-Study Work may appear in visa records.



### R007 — Stored conditional; pre-application

The former Replacement stream accepted eligible primary applications from 1 July 2022 through 30 June 2024. From 1 July 2024 the stored pathway is limited to family members of an existing primary Replacement visa holder; eligible Replacement holders may instead investigate subsequent regional PHEW.

**Limits / exceptions:** Do not offer Replacement as an ordinary new-primary stream.



### R187 — Stored support; historical application

The former primary Replacement pathway restored the same stay length as the first 485 by qualification level, regardless of how much time was spent in Australia on that first visa.

**Limits / exceptions:** Historical explanation only; do not offer a new primary Replacement application after its closure.



### R188 — Stored conditional; historical application

The stored historical Replacement cancellation restriction required a cancelled 485, or a cancelled visa granted after holding a 485, to have been cancelled at the applicant's request on the specified ground, or the cancellation decision to have been set aside by the Tribunal.

**Limits / exceptions:** This is a historical Replacement validity branch, not a universal cure for cancellation or a new-primary route.


<a id="application-method-and-charge"></a>
## Application method, exceptional paper route and charges



### R008 — Stored mandatory; application

Application validity is separate from grant eligibility. Use the approved form and prescribed place/manner, pay the applicable charge, and satisfy the location and pathway gates at lodgement.

**Limits / exceptions:** A complete evidence file does not itself make an invalid application valid.



### R009 — Stored mandatory; application

The stored ordinary method is online through ImmiAccount using form 1276 (internet). Paper form 1409 requires prior authorisation by email; inability to lodge online is not itself permission to use paper.

**Limits / exceptions:** Verify the applicable form and lodgement instrument before use.



### R010 — Stored conditional; pre-application

A recognised departmental system error may support paper authorisation where electronic lodgement is prevented, repair is expected outside business hours, and the person would become unlawful before the next business day.

**Limits / exceptions:** All listed circumstances belong together; authorisation is still required.



### R011 — Stored conditional; pre-application

A likely data-matching error may support paper authorisation if the departmental system prevents lodgement, law does not itself prevent electronic lodgement, and the Department cannot rectify the issue.

**Limits / exceptions:** Not a way around a legal application bar.



### R012 — Stored conditional; pre-application

Other illustrative paper-authorisation situations include an expired passport or the 28 days following a Tribunal decision setting aside cancellation or a decision not to revoke cancellation where that cancellation prevents online lodgement.

**Limits / exceptions:** The examples are non-exhaustive and do not guarantee authorisation.



### R013 — Stored practical; pre-application

For an online system problem, preserve the error and seek technical-support assistance through the public support process. Do not assume an enquiry, failed attempt or pending authorisation is a lodged application.

**Limits / exceptions:** Do not use internal officer channels.



### R014 — Stored mandatory; application

An authorised paper application must follow every direction in the authorising email, include that email, and be submitted before 11:59 pm AEST or AEDT as applicable on the day following the date the authorising email was sent.

**Limits / exceptions:** Failure to meet these directions makes the paper application invalid under stored guidance; confirm the applicable process rather than inventing an extension.



### R015 — Stored mandatory; application

Pay the visa application charge applicable to the applicant and application date; do not quote a remembered amount as current.

**Limits / exceptions:** Fee amounts require current verification.



### R016 — Stored policy; application

The stored guidance excludes this subclass from the subsequent temporary application charge.

**Limits / exceptions:** This does not remove the ordinary visa application charge or establish current fee settings.


<a id="application-location-and-family-entry"></a>
## Application location and family entry mechanisms



### R017 — Stored conditional; application

A person claiming family-unit membership of someone who already holds a primary-granted 485 may apply inside or outside Australia, but not in immigration clearance.

**Limits / exceptions:** This later-entrant exception does not automatically extend to family applying with an undecided primary applicant.



### R018 — Checked mandatory; application

Applicants outside the specific Schedule 1 item 1229(3)(f) exceptions must be in Australia, not in immigration clearance, under (3)(g). This includes ordinary new-primary and regional second/subsequent PHEW applications.

**Limits / exceptions:** The already-primary-holder family exception is separate. Preserved Replacement/family wording in (3)(f)(iii) does not reopen new-primary Replacement applications after their closure. A qualifying-visa alternative does not override the location gate.

**Checked legal anchor:** Migration Regulations 1994, C289/F2026C01017, compilation 2 October 2026, checked 16 October 2026; [applicable provisions](https://www.legislation.gov.au/F1996B03551/2026-10-02/2026-10-02/text). Refresh for the actual application/decision dates.


### R019 — Stored conditional; application

Eligible family-unit members may apply at the same time and place as the primary applicant in a combined application; check each person's location separately.

**Limits / exceptions:** A claim of family membership must meet the applicable definition.



### R020 — Stored conditional; application

Family may use a combined application or a separate family application. A separate later-entrant application after primary grant can be made inside or outside Australia, not in immigration clearance, subject to application bars.

**Limits / exceptions:** Do not treat a separate application before primary grant as enjoying the already-holder offshore exception.



### R021 — Stored conditional; pending application

A dependent child may be added after lodgement but before decision through the dependent-child addition mechanism.

**Limits / exceptions:** Do not conflate this mechanism with the newborn mechanism.



### R022 — Stored conditional; pending application

A child born after lodgement but before decision is handled under the newborn-child application mechanism; record the birth and obtain the applicable procedure.

**Limits / exceptions:** Birth after grant has a different timing context not resolved by this statement.



### R023 — Stored mandatory; application

A dependent child needs an individual application unless combined with a parent's application.

**Limits / exceptions:** Applicable dependent-child/newborn addition mechanisms must be assessed separately.


<a id="qualifying-visa-and-age"></a>
## Qualifying visa, age, grant history and application bars



### R024 — Stored conditional; application

One qualifying-visa limb is holding an eligible student visa at lodgement.

**Limits / exceptions:** Eligible student status has exclusions; satisfying this limb does not settle location.



### R025 — Checked conditional; application

Item 1229(4)(a)(ia) retains an offshore former-eligible-student alternative within the qualifying-visa test.

**Limits / exceptions:** This is an immigration-status limb, not standalone permission to lodge offshore. Apply the independent 1229(3)(f)/(g) location gate as well. U01 is resolved for inspected C289 provisions, not for all applicant circumstances or historical versions.

**Checked legal anchor:** Migration Regulations 1994, C289/F2026C01017, compilation 2 October 2026, checked 16 October 2026; [applicable provisions](https://www.legislation.gov.au/F1996B03551/2026-10-02/2026-10-02/text). Refresh for the actual application/decision dates.


### R026 — Stored conditional; application

A Bridging A or Bridging B holder may meet a qualifying-visa limb if that bridging visa was granted on the basis of a valid visa application and an eligible student visa was held at some time in the six months immediately before the application day.

**Limits / exceptions:** Other bridging subclasses or bridging visas without the specified basis are not included in this limb.



### R027 — Stored conditional; application

A substantive-visa holder may meet a qualifying-visa limb if an eligible student visa was held at some time in the six months immediately before the application day.

**Limits / exceptions:** The six-month history test is distinct from the qualification-completion window.



### R028 — Stored conditional; application

A cancellation-review limb concerns a previously held eligible student visa and notification in the 28 days before application that the Tribunal set aside and substituted the decision not to revoke its cancellation. Obtain the decision and notification record, not just the hearing date.

**Limits / exceptions:** Do not generalise this precisely worded limb to every cancellation reversal.



### R029 — Stored conditional; application

A subsequent PHEW applicant may rely on holding PHEW or former Post-Study Work; a Replacement holder needs the applicable earlier Post-Study Work history for the subsequent pathway.

**Limits / exceptions:** Application and regional grant criteria both still apply.



### R030 — Stored mandatory; application

An eligible student visa excludes one granted to a Foreign Affairs or Defence student and one granted on family membership of a student holder. Historical student subclasses as well as Student 500 appear in the stored definition.

**Limits / exceptions:** Do not infer eligible primary-student status from the visa subclass number alone.



### R031 — Stored mandatory; application

Ordinary primary-applicant age is 35 years or younger at lodgement. A Hong Kong or British National (Overseas) passport holder, or someone who completed a research master's or doctoral degree, must instead be under 50 at lodgement.

**Limits / exceptions:** Age exceptions do not waive other requirements; do not extend research-master treatment to coursework or extended master's merely by title.



### R032 — Checked mandatory; application

Under item 1229(4)(aa), include the proposed visa: if granted, the total Subclass 485 count must be no more than four, including no more than one PSW/PHEW visa granted on the basis of meeting 485.232, .233, .234 or .235.

**Limits / exceptions:** The overall count is not written as primary-grants-only. Collect every grant, historical stream, capacity and regional basis. This prospective numerical test is independent of prior-primary exclusions and prior-stream counts; it does not confer an entitlement to four visas.

**Checked legal anchor:** Migration Regulations 1994, C289/F2026C01017, compilation 2 October 2026, checked 16 October 2026; [applicable provisions](https://www.legislation.gov.au/F1996B03551/2026-10-02/2026-10-02/text). Refresh for the actual application/decision dates.


### R033 — Checked mandatory; decision

Clause 485.211(a)–(c) separately excludes prior primary-granted 476, prior primary-granted GW/PVEW, and prior primary-granted PSW/PHEW unless the present application nominates PHEW and meets .232–.235.

**Limits / exceptions:** The primary-grant qualification applies to all three limbs. A secondary-held visa is not automatically a prior-primary bar, but it must not be discarded from the separately worded numerical tests.

**Checked legal anchor:** Migration Regulations 1994, C289/F2026C01017, compilation 2 October 2026, checked 16 October 2026; [applicable provisions](https://www.legislation.gov.au/F1996B03551/2026-10-02/2026-10-02/text). Refresh for the actual application/decision dates.


### R034 — Checked mandatory; decision

Clause 485.211(d) separately addresses previously holding three Post-Study Work visas and two Post-Higher Education Work visas.

**Limits / exceptions:** Keep those historical stream names and thresholds separate, not a combined three-visa total. Unlike (a)–(c), this paragraph does not say primary grants only; unlike the prospective Schedule 1 count it concerns previously held visas. A complete grant ledger is needed.

**Checked legal anchor:** Migration Regulations 1994, C289/F2026C01017, compilation 2 October 2026, checked 16 October 2026; [applicable provisions](https://www.legislation.gov.au/F1996B03551/2026-10-02/2026-10-02/text). Refresh for the actual application/decision dates.


### R035 — Stored conditional; application

Stored guidance says condition 8534 does not require waiver for a primary or secondary 485 application.

**Limits / exceptions:** Other bars and qualifying-visa rules remain independently relevant.



### R036 — Stored conditional; application

Stored guidance treats condition 8535 as preventing a valid 485 application unless an applicable waiver is obtained; compelling and compassionate circumstances that developed may support waiver consideration.

**Limits / exceptions:** A waiver is not automatic. Foreign Affairs/Defence student status may independently defeat the eligible-student requirement even if the condition is waived.



### R037 — Stored practical; pre-application

Review other application bars and no-further-application conditions against the actual visa record and applicable law; this knowledge does not reproduce every general limitation.

**Limits / exceptions:** U09: do not declare the application unrestricted merely because 8534/8535 have been checked.



### R185 — Stored conditional; pre-application

Defence-student classification involves Defence Minister approval of full-time study/training under an approved scholarship/program, the relevant student-visa holding or application intention, and, for holders, not having ceased, completed, withdrawn from or been excluded from the relevant or approved substitute course.

**Limits / exceptions:** Obtain the approval, visa and course-status records; do not infer classification from a scholarship label alone.



### R186 — Stored conditional; pre-application

Foreign Affairs student classification involves Foreign Minister/AusAID Minister approval for full-time study/training under the approved scheme, the relevant Foreign Affairs student-visa holding or application intention, and, for holders, not having ceased the relevant or approved substitute course.

**Limits / exceptions:** Do not treat every government-assisted student identically or assume a change of current study status retrospectively changes the basis of an earlier visa grant.

## Common timing, English and character evidence

Read the [English instrument and timing reference](english-requirement.md) for any English requirement comparison.



### R038 — Stored mandatory; decision

Primary and secondary grant criteria are generally assessed at decision unless a criterion specifies another time. A primary applicant needs common criteria plus their nominated stream's criteria.

**Limits / exceptions:** Meeting later evidence requirements cannot retrospectively create an application-time fact.



### R039 — Stored conditional; application

For the ordinary first-primary pathway, the application must be accompanied by English evidence: an eligible specified passport or an eligible test with the required scores achieved within the specified period.

**Limits / exceptions:** Applicants meeting the regional second/subsequent criteria are exempt from this specific English criterion.



### R040 — Stored conditional; application

Stored passport alternatives list the United Kingdom, United States, Canada, New Zealand and Republic of Ireland. Verify the specified passport type, rather than assuming nationality or residence is enough.

**Limits / exceptions:** U04: applicable instrument controls passport eligibility.



### R041 — Checked conditional; application

Test eligibility follows the application-date instrument and test-date branch in the [English reference](english-requirement.md). LIN 25/089 applies to applications from 7 August 2025, with separate schedules for earlier tests and Hong Kong/BNO passport holders.

**Limits / exceptions:** C1 Advanced is excluded for the new-test branch despite appearing in the instrument’s general test list; it can be relevant under the saved pre-7-August-2025 test branch. Do not treat the stored list of test brands as a current acceptance or score table.

**Source and limits:** see [verification](verification.md) and the linked topical reference. Checked 16 October 2026; limited to the identified instruments/public directions.


### R042 — Checked conditional; application

Where 485.213(1) applies, the visa application must be accompanied by evidence that the primary applicant and every included person aged at least 16 applied for an AFP check within the 12 months immediately before the application day. Apply no later than the day before visa submission, not earlier on the same day.

**Limits / exceptions:** 485.213(2) excludes an applicant who actually meets .232–.235, not everyone merely selecting a subsequent stream. This is not a blanket character exemption and is not automatically imposed on a separately lodged secondary application. The AFP application date is distinct from receipt generation or certificate issue.

**Checked legal anchor:** Migration Regulations 1994, C289/F2026C01017, compilation 2 October 2026, checked 16 October 2026; [applicable provisions](https://www.legislation.gov.au/F1996B03551/2026-10-02/2026-10-02/text). Refresh for the actual application/decision dates.


### R043 — Stored mandatory; application

The stored acceptable police product is an AFP-issued National Police Certificate for Immigration/Citizenship, Australia Name Check Only, disclosure code 33. A State/Territory check cannot substitute; a third-party route is acceptable only if the actual certificate is AFP-issued and the correct type.

**Limits / exceptions:** Check the current application instructions and certificate purpose.

**Checked public-guidance clarification — 16 October 2026:** Certificate product — use the complete-disclosure AFP-issued Immigration/Citizenship — Australia Name Check Only certificate. Public pages reject standard-disclosure and State/Territory or other-organisation-issued certificates. Any intermediary must not be mistaken for the certificate issuer.



### R044 — Stored support; application

Evidence of applying for the AFP check may be the certificate, an AFP payment receipt with national-police-check reference number, or the AFP confirmation page with reference number.

**Limits / exceptions:** A check application made after visa lodgement does not establish the required earlier fact.

**Checked public-guidance clarification — 16 October 2026:** Delayed AFP receipt — the receipt may take up to 24 hours to generate. Save the submission-confirmation page showing the actual AFP application date; do not confuse a later receipt date with the date of applying, or use the delay to justify a same-day AFP application.



### R045 — Stored conditional; pending application

A later request may seek the AFP certificate when only a reference/check ID was supplied, or seek the correct certificate type. If certificate and application reference numbers differ, establish that the relevant check was applied for within the required preceding 12 months.

**Limits / exceptions:** A request is discretionary, not a promised chance to repair omission. Stored guidance calls for refusal where no evidence is provided.

**Checked public-guidance clarification — 16 October 2026:** Regional second/subsequent character — exemption from the particular 12-month application-time AFP evidence criterion is not exemption from character assessment. The public second-stream page reviewed requires an AFP check for grant; do not require the primary first-stream lodgement timing solely by analogy, and do not mark character evidence Not applicable.

## Health insurance through application, decision and stay



### R046 — Stored mandatory; application

An applicant without reciprocal health cover must provide evidence of holding OSHC or OVHC at application; cover must meet the applicable minimum level.

**Limits / exceptions:** Reciprocal-cover status and minimum-cover requirements need verification; do not treat any policy as adequate.



### R047 — Stored mandatory; application to decision

Show adequate health-insurance arrangements in Australia continuously from lodgement through decision, not just a policy purchased for grant day. Secondary applicants have their own insurance criterion.

**Limits / exceptions:** Assess any policy transition or gap rather than assuming a new policy fixes an earlier uninsured period.



### R048 — Stored conditional; application and decision

An offshore secondary applicant must evidence a policy at application and decision, with cover commencing no later than arrival in Australia.

**Limits / exceptions:** This commencement rule is distinct from onshore continuity.



### R049 — Stored mandatory; post-grant

All 485 visas carry condition 8501 requiring adequate health-insurance arrangements while in Australia; prepare cover for the stay and check actual grant conditions.

**Limits / exceptions:** Do not confuse application evidence with ongoing compliance.


<a id="public-interest-and-return"></a>
## Public-interest, return and family-linked grant gates



### R050 — Stored mandatory; decision

The primary applicant must meet public-interest criteria 4001, 4002, 4003, 4003B, 4004, 4005, 4010, 4020 and 4021; values criterion 4019 also applies if aged 18 or over at application.

**Limits / exceptions:** U09: criterion content and exceptions require separate applicable-authority checks; the list is not a complete health/character/integrity procedure.



### R051 — Stored mandatory; decision

Each family-unit member also applying must meet criteria 4001, 4002, 4003, 4003B, 4004, 4005, 4010 and 4020; failure can prevent primary grant. Applying family aged 18 or over at application must also meet values criterion 4019.

**Limits / exceptions:** Do not apply this linked effect indiscriminately to non-applying relatives.



### R052 — Stored conditional; decision

If an under-18 family-unit member is a 485 applicant in a combined application, failure of criteria 4015 and 4016 can prevent the primary applicant's grant.

**Limits / exceptions:** Keep these primary-linked safeguards distinct from the child's own secondary criteria.



### R053 — Stored mandatory; decision

The primary applicant must meet special-return criteria 5001, 5002 and 5010.

**Limits / exceptions:** U09: exclusion-period details are not reproduced here.



### R054 — Stored conditional; decision

Each family-unit member applying who has previously been in Australia must also meet special-return criteria 5001, 5002 and 5010; their failure can prevent primary grant.

**Limits / exceptions:** The prior-Australian-presence trigger matters.



### R055 — Stored conditional; decision

A grant cannot exceed an applicable annual subclass cap or an applicable cap covering classes including this subclass.

**Limits / exceptions:** The stored rule does not establish that a cap is currently in force; verify any relevant instrument.


<a id="pvew-study-and-relatedness"></a>
## PVEW study and occupational relationship



### R056 — Stored mandatory; application

Complete an eligible qualification within the six months immediately before the application day. With multiple qualifications, an eligible qualification completed in that window may satisfy the timing gate, while all relied-on study must meet the other study rules.

**Limits / exceptions:** Use the completion rules and separate study ledgers in study-requirement.md, not graduation-day shorthand.



### R057 — Stored mandatory; application and decision

Every qualification relied on for the PVEW study requirement must be closely related to the nominated skilled occupation. Eligible forms include diploma, advanced diploma, associate diploma, associate degree and a qualifying trade qualification.

**Limits / exceptions:** A successful skills assessment does not by itself establish that every relied-on Australian qualification is closely related.



### R058 — Stored policy; decision

Compare the whole qualification against the whole occupational task set and relevant occupational hierarchy/unit group, including career-level tasks rather than only entry-level tasks.

**Limits / exceptions:** This is a judgment-based assessment, not a course-title matching rule.



### R059 — Stored support; pre-application

Illustrative related combinations are building/construction advanced diploma with Construction Project Manager; automotive electrical Certificate III plus automotive technology Certificate IV with Automotive Electrician; commercial cookery Certificate III plus hospitality-management diploma with Chef.

**Limits / exceptions:** Examples support evidence analysis, not automatic acceptance of another course or occupation.



### R060 — Stored support; pre-application

An IT associate degree is not made closely related to Carpenter and Joiner merely because basic IT skills are useful at work, even where combined with a furniture-making qualification.

**Limits / exceptions:** General usefulness does not establish the necessary occupational link.


<a id="pvew-skills-assessment"></a>
## PVEW skills assessment: application versus decision



### R061 — Stored mandatory; application

Nominate an occupation specified for this visa in the applicable occupation instrument and apply to the relevant assessing authority for that nominated occupation before or by visa lodgement; include evidence of that assessment application.

**Limits / exceptions:** The occupation choice is the applicant's responsibility and cannot be changed after lodgement.



### R062 — Stored support; application

Assessment-application evidence may be an authority acknowledgment, or a yes answer to the form's assessment-application question accompanied by an assessment reference number.

**Limits / exceptions:** A reference for another occupation/authority is not enough without applicability checks.



### R063 — Stored conditional; pending application

If evidence was omitted, a request may seek proof of an assessment application already made at visa lodgement; a later-dated assessment application or no evidence within the prescribed response time leads to refusal under stored guidance.

**Limits / exceptions:** Do not promise a request or advise applying for the assessment only after visa lodgement.



### R064 — Stored mandatory; decision

At decision, obtain a suitable assessment for the nominated occupation from the relevant authority. Applying on time satisfies only the application-stage limb; an unsuitable result fails the separate decision-stage limb.

**Limits / exceptions:** Where assessment is absent or adverse, withdrawal before decision or an authority assessment appeal may be considered; neither assures a migration remedy or decision delay.



### R065 — Stored conditional; decision

If the specified assessing authority changed after visa application, stored guidance accepts a suitable assessment by the authority specified in the instrument in force at application.

**Limits / exceptions:** Check the occupation-specific instrument and applicable date rather than using only today's assessor list.



### R066 — Checked mandatory; decision

Read both 485.224(1) and (1A): a suitable assessment must have been made during the last three years at decision; if it states a validity period, that period must also not have ended.

**Limits / exceptions:** A stated expiry in the future does not by itself displace the three-year limb. Keep assessment date, stated expiry and decision date separate. Refresh applicable legislation for the actual case; do not promise the timing of a future decision.

**Checked legal anchor:** Migration Regulations 1994, C289/F2026C01017, compilation 2 October 2026, checked 16 October 2026; [applicable provisions](https://www.legislation.gov.au/F1996B03551/2026-10-02/2026-10-02/text). Refresh for the actual application/decision dates.


### R067 — Stored mandatory; decision

Professional-society membership or professional registration is not a substitute for an assessment by the relevant skills-assessing authority.

**Limits / exceptions:** An organisation may perform several functions; identify the actual assessment product.



### R068 — Stored conditional; decision

If the assessment relies on an Australian qualification obtained while holding a student visa, that qualification must result from a registered course.

**Limits / exceptions:** This is separate from the registered-course checks for study relied on to meet the visa study requirement.



### R069 — Stored conditional; decision

An assessment based on an overseas qualification may satisfy the assessment limb, provided separate eligible Australian study is completed in a closely related field for the study and occupational-link limbs.

**Limits / exceptions:** The assessment qualification and Australian-study qualifications need not be the same; all separate tests must be met.


<a id="first-phew"></a>
## First PHEW: degree, institution and timing



### R070 — Stored mandatory; application and decision

The same degree or degrees must meet the interrelated eligible-degree-kind, eligible-institution and study requirements; do not use an unrelated degree to satisfy one gate while using another only for a different gate.

**Limits / exceptions:** Applicants who meet an eligible regional subsequent pathway are exempt from the first-degree criterion.



### R071 — Stored conditional; decision

Stored eligible qualification kinds at AQF level 7 or above include bachelor, bachelor honours, eligible graduate diploma, master's coursework/research/extended, doctoral research/professional and higher doctoral degrees; combinations may be completed in any order subject to special graduate-diploma sequencing.

**Limits / exceptions:** A graduate certificate is not independently an eligible degree.



### R072 — Stored mandatory; application and decision

A graduate diploma must follow an Australian-awarded bachelor, master's or doctoral degree, have a related course of study and commence in the same or next academic year as the academic year in which that preceding degree was completed.

**Limits / exceptions:** A standalone or unrelated graduate diploma, or one commencing too late, is not brought within this branch merely by being AQF 8.



### R073 — Stored conditional; decision

Check formal Australian AQF degree status and entry level: bachelor entry is Year 12 or equivalent; master's entry is bachelor or equivalent; doctorate entry is honours bachelor/master's or equivalent; graduate-diploma entry uses the eligible preceding degree.

**Limits / exceptions:** Do not decide the qualification category solely from its marketing title.



### R074 — Stored mandatory; application

The relevant eligible degree must be completed in the six months immediately before the application day; where several degrees are used, identify which eligible degree completed in the window supports timing and retain all study evidence.

**Limits / exceptions:** Completion is academic completion rather than formal conferral; see study-requirement.md.



### R075 — Stored mandatory; decision

Degrees must be awarded/conferred by an institution specified for this purpose. Stored guidance includes CRICOS-registered Australian universities and non-university providers offering bachelor-level or higher courses.

**Limits / exceptions:** Verify the applicable specified institutions; university status alone and CRICOS provider registration alone are not substitutes for all course/award gates.



### R076 — Stored conditional; pre-application

Juris Doctor and certain degrees called Doctor in medicine, physiotherapy, dentistry, optometry or veterinary practice may be extended master's, not AQF 10 doctorates. Use the actual AQF classification for eligibility and duration.

**Limits / exceptions:** The word Doctor does not establish a doctoral age exception or doctoral stay period.



### R077 — Stored support; pre-application

Illustrations include a three-year bachelor, a two-year master's, and an Australian bachelor followed by a related medical-sonography graduate diploma commencing in the next academic year.

**Limits / exceptions:** These examples do not remove the degree-kind, institution or timing tests.



### R078 — Stored conditional; pre-application

A graduate certificate credited into an eligible 92-week registered master's illustrates how an ineligible standalone award may contribute to the eligible resulting degree. Stored guidance counts time spent on the credited units for the calendar ledger and the master's registered period for the academic-load ledger.

**Limits / exceptions:** U02: do not double count credit or assume this example settles every RPL calculation.


<a id="regional-routing"></a>
## Regional routing and postcode categories



### R079 — Stored mandatory; application and decision

Distinguish a second grant while holding a first PHEW/former Post-Study Work visa from a subsequent grant involving Replacement or a qualifying historical Post-Study Work application. Do not apply one route's prior-visa test to the other.

**Limits / exceptions:** Complete grant history and the qualifying historical application basis are required.



### R080 — Stored conditional; pre-application and post-grant condition checks

Designated regional area comprises Category 2 designated cities/major regional centres and Category 3 regional centres/other regional areas. Category 1 major cities are non-regional. Exact instrument postcodes, not a broad city label, control the location check.

A post-grant condition 8610 check is separate from regional eligibility before application. Read R134 for the condition's scope. Establish the holder's own visa grant date and the primary regional grant basis, including for a related secondary holder. Read the grant notice/VEVO and relevant grant information; a bare “8610” code may not identify the permitted branch. While the holder is in Australia, the saved Schedule 8 wording distinguishes:
- **8610(1) and (2):** a primary grant under 485.232 or 485.234, and its related secondary grant, require Category 3 (regional centres or other regional areas).
- **8610(3) and (4):** a primary grant under 485.233 or 485.235, and its related secondary grant, permit designated regional areas (Category 2 or Category 3).

For each holder, classify the proposed location using the definitions and postcode instrument applicable **at the time that holder's visa was granted**, not automatically today's list or the primary holder's grant date. Verify any historical/transitional scope. A regional city label alone does not establish compliance with that holder's tier. Give a direct answer only for the limited location finding supported by the evidence; otherwise identify the missing grant-basis, date or classification fact and ask for only the next material fact. Do not treat this check as complete permission to relocate or a legal assessment of every visa condition. See [source coverage](official-sources.md) and [verification](verification.md) before claiming applicable legal compliance.

**Limits / exceptions:** The stored broad exclusion is Sydney, Melbourne and Brisbane; verify boundaries and date changes. Do not infer a particular holder's condition or permitted tier from the visa subclass alone. The condition does not itself require employment or study; R134 preserves the physical-location and remote-work/study distinctions.



### R081 — Stored support; pre-application

Stored Category 2 examples are Perth, Adelaide, Gold Coast, Sunshine Coast, Canberra, Newcastle/Lake Macquarie, Wollongong/Illawarra, Geelong and Hobart. Category 3 is the remaining regional tier outside these and the non-regional major cities.

**Limits / exceptions:** This is orientation only, not a substitute for postcode classification.



### R082 — Stored policy; decision

Category 3 study and two-year immediate pre-application residence support an ordinary two-year regional grant; Category 2-only or mixed Category 2/3 qualifying study and residence ordinarily support one year.

**Limits / exceptions:** The Tasmania exception can produce two years under the wider designated-regional route. A grant period is policy, not a promise.



### R083 — Stored conditional; pre-application

Category 1 residence in the relevant period, or Category 1 study as the basis of the earlier visa, is not an eligible regional combination under the stored table.

**Limits / exceptions:** Short-holiday policy and historical classification issues require separate assessment; they are not an unrestricted Category 1 residence allowance.


<a id="regional-second-category-3"></a>
## Regional second grant: Category 3 route



### R084 — Stored mandatory; application

For the Category 3 second-grant route, hold the first PHEW or former Post-Study Work visa when applying.

**Limits / exceptions:** A Replacement-held subsequent application belongs to a different route.



### R085 — Stored mandatory; decision

The first visa must have resulted from study in Category 3 at an educational institution or campus located there, and the applicant must have lived in Category 3 while undertaking that study.

**Limits / exceptions:** A regional campus can qualify despite metropolitan institutional headquarters.



### R086 — Stored mandatory; application

Live only in Category 3 for at least the two years immediately before application, with any work/study undertaken during that period physically in Category 3.

**Limits / exceptions:** There is no obligation to work or study; assess holiday, remote-work and distance-study policy separately.



### R087 — Stored mandatory; application

The primary applicant and every family member making a combined application must declare intent to live only in Category 3 and, if intending to work or study, do so only there.

**Limits / exceptions:** The declaration is not a substitute for actual historical residence evidence.



### R088 — Stored mandatory; decision

At decision the primary applicant must still be living and, if applicable, working or studying only in Category 3.

**Limits / exceptions:** Remote work/distance education may qualify through physical presence in Category 3.



### R089 — Stored policy; decision

Meeting this Category 3 route supports a two-year regional visa under stored duration policy.

**Limits / exceptions:** Check the actual grant notice and applicable duration policy; no guaranteed term.


<a id="regional-subsequent-category-3"></a>
## Regional subsequent grant: Category 3 historical route



### R090 — Stored mandatory; application

For the historical Category 3 subsequent route, previously hold a Post-Study Work visa and at current application hold either a Replacement visa or a Post-Study Work visa granted on the specified historical application-charge basis.

**Limits / exceptions:** The exact historical application basis must be verified from the record; ordinary possession of any 485 is insufficient.



### R091 — Stored mandatory; decision

The relevant earlier Post-Study Work grant must have resulted from study at an institution/campus in Category 3, and the applicant must have lived in Category 3 while undertaking that study.

**Limits / exceptions:** Keep campus location distinct from headquarters.



### R092 — Stored mandatory; application

Live only in Category 3 for at least the two years immediately before application; any work/study in that period must be physically in Category 3.

**Limits / exceptions:** No obligation to work/study; remote/distance and short-holiday policy remain relevant.



### R093 — Stored mandatory; application

The applicant and combined family applicants must declare intent to live only in Category 3 and, if working/studying, do so only in Category 3.

**Limits / exceptions:** Do not assume declarations by only the primary cover every combined family applicant.



### R094 — Stored mandatory; decision

At decision the primary applicant must live and, if relevant, work/study only in Category 3.

**Limits / exceptions:** Do not stop checking circumstances at application day.



### R095 — Stored policy; decision

Meeting this historical Category 3 subsequent route supports two years under stored grant-period policy.

**Limits / exceptions:** Prior-grant counts and other common requirements remain independently relevant.

This wider designated-regional route ordinarily supports one year, with a separate stored Tasmania two-year policy exception.

<a id="regional-practical-branches"></a>
## Regional evidence, remote activity, holidays and Tasmania



### R108 — Stored support; application and decision

Establish residence with leases/rental agreements, utilities showing home addresses and other reliable residence evidence. Construct a dated address, work, study and absence timeline for the immediately preceding two years.

**Limits / exceptions:** These examples are not an exhaustive or mandatory fixed document list.



### R109 — Stored conditional; application and decision

Physical work location controls, not where the employer is incorporated or headquartered. A regional local office, branch or remote home can qualify even if the employer is metropolitan or overseas, provided the actual work location is in the route's permitted tier.

**Limits / exceptions:** Routine work can occur in more than one permitted location. Do not infer permission for regular non-permitted work travel from this rule.



### R110 — Stored conditional; application and decision

For study during the relevant 485 period, attendance must be at an institution/campus in the permitted tier, while distance education may qualify where the student is physically residing in that tier.

**Limits / exceptions:** This later-regional study rule does not automatically satisfy the separate first-visa Australian study requirement.



### R111 — Stored policy; application

Short holidays outside the relevant regional tier, including overseas trips, need not interrupt residence. A three-week visit outside the tier is an illustration consistent with ongoing residence.

**Limits / exceptions:** No universal maximum absence or safe-harbour number is supplied; examine residence continuity, purpose, pattern and evidence instead of promising three weeks is always allowed.



### R112 — Stored conditional; decision

The wider designated-regional route may support two years where all relevant requirements are met using only prescribed Tasmanian Category 2 postcodes or Tasmanian Category 2/3 combinations. The relevant first Post-Study Work visa must have resulted from study at a Tasmanian institution/campus in the designated area, with residence in that Tasmanian area during study.

**Limits / exceptions:** Do not award the exception merely for a Tasmanian current address; U03/U08 require checking exact route wording, all qualifying locations and prescribed postcodes.



### R113 — Stored conditional; pre-application

Postcode reclassification or a campus move during student study is a complex eligibility issue. Preserve dated postcode/campus evidence and obtain applicable-version analysis before concluding the regional route is met.

**Limits / exceptions:** Do not apply today's classification retrospectively or guess a transitional concession.

## Secondary applicants and family changes



### R114 — Stored mandatory; decision

At decision, a secondary applicant must be a qualifying family-unit member of a person who holds a 485 granted on primary criteria, whether following a combined application or a separate family application.

**Limits / exceptions:** A combined application does not remove relationship-at-decision requirements; verify the applicable family-unit definition.



### R115 — Stored mandatory; pending application

Notify changes that make an application answer incorrect, including birth, death or relationship-status changes affecting the family unit.

**Limits / exceptions:** A prior declaration is not enough once circumstances change.



### R116 — Stored mandatory; application to decision

A secondary applicant must supply their own application-time insurance evidence and maintain adequate arrangements in Australia through decision.

**Limits / exceptions:** For offshore applicants apply the policy-held-at-application/decision and arrival-commencement rule in the insurance section.



### R117 — Stored mandatory; decision

Secondary applicants must meet public-interest criteria 4001, 4002, 4003, 4003B, 4004, 4005, 4010, 4020 and 4021.

**Limits / exceptions:** Do not substitute the primary-linked family list for the secondary applicant's own criteria.



### R118 — Stored conditional; decision

For secondary applicants aged 18 or over at application, values criterion 4019 also applies. The stored under-18 secondary branch calls for criteria 4017 and 4018.

**Limits / exceptions:** Check applicable age timing for the child criteria and obtain appropriate custody/best-interests evidence; do not conflate these with the combined-child primary-linked criteria.



### R119 — Stored conditional; decision

A secondary applicant previously in Australia must meet special-return criteria 5001, 5002 and 5010.

**Limits / exceptions:** Do not apply the prior-presence-triggered requirement without checking history.



### R120 — Stored practical; pre-application

Do not impose primary study, occupation, skills-assessment, English or age gates indiscriminately on family applicants; use the secondary route and separately triggered common family evidence, such as the ordinary application-time AFP rule for those aged at least 16.

**Limits / exceptions:** Regional-family declarations and post-grant conditions still apply where triggered.


<a id="grant-duration-and-conditions"></a>
## Grant location, duration and visa conditions



### R121 — Stored mandatory; decision

Applicants may be in or outside Australia at grant, but not in immigration clearance. Application-location requirements are a separate earlier gate.

**Limits / exceptions:** Permission to receive a grant offshore is not permission to lodge offshore.



### R122 — Stored policy; decision

The visa permits travel, entry and stay until a date specified at grant. Stored standard periods are policy settings, not an assurance of the term an individual will receive.

**Limits / exceptions:** Check applicable settings and the actual grant end date; U08 remains unresolved.



### R123 — Stored policy; decision

For a first 485, stored policy gives five years to current or previous Hong Kong or British National (Overseas) passport holders at decision, irrespective of nominated stream.

**Limits / exceptions:** This decision-stage current/previous passport policy is distinct from the application-stage age exception for passport holders.



### R124 — Stored support; pre-application

A historical extension for Hong Kong passport holders with visas granted on 9 July 2020 ran to 8 July 2025 and also covered eligible secondary family regardless of their passport.

**Limits / exceptions:** This is historical, not a new five-year extension available by request now.



### R125 — Stored policy; decision

For PVEW primary applications lodged on or after 3 September 2023, the stored ordinary duration is 18 months from grant.

**Limits / exceptions:** Check applicable date and any special passport policy; not a current verified promise.



### R126 — Stored policy; decision

Stored first-PHEW ordinary terms are two years from grant for bachelor, bachelor honours, master's coursework and master's extended; three years for master's research and AQF doctoral degrees.

**Limits / exceptions:** Use actual AQF level rather than the word Doctor; special passport and India branches can differ.



### R127 — Stored policy; decision

An eligible graduate diploma has a default two-year term, potentially longer if the preceding degree would have attracted a longer term.

**Limits / exceptions:** Check the qualifying preceding-degree relationship and applicable duration policy rather than adding the two terms together.



### R128 — Stored conditional; decision

The India agreement branch gives a stored three-year first-PHEW term for an Indian passport holder applying on or after 29 December 2023 with an eligible Australian onshore bachelor degree with first-class honours in a specified STEM/ICT discipline.

**Limits / exceptions:** Honours without first class, a different field or passport status does not establish this branch.



### R129 — Stored support; pre-application

For that first-class-honours India branch, the stored CRICOS broad fields are 01 natural/physical sciences, 02 information technology, 03 engineering/related technologies and 05 agriculture/environment/related studies.

**Limits / exceptions:** Verify the actual course code and qualification classification; do not infer eligibility from an informal STEM label.



### R130 — Stored policy; decision

Stored India agreement settings retain three years for an eligible master's and four years for an eligible doctoral degree for Indian nationals.

**Limits / exceptions:** Check applicable qualification, nationality/passport and date conditions; do not transfer this term to all nationalities.



### R131 — Stored policy; decision

Eligible secondary family visas ordinarily end on the same date as the primary holder's visa, not a fresh full primary duration starting from the family's later grant.

**Limits / exceptions:** Check the actual primary grant and secondary notice.



### R132 — Stored mandatory; decision and post-grant

All 485 grants carry insurance condition 8501. For a person offshore at grant, a first-entry-by date must be specified; it is distinct from the visa end date.

**Limits / exceptions:** Read the notice for both dates and do not assume they are identical.



### R133 — Stored conditional; post-grant

For an applicant offshore at grant, condition 8515 may be imposed, prohibiting marriage or entry into a de facto relationship before entry.

**Limits / exceptions:** This condition is discretionary, unlike mandatory insurance; check the actual notice.



### R134 — Stored mandatory; post-grant

Condition 8610 must attach to regional second/subsequent PHEW primary grants and their related secondary family grants. Live only in the areas matching the primary regional criteria; if working/studying, do so only in permitted areas.

**Limits / exceptions:** No obligation to work/study. Remote work for an outside employer and distance education from an outside provider can be compatible when physically in the permitted area; confirm exact grant conditions.

## Source issues and continuing gates

Read these with [verification](verification.md). Resolved source defects do not establish any applicant’s outcome; unresolved matters block only dependent findings.

**U01 — Resolved for inspected C289 provisions; case/date refresh required.** Application location and qualifying-visa alternatives are independent. Item 1229(3)(f)/(g) controls location; (4)(a)(ia) does not authorise ordinary-primary offshore lodgement. Grant location .411 is separate.

**U02 — Unresolved material interpretation.** Stored RPL guidance preserves gross academic-duration treatment; official study guidance excludes certain credited study and describes making up registered weeks. Keep gross/net credit and calendar/location ledgers, show material alternatives and withhold only the affected compliance conclusion. Obtain provider credit decisions and applicable authoritative clarification.

**U03 — Citation/geographic defects resolved; policy duration not guaranteed.** Use .233(4)/.235(4) for wider-route declarations and .233(3)/.235(3) at decision. Reg 1.15M(1)/(2) defines different categories. Postcode history and policy-based Tasmania/duration questions remain date-specific; .513 does not legislate a fixed duration.

**U04 — Instrument architecture checked; exact product/score/date refresh needed.** Use LIN25/089 ss6–11 and relevant schedule for applications from 7 August 2025, preserving earlier-test routes. One-year requirement is not general three-year English validity. Check each actual score/product and retake date; do not extrapolate primary criteria to secondaries.

**U05 — Unresolved official format conflict.** The dedicated 485 study page requests certified completion/transcript copies; stream upload instructions say no certification needed. Both were read on 16 October 2026. Preserve both; follow an actual request, seek clarification or consider certified copies without calling them universally mandatory.

**U06 — Partially verified; material historical/percentage questions unresolved.** Provider one-third delivery rules differ from 485 counting. Public PHEW guidance preserves online study during 1 February 2020–25 November 2023; the precise continuing legal/policy mechanism, boundary and 25% denominator need verification where outcome-dependent. Do not discard the positive concession or treat all offshore study as eligible.

**U07 — Count structure checked; individual ledger needed.** Keep the prospective four-total/one-regional-grant check, the prior-primary exclusions and the separate PSW/PHEW prior-count limbs distinct. Do not disregard secondary history from numerical tests or convert it automatically into a prior-primary exclusion.

**U08 — Ongoing refresh gate.** Fees, duration policy, postcodes, occupation/assessor instruments and provider/course records need applicable-date refresh. Inspected C289 law covers the stated current provisions as retrieved on 16 October 2026, not every instrument or a future date.

**U09 — Partial procedural support; general coverage not exhaustive.** General Act notification/request authorities remain dated unless freshly checked. Targeted C289 reg2.08B confirms Class VC coverage and latest-of request/payments timing; newborn and dependent-child mechanisms are separate. No exhaustive bars, family-definition, health, character, return or immigration-status audit is claimed.

**U10 — Publication/professional review outstanding.** Local practice candidate only. Rights/confidentiality, distribution permissions, attribution identity and applicable professional/regulatory obligations need review before publication or regulated use. A content licence or disclaimer does not resolve those questions.
