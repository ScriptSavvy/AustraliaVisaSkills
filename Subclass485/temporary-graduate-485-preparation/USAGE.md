## Start with your actual task
Choose the language you prefer. You can ask one question without completing a full interview. The agent asks one necessary question at a time and shows question progress; answers may change the number of follow-ups. “Not sure” is allowed and remains unverified.

Copy one of these prompts:
- **Narrow question:** “Use temporary-graduate-485-preparation. My inspected completion letter says academic completion on 12 June, while my degree certificate says conferral on 20 August. Which date should my preparation timeline use?”
- **Checklist:** “Use temporary-graduate-485-preparation to prepare a provisional first PHEW document checklist. Ask one question at a time. Do not assess eligibility or submit anything.”
- **Document review:** “Use temporary-graduate-485-preparation to review only these redacted documents for dates and consistency. Tell me what you actually read and any pages you cannot read.”
- **Regional:** “Use temporary-graduate-485-preparation to organise my regional residence and study evidence for a second visa. Keep postcode-date checks and any unknowns visible.”
- **Official request:** “Use temporary-graduate-485-preparation to turn this redacted Department request into an evidence/action list. Do not send it or assume an extension.”

## Deadline questions
This draft is not a deadline calculator. It must not tell you that a response, submission or immigration-status deadline has passed or is still ahead, or invent how much time remains. It can record the deadline as written and organise the requested evidence, while leaving temporal status **Needs verification**. Obtain timely official or appropriately qualified assistance where the deadline affects what you should do. Asking for an extension does not establish that it was granted.

## Share less information first
Use a summary or redacted relevant pages when enough. Keep names, passport numbers, addresses, financial account details and application reference numbers out unless necessary. Do not upload passwords or payment credentials. Check your host's privacy/retention settings: a hosted AI may process files remotely. The skill requires no external OCR service; additional uploads need separate permission.

## Understand the result
Ready means only **Ready for the stated preparation check**, not visa eligibility, valid lodgement or approval. Missing can mean not supplied for this review, rather than you do not own it. Needs correction identifies an observed defect. Needs verification identifies an unknown fact, unreadable evidence or rule/source gap. Not applicable requires a known false trigger or established exception.

A broad report separates presence, readability, completeness, consistency, currency and verified-rule compliance. A filename cannot prove contents. Unreadable pages may need a clearer copy; they do not prove information is absent. Unknown credits, test versions, location or family circumstances do not become exemptions.

If browsing is unavailable, the agent can still compare readable documents and organise evidence. It must not call changing requirements verified. If an official request or status deadline is close, obtain timely official/qualified help; asking for an extension does not grant one.

Preparation action lists may contain several items. During an interactive follow-up, the agent should request just the next material fact/document and continue its question numbering. It should not restart the interview or repeat the full notices on every short reply.

No submission, payment, withdrawal or third-party contact is included. For consequential interpretation, take the specific question and relevant redacted records to an appropriately qualified professional.
