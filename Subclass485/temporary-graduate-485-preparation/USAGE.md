## Deadline restriction
This draft is not a deadline calculator. It must not tell you that a response, submission or immigration-status deadline has passed or is still ahead, or invent how much time remains. It can record the deadline as written and organise the requested evidence, while leaving temporal status **Needs verification**. Obtain timely official or appropriately qualified assistance where the deadline affects what you should do. Asking for an extension does not establish that it was granted.

## Other specific limits
Unknown credits, test versions, location or family circumstances do not become exemptions. For a family review, the skill first distinguishes the application date and mechanism: applications from 13 November 2026 have new inclusion and combined-application rules. See [family reform](references/family-reform.md) and [source-verification limits](references/verification.md), including the unresolved dependent-child interaction, credit/RPL, document-certification and historical online-study issues. The 17 November 2026 supplement has only its stated verification scope; guide wording alone does not establish current law.
