## Start

## Tailored prompts
- **Checklist:** “Use partner-801-preparation. I hold an 820 visa and have not received 801. Make a preparation checklist using what I have told you. Leave unknown requirements visible.”
- **Review:** “Use partner-801-preparation. Review these redacted relationship documents for readability, gaps and consistency. Do not infer contents from filenames.”
- **Department request:** “Use partner-801-preparation. I received a Department request. Help organise the requested evidence using this redacted notice. Do not submit or contact anyone.”
- **Changed relationship:** “Use partner-801-preparation. My relationship has changed. Help me organise relevant records without assuming I must stay with or contact my sponsor.”

## Partner-specific scope and limits
The agent should not give blanket permission to upload at 22 months, promise approval, require every example document or order repeat medicals automatically. Neither a focused answer nor a broad report gives an overall readiness score or approval probability.

The creation-time legislation check did not establish complete coverage from 2 October 2026 onward. Special relationship/child/review questions may require a registered migration agent or Australian legal practitioner with the actual records. See [verification limits](references/verification.md). Agent-generated explanatory translations are not accepted formal translations by default.
