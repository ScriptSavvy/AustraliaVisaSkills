# Terms

- **820:** temporary onshore Partner visa; usual first stage of the combined 820/801 process.
- **801:** permanent onshore Partner visa pathway; “onshore” does not alone settle location at grant.
- **445:** Dependent Child visa; child route is not a primary partner route.
- **Primary/secondary applicant:** applicant assessed against primary partner criteria versus relevant family applicant criteria. A relative disclosed in a form is not necessarily an applicant.
- **Sponsoring partner:** ordinarily the original partner specified for 820; ministerial grant cases have a grant-time definition. Not every authorised recipient is a sponsor.
- **De facto partner/spouse:** legally defined relationship terms, not merely a dating label or a certificate-based conclusion.
- **PIC:** public interest criterion, covering specified matters such as character, health, integrity or child interests. Applicable criteria differ by role, age, grant basis and stage.
- **MoFU:** member of the family unit, a defined and date-sensitive term, not every relative.
- **VAC:** visa application charge; not every upload or Stage 2 form creates another charge.
- **ImmiAccount:** official online application/records service. Do not share its password with the agent or sponsor.
- **Authorised recipient:** person appointed to receive correspondence; receipt can have legal consequences. Distinct from professional authority to give immigration assistance.
- **Grant, notification, effect:** different events; a progress message or prior assessment is not a visa grant. Review/lawful-stay consequences need exact records/current law.
- **Remittal:** tribunal sends a decision back for reconsideration under its actual orders; not a guaranteed visa grant. Historical AAT wording must not be treated as current review law.
- **Readiness:** only the named preparation checks, never legal eligibility, validity, authenticity or approval probability.
