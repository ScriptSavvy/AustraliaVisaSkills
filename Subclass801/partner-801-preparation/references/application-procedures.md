# Application and correspondence preparation

## Stages and records
Separate original lodgement validity (Schedule 1), permanent-stage information upload, later requests, decision criteria, grant location and visa effect. “No primary application-time Schedule 2 criteria” does not mean no validity requirements. Routine primary criteria are at decision, while secondary application-stage criteria exist. Onshore original application location is not a requirement to remain in Australia for all processing: the read grant clause allows Australia outside immigration clearance or outside Australia. This is not permission to travel on a bridging visa.

Ordinary combined application usually needs no new legal application/form/charge at permanent stage; ministerial/no-prior-application and child routes need separate checks. Do not use general email/fax policy as authority to lodge outside the prescribed channel. Written Department alternative directions require their actual date, form and place/manner conditions. Do not conclude a deficient application is valid, cured or backdated from payment or an acknowledgement.

Initial validity complications include section 48 and historical recent partner-refusal restrictions. Stored section-48 rules include signed sponsorship and two specified third-party statutory declarations declared within six weeks, with specific status requirements and dependent-child rules. These are not universal 801 stage-two witness instructions. Refusal/cancellation/bridging travel facts require current legal review; inventory only rather than advise a new application route. The general ability to lodge without sponsorship form is qualified by that initial section-48 rule.

## Requests and communication
Read the full actual request before preparing a response. Distinguish an ordinary evidence invitation, adverse-information response, interview, third-party check and unsolicited update. Inventory requested items, notice date, stated deadline, actual/deemed receipt uncertainty, authorised recipient, channel and evidence of response. Do not assume applicant's reading date controls receipt, invent a deadline, or set historic seven/28/70-day periods. Explain any urgent deadline risk immediately.

An extension **request** is not an extension **grant**. Preserve request and written outcome; if pending, plan around the existing stated deadline and seek appropriate urgent clarification. Evidence of steps to obtain a third-party certificate may be useful but does not necessarily satisfy the requested final evidence. A late response does not prove refusal has occurred, and a non-response is not withdrawal. Do not promise another invitation or a pause in decision-making. Stored wording about delaying all decisions was too broad: current-law refusal timing must be read in its exact provision.

Use the actual notified processing channel and required application identifiers in an authorised draft; keep identifiers out of public examples. Confirm substantive telephone information truthfully in writing through the appropriate channel; keep receipts and copies. A draft/sent email is not proof it reached the correct record or will be accepted. A portal upload is not grant. The AI does not communicate or submit under this skill.

## Changes and safe contacts
Organise relevant changes in passport/name, residential and correspondence addresses, contact details, relationship and children. Distinguish actual residence from correspondence and an authorised recipient from a sponsor/professional representative. Use current official change procedures; do not assume all changes use one form. A prior application statement may need updating. For safety concerns, avoid sending information to an unsafe sponsor address and identify appropriate private official contact/qualified support. No promise that notification stops cancellation/refusal.

## Ending or reconsidering an application
Withdrawal is a consequential authorised written action, not deletion from a file, failure to reply or a new application. Different family applicants' records and consent/guardian authority require separate consideration. Death, withdrawal, refusal and grant have distinct consequences; do not calculate refunds, review access, other-visa cessation or lawful stay from stored shorthand. Prepare the precise facts and documents for qualified review instead.
