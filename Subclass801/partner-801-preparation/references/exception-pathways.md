# Conditional pathways and referral triggers

Load when relevant or unclear. These are preparation branches, not determinations that criteria are met. Check applicable legal version and [verification limits](verification.md). Preserve independent evidence work while resolving legal questions.

## Long-term relationship
Stored definition: at original application, relationship at least three years, or at least two years with a dependent child of both partners other than a step-child. Do not use the date of today's review, mere dating without the relevant partner relationship, or any child of one partner as proof. Obtain chronology and relationship evidence at application; check dependent-child status and definition only if relevant. Subclause 801.221(6A) removes specified two-year requirements, not all grant requirements. Potential early consideration is not a promised processing time or simultaneous grant.

## Sponsor death
First distinguish death **before** 820 grant (previous exceptional 820 grant basis) from death **after** it. For the latter, prepare death certificate, previous genuine partner relationship and evidence it would have continued. Stored policy requests original/certified death evidence and close business, cultural or personal ties in Australia. The read C288 text of 801.221(5) does **not** contain that close-ties limb. Do not impose close ties as verified current law; keep it optional background if relevant, with the applicable-law question unresolved beyond dated coverage. Ordinary 801 page scanning directions do not settle a specific original/certified request. A death exception can remove the routine wait where applicable but is not automatic approval.

## Separation and family violence
Do not state that leaving the relationship necessarily removes a possible permanent pathway. Ask only safe, necessary questions; do not require staying with or contacting the sponsor. If immediate safety risk is reported, prioritise locally appropriate emergency/safety help and qualified assistance. Do not collect a trauma narrative by default.

Stored post-820 family-violence route covers the applicant and/or a dependent child of applicant, sponsor or both, where the sponsoring partner committed family violence. It requires relevant violence or part of it while the married/de facto relationship existed; the violence need not have caused the relationship's end. Preserve truthful former-relationship evidence and relevant existing judicial/non-judicial records, but do not invent a mandatory report, demand a police conviction or decide that an account satisfies regulation 1.23. Full applicable Division 1.5 evidentiary rules and accepted non-judicial pathways need live source review and appropriate professional assessment.

## Separation and child rights
Shared children alone do not establish the exception. Stored route concerns the applicant's custody/joint custody/access or relevant residence/contact order, together with the sponsoring partner's specified court-granted joint custody/access, order or formal maintenance obligation for that child. Identify actual parenting/court/maintenance documents and relevant rights; a parenting plan may be useful evidence but not necessarily identical to a court order. The prior policy's inference about parents' custody does not replace review of actual rights, sole-custody circumstances and current law. Refer the specific rights/provision question without fabricating an order or advising litigation strategy.

## Prior exceptional 820 grant and ministerial intervention
Read actual grant basis. Earlier 820 grant following sponsor death or family violence/child rights has distinct 801 routes (including legacy 801.221(3)/(4)). C288 also includes 801.221(6AA) linked to 820.211(7)/(8) and (6AB) linked to 820.211(9), absent from the stored overview. Do not exclude applicants whose grant fits those provisions or guess their full triggers from their numbers. Obtain grant records and verify the corresponding applicable 820 provision, amendments and transitions before a route conclusion.

Ministerial cases use the relevant grant-time sponsoring-partner definition, without ordinary formal sponsorship requirement. Where no initial 820 application exists, a prescribed 801 application and relevant charge may be needed. The stored ministerial waiting clock starts at the Minister's 820 decision, not ordinary combined lodgement. Verify current powers, form/instrument, fees, dates and exceptions. Do not reuse old section references as confirmed current law.

## Secondary applicants, added children and newborns
1. Identify who is an actual visa applicant versus a disclosed relative; establish primary/secondary role. Secondary application-stage family/dependency criteria differ from decision criteria. Do not demand continued financial dependence at 801 decision solely because the child is now older: stored instructions/read C288 do not reimpose dependency at decision. Changes still need truthful disclosure; fraud/status concerns remain separate.
2. A child already included with 820 grant is distinct from a later dependent child. Stored post-lodgement additions cover certain dependent children, not a new spouse or any family member. No addition after permanent grant. Check whether primary application remains pending, actual child visa, dates/location and current regulation.
3. After parent's 820 grant, ordinary public guidance says a dependent child first applies for 445. For a 445 holder, stored alternatives include a parent's written addition request under regulation 2.08A **or** Form 1002; an uploaded/emailed Form 1002 may function as a written request. The source says no 801 charge for the 445-holder route and onshore whereabouts at relevant application. Verify current form/channel, exact route and charge provision; do not require a new full paid partner application or erase the written-request alternative.
4. A newborn born while applications are pending is a separate mechanism under regulation 2.08, not an ordinary 445 demand. Gather its material facts one at a time: parentage is a separate question from a parent's citizenship/visa status at birth. Use already supplied facts rather than asking again. Record birth country/date, parent citizenship/visa at birth, pending application stages and birth certificate. A child born in Australia with a citizen/permanent-resident parent may already be a citizen; verify that status. Potential overseas citizenship by descent is not the same as citizenship automatically acquired at birth. Stored section 78 visa effect for a non-citizen child born in Australia needs actual parent visa and applicable law verification. Notification, deemed application and visa grant are separate; never promise automatic 801 grant.
5. Minor custody/best-interests checks apply with their own primary-linked and secondary PIC provisions; keep consent/parental responsibility and changed circumstances visible. Prior 820 custody checks may support the review, but doubt/new adverse facts can require fresh evidence. Sponsor police-check policy for sponsorship involving an under-18 applicant may already have been met at 820, absent relevant adverse information; verify rather than universally demand repetition.
6. Secondary visa-status alternatives include ordinary 820/445 holding, specified refusal-cessation/remittal circumstances and ministerial grant cases. Primary 801 grant must precede secondary grant under the relevant criterion; do not promise a child grant independent of the primary.

## Narrow public-interest concessions
Stored/read dated legislation includes primary concession for certain 820.211(3)/(4)/(5) grant bases, with (3)/(4) described as repealed in the instruction. The surviving (5) concerns specified former 300 circumstances, not all former 300 holders. A dependent-child concession also requires the relevant grant basis and same-class entry circumstances; check exact 801.224(3)/801.323(2). Values requirements where triggered and integrity/passport criteria are not blanket waived. Prepare grant/entry evidence and refer unresolved application of a concession; do not require inapplicable repeat checks just because the source is hard to access.

## Refusal, cancellation and remittal
Read redacted decision, notification, grant and review/remittal documents. Stored AAT terminology is historical; read legislation uses ART. A specified refusal-cessation/remittal route can address the missing current 820 holding criterion, but not every refusal or cancellation. Separate 820-remittal policy for prompt 801 consideration absent significant new relationship concerns, subject to other criteria, from 801-remittal visa-status provisions.

Stored cancellation instructions both describe waiting for review outcomes in specified exceptional cases and procedural finalisation after relationship-breakdown cancellation. Do not invent a universal processing sequence from them. Refusal notification, review rights/time/location and bridging visa effects require current law and the exact notice, not historic fixed deadlines or a rename of AAT. Offer an urgent evidence inventory and qualified review question. Do not calculate lawful stay or assure overseas review access/deferred refusal. Stored possible short deferral where contact fails abroad is officer discretion, not an applicant entitlement.
