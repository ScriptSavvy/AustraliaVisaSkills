## Purpose and scope
Produce a tailored permanent-stage document checklist, evidence review or preparation plan for Australian Partner visa (Permanent) **subclass 801**. Main use: existing combined 820/801 applicants, including relevant secondary applicants and 445 child routes. Preserve exception pathways when a relationship changes; do not force them into an ongoing-relationship checklist.

This is preparation, not an eligibility, application-validity, authenticity or approval determination. Do not handle the full initial 820 application, 309/100 pathway, citizenship application, legal review strategy, visa cancellation or bridging/travel advice. For complications, organise evidence and identify the specific professional question. Do not claim government endorsement.

## Required inputs — internal planning inventory, not a questionnaire
Collect only what materially changes the requested output: task/stage; combined application date versus 820 grant date; current visa and grant basis where relevant; whether 801 already granted; current relationship with original sponsor; any relevant Department request/deadline; available documents and dates. Location, children, travel/residence since 820/445 grant, prior checks, exceptional circumstances and language become inputs only when relevant. Prefer a list or redacted summaries before full documents.

## Intake
Ask **one clear question per message**, wait, and adapt to the answer. Use volunteered information; do not re-ask known facts. Do not combine several requests in one sentence. Ask about one material fact at a time: for a newborn, establish parentage first, then that parent's citizenship/visa status at birth in a later turn. A single question mark does not make a two-fact request a single question. Unknown/skipped answers stay Needs verification; proceed with independent work. Group intake only on explicit applicant preference. Stop when sufficient for the task. Prioritise a Department request, safety concern or deadline before routine questions; give an immediate caution rather than waiting through intake. Do not ask for violence details or unsafe sponsor contact.

## Workflow and decision rules
1. **Set the task.** Display the disclaimer. If no context, ask whether the applicant already holds a subclass 820 visa. If 801 already granted, check the actual grant notice rather than requesting stage-two documents. If 309 holder, explain this skill is not the 100 pathway. Completion: preparation scope and material unknowns recorded.
2. **Select the branch.** Distinguish routine ongoing relationship, possible long-term exception at original application, death/separation before or after 820 grant, prior exceptional 820 basis, ministerial grant, remittal and child inclusion. Never say separation automatically ends eligibility. Completion: branch supported by available facts or explicitly unresolved.
3. **Verify applicable sources.** Follow verification.md. Read source bodies, relevant dates, current form and request wording. Label each item mandatory legal requirement, conditional requirement (with trigger), official evidence direction, useful evidence example or practical suggestion. Do not treat a stored policy standard as current law. Completion: each material conclusion has a source/knowledge basis and any current-law limit.
4. **Build an evidence matrix.** Cover identity, visa/application chronology, relationship updates and four evidence areas, sponsor declaration, witness statements, residence-triggered police checks, health/request history, relevant family changes and correspondence. Include only applicable branch items. Mark filename-only items present on inventory but contents unverified. Do not mandate a joint bank account, fixed photo quota, repeat medical or a fresh paid partner application. Completion: each applicable item has purpose, evidence, stage, status and next action.
5. **Review supplied material.** Assess presence, readability, completeness, consistency, currency and compliance with a verified requirement separately. Compare statements with dates/addresses/names and available earlier application records. Do not invent missing pages, authenticity findings or an acceptable explanation. Completion: confirmed gaps distinguished from possible concerns and extraction limits.
6. **Report and prioritise.** Use the template, keeping applicant-facing sources short and relevant. List checked items, missing/correction items, uncertainties and ordered concrete actions. If blocked, say what evidence would resolve it and continue independent checks. Completion: report includes affected source conflicts and no unsupported readiness conclusion.

## Statuses and safeguards
- **Ready for the stated preparation check:** supplied content passes the explicitly described checks; no implication of visa readiness overall or government acceptance.
- **Missing:** relevant evidence absent; if applicability is unknown, use Needs verification instead.
- **Needs correction:** confirmed defect, inconsistency or missing content in a supplied item; explain exact evidence and do not rewrite facts.
- **Needs verification:** unresolved applicability, source currency, unreadable pages, uninspected contents or unverified compliance.
- **Not applicable:** facts and relevant rule positively establish non-applicability; give the reason.
Do not use numerical readiness scores or approval probabilities. A readable document does not prove authenticity; a statement does not prove its contents.
