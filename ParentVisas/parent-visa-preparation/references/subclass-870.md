# Subclass 870 — sponsor approval and visa application

Subclass 870 is temporary; it does not itself lead to permanent residence. Sponsor approval precedes each named parent's visa application. The same approval may cover up to two parents, but each files an individual visa application. Check the sponsor linked to the parent at lodgement and again at decision; a later replacement can differ from the original. A pending sponsorship, lodged visa, grant, visa expiry and cessation of sponsor approval are different events. Use [verification](verification.md) and [family evidence](family-evidence.md).

## Stage 1 — sponsor preparation

1. **Application and household.** Use the current prescribed online sponsorship (typically 1501) and separate fee. Individual sponsor, at least 18; application/approval location is distinct from usual residence. Check whether either partner has a pending sponsorship, and separately whether either already has an approval and which people remain covered. Distinguish a lodgement bar from an approval bar. Record earlier sponsorships by either current partner and any duty failures. Do not replace a lodged sponsorship applicant to cure an income shortfall; a different person needs their own application. Verify any application closure instrument and not merely program visa caps.
2. **Named people and relationships.** Verify legal child-to-parent or ongoing step-parent chain (own parent or qualifying partner's parent). If sponsoring a partner's parent, separately check that partner's eligible citizenship/residence status. Do not rule out having a temporary-visa partner when sponsoring one's own parent solely on that basis. Divorce, separation or death can defeat an ordinary step relationship; verify specific provisions, not evidence of non-remarriage alone. A narrow surviving-partner route involves the deceased partner sponsoring the person immediately before death, the person already holding 870, and the survivor making the qualifying new sponsorship application within 90 days; it can include the parent or that parent's partner as defined. Other requirements still apply. Do not assume a variation is enough for this particular relationship route.
3. **Household capacity.** Check reg 2.60U and definitions for prior named parents whose 870 ended, who have not since departed and who lack permanent status. With one such parent, generally one name or two including that parent; with two, only those two under the relevant rule; with three or more check the separate ineligibility rule. Verify all predicates and histories for both partners. Where relevant, document a change such as a later departure or permanent grant, and consider whether a variation can add a slot. Do not label application invalid on an approval limit alone.
4. **Identity, status, residence and consent.** Prepare eligible Australian citizen, permanent resident or protected NZ Special Category Visa status proof. For PR/eligible NZ branches record usual Australian residence over the four years before making the sponsorship application and screen unlawful or disqualifying bridging periods (not all bridging visas are treated the same). Check travel duration and purpose plus Australian home/work/study ties; qualifying earlier temporary status is not automatically excluded. Do not impose the same residence limb on Australian citizens without reading 2.60V. Verify required disclosure consent to named parents; missing evidence may be followed up or assessed on record, not automatically a remedied defect.
5. **Taxable income.** Check [LIN 19/146](https://www.legislation.gov.au/F2019L00542) and reg 2.60W for the amount and most recent completed income year before sponsoring. Use ATO Notices of Assessment and taxable income, not tax returns alone, net pay, assets or averaged years. Modes: sponsor alone at full threshold; or sponsor at least half and total reaches threshold using qualifying partner, qualifying other child of named parent, or both. Verify contributor connection/status, consent and matching year/NoA per person; verify linkage for each named parent. If latest Notice of Assessment is not yet available, earlier notices may explain history but do not substitute an earlier year for the prescribed year without an applicable basis; distinguish evidence availability from statutory income year. No shortfall waiver or actor substitution. Application charges and the parent's own funds are different questions.
6. **Conduct, debts and discretion.** Distinguish residence-triggered sponsor police checks under 2.60X (dated text: **more than** 12 cumulative months in Australia or each relevant foreign country since later of 10 years before application and age 16), adverse information concerning sponsor/associates, and compliance with earlier sponsorships for each relevant role. Consider whether the specific legal criterion permits reasonable disregard; do not pre-judge seriousness, substantiation, response or official exercise of discretion. Keep sponsor Commonwealth liability distinct from defined public health debts of sponsor, relevant partner, proposed and prior parents. For debt record expense type/date, notifying authority, reporting status and evidence of payment in full or appropriate arrangements, rather than labelling all unpaid medical costs a statutory debt. Check applicable reg 1.15K for aged-care period and provider/report predicates. For ordinary Commonwealth debt check due status and any arrangement.

## Handoff and Stage 2 — individual applicant

Keep a dated timeline for each parent: sponsor approval or variation, which parent is named, valid period, possible permission to apply in Australia (PTA) and date issued, actual visa lodgement location and date. Record exact dates and identifiers locally; redact them in reports. Use the current prescribed individual online form (typically 1502), first visa application charge, minimum age 18 and named approved sponsorship. Verify no current 870 and prior cumulative effect under item 1239; no usual family combining or secondary grant. Don't confuse a pending regular Parent application with permission to make another one while applying for, holding or after ceasing an 870; see [overlap verification](verification.md#known-material-verification-gates). Existing Parent application does not automatically preclude 870 but explain temporary intentions distinctly.

- **Offshore ordinarily.** Check LIN 19/185 s 6: six months measured from the applicable approval or variation adding that applicant. For **onshore** the person must not be in immigration clearance and must already have written PTA. Sponsor may request for a named parent with consent, during sponsorship or before visa lodgement while sponsorship remains effective. Prepare evidence of exceptional impediment to departing, e.g. well-supported inability to travel or serious disruption; general cost, convenience, planned events, frailty without a specific evidenced impediment or ordinary family difficulties do not establish exception. Reason not document title controls. A request or missing upload is not evidence of approval/denial. The instrument counts 60 days from permission, not automatically from sponsorship approval; public guidance also uses approval wording. Inspect both notices and effective sponsor period, obtain case confirmation for conflicts before the earliest apparent deadline. An expired window is not permission; check further permission request/offshore alternatives if still possible. Do not infer bridging grant or travel authority. See [verification](verification.md).
- **Further 870: three separate rules.** No existing 870 and total earlier periods of *visa effect*, not days in Australia, **less than 10 years** at lodgement under item 1239(3)(f) in the inspected dated text. For the historic 18-month COVID mechanism (in effect and offshore on 1 July 2021), preserve original specified and actual cessation dates. Item 1239(3)(f) has no express carve-out, while cl 870.511(2)(b) excludes a visa meeting its specified cessation predicate from the grant-duration calculation. Do not automatically subtract 18 months for both gates or apply the grant carve-out to lodgement. For any affected history, leave the calculation Needs verification pending the controlling interpretation and evidence. Locate all previous grants, actual cessations and departures. For offshore lodgement by prior holder, normally at least **90 consecutive days outside Australia at decision**, not a universal 90-day bar to applying. If in Australia at previous cessation, count from first departure after it; if offshore then, from last departure while it was in effect. Test exceptional circumstances without treating examples as outcomes. At grant, generally at most five years for one visa and new total not exceeding ten. Apply dated provisions and actual notices before any conclusion; don't confuse either limit with the 35/90-day sponsor-replacement events.
- **Financial, health and intention evidence at decision.** Parent's access to funds for planned duration is distinct from sponsor taxable income and ongoing support. Relevant evidence may include pension/available funds or documented accessible support. Check adequate health insurance across intended stay, and after grant under actual condition. Review coverage dates, covered people, Australian medical costs and any verified reciprocal Medicare status; do not assume a product or reciprocal arrangement suffices. Keep authentic compliance history for last substantive and following bridging visas plus return plans, actual ties, travel and immigration circumstances; no guaranteed genuine-temporary assessment. Check parent public health debt separately with payment/arrangement alternative. Verify applicable PIC 4005 if offshore at lodgement / 4007 if onshore, and other applicable character, debts, identity, values and return criteria (870.228–229). Do not infer a health waiver by onshore location alone.
- **Grant and second charge.** Grant can be in or outside Australia, not in immigration clearance, subject to actual rule. Keep both visa charge stages, grant-duration cost tier and actual payment request separate. Verify statutory receipt and relevant payment period for onshore/offshore notices (possible 28/70 days), not from email-opening date. Visa generally takes effect on grant, permits multiple entry until cessation and carries conditions; use actual grant notice and VEVO including 8103 (work restriction with written permission branch), 8303, 8501, 8531, 8564 and historic 8550/current 8609 where applicable. No status, travel, work or renewal guarantee.
- **Newborn.** No ordinary dependent inclusion. Birth after application, before decision, may create a deemed application without providing a visa criterion for the child. For a birth in Australia after grant, check s78 and citizenship/status at birth; for an overseas birth after grant don't assume same automatic visa mechanism. Prepare birth record, legal parentage, dates and parental visa evidence and notification. Don't recommend withdrawal of a child's application without specific professional review of alternatives and any s48 implications. In any refusal use the actual notice and applicable review sections; don't assume applicant/sponsor or review deadline solely from physical location today.

## Sponsor obligations, variation and early cessation

- Keep duty periods separate from approval and visa effect. Check record-keeping instrument and record-production request (including channel, required items and actual time); an unprescribed keeping duty doesn't erase production duties. Track 28-day notification categories and trigger: relevant information changes, charges/convictions, overdue Commonwealth debt, orders and death. Record overdue health debt liability even if sponsorship stopped, for the applicable expense period until next departure or permanent grant where prescribed. Check finance/accommodation support from visa grant, and possible continuation following cessation if parent remains unlawfully or on a non-substantive visa. Maintain evidence; no agent submission or payment.
- Adding a second eligible parent is a variation, not transferring sponsor identity. Verify scope of approval, prior-parent events, debts, disclosure consent, adverse information/compliance and any reasonable-disregard route. Substituting one of two previously named people requires its own cessation/variation assessment; don't promise same-day transfer or ask the sponsor to withdraw before understanding consequences. Check cancellation/bar separately, including false information, noncompliance, debt, threatening conduct, changed material circumstances and narrower waiver of bar in exceptional circumstances with new evidence—not waiver of eligibility. Use actual review notice.
- **Sponsorship ceases (reg 2.64B):** separately consider sponsor permanent-visa cancellation, sponsor death, receipt of withdrawal, no application by a named parent within relevant initial or variation period, and visa cessation. One parent or both may be affected depending on event. Before visa lodgement, loss may remove the basis to apply; after lodgement and before decision, verify approved replacement for grant. Review new application or qualifying variation with surviving relatives where applicable. A new pending sponsorship is not approval.
- **Granted visa may end early (870.511):** verify ordinary expiry and cancellation of sponsorship approval (35-day limb), Department receipt of sponsor withdrawal (35-day replacement limb), death (90-day replacement limb) and effect of a relevant timely replacement application, subsequent refusal or final review. For applicable withdrawal/death refusal scenarios a further 35-day limb may apply; check exact event and rule, not a generic extension. Do not substitute the sponsor's permanent-visa cancellation for cancellation of sponsorship approval. No replacement process extends ordinary visa expiry by itself. Preserve cancellation, withdrawal receipt, death, replacement, decision and review records, and check VEVO urgently if affected. Relationship change is a notification and reassessment trigger, not a free-standing automatic expiry date.

For a combined report list Stage 1, Stage 2 for **each parent**, and a short dependency finding. If missing permission, sponsor identity/status or relevant dates, give one next intake request, not a full evidence questionnaire. In a nuanced timing case prepare the timeline and blocked facts rather than determining lawful stay.
