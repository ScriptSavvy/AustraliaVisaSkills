# Relationship, inclusion and sensitive evidence

Use three distinct tests: (1) legal link needed for the visa or sponsorship, (2) count for balance of family, (3) migrating family-unit membership and any other family obligations. One person can count for one purpose but not another. Read case-dated Migration Act ss 5, 5CA and 5G, Migration Regulations 1.03, 1.04, 1.05, 1.05A, 1.12 and 1.14A and the subclass provision(s), not an old definition by analogy.

## Proving the relationship

| Situation | Preparation action and limit |
|---|---|
| Birth record or legal parent | Start with full civil birth record/family register, identity/name changes, earlier declarations. Reconcile dates, parent names and identity. If missing/unreliable, organise credible alternative records (e.g. official record search, hospital, school or family records) and note why the ordinary evidence is unavailable; alternative evidence does not waive a requirement. An adult can still be a legal child. Check recognition and the basis of presumptions, qualifying findings or declarations. Birth entry or biology alone may not settle adoption, donor or surrogacy cases. |
| Adoption | Establish whether formal, step-parent, customary, adoption-back or not recognised; obtain order/certificate, jurisdiction, date/age, legal effect and later cancellation/re-adoption. Trace adult and sibling links through recognised legal parents rather than assuming biological ties are retained after full adoption. Customary adoption need not sever birth ties; confirm local law and migration recognition. Any permission to adopt or Family Law Act order is separate from operative migration criteria. |
| Step relationship and orders | Prove current partnership and the partner's legal link to the child. Former partner may have a narrower age/order/custody branch for ordinary Parent rules. Read operative terms, parties, jurisdiction, effective date, amendments, age at relevant event, responsibilities, residence and whether in force. A contact/order heading is not sufficient. Do not import Child 101/802 age-out provisions. Check historical lodgements before 1 November 1999 and 1 July 2011 if relevant. For 870, apply its ongoing relationship test and narrow deceased-partner route, not former-step rules from other subclasses. |
| Assisted conception/surrogacy, competing orders | Prepare birth, relationship and consent evidence, orders, care/custody details and jurisdiction. Separate ss 60H/60HB Family Law Act and any foreign recognition issue; do not determine legal parentage from a donor test or certificate alone. Name the exact question and evidence for qualified review. Do not direct a Child or citizenship application. |
| Consent and parental responsibility | A parenting order can address contact, communication or maintenance without the applicable custody or responsibility. Separate former-step, minor's permission to migrate, best interests and authority to consent to DNA. Do not contact a disputing or unsafe family member. Verify recognition and later variations where an order is foreign or unclear. |

## Family unit and newborn events

Keep separate rosters for the sponsor relationship, balance of family, migrating family and other declared family. For the general family-unit rule (reg 1.12(2)), check the family head's spouse or de facto partner; a child or step-child of the family head or that partner; and the dependent child of a qualifying child. For the child/step-child limb check engagement, spouse or de facto partner exclusions and the age at the relevant event: under 18, 18 to under 23 if dependent on the family head or their partner, or 23 or over if dependent on either of them under the incapacity limb. A **dependent child** under reg 1.03 is not identical to a **member of the family unit**: use the definition the provision actually calls for rather than imposing the general family-unit age cap on every dependent-child test. Check criteria at their specified dates, including changes before decision, non-migrating family and any linked health or character criteria. Do not assume all family must migrate, all non-migrating family face identical checks, or declaring a person adds them to an application.

**Prepare dependency evidence by limb (reg 1.05A(1)).**

- **Basic needs:** at the relevant time and for a substantial period immediately before it, examine whole or substantial financial reliance for food, clothing and shelter. Separately compare reliance on the claimed supporter with each other person or source for those needs. One source being the largest does not alone establish substantial reliance. Build a dated ledger of basic-needs costs, who actually met them (including relevant direct or in-kind payments), wages, benefits, other support, and any gap or period of independence. Useful evidence includes bank/payment summaries, receipts, wage or benefit records and housing or meal arrangements. Co-residence, study, financial gifts or emotional support alone do not establish the test. Separate tuition and discretionary costs from housing or meals paid for a student. Employment or benefits are not automatic disqualifiers: compare amounts and the person's actual needs. Twelve months may be a policy benchmark, not a fixed statutory minimum; record shorter, interrupted or resumed support and check the applicable policy instead of treating it as elapsed eligibility.
- **Incapacity for work:** separately check whole or substantial financial reliance because loss of bodily or mental function incapacitates the person for work. Organise appropriate clinical evidence of functional effect and work capacity and dated evidence of actual support, earnings or benefits. Do not re-use the basic-needs limitation or its substantial-period test as requirements for this limb. A diagnosis, part-time work or support payment alone does not settle it; keep health/PIC assessment separate. Use redacted summaries where possible.
- **Contributory carry-forward:** for 173→143 or 884→864 check reg 1.12(5) and the applicable secondary criteria. Verify the person is included in the permanent application and holds the specified temporary visa at the relevant lodgement, that it was granted on the relevant earlier family-unit basis, and whose old and new primary roles are involved. Keep this distinct from the general dependency test and any former-holder concession. Do not infer an age-out result or automatic carry-forward.

### Ordinary addition after lodgement

- **Permanent 103/143/804/864:** check reg 2.08A and the matching Schedule 1 class and Schedule 2 subclass. For a qualifying original application that permits combination and is still undecided, check the original applicant's written request and express relationship claim for their spouse, de facto partner or dependent child; not simply any other family member. Prepare the current [Form 1436](https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/form-listing/forms/1436.pdf) where directed, proof of relationship and passport/identity summary, the request acknowledgement and evidence of any applicable additional-applicant charge. If refused and remitted, inspect the actual order and scope; a pending review is not itself an undecided departmental application for this purpose.
- **Clock and location:** under reg 2.08A(1)(da), (f) in the checked text, the deemed application time is the later of ministerial receipt of the written request and payment of the applicable additional-applicant charge, if any. Check Schedule 1 required whereabouts when both have occurred (e.g. onshore/clearance or retirement-spouse limits as applicable), not from the postal/upload date or the original lodgement date alone. Read actual timestamps, payment rules, decision timing and any transitional provision. Do not assume that payment after a decision cures the case, that an additional applicant is free of charge, or that application/decision secondary criteria, sponsorship, health or character are waived. Separate deemed combination from acceptance or grant; do not use 2.08A to conclude the added person satisfies primary criteria.
- **Temporary 173/884:** Home Affairs describes a possible pre-decision Form 1436 route, including an onshore instruction for 884. But regs 2.08A and 2.08B do not establish ordinary late addition for Class UT/UU in the checked text; the Schedule 1 combination provisions concern same-time/same-place applications. Keep the Department's practical instructions and the unresolved legal basis separate. Collect the actual stage, relationship, person’s location at relevant times and written Department instructions; ask the Department to confirm the applicable mechanism, timing, fees and effect for the case before relying on it. Do not declare the route legally available or categorically impossible from a webpage or checklist. This does not restrict the separately verified newborn pathways. For 870, do not use Form 1436 as an ordinary inclusion route.

### Birth before or after a decision

Record only the next material fact about birth date/place, parentage, parents' citizenship and visa status at birth, any bridging and last substantive visa, whether and when the relevant application was lodged and decided, and later grant/clearance. Check citizenship first where it could change the route; a birth in Australia and a birth abroad have different rules. For a non-citizen child born after a parent applied and before decision, reg 2.08 can deem an application and combination at birth without a separate late-addition request; it excludes a contributory parent newborn child. Check applicable decision criteria and any required sponsorship at decision. An adoption after application is not a birth under this mechanism. Separately, s78 concerns deemed *visa grant* in Australia at birth when its conditions are met, not the grant of a pending application. Keep birth notification (see [Form 1496i](https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/form-listing/forms/1496i.pdf), identity/birth evidence and current channel) separate from application effect, citizenship, grant and travel. For 870 read its [own newborn branch](subclass-870.md#handoff-and-stage-2--individual-applicant); deemed application does not create secondary grant criteria.

**Special contributory newborn:** verify the reg 1.03 definition for a non-adopted child born while the parent held 173/884 or a qualifying bridging visa with that last substantive visa. Check s78 where the child is born in Australia and the reg 2.07AL/UT or UU written-birth-notification and visa process where needed, including the child's grant and travel prerequisites. Under reg 2.08AA(1) separate applicable temporary decision criteria from the displaced secondary application criteria. For permanent 143/864, check whether the child holds 173/884 and the parent has applied and is not finally determined or has already been granted the permanent visa. If the combination rule applies, its timing is on the child's temporary grant in Australia or immediately after immigration clearance if the temporary grant was offshore, not automatically at overseas birth. If the parent has not applied permanently, check inclusion in the later application. Do not import the older Australia-birth-only shortcut or advise travel based on a notice. Verify status, grant and clearance records, and leave unmet or disputed predicates unresolved.

## Voluntary DNA evidence

Use documentary evidence first. DNA can address an identified biological link, not legal parentage, migration consent, dependency or visa eligibility. Do not recommend routine tests or private tests simply to strengthen a checklist. Before any cost or booking, read the actual departmental offer and [Form 1259i](https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/form-listing/forms/1259i.pdf). Testing is voluntary; declining or not providing a test can lead to decision on other evidence, not automatic refusal or automatic acceptance. Provide a clear opportunity to organise credible alternatives and explain the exact evidence gap. Where applicant raises risk or family conflict, use safe communication.

If the Department has offered testing, verify the nominated participants, laboratory accreditation, identity, witnessed consent and chain of custody against the letter and current guidance. Distinguish information sheet, consent/disclosure (1526), cover and collection forms (1527/1528) and kit documentation as needed. For minors confirm actual guardian consent authority/delegation; exceptional remote arrangements are for the Department, not to arrange here. Don't request a raw genetic report, samples or a child's identifiers; access to a result is not authorisation to share it. Direct parentage and kinship reports have different evidentiary weight; don't apply the same benchmark. Pre-existing reports require independent identity, accreditation, witnessing and custody checks, not automatic exclusion or acceptance. If unexpected results, missing identity, withheld disclosure or conflicting documents arise, offer an evidence comparison using redacted summaries and the exact next verification; distinguish sharing with the Department from disclosure to sponsor or a third party. Counselling/safe contact may be useful, not a visa mandate.

## Inspect and record

Identify document issuer, title, subject and pages actually reviewed; distinguish copies from originals as required. Compare birth names, aliases, dates, translations, signatures/authorities, amendments and earlier declarations without overwriting the facts. Request one missing/clearer page only if it materially resolves the next issue. Verify official translation/certification requirements for location and channel; an AI explanation is not a certified translation. Preserve discrepancies and alternative evidence. For a report, use anonymised person labels and point to the minimum required supporting record, never store or copy case files into the reusable skill.
