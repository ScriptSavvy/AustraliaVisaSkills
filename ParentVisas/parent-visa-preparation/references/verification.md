# Verify requirements, dates and evidence

## Evidence hierarchy and dated checks

Use the applicable [Migration Act 1958](https://www.legislation.gov.au/C1958A00062) and [Migration Regulations 1994](https://www.legislation.gov.au/F1996B03551), controlling instruments and any transitional text. Home Affairs and Services Australia explain procedure and evidence, but summaries are not the legislative rule. An applicant's verified correspondence establishes their requested steps, not an entitlement or override. Bundled guidance identifies what to consider; it is not independent proof of a legal requirement or factual claim.

1. Identify subclass, visa class, person, event and date. Separate Schedule 1 (making an application) from Schedule 2 (criteria and grant). For 870 sponsor use Part 2 family sponsorship divisions. For 103, 143, 173 use vol. 2; for 804, 864, 870, 884 use vol. 3. Follow incorporated definitions, cross-references, instruments, exceptions and transition clauses. Check the compilation effective interval and commenced amendments; “Latest” alone does not establish applicable coverage. Do not assume there is a blanket waiver or that an exception described for a different date or role carries over.
2. Read the relevant official body and operative passage, not a search snippet, navigation or loading page. Read the current form, instructions and notice before taking its procedural wording as established. Record source title, exact section, URL, date checked, version/interval and case event. Name unresolved conflicts and retain conditional possibilities without choosing one for convenience. If browsing fails, do not use remembered fees, current queues, income instruments, age requirements or dates as verified. Continue document inventory and consistency checks.
3. For notices, record the issuing body, recipient, date/time, method, reference, relevant visa and due-date wording. Distinguish date of document, transmission, actual receipt and applicable statutory receipt; an extension request is not a granted extension. Ask for a redacted excerpt, not an account login. When there may be an immediate deadline or status issue, say what action can preserve options (retrieve the complete notice and urgently verify with the issuing authority or appropriately qualified professional); never assume a pending request keeps an application or visa alive.
4. Retain only the public official sources necessary to check the case in the answer. Link the applicable dated text where possible. Do not include a full research log or sensitive identifiers.

## Known material verification gates

- **Permanent lodgement method:** F2026L00441 commenced 22 April 2026. Follow its sections 12–13 and Schedule 1, not a generic paper checklist. The permanent online route has a written paper authorisation exception and a saved pre-commencement mail/courier branch. Don't apply those branches automatically to temporary 173/884. Verify current delivery instructions before posting.
- **870 onshore timing:** [LIN 19/185 s 6](https://www.legislation.gov.au/F2019L00887/asmade/2019-06-26/text/original/pdf) counts from ministerial permission; the Home Affairs step-by-step page also uses sponsorship approval wording. Check the applicable instrument, written permission and sponsorship notice. Do not use sponsorship approval as the universal starting date. Flag mismatched or missing notice dates and confirm with the Department before the earliest apparent deadline. No unverified time or extension advice.
- **Pending vs held 870 and another Parent visa:** some Home Affairs pages refer broadly to having applied for or holding 870. Inspect the exact Schedule 1 item, any general validity provision, date and whether the person holds 870 or has left Australia since its cessation. Do not convert a pending 870 into an automatic blanket legal conclusion or infer that two applications may coexist. Separate an already lodged Parent application from a new one. Never recommend withdrawal to test validity.
- **Legacy or specialist routes:** do not infer current effect of retired visa/sponsor provisions, COVID concessions, statutory parentage, newborn or post-lodgement family inclusion from old documentation. Retrieve the exact event version and pathway. Prepare a fact and evidence handoff if legal applicability remains unverified.
- **Post-lodgement addition:** read regs 2.08, 2.08A, 2.08AA or 2.08B as applicable, the matching Schedule 1 item and secondary criteria; check the current form and departmental instructions separately. For ordinary permanent addition, verify both request receipt and any applicable payment, applicant whereabouts and original application's decision status. For temporary [173](https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/visas/getting-a-visa/visa-listing/contributory-parent-temporary-173) and [884](https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/visas/getting-a-visa/visa-listing/contributory-aged-parent-temporary-884), the public Form 1436 directions do not establish ordinary late addition under the checked provisions. Keep the legal route **Needs verification** and obtain the authority for the specific case; do not conflate it with the newborn routes.
