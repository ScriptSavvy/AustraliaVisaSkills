# Parent migration program — preparation by milestone

This covers the six subclasses in the permanent migration program, including the two temporary contributory stages. Do not import the visa or sponsor rules for 870. Use [verification](verification.md), [family evidence](family-evidence.md) and the applicable Schedule 1 and 2 text. Treat the following as preparation rules subject to the applicable legislation, not authority to lodge, travel or withdraw.

## Application and grant location

| Subclass / class | Ordinary application preparation | Ordinary grant preparation |
|---|---|---|
| 103 Parent / AX | Online through ImmiAccount; can generally apply in or outside Australia, not in clearance if in Australia (confirm the item and any related person's location). | Outside Australia under 103.411 in the inspected dated regulation; check retirement and legacy concession exceptions. If the public overview says a decision can be made either side of the border, distinguish decision from **grant** and verify before any travel decision. |
| 143 Contributory Parent / CA | Online; in or outside Australia subject to branch and validity. Identify direct application vs holder/former-holder of 173. | Outside ordinarily. Check 143.411–143.412: 173 holder at application, holder of relevant ministerially substituted 600 or their family unit, specified family member of a 173 holder where both were in Australia at application, retirement and historical concessions. These gates do not excuse immigration-clearance restrictions. |
| 173 Contributory Parent (Temporary) / UT | Ordinary paper 47PA; in or outside Australia subject to relevant item. Special contributory newborn branch. | Outside ordinarily; distinct qualifying newborn and legacy concession conditions. Read 173.411. For stays, distinguish first entry, travel facility and actual visa end dates. |
| 804 Aged Parent / BP | Online; Australia, not in immigration clearance. Check primary age gate and each included person's location. | Australia, not in immigration clearance ordinarily. Check any historical clauses for the actual decision date. |
| 864 Contributory Aged Parent / DG | Online; Australia, not in immigration clearance. Separate direct application, 884 holder and former holder. | Australia, not in immigration clearance ordinarily under 864.411. Do not use a general description of the deemed-holder concession to infer offshore grant. The historical offshore exception has distinct date, location and concession-period conditions. |
| 884 Contributory Aged Parent (Temporary) / UU | Ordinary paper 47PA; Australia, not in immigration clearance, unless the special contributory newborn event is established. | Australia, not in immigration clearance ordinarily; verify the newborn/legacy alternatives and actual grant record. |

- From **22 April 2026**, the [arrangements instrument](https://www.legislation.gov.au/F2026L00441/asmade) specifies internet form for AX/CA/BP/DG. Use paper for those four only if the Department specifically authorises it in writing and its method, address and deadline are met. A saved transport rule covers specified items posted/couriered before commencement and received afterwards; preserve dispatch and receipt evidence. UT/UU ordinary paper route distinguishes post to the named postal address and courier to the delivery address. Verify current instructions; don't assume hand delivery, redirection or a general upload works. A qualifying contributory newborn has a different written-notification route. See [47PA](https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/form-listing/forms/47pa.pdf), class/item, actual acknowledgement and Home Affairs guidance.
- First application charge and any additional-applicant amount are checked at lodgement; applicable second instalment due before grant. Check date, subclass, status at lodgement, age/cohort or newborn and any applicable citizenship/passport concession; verify applicable rates and actual request. Attach acknowledgements and receipts separately; payment is not lodgement. Keep migration status, bridging eligibility and grant/travel evidence separate. The permanent travel facility is not a limit on residence in Australia. For ordinary 173 verify first-entry trigger; for ordinary 884 check duration from grant; use the notice rather than predicting expiry. Read any conditions for the actual holder, including 8502/8515 for relevant secondary visas.

## Aged, status and overlap gates

- For 804/864/884, check pension-age definition, date of birth, Schedule 1 age-at-application rule for relevant applications from 17 December 2024 and separate Schedule 2 criterion. Check any substituted-600 provision; ordinary visitor 600 is not that exception. Verify current/last substantive 771, refusal/cancellation and s48 exposure, no-further-stay conditions or actual waiver, reg 2.07AI (173/884 held since entry), Schedule 3 including 3002 and any specific switch exception. Verify separately for each person. Do not infer a grace period or a bridging visa; record own grant, current conditions and next independent status step. For a material deadline or status problem check actual notices and seek specifically scoped, urgent help, not travel advice.
- Check unfinalised listed Parent applications for each person, current 870, any prior 870 cessation and subsequent departure, refusal or pending review, before advising on another application. A pending versus held 870 and prior-lodged vs new applications are different forks; see [known verification gates](verification.md). Schedule 1 overlap before lodgement is distinct from other Parent matters (including review) still outstanding at decision. Do not treat an online form or a withdrawal section as cancelling tribunal/court proceedings. Obtain written scope and record for each applicant, unresolved person and live proceeding. If capacity to sign is in question, get the actual authority and timing reviewed before accepting a proxy signature.
- On a 103/804 to contributory switch collect the exact previous application and queue dates, target class, unfinalised status, notice and any earlier withdrawal. Check the [current processing directions](https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/Visa-subsite/Pages/Processing-times/Family-visa-processing-priorities.aspx), actual Direction 103 cohort and timing. [Home Affairs switching instructions](https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/form-listing/Pages/parent-visa-processing-centre-form.aspx) also describe switches to 173/884 with earlier dates retained; don't treat permanent-target queue rules as proof for those temporary targets. Check the precise cohort and obtain written confirmation of the affected dates and sequence if unresolved. Direction 117 replaced 102 on 25 July 2026 and is distinct from Direction 103. Do not say one queue or fee transfers automatically or recommend withdrawal until the overlap and sequence are resolved. Keep acknowledgement, initial core assessment, **written queue date**, queue release, final request and final decision separate. A release date is not a grant time; very old contributory cases may have a different queue-date basis. Record the dated official milestone, not a promise.

## Two-stage contributory transition and retirement

For 173→143 or 884→864, obtain prior application and visa grant notices, whether held at permanent lodgement for every applicant, location and cessation dates. Holding the matching temporary visa can alter balance of family, sponsorship/settlement, minor criteria, health/PIC and fees; check each criterion before treating it as waived, including the earlier family-unit basis if relevant. If the temporary visa expired, check the distinct holder-definition limb for a visa held at any time in the 28 days immediately before permanent lodgement (143.111/864.111), and whether the individual criterion uses that definition. Outside that limb, prepare a dated chronology and evidence of compassionate and compelling circumstances for the separate former-holder route in those clauses. Mere forgetting or hardship is not automatic. Check validity and Schedule 3 independently. If the deeming route is not accepted, assess what a direct application would need. Record any fee or Medicare provision tied to 28 days separately from migration validity; no general grace period, restoration of status or promised work/benefit rights. A deemed holder is not a reason to assume offshore 864 grant.

Retirement pathway is not for anyone who has simply retired. Check 103/143 only, status on **8 May 2018** (held, or last substantive, eligible 405/410), no incompatible substantive visa between that date and applying, and 103/143 onshore application location for primary and partner. Check separate grant location and exemptions for sponsorship, balance of family and AoS. Verify actual subclass text before issuing any finding. A qualifying ministerially substituted 600 (or specified older 676) is a separate legal history; examine notice and grant basis. It can change selected age, balance of family, PIC 4004 and health branches for specified cohorts, not all requirements. Neither 602 medical treatment nor intervention strategy is assessed by this skill.

## Sponsor, assurer and balance of family

Keep primary applicant, other applicants, settled sponsor and Assurance of Support (AoS) assurer distinct. Check sponsor legal relationship, eligible status, settled evidence and timing at or before application as a Schedule 2 issue (not automatically a Schedule 1 invalidity). Keep citizenship/PR/eligible NZ evidence, residence chronology, sponsorship declaration (including applicable [Form 40](https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/form-listing/forms/40.pdf)), previous sponsorship and capacity evidence as relevant. Gather current and prior sponsorships for replacement. If the direct child sponsor is under 18, check permitted adult relative, guardian or community organisation routes in the applicable criteria; for an organisation support authority, establishment, operating history, capacity and accommodation/support arrangements. Do not assume any sponsor can also act as assurer. AoS is a distinct financial undertaking; typically relevant to 103/804 and 143/864 rather than 173/884, with retirement exception. Verify who is covered, process, acceptance, applicable duration and any bond requirements against [Services Australia](https://www.servicesaustralia.gov.au/assurance-support), subclass rules and actual request. Don't present a bond as the visa charge or ask someone to lodge an AoS early based on queue data.

**BoF ledger.** Identify all potentially countable children (including recognised step and adopted children); do not prune by migration intention, dependency or strained ties. Record each legal family link, status and usual country at the legally relevant event, source evidence and reason for any exclusion. Include Australian citizens per the legal definition; permanent residents/eligible NZ citizens need usual residence in Australia. Temporary residence there alone does not establish a count; verify special rules for ineligible children in Australia or unknown residence, using past usual residence/citizenship as prescribed. Count each child once. Pass arithmetic if at least half of countable children are eligible, **or** the number eligible is strictly larger than the children in each individual other country. A tie fails the second limb but can still pass the first. Example: of five countable children, two eligible, two in country A and one in B; neither limb is satisfied. If an unresolved count can change either limb, report alternatives as conditional, not a pass/fail. Use the relevant version of reg 1.05 and Schedule 2 to confirm exact timing; do not blanket-retest at decision. No general compassionate waiver; check specific retirement, 173/884 holder and substituted-600 branches separately.

Apply exclusions only after checking the exact rule and evidence: death, adoption/severance, a parent's exclusive custody removed by order/operation of law, individually supported persecution/abuse plus no possible reunification elsewhere, or UNHCR registration **and** residence in a qualifying camp. Missing papers/unknown whereabouts, estrangement, shared-custody loss or refugee registration on its own is not enough. Check former-step relationship and historic lodgement dates in [family evidence](family-evidence.md).

## Final assessment, family changes and requests

- Track identity, character, public interest criteria (PICs), health and special return criteria separately. Use the provision for each primary, secondary, migrating and non-migrating family member, including custody/consent and best interests for minors where required. Distinguish 4005 from 4007 and any specific cohort or dispensing provision; no generic health waiver. Use the Health Assessment Portal ID and case letter before requesting a repeat check; do not assume a document expiry or statutory response deadline from the queue.
- Document death, relationship change and new dependants promptly for appropriate notification under s104. If a primary applicant dies, prepare proof and survivor's written wish to proceed; check surviving primary criteria as at the relevant events (especially aged routes), queue and separate refund rules. A sponsor's death calls for replacement eligibility first. In temporary-to-permanent cases distinguish death before permanent lodgement (temporary sponsor and cessation dates; possible substituted-600 branch) and after lodgement (approved sponsor's usual residence and holder status at application); check no eligible alternative sponsor before any deemed sponsorship result.
- A secondary spouse/de facto partner in **103/143/804/864** may have a family-violence continuation route. Preserve original combination and qualifying relationship, later cessation, qualifying conduct/person, independent location/entry rules and the reason/status of the primary's grant or refusal. Unrelated primary refusal does not satisfy a violence-linked decision criterion. Check associated children, family-unit/PIC dependencies and any review fee provision separately. Do not require detailed violence disclosures; agree safe communication and do not seek evidence from an unsafe contact. Do not use this branch for 173/884/870.
- At refusal, remittal or withdrawal preserve actual notice, issuing body, recipient, decision basis, locations and review/bridging dates. Who can seek review may depend on case and role. Do not calculate review periods or predict lawful stay from a general rule; hand off the specific question and records urgently. No validity, status or review-rights determination.
