## Main finding and priority actions
- State the specific preparation result, important blocker, and most useful action first. Give an exact verified date or explicitly state the date is unresolved. Do not infer extension or lawful stay.
- For 870, distinguish sponsor preparation status and applicant preparation status; show which shared dependency prevents the next step, if any.

## Scope
Case date, branch, stage(s), known location(s), and actual items inspected; keep names/identifiers redacted. Distinguish dates of application, approval, queue and grant as applicable.

## Sources and limits
List only material official source sections, effective periods and checked dates, actual relevant notices, and conclusions blocked by source/factual gaps.
