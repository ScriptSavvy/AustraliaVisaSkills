# Evidence and application procedures

Use only the relevant items. Record whether each item is a legal requirement, a conditional or official submission direction, a useful source, or a practical suggestion. Do not impose examples as compulsory attachments. Keep applicant names and identifiers out of case summaries where labels suffice. Refer to [pathway rules](pathways.md) and [live-source gates](verification.md).

## Build a reliable evidence file

- Per person, reconcile passport identity, name changes and linked old/new passports; grant/status records; movement history; and actual or intended application date/location. Use the whole relevant page including dates, issuer and qualifications. Label the source for each claim (applicant report, filename, viewed page, official record) and conflicts explicitly. For uncertain scan digits, record the unresolved field and request just the relevant page or official record, not a complete unredacted passport.
- In a chronology use source anchors for dates, status at movements, lawful presence, travel-facility end, visa cessation/cancellation, and relevant gaps. Official travel records can be requested by the applicant; match multiple passports, names and spelling variants. Boarding records, stamped passports, school or work records can help corroborate, not silently substitute for missing status. If verified travel history is enough for routine residence, do not require unrelated ties evidence. Do not infer full travel history from last trip or grant date.
- Prepare non-English originals and English translations in line with current Home Affairs instructions for location, translator credentials and document presentation. Confirm actual current instructions for colour/legibility, required pages and the upload channel. Do not impose a universal certification, witness or translation format without checking applicability. Check for inconsistent spelling, dates and missing pages; never correct official facts on the applicant's behalf.

## Substantial ties evidence

For each claim state the tie, duration/scale and Australian linkage, the demonstrable benefit, actual records and date, and the next evidentiary gap. Assess collectively; substantial does not mean the applicant's Australian ties must outweigh overseas ties. A benefit must exist and be linked to the ties; do not invent a minimum dollar value for the benefit itself. Past residence, active Australian links and credible return plans may make different contributions. Evidence of an intention to return (school enrolment, housing, move arrangements) is useful but not a universal substitute for documented ties.

| Type | Useful records and practical checks |
|---|---|
| Business | Role, authority and Australian connection; active trading, commercial/profit intent and records; transactions, tax assessments, company material, signed contracts or JV/partnership records. Show revenue, jobs, production, exports, technology, trade links or other demonstrable benefit. Passive shares alone differ from materially supporting a family business or professionally and actively managing substantial investments. |
| Cultural | Community, arts, sport, religious, intellectual or cultural activity; active participation, professional level or recognition if relevant. Publications, contracts, membership, programmes or credible public coverage. Explain the link and benefit rather than rely on a membership card alone. |
| Employment | Paid job, accepted offer or relevant government/overseas posting, duration, duties, employer-Australia link, pay evidence and commencement. Contracts, offers, payslips, tax records or posting letters. Casual work (stored policy generally looks for at least one year living in Australia in the prior two years for that example), repeat unfulfilled offers, work rights and concrete job-search evidence can affect weight; no automatic result. Offshore work for Australian organisations, governments, Australian office of an international charity or documented representation of Australia may be relevant, not generic offshore employment. |
| Personal | Time and lawful status in Australia, participation, close family links, residence and status of relatives, children and best interests, and home/relocation plans. Verify whether property is lived in or actively used/rented; ownership alone may not show a benefit. Family overseas with documented Australian ties and plans can be relevant. Consider continued need for safety for humanitarian applicants without eliciting unnecessary details. |

Stored policy examples are prompts to gather evidence, not alternative statutory thresholds or mandatory gates. Examples include close relatives with Australian citizenship or permanent residence (substantial Australian residence for the PR relative in the ordinary policy example, with a distinct minor child/parent/guardian best-interests consideration); substantial lifetime or recent residence (including 50% of lifetime with qualifying citizenship/PR, or 365 days in the last five years including temporary residence as a **tie**, not the 730-day legal ledger); qualifying Australian defence service (stored example: at least three continuous months, check the applicable definition); property value against a current relevant ABS state/territory mean; a valid Convention Travel Document/Certificate of Identity with current or former humanitarian status and no contradictory protection finding; paid ongoing Australian work with pay above the operative policy benchmark; past specified occupation-assessment/list scenarios within ten years (and same-region residential purchase for the regional-list example); one year of Australian work in the previous two; ongoing public-sector work or current official Australian representation in an applicable international organisation. Verify exact current meaning, date, level, list and policy applicability before relying on any benchmark. Do not replace a historical TSMIT reference with a guessed current salary threshold, use an occupation list as mandatory, or decline to organise evidence because a benchmark is unavailable. If an example cannot be confirmed, report a potential tie, not that the officer must accept or reject it.

## Family records

Get each applicant's own status evidence and application record, head's 155/157 grant and effect or separate application and basis, and evidence of family link at lodgement. For spouses use marriage evidence plus genuine and continuing relationship evidence. For de facto consider registration or evidence of duration and genuine ongoing relationship under applicable requirements/exceptions; Home Affairs' ordinary evidence guidance describes 12 months or registration, not a licence to ignore applicable exceptions or add a blanket joint-account requirement. For children: birth/adoption/family records, relevant stepchild links, dependency or incapacity evidence only as applicable. Separate “close relative” ties evidence from “member of the family unit” evidence. Minimise children's data. No routine RRV Form 1229 or automatic child-specific PIC; any court order, disputed authority or child travel restriction is a separate urgent question, not overridden by a visa grant. Do not advise circumventing a restriction or contacting someone unsafe.

## Lodgement and Department requests

- Normally Form 1085E through ImmiAccount. A technical failure does not by itself permit paper or email filing. Use Home Affairs' technical support route. Verify the applicable LIN 22/019 text and actual written Department invitation before using Form 1085. The checked compilation provides an alternative only if online cannot be used, written invitation is attached and the application is **received** by 11:59 pm AEST/AEDST as applicable where received, **two calendar days after** the Department sends the notice. Confirm destination from the instrument and notice. Do not use applicant local time, business days, a send timestamp in place of receipt or the date it was read. Check timezone/deadline with an actual date tool; flag imminent deadlines now. Do not promise acceptance, cure or extension. Distinguish paying from submitting and check the acknowledgment separately.
- Check the current base charge per separate application, any concession and the non-Internet application charge (NIAC). Verify Schedule 1 item 1128, reg 2.12C and IMMI 16/099 against official pricing/invitation. Stored operational handling of an initial payment and possible refund is not the statutory exemption itself. If directions appear to require payment despite an apparent exemption, flag the discrepancy and ask for official clarification before acting; do not promise refund, tell the applicant to omit payment, or override a notice. Possible exemptions include historical movement-record/visa status, a missing required travel document, specified status/bar or a broader instrument circumstance; applicability must be checked case by case. The Pacific-regional valid passport concession introduced in 2026 is separate from paper exemptions; verify the relevant application date, issuing country/passport and actual charge without collecting passport numbers. Never hard-code fees.
- RRV forms do not themselves apply for a bridging visa. If lawful status is uncertain, a temporary substantive visa is expiring, or the applicant may be unlawful onshore, urgently check actual visa/status and seek advice on the appropriate separate bridging/status process before expiry or travel. No promise of bridging grant, backdating, lawful stay or right to travel. A delay or possible review likewise supplies no status.
- For a Department request, record what is requested, issue/send date, notification details, deadline/time zone, response channel and actual evidence. Treat an extension request as a request, not an extension. Separate lodgement requirements, decision-stage passport/status facts and later responses. There is no guarantee the Department will request a missing item before deciding. No submission/payment/contact in this skill.

## Legacy and high-consequence branches

- An apparent Authority to Return or Return Endorsement (ATR/RE; sometimes BF-111, BF-R ambiguous), pre-1994 permanent entry, absorbed-person visa, special circumstance visa, or ex-citizen grant requires the actual record, not a code-only conclusion. A still-effective reg 9 transitional permanent visa can bar a valid RRV. Other transitional visas, concurrent visas or incomplete digital records differ. Obtain historical passports, stamps, travel records or archive records, dates/status at arrival/departure and evidence of ongoing effect; for cases before modern records consider National Archives passenger/card evidence. Pre-1994 exempt non-citizen or New Zealand status needs assessment of applicable historic law, residence and evidence of a home, work, family, property and travel; do not assume modern Special Category Visa status is permanent status. Presence, offshore departure-status and visa-holder family pathways still have their own predicates. Former citizen: record where/when citizenship ceased, visa by operation of law if applicable (s35), and subsequent travel; check status before departure, and treat resumption of citizenship as a separate possible official pathway, not a substitute conclusion. Refer exact legacy issue if records/law do not settle it.
- Where the most recent permanent visa was subject to a s135 notice or s134 cancellation, distinguish dates, which person's visa was affected, whether notified cancellation would not proceed, whether cancellation or set-aside actually happened, and common-status gateway vs validity. Dependant history or a later RRV implicated in business grounds needs official records; other cancellation grounds may separately affect former status. Do not infer a bar or its removal from a visa subclass, relative's notice or temporary visa alone.
- If humanitarian/protection history, identity conflicts, no valid passport, s501 character, deportation, or child welfare concerns apply, organise minimal evidence without pushing unsafe disclosure or contact (including applying for a national passport without considering protection issues). Check exact passport/passport-exception rules and actual request; credible current humanitarian documents/ongoing need for safety may be relevant, not proof of status or waiver. No routine police request inferred, and no assurance a police record is irrelevant.
- If refused, obtain a redacted notice and notification evidence; identify the exact decision and date made, notice received, applicant presence at lodgement, decision and proposed review application; preserve all dates separately. The ART pathway has different location tests under applicable Act and Regulations. In the checked Act, s338(2) addresses an onshore application with immigration-clearance exceptions, and s347A(2) requires presence in the migration zone when seeking review. Section 338(7A) addresses a visa application made outside the migration zone for a permanent visa that could be granted in or outside it; s347A(3) requires presence in the migration zone both when the decision was made and when seeking review. Check the applicable version, every predicate, notification and strict review time limits; this comparison is not a finding of review rights. Do not conclude review rights or compute a binding deadline from old citations, guess from notice receipt or tell anyone to travel to establish review rights. Urgently identify the particular date/location/notice ambiguity for a qualified practitioner or the official ART guidance. For possible invalid grant, wrong subclass or defective notification, preserve notices and chronology; never declare a decision void, promise reconsideration/re-notification or assume an extended deadline.
