# Source verification and uncertainty gates

Read the actual body and applicability on the applicant's dates, not just a title, snippet or the fact a page loads. Record source title, section or clause, exact URL, version/effective coverage where shown and date checked. Use neutral searches; don't put applicant details, documents, account IDs or case facts into a query. If the official visa page is a navigation shell, inspect the substantive About, Eligibility and Step by step sections through the host's supported tools. If not accessible, keep material points unresolved rather than simulating a lookup.

## Known reconciliation gates

- **Time thresholds:** the detailed 155 provisions control over loose public summaries. Do not change “5 years or more” continuous into “more than,” or the cumulative “more than 5 years” into “at least five.” The 157 absence test is **more than three months** immediately before lodgement, not a no-absence shortcut, years or 90 days. Exact dates may still be unresolved separately from the rule.
- **Residency status:** Schedule 2 text in the checked compilation requires permanent visa or citizenship for counted residence. Do not treat historical entry permits or mapping to visa status as an independent modern statutory category without a dated authority.
- **Family and charges:** generic family-duration and additional-charge descriptions do not authorise a combined Class BB application. Distinguish 155/157 family head subclasses, visa still in effect vs unused facility, and independent qualifying separate applications. A statutory non-Internet charge exemption and Home Affairs payment/refund treatment may differ; check exact operative condition, invitation and official billing, and leave discrepancies unresolved rather than merging them.
- **Status and special cases:** verify pre-1994/reg 9, competing substantive visa, bridging, character/return exclusion, passport discretion and historical humanitarian rules individually. No universal tie benchmark; current ABS valuations, list definitions and any policy pay reference may be unavailable; use relevant underlying evidence without assuming acceptance. No independent current-law finding on those benchmarks from this bundle.
- **Refusals:** check the applicable 338 and 347/347A provisions and commencement rules. The consulted 2026 Act places who can apply and location requirements in s347A, not old s347(2)/(3)/(3A) shorthand. Do not generalise an offshore lodging case or give a binding deadline; review notice, response and professional handoff are separate from document readiness.

For each live discrepancy, record: affected question → what each source says and its reference/date → applicability/conflict → conclusion blocked → the exact next source, record or qualified interpretation needed. If dated law resolves the wording, state why and keep the difference visible only where it affects the applicant. For imminent travel or reply deadlines, caution first and continue unaffected evidence checks.
