## What it does

Prepares a checklist, reviews redacted evidence or helps organise your next steps. It distinguishes an expiring travel facility from the underlying visa, counts supported days from a dated travel/status timeline, and separates the residence, ties, absence and family pathways for 155 and 157. It can organise a Department request or refusal notice for timely follow-up, without deciding review rights.

This is not a visa, status, eligibility, validity, approval or travel-clearance decision. Material case-specific rules are checked against applicable official sources where possible; unavailable checks stay unresolved. No government endorsement is claimed.
