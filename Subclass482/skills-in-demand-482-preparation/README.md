## What you receive
A tailored checklist, document-quality review or preparation plan. Findings identify the employer/sponsor or applicant responsible, the applicable stage, evidence checked, gaps, uncertainty and concrete next actions. Nomination readiness, visa readiness and shared consistency remain separate. No combined percentage score or approval probability.

## Important limits
Targeted official checks dated 21 October 2026 supplement stored preparation guidance; they do not establish comprehensive current-law coverage. Read [current-source gates and unresolved matters](references/verified-updates.md). Salary, English, occupation, assessment, dates and agreement rules need relevant-case verification. Ambiguous placements, SAF corrections, family/duration and review/work-rights questions remain explicitly qualified where unresolved. Browsing failure does not prevent independent document-quality checks, but it prevents a verified compliance finding on the affected requirement.

Full sponsorship applications, agreement negotiation, historical TSS applications, representation, submission, payment, withdrawal and third-party communication are outside scope. Current-holder transitions are covered only with their specific source/date gates. The skill does not determine individual interim work permission or certify family expiry in unresolved special-family, age-23, TSS or passport-class branches. Source-rights, rights-holder/contact and professional/regulatory review are not cleared by this local draft. No native-host installation or government endorsement is claimed.

Use redacted or synthetic information for your first test. Your agent provider may process uploaded files. No additional external document processor is required or authorised by this skill.
