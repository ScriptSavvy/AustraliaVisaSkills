## Next steps and limits
Order actions by urgency and dependency, with employer/sponsor and applicant actions separate. A report preparation list is not a demand for several immediate intake answers. Stop at the stated check: no eligibility, validity, work-permission, authenticity, acceptance or approval guarantee. No unexplained scores/probabilities. No submission, payment, withdrawal or communication has been authorised by this preparation report.
