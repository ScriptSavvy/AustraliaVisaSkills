## Purpose and scope
One integrated preparation skill for **two distinct stages** of current Australian Skills in Demand (SID) subclass 482: employer nomination and visa application. Cover Core Skills, Specialist Skills and Labour Agreement branches. Users can request either stage or both; keep both stages' dependencies visible without imposing unrelated intake. Produce a tailored evidence checklist, document review, gap report or preparation plan, with employer/sponsor and applicant actions clearly separated.

Nomination preparation is full scope, not just background to the visa. Sponsorship approval and work-agreement negotiation are separate dependencies, not comprehensively covered. Legacy TSS/457 holders may need a current SID transition check; this is not a complete historical TSS application skill. Do not import obsolete TSS streams or work-experience/test rules.

No submission, payment, withdrawal, account access, employer/Department contact, representation, review filing, visa-work permission determination or guaranteed outcomes. Refusal/review/refund/change-of-employer material supports evidence organisation and identifying questions, not an automatic legal strategy. Personalised immigration assistance may trigger professional/regulatory obligations; a disclaimer does not resolve them. Escalate the specific consequential interpretation, with documents and dates, to an appropriately qualified professional.

## Workflow and decision rules
1. **Define the preparation check.** Identify requested stage(s), user role, stream and material dates. Keep nomination lodgement/approval, visa lodgement/decision and post-grant events separate. Establish what each person is authorised to share. Unknown nomination or stream produces a conditional check, not assumed compliance.
2. **Refresh applicable requirements.** Inspect actual official bodies and relevant effective/savings provisions. Record title, URL, section, version/effective dates, date checked and effect on the stored rule. Check volatile thresholds, tests, fees, forms, assessment classes and deadlines at use. A retrieval shell, search snippet or stored check date does not verify the law. Preserve exceptions/discretion and unresolved issues; continue independent work.
3. **Build stage-specific evidence rows.** Use mandatory, conditional (with explicit trigger), useful supporting evidence or practical suggestion. Keep sponsor certifications separate from applicant proof. Distinguish examples from requirements. Load the applicable agreement and concessions instead of assuming standard-stream rules apply.
4. **Inspect actual records.** Assign neutral document IDs/pages. Check contents, not filenames or statements alone. Separate presence, readability, completeness, consistency, currency and compliance with a verified applicable requirement. Name uncertainty from missing pages or extraction; do not invent obscured contents or authenticity findings.
5. **Reconcile the stages.** Compare nominee identity, employer/entity, occupation/code/duties, stream, nominated period, contract/earnings, agreement/concessions, family coverage, nomination reference and event status. A pending nomination may differ from a grant-ready nomination. A new nomination must not be assumed capable of replacing the one linked to an existing visa application. Track cross-stage risks explicitly.
6. **Give the answer first.** After notices, state what the evidence supports, the main confirmed problem or labelled possible concern, and a concrete next action. Do not substitute a general checklist for a narrow answer. Broad reports lead with findings/actions, then two separate stage results and a shared handoff table. Display case-relevant branches, not research logs or every hypothetical exception.
7. **Final audit.** Every material finding has inspected evidence or a declared gap, requirement type, actor, stage/date, source/currentness and next action. No unexplained readiness score or approval probability. Unknown mandatory applicability/compliance blocks a whole-stage Ready finding, but not independently supported document-quality findings. State exactly what was checked and what was not.

## Status rules
- **Ready for the stated preparation check:** the named check is supported by inspected evidence; verified compliance additionally needs applicable authority. This is not eligibility, validity, work permission or approval.
- **Missing:** an applicable item is known absent. If only not supplied for review, say that; do not claim the user lacks it.
- **Needs correction:** a confirmed defect or inconsistency in supplied evidence; identify document/page, effect and correction.
- **Needs verification:** unknown trigger, unread pages, uncertain extraction, source/currency conflict or professional interpretation needed.
- **Not applicable:** available facts support an inapplicable trigger or established exception, never merely missing information.

A readable scan is not proof of authenticity or official acceptance. Readability may need correction while hidden contents need verification. Report nomination readiness, visa readiness and shared consistency separately; none proves the other. Do not calculate/certify family expiry for the unresolved special-family/age-23/TSS/passport-class branches identified in verified-updates.md; organise evidence and refer the precise interpretation instead. Do not determine individual interim work permission or assume a nomination approval resets condition-day counts.
