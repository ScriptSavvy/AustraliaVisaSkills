# Verified-source amendments and refresh gates

Read this before the topical references. It supplements the stored preparation knowledge; it does not replace its conditions, evidence alternatives or discretion. **Targeted checks: 21 October 2026.** This is not comprehensive current-law or legal/professional clearance. Every live case needs relevant-date applicability checking. A source's registration, compilation, effective and check dates are different.

## Source authority and refresh procedure
Applicable legislation and instruments control legal requirements. Department guidance describes administration/preparation and must be read with the law. Stored operational guidance can support preparation but does not alone prove current legal compliance. Applicant accounts are evidence leads, not legal authority. Examples and suggestions are not mandatory documents.

Before a material conclusion:
1. Identify nomination date, visa-application date, test date/evidence date, decision or event date as relevant. Do not use one date for everything.
2. Retrieve the official source body and applicable version. Read effective intervals, commencement, savings, amendments and agreement-specific rules. A `latest` URL can be unsuitable for an earlier date.
3. Record title, URL, provision/section, version/effective period, actual check date and the rule affected. Do not record a pending retrieval as verified.
4. Reconcile a conflict by authority, scope and dates only when those resolve it. Otherwise record both positions, missing evidence, effect and next step. Continue independent evidence-quality checks, but not a Ready compliance finding for the disputed requirement.
5. If browsing fails, say which conclusion remains unverified. Do not turn absent online corroboration into automatic deletion of useful stored preparation guidance, nor into confirmed law.

## U02 — English: application date, test date and evidence date are distinct

The [English instrument, LIN 25/082](https://www.legislation.gov.au/F2025L01066/latest) was retrieved as the latest/in-force instrument on 21 October 2026. The [operative text](https://www.legislation.gov.au/F2025L01066/asmade/2025-09-10/text/original/epub/OEBPS/document_1/document_1.html) commences on **13 September 2025** and applies to applications made on or after that date (sections 2 and 8). It controls the Specialist/Core streams, not automatic Labour Agreement equivalence.

### Section 6 route
For a non-exempt applicant, section 6 provides approved tests and component scores below. Section 6(2) expressly accommodates a component re-sit. It measures the test day's age against **the day the required score evidence is provided**, not a universal application-date anchor: no more than three years. Do not invent unlimited score pooling, re-sit time limits or test-provider acceptance; verify the precise test, sitting/re-sit record and applicable official requirements.

### Section 7 transitional route
A test **before 13 September 2025** can support an application on/after commencement through section 7 if the specified scores were achieved in a single attempt and the test day is in the three years immediately before the application. This is a separate alternative, not section 6's evidence-date/re-sit mechanism.

| Older specified test | Section 7 scores |
|---|---|
| IELTS | Overall at least 5.0 and each component at least 5.0 |
| OET | At least B in each component |
| TOEFL iBT | Overall at least 35; listening/reading at least 4 each; speaking/writing at least 14 each |
| PTE Academic | Overall and each component at least 36 |
| Cambridge English: Advanced | Overall and each component at least 154 |

For applications before 13 September 2025, retrieve the applicable earlier instrument/savings instead of using these tables indiscriminately. The stored TOEFL non-acceptance window of 26 July 2023–4 May 2024 needs the official test-date guidance before accepting a result in that interval; a transitional table is not blanket acceptance of every test format. Remote/at-home delivery, identity verification, retake rules and provider updates require a specific official check.

### Exemptions
Section 4 of the inspected instrument supports distinct alternatives:
- Citizenship **and a valid passport** of Canada, New Zealand, Republic of Ireland, United Kingdom or United States.
- At least five years qualifying full-time secondary/higher education with instruction in English. Higher education full-time is at least three subjects each semester/trimester; secondary study uses the country's standard contact hours. Collect institution, qualifications, transcripts, contact hours, teaching language and study periods as needed. Do not invent a consecutive-study requirement.
- A standard-sponsor nomination for work at a foreign diplomatic/consular mission or the Office of the Authorities of Taiwan in Australia.
- Required professional licence/registration/membership already granted, with English demonstrated at an equivalent or higher level for that grant. Future registration alone is not this exemption.
- Employment by an established overseas company, nomination by that company or its associated standard sponsor, **and** annual earnings at least AUD96,400. Salary alone is insufficient.

Refresh each exemption against the applicable version. The English earnings exemption, advertising-disclosure threshold, stream income thresholds and skills-assessment exemption are different tests even where amounts coincide.

## U03 — LMT: preserve the legal overlapping-advertisement route

[LIN 18/036](https://www.legislation.gov.au/F2018L01108/latest), retrieved as latest on 21 October 2026, exposed compilation 5 dated 7 December 2024. The [body](https://www.legislation.gov.au/F2018L01108/2024-12-07/2024-12-07/text/original/epub/OEBPS/document_1/document_1.html), sections 6–9, and the [Department LMT page](https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/visas/employing-and-sponsoring-someone/sponsoring-workers/nominating-a-position/labour-market-testing) were inspected.

For the standard route, check at least two qualifying, sponsor-commissioned/authorised English advertisements, required content, eligible media, the four-month nomination window, recruitment evidence and any redundancy-triggered restart. Section 8(5) requires applications/expressions of interest accepted for at least four weeks. Sections 8(6)–(7) explicitly permit a continuous period across overlapping advertisements and count an overlapping day only once. **Do not reject solely because every individual ad did not run for four weeks.** Stored stricter individual-ad expectations must not erase the statutory overlapping alternative. The Department page also contains both a four-week-ad expectation and overlapping-period language; explain any case-specific operational uncertainty rather than treating policy as an additional universal legal condition.

Preserve the number/content/media/evidence conditions while evaluating overlap. Do not add nonoverlapping short campaigns arithmetically to assert continuity. Use a deterministic date ledger, keep exact start/end dates and inclusivity assumptions, and verify any boundary case. Two valid advertisements and a continuous acceptance period are not proof the substantive labour-market test is met.

A select-position/occupation written submission under section 9 is **alternative evidence**, not a blanket LMT exemption. The inspected definition includes qualifying exceptional achievement, intra-company transfers, specified same-position restructure/earnings changes, high earners and specified medical/paramedic cases, with actual limits. ITO exemptions require the applicable trade determination and individual facts; do not assume citizenship or accreditation alone is sufficient. Labour Agreement LMT follows its actual agreement/authoritative arrangements, not automatic standard-route ITO relief.

## U06 — unresolved matters: record the effect, not a guessed answer

Keep applicable issues in the case report; do not show every untriggered research item:
- **Study placements versus unpaid-work exclusion:** stored industry flexibility coexists with a general voluntary/unpaid exclusion. Public guidance confirms placement possibilities but does not settle every unpaid arrangement. Keep paid/unpaid status, course, duties, skill level and supervision evidence. Do not automatically count or reject a disputed placement; qualified interpretation may be needed.
- **SAF corrections:** limited top-up language differs from changing locked turnover/period inputs requiring withdrawal/re-lodgement. Identify payment error versus changed inputs and obtain an authorised correction route. Do not recommend withdrawal without assessing the existing linked visa application.
- **Family and duration:** special family definitions, exact-age boundaries, existing secondary-holder continuity and duration exceptions must be checked against regulations 1.12, 482.312 and 482.511. Do not apply a generic attachment definition or universal age-23 expiry.
- **Nomination cessation:** visa refusal/withdrawal is not an instant universal cessation rule; nor is it proof the nomination lasts indefinitely. Check all earliest cessation events, approval age, final determination and withdrawal dates under 2.75 before proposing reuse.
- **Salary discretion/entity structure:** use the operative provision, not a suspected cross-reference error. No assumed waiver of a monetary floor or automatic acceptance/rejection of an owner/director/partnership arrangement.
- **Review and interim work:** historical review labels, mixed offshore bullet logic and employer-change summaries do not establish current standing, deadlines or work permission. Read actual notices, visa grant/conditions, occupation and event dates; organise an urgent qualified review where consequences are material.
- **Insurance separate entrants and health/character:** preserve declaration/evidence alternatives, but verify the actual separate-entrant mechanism and requested evidence. No automatic health waiver, police-clearance acceptance or authenticity conclusion.

For each unresolved finding record: question; both source positions with references; missing fact/authority; stage and practical effect; status Needs verification; exact evidence/clarification needed. Do not certify a whole stage Ready while its material mandatory issue is unresolved. Continue supported document-quality findings.

## U07 — bounded regulation reconciliation: qualifications to stored shorthand

The official bodies for [volume 1](https://www.legislation.gov.au/F1996B03551/2026-10-02/2026-10-02/text/original/epub/OEBPS/document_1/document_1.html), [volume 2](https://www.legislation.gov.au/F1996B03551/2026-10-02/2026-10-02/text/original/epub/OEBPS/document_2/document_2.html) and [volume 3](https://www.legislation.gov.au/F1996B03551/2026-10-02/2026-10-02/text/original/epub/OEBPS/document_3/document_3.html) were inspected for the specific provisions below on 21 October 2026. Proven textual coverage is the compiled law **as at 2 October 2026**. An exhaustive check of intervening commencements, modifications and savings through the case date was not completed. These are bounded source resolutions, not legal clearance for every current case.

### Salary, part-time roles and agreement ceilings
- Regulation 2.72(15) separates AMSR method, comparator monetary threshold, nominee earnings versus AMSR, nominee monetary threshold and market consistency. In the inspected text, comparator-threshold discretion is **2.72(16)(a)**, linked to (15)(d) with its cumulative conditions; nominee monetary-threshold discretion is **(16)(b)**, linked to (15)(f). Use these actual provisions, not a repeated/misdirected comparator cross-reference. Non-monetary earnings, monetary earnings and excluded payments under 2.57A must remain separate. Do not assume a discretionary disregard is granted.
- Full-time under (10)(b) has a reasonable-disregard power under (10A). Part-time remuneration disregard under (16)(aa) has an additional reasonable-in-circumstances gate. Do not automatically pro-rate a threshold or erase financial capacity under (17A). The regulation does not establish a universal hours conversion for this preparation calculation.
- Agreement ceiling under 2.72(19)(c) compares the person's made-and-approved nominations in the named visa categories against the agreement's **permitted yearly number**, not against an identical count of approvals or simply current employee headcount. Verify the actual agreement, annual period and occupation-specific limits; do not invent replacement credits.

### Nomination status and application timing
Item 1240(3)(f) supports the distinction between a nomination already approved/unceased or still awaiting the section 140GB decision at visa lodgement, with matching-stream/identity/reference and sponsor-bar requirements. Clause 482.212(1) at decision requires the **identified** nomination approved, sponsor approved at nomination approval and nomination approval unceased. These checks do not establish all application-validity criteria or a promise the Department will wait for a pending nomination. A different nomination is not automatically a replacement link.

Regulation 2.75(2) uses earliest cessation events. The ordinary 12-month rule is qualified where a linked application has not been finally determined at that time; final determination/withdrawal after 12 months has its own cessation event. Other events include written nomination withdrawal received, visa grant and specified sponsorship/agreement events. Refusal before 12 months is not a universal immediate cessation event, but neither refusal nor review guarantees indefinite survival. Sponsorship-renewal timing and the defined nomination end day have specific conditions; do not invent a universal three-month grace period. Verify final-determination/review meaning and actual dates before reuse or withdrawal guidance.

### Family definition: default and special existing-family route
The general rule in **1.12(2)** includes a partner; qualifying unmarried/non-engaged child/stepchild under 18; child/stepchild aged 18 to under 23 **who is dependent**; and age 23 or older with the incapacity-related dependency in **1.05A(1)(b)**. It also includes a dependent child of a qualifying child. Ordinary financial dependence alone is not the 23-plus incapacity limb. **Exactly 23 belongs to the 23-plus limb**, not an age gap.

The special **1.12(5)** existing-secondary-visa route has its own exact status/application predicates. Only where those circumstances are established does **482.312(2)** supply its special under-23 wording without the ordinary dependency limb. Do not generalise this to every SID family applicant, assume holding any old visa suffices, or infer permission to change primary applicant. The stored same-primary expectation and changed-family circumstances still need focused professional interpretation where material.

### Family grant duration is not a combined/separate shortcut
Read **482.511(1), table items 3–5**, rather than assuming the age cutoff concerns only separate entrants or that everyone joining an existing 457/482 holder is exempt. Item 4 has precise family-category predicates, incapacity exclusion and item-5 priority, with the earlier of primary stay end and the end of the day before the secondary applicant's 23rd birthday. Item 5 has specific primary Hong Kong/British National (Overseas) passport circumstances and, where relevant, a class instrument. Do not apply a universal non-partner cutoff, a blanket joining-holder exemption, or a universal partner exception to the literal special-route wording.

Unusual effects for a partner/descendant within 1.12(5), already-23 cases, a primary TSS visa outside the displayed item-3 list and applicable passport classes remain unresolved for individual outcomes. **Do not calculate or certify a family expiry date in those branches** without the applicable authority and qualified interpretation. Continue evidence organisation and distinguish family eligibility from duration. Primary Hong Kong/BNO five-year rules likewise need actual passport/class and grant-date facts; no promised period.

### Interim work and condition 8607
The inspected **8607(5)** is a limited departure from ordinary occupation/employer restrictions, not just days of unemployment: at most 180 consecutive and 365 cumulative days during the visa period of work not in accordance with (1)/(2). Do not count only unemployed days, declare all interim work prohibited or assume unrestricted work. Preserve licensing and other applicable conditions. Do not infer a nomination approval automatically resets cumulative days or resolves every identified-nomination issue; the precise approved-change and inclusive-day mechanics require further authority for individual work-permission advice.

Schedule 13 clause **14502(4)** preserves earlier condition text for specified pre-7 December 2024 visa/application cohorts. Do not apply SID wording automatically to all TSS/457 holders. Ask for the actual grant/conditions and dated employment history when material, and identify the specific work-rights/counting question for qualified review. This skill can organise that evidence but does not determine individual work permission.
