# Integrated nomination and visa workflow

## Two preparation tracks, one consistency review

Do not collapse nomination approval into visa approval or assume all stages occur strictly one after another. Sponsorship approval or an executed agreement is a separate upstream dependency; its full application/negotiation is outside this skill. Nomination and visa are substantively covered. The visa's lodgement link and decision-stage nomination gate are different. Apply relevant-date rules in [verified updates](verified-updates.md), then use [nomination](nomination.md) and [visa application](visa-application.md).

| Track | Main owner | Preparation output | Connection |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sponsorship/agreement dependency | Employer/sponsor | Inspect approval/agreement identity, status, scope and limits supplied | Do not confuse pending sponsor application with approval; do not assess a complete sponsorship application from these references |
| Nomination | Employer/sponsor | Role, occupation/stream/caveats, genuine/full-time or permitted part-time position, contract/entity, salary/AMSR/capacity, LMT or specific alternative/exemption, fees/SAF and agreement conditions | Identifies nominee, role, period and application reference used by visa stage |
| Visa | Primary applicant and included family | Identity, location/status, skills/experience/assessment, English/exemption, insurance, family, health/character/request evidence | Uses correct nomination and stream; sponsor certifications do not prove every applicant criterion |
| Shared consistency | Both, separately authorised | Matching table and dated event ledger | State each side's gap and who can supply/correct it; never contact either party automatically |

An adviser label identifies a review responsibility, not a claim the agent is licensed. The agent may organise evidence and explain sourced preparation rules; regulatory/professional obligations need appropriate review before personalised assistance.

## Minimal branching

Use known facts first. Ask one numbered material question only if necessary. For a broad request with no facts, determine whether the user wants nomination, visa or both; if already known, do not ask. Next identify the earliest unknown that changes the immediate task (for example stream or an actual request). Keep role/authority, stage, dates and evidence inventory internal. Do not request a complete personal or business history by default.

- **Pre-lodgement:** assess relevant preparation gates at the actual/proposed nomination and visa dates. Distinguish documents useful now from those required at lodgement or decision.
- **Already lodged:** use actual acknowledgement/reference/status; do not pretend a proposed correction can relink an existing application. Read actual Department requests and deadlines.
- **Nomination approved:** inspect approval details and cessation events; approval alone does not establish visa readiness or employer work permission.
- **Existing holder changes:** first identify current grant, nominated occupation/employer and proposed change. Nomination-only versus new visa, exempt occupations, interim work and agreement transitions need their own checks.
- **Family/subsequent entrant:** establish the specific route, primary status, relationship and sponsor inclusion/agreement; distinguish unborn/newborn mechanisms from adding another family member after lodgement.
- **Labour Agreement:** inspect the actual executed agreement, applicable schedule, occupation, ceilings, LMT, salary/skills/experience/English concessions and dates. An agreement possibility or negotiation is not an executed concession.

## Shared evidence matching matrix

For each relevant field record nomination evidence, visa evidence, match status, uncertainty and next action. Do not require both stages' documents when reviewing a question independent of the other stage.

| Field | Check |
|---|---|
| Nominee identity | Compare inspected passport/identity with nomination/visa records; record explainable name variants without alleging fraud |
| Sponsor and employing legal entity | Distinguish business/trading name, legal person, associated entity, employer and worksite; an unrelated worksite alone does not disprove direct employment |
| Stream and legal route | Match Core/Specialist/Agreement and any supported transitional route; do not silently substitute a different stream |
| Occupation/code/duties | Match exact classification/version and core tasks, applicant skills and position; title alone is not occupational proof |
| Earnings and period | Compare contract, nomination, monetary/non-monetary breakdown, hours and proposed period; different monetary tests need separate rows |
| LMT and recruitment | Track sponsor-authorised advertisements/submissions/exemption evidence and nomination-date relevance; applicant English exemptions are unrelated |
| Skills and licensing | Sponsor's role certification does not establish applicant competence; separate assessment-at-lodgement and suitability-at-decision, registration and work-start rules |
| Agreement concessions | Check exact agreement terms and whether each concession applies to nomination, applicant, both, or neither |
| Family coverage | Family eligibility and sponsor inclusion/written agreement are distinct gates; an omitted dependant is not automatically added later |
| Nomination reference and status | Confirm identified reference, pending/approved/withdrawn/refused/ceased events and sponsor status; status is not inferred from a filename |

## Event ledger

Record only material events and evidence: sponsor approval/expiry; agreement execution; LMT start/end/redundancy dates; nomination lodgement/approval/cessation; visa lodgement/request/decision; assessment commencement/result; English test/re-sit/evidence dates; current grant expiry; employer/occupation changes; family birth/status events. Use source-specific date anchors. Record date unknown rather than estimating. Calendar-month, year and day periods are not interchangeable; use deterministic tools and state boundary assumptions. Do not invent full-time hours or double-count concurrent experience/advertisements.

Before suggesting a nomination correction, withdrawal or new application, flag the effect on any already lodged visa. The skill does not execute these actions. Nomination refusal, withdrawal, cessation and review are not equivalent events. A new nomination cannot be assumed to replace the one linked to a pending visa; actual legal route needs verification.

## Outputs

For narrow questions, after notices give a direct answer, specific finding, practical action, material uncertainty and brief source note. Do not dump both stage checklists.

For broad reviews use the [report template](../templates/report.md):
1. Main supported findings and priority actions.
2. Separate nomination and visa preparation results, each limited to what was actually checked.
3. One shared matching table, not repeated findings in multiple places.
4. Evidence-dimension rows and case-relevant uncertainties with source/date and resolution action.

For an unrequested stage, record **Not assessed — not requested** as the scope note; do not assign a readiness status or use Not applicable as a substitute. Both stages can contain independent Ready document-quality findings while an overall readiness conclusion remains unavailable. Do not create a combined percentage score. A user can choose to prepare records without an eligibility conclusion.
