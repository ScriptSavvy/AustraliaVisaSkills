## Pick the result you need
You can review employer nomination, visa application or both. The agent should answer your actual question, not force a complete application questionnaire. It asks one necessary question at a time with a progress label, for example `Question 1 — total not yet known`. Follow-ups continue the numbering. You can say unsure or skip; that remains unresolved, not Not applicable. Ask explicitly if you prefer grouped intake.

The agent must display both notices above before guidance/intake in a new task, even for a narrow read-only question. It repeats them at the start of standalone reports/checklists/plans, not on every short same-task follow-up. Loading the skill alone is not displaying the notices.

## Example prompts
**Both stages:**
> Use skills-in-demand-482-preparation. Help me prepare the employer nomination and my visa application together. Keep the two stages and responsible people separate. Ask one question at a time.

**Employer nomination:**
> Use skills-in-demand-482-preparation. Review our redacted nomination evidence for the stated stream and date. Check role, contract, salary evidence and LMT. Tell us which items need verification; do not submit anything.

**Visa documents:**
> Use skills-in-demand-482-preparation. Review the documents I provide for my visa application and their match with my nomination. Do not infer document contents from filenames.

**Focused consistency question:**
> Use skills-in-demand-482-preparation. My inspected contract and nomination show different job titles. Explain whether the duties/code need checking before calling it an error. Do not start full intake.

**Official request:**
> Use skills-in-demand-482-preparation. Help me organise evidence for this redacted Department request. Start with the actual deadline uncertainty and requested evidence, not a general checklist. Do not contact the Department.

**Existing holder/change:**
> Use skills-in-demand-482-preparation. Organise the records needed to check a proposed employer or occupation change. Do not treat a lodged nomination as permission to work.

## Reading results
- **Ready for the stated preparation check:** supported only for that named check, not visa eligibility, validity or approval.
- **Missing:** an applicable item is known absent; not supplied for review is reported separately.
- **Needs correction:** a confirmed document defect or inconsistency.
- **Needs verification:** unclear facts, unread pages, source currency, conflict or professional interpretation.
- **Not applicable:** facts support that the requirement does not apply. Unknown is not enough.

Broad reports distinguish presence, readability, completeness, consistency, currency and verified compliance. Nomination, visa and shared consistency results remain separate. An unrequested stage is Not assessed — not requested, not a favourable readiness finding. The same issue should not be repeated in multiple tables.

## Privacy and limits
Start with redacted copies or summaries. Do not post passport/account identifiers, employer financial details or client records in public issues. Hosted agents may transmit supplied files to their provider; this skill requires no extra external processor and does not authorise one. Keep case reports outside the reusable folder. Never invent missing contracts, references, advertising, pay records or explanations.

The [verification reference](references/verified-updates.md) contains targeted source checks and unresolved matters. Changing rules must be refreshed for your dates. If browsing or OCR is unavailable, the agent should continue independent checks and identify what cannot be verified. Full sponsorship applications/agreement negotiation are separate dependencies. Refusal, review deadlines, complex legal interpretation and work permission require a focused qualified review, not a disclaimer alone.

The skill cannot certify family expiry dates in unresolved special-family, age-23, TSS or passport-class branches. It can organise the relevant records and the question for qualified review. It does not determine individual interim work permission or assume nomination approval resets condition-day counts.

The skill does not lodge, pay, withdraw, access accounts, send messages or represent you. Any such action needs separate explicit authorisation and may remain outside professional/legal scope.
