## Choose one stage or both
- **Both:** “Use skills-in-demand-482-preparation. Help me prepare the employer nomination and my visa application together. Keep the two stages and responsible people separate. Ask one question at a time.”
- **Nomination:** “Use skills-in-demand-482-preparation. Review our redacted nomination evidence for the stated stream and date. Check role, contract, salary evidence and LMT. Tell us which items need verification; do not submit anything.”
- **Visa:** “Use skills-in-demand-482-preparation. Review the documents I provide for my visa application and their match with my nomination. Do not infer document contents from filenames.”
- **Focused consistency check:** “Use skills-in-demand-482-preparation. My inspected contract and nomination show different job titles. Explain whether the duties/code need checking before calling it an error. Do not start full intake.”
- **Department request:** “Use skills-in-demand-482-preparation. Help me organise evidence for this redacted Department request. Start with the actual deadline uncertainty and requested evidence, not a general checklist. Do not contact the Department.”
- **Existing holder/change:** “Use skills-in-demand-482-preparation. Organise the records needed to check a proposed employer or occupation change. Do not treat a lodged nomination as permission to work.”

## Stage-specific results and limits
Nomination, visa and shared consistency results remain separate, with the responsible employer/sponsor or applicant identified. An unrequested stage is **Not assessed — not requested**, not a favourable readiness finding. The same issue should not be repeated in multiple tables.

The [verification reference](references/verified-updates.md) contains targeted source checks and unresolved matters. Changing rules must be refreshed for your dates. If browsing or OCR is unavailable, the agent should continue independent checks and identify what cannot be verified. Full sponsorship applications/agreement negotiation are separate dependencies. Refusal, review deadlines, complex legal interpretation and work permission require a focused qualified review, not a disclaimer alone.

The skill cannot certify family expiry dates in unresolved special-family, age-23, TSS or passport-class branches. It can organise the relevant records and the question for qualified review. It does not determine individual interim work permission or assume nomination approval resets condition-day counts. Never invent contracts, references, advertising, pay records or explanations; protect employer financial records as well as applicant records.
