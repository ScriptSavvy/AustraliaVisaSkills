## Main findings and priority actions
Answer the actual question in the first substantive sentence after notices. Briefly state the supported conclusion, responsible person and priority action; point to detailed issue IDs below rather than repeating their full evidence, uncertainty and actions. If blocked, name the exact missing fact or unresolved rule. Do not invent a problem if none is supported. This template is for broad reviews, not short same-task follow-ups.

## Material unresolved questions
For each applicable issue not already fully covered in a detailed row: exact question; competing statements or source positions/references; what is missing; effect on stage(s) and which conclusion is blocked; precise evidence/official clarification or qualified question needed. Before finalising, compare the report with visible earlier answers. Explicitly correct a contradicted earlier finding and identify the evidence, changed facts/stage or verified source version supporting that correction; otherwise leave the conflict visible. Do not repeat issues already fully covered or expose an internal research log. Omit this section when the detailed rows contain all material uncertainty.

## Next steps and limits
Order actions by urgency and dependency, with employer/sponsor and applicant actions separate. A report preparation list is not a demand for several immediate intake answers. Stop at the stated check: no eligibility, validity, work-permission, authenticity, acceptance or approval guarantee. No unexplained scores/probabilities. No submission, payment, withdrawal or communication has been authorised by this preparation report.
