# Changelog

## 0.2.2 — 2026-11-20

- Offer an optional redacted résumé or short typed summary when several work-history details are needed. Use it to organise a preliminary timeline and ask only about the next material gap. A résumé does not replace required evidence.

## 0.2.1 — 2026-11-20

- Fix download links and the optional update check to use the Employer-Sponsored Visas release. If you installed version 0.2.0, update manually from that release page.
- Clarify how to switch from the former standalone subclass 482 skill. Visa-preparation guidance is unchanged.

## 0.2.0 — 2026-11-20

- Unify subclass 482, 186 and 494 nomination and visa preparation in one employer-sponsored skill. Include a separate 187 transitional TRT workflow and a 494 → 191 evidence handoff, not a full 191 assessment.
- Add standard business sponsorship applications, renewal and accreditation; labour agreement requests and variations; ongoing sponsor obligations; and worker-protection and sponsor-charge checks.
- Add a 482 → 186 TRT evidence handoff that keeps visa holding and qualifying employment separate.
