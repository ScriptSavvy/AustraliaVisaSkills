# Subclass 187 RSMS — transitional TRT preparation

Use this branch for the **Regional Sponsored Migration Scheme (RSMS), subclass 187, Temporary Residence Transition (TRT)** only. New Direct Entry nominations and primary visa applications closed on **16 November 2019**. Do not reopen Direct Entry because legacy criteria are still printed. A regional job, a visa numbered 482 or access to an online form does not by itself establish this route. If the records identify subclass 186 instead, use the [ENS visa reference](ens-186-visa.md) for that route only; do not import its TRT tests into 187 or assume that every employer starts with a new sponsorship application.

## 1. Prove the cohort before costing or counting time

Apply the regulation 1.03 definition separately at the date the employer makes the 187 nomination (5.19(2)(aa), (2A)) and at the date the primary applicant makes the 187 visa application (Schedule 1 item 1114C(3)(aa), (3A)). These are historical facts tested at two different lodgements, **not** a requirement still to hold the particular historical visa at both lodgements.

| Cohort | Qualifying event | Evidence and missing-fact trigger |
|---|---|---|
| Transitional **457** worker | Held a subclass 457 Temporary Work (Skilled) visa at **any time on or after 18 April 2017**. This is actual holding, not a pending application alone; it need not have been held on that exact day. | Obtain grant and effective/cessation history in this worker’s name. If the file shows only a 457 application, establish whether and when it was granted and whether the worker held it at any time on or after the cutoff. A 457 that ceased before the cutoff is not enough. |
| Transitional **482** worker | **On 20 March 2019**, held a **Medium-term stream TSS** visa, **or** was an applicant for a Medium-term stream TSS visa on that day and that same application was subsequently granted. | Use the worker’s grant/stream and primary or secondary grant basis, or the acknowledgement and linked eventual grant. Prove holding on the day or pending status on the day (not just a date on a receipt), then grant of that same application. If a record just says “482”, establish TSS versus SID, stream, who satisfied the Medium-term criteria, effective dates and, for a pending case, the eventual result. Do not infer the cohort from a dependent grant linked to somebody else’s Medium-term occupation, a Short-term visa, a later first Medium-term application, or a still-pending, refused or withdrawn application. |

A person may have evidence for both cohorts; record the supported one(s). Do not confuse the **457 cohort** with the narrower **under-50 age exemption**, or the **482 cohort** with the work/earnings age exemption. If neither is established, report either a confirmed gate failure on known facts or **Needs verification** with the exact missing event, document and stage blocked. Continue independent document checks if useful; do not recommend lodging on an assumption.

## 2. Build the employer’s nomination evidence

### Use two separate ledgers ending at nomination lodgement

1. **Visa status at nomination** — under 5.19(5A)(a), identify which visa the worker actually holds when the employer makes the nomination: 457, TSS (this particular limb is not confined to the Medium-term stream), or a bridging visa granted on the basis of an application for 457, TSS, 186 or 187 **and** the last substantive visa held was 457 or TSS. Record grant basis and whether the bridging visa remains held at the required time, not just whether the underlying application is pending. If the underlying application was refused or withdrawn, get the cessation and review records before deciding whether the bridge is still held. **SID does not pass this nomination-status limb**; a SID-linked bridging visa or a bridge following a last substantive SID visa does not meet the stated bridging alternative. A saved pre-7 December 2024 TSS application may result in a TSS grant after that date: use the application and grant records, not the grant date or number alone.
2. **Visa holding duration** — under (d), clip proven 457 and TSS holding intervals to the three years immediately before nomination lodgement and test for at least two years in total. Do not count overlapping dates twice. Exclude SID and SID-linked bridges. Record gap dates independently. A bridge between eligible visas, linked to a further 457/TSS application that was subsequently granted, is a possible policy treatment, not a visa listed in (d); show a literal total and a separate conditional total. Do not count a bridge to the first 457/TSS or an unrelated visa under that treatment. A few days short is not a statutory grace period. If the outcome turns on bridging discretion/interpretation, near-shortfall or a claimed different-period instrument under 5.19(6), verify the version and basis before relying on it.
3. **Employment duration** — under (e), use the same three-year lookback but prove at least two years in the position for which the relevant visa(s) were granted, on a full-time basis in Australia, excluding unpaid leave. Use start/end dates, contracts/variations, duties, rosters or timesheets, payroll and leave ledger, and actual worksite/travel records. Separate paid leave from unpaid leave. For half-pay parental leave, keep evidence of the underlying full-time arrangement and the agreed paid leave; policy can treat supported paid leave at elapsed time, not half-duration. Verify the award/agreement and roster when hours differ from the ordinary full-time pattern. If travel was necessary and incidental to Australian employment, isolate the dates and duties and show who continued paying; do not count substantial overseas work by analogy. Report an undisputed total and any conditional time separately.
4. **Independent occupation alternative** — under (f), if the visa(s) counted under (d) were granted in relation to a specified occupation, test two years employed **in that occupation** within the same three-year window, excluding unpaid leave. Check the occupation instrument and the whole set of visas relied upon; do not switch to (f) merely because the latest grant was for listed work or assume only medical occupations qualify. Keep the (d) holding test and the other applicable nomination and visa gates. Do not import (e)’s same-position and full-time-in-Australia wording into (f); document duties, engagement and dates to prove the occupation actually worked. Separately, under 5.19(7), when the **nominated occupation** is specified under the same instrument, (h), (i) and (j) do not apply. That does not waive (k)–(o), licensing, the last-nominator test, the visa’s regional-position criterion or an age/English criterion.

Use separate rows for each change of employer entity, approved nomination, occupation, position, location, visa or leave status. Capture dates and source page/field for each interval; record inclusions and exclusions with reasons. Check the nominated ANZSCO occupation and four-digit unit group against the occupation of the most recently held 457/TSS ((b)), using the edition prescribed for **187** (27 June 2013 under the current instrument), and test real duties against the classification ((c)). A matching code does not prove it was the same position under (e). Compare duties, skill level, worksite, remuneration and reporting lines before and after changes. Ordinary progression, work for an associated entity or a restructure/takeover can preserve a position on the evidence; it does not automatically validate every employer change. Obtain ASIC/entity-chain or takeover records, historical approvals and the old/new job evidence. Unknown association, control or role continuity means that affected period is not verified.

### Identify the nominator and the actual position

| Employer check | Evidence to reconcile |
|---|---|
| Who can nominate; last approved nominator | Match exact legal entity, ABN/ACN and, if applicable, trustee in its legal capacity (not the trust or trading name), against the latest **approved** s 140GB nomination identifying the worker. Under 5.19(5A)(g) it must be the standard business sponsor or work-agreement party who last so nominated, and must actively and lawfully operate a business in Australia. Ask for the approval notice, not just payroll branding or a pending application. A changed entity needs an evidenced legal chain and a fresh check of (g), even if past work counts under (e). Verify live trading with registrations and recent BAS/accounts, payroll, trading invoices or premises evidence as needed. |
| Form, costs and integrity (5.19(1)–(4)) | Match form 1395 (Internet), employer, worker, precise position, occupation, 187 TRT and worksite. Save submission and payment acknowledgements; establish turnover on the required basis, SAF charge and any debt under Act s 140ZO, employment-law compliance, and adverse information about the entity/associates. The TRT nomination fee is nil for a regional position under 5.37(2); that does **not** waive the employer’s SAF charge. The employer certifies relevant s 245AR conduct; check who paid what and why. Mandatory licensing eligibility is tested at nomination lodgement under 5.19(4)(c). |
| Need, direct control and proposed work ((h)–(k)) | Unless 5.19(7) disapplies the named limbs, explain why the role fits ongoing business activity and why this position is genuinely needed; test who appoints, directs, pays and supervises, and who controls safety and termination. Use the contract, org chart, rosters, orders or business expansion evidence. Explain contrary staffing changes, outsourcing or shared control with related entities. Prove the position offers full-time employment for at least two years where (j) applies; separately check no express bar to extension under (k). A probation clause or a fixed term is not by itself decisive; check actual terms, comparators and workplace law. |
| Financial capacity and remuneration ((l)–(o)) | Show the business can employ for two years and pay at least the annual market salary rate (AMSR) each year: recent accounts, BAS, payroll and forecast, with an explanation for losses, new activity or financial distress. Separately apply **2.72C(15)–(16) as modified by 5.19(5A)(m)**, not the SID or ENS thresholds. First check the instrument’s high-earnings trigger. Where the salary tests apply, document the AMSR (same workplace/location and equivalent Australian worker, or the applicable industrial/market evidence if no comparator); compare proposed earnings with AMSR, and compare the monetary components of **both** AMSR and proposed earnings with the applicable **TSMIT**. For nominations from 1 July 2026 the TSMIT is specified in regulation 1.03; use the historical instrument for earlier cases and check any subsequent indexation. Separate guaranteed earnings, agreed non-cash benefits, superannuation, reimbursements and variable payments. The two threshold disregards are separate and discretionary under 2.72C(16); neither waives capacity. Check unfavourable non-pay terms separately under (n) and give the material required under (o); flag any discrepancy between offer, payroll and workplace instrument. |

**Regional position check:** record the actual place(s) where the offered work will be done, work pattern, start date, address and postcode, rather than a head office or the worker’s home by default. Map the offered position to 5.19(16) and the applicable postcode instrument for the nomination record/fee and again to **187.223(4)** for visa grant. For multiple sites, relocation, remote work or a changed location, show contracts, rosters and site allocation and resolve which location the actual nominated position relates to. Keep past worksite rows too: Home Affairs describes the usual path as work for the employer in regional Australia, but (e) expressly tests full-time work **in Australia** and the same position; the visa tests a **regional offered position**. A site move can still matter to continuity. Regional work is an express separate test for the regional medical age exemption below. Paying a nonregional nomination fee cannot cure a nonregional visa position.

## 3. Hand the nomination to the visa applicant without mixing clocks

Pass the lodged nomination reference, exact employer entity, named worker, stream, occupation and duties, worksite/postcode, contract version, lodgement date and any approval/withdrawal notice. The visa declaration must point to that existing nomination. A visa can be prepared and, if all validity gates are met, made while that nomination is pending; approval is needed by visa decision, not invariably before visa lodgement. If approved first, the visa application must be made **no more than six months after approval** under 187.223(6). This is a Schedule 2 grant criterion, not the Schedule 1 cohort gate. Use the approval date, not when someone read the notice; use the actual visa lodgement timestamp (regulation 2.10C for Internet applications) and verify boundary dates urgently. Do not substitute a different nominee, position, nomination or stream. For a wrong link, a withdrawn or refused nomination, or a material offer change, obtain both files and notices; do not promise relinking, refund or a replacement application. Check validity, status, ageing, timing, review rights and potential refund before any withdrawal. Prior approval does not establish that the position is still available.

## 4. Prepare the applicant’s visa file in two passes

**Schedule 1 — Class RN, item 1114C.** Verify the primary transitional gate on visa lodgement, form 1408 (Internet), first instalment (including any combined applicant charges; check the current Pacific-regional passport provision), nominated-position declaration and truthful s 245AS declaration covering the primary and combined application. Applicants may be inside or outside Australia, not in immigration clearance. An applicant in Australia ordinarily needs a substantive visa or BVA/BVB/BVC under this item; separately check applicable statutory bars, including s 48 and no-further-stay conditions or actual waiver where relevant. Get the visa/bridge in effect, its conditions, prior refusal/cancellation notices and arrival history as relevant. **This onshore visa-validity test is not 5.19(5A)(a).** For example, holding SID at visa lodgement is not excluded by the substantive-visa limb of item 1114C alone; it does not cure a nomination that fails 5.19(5A)(a). Neither an uploaded draft nor a payment alone proves valid lodgement.

**Schedule 2 — assessment for grant.** Verify at decision, retaining earlier fact dates expressly specified by the clauses:

- **At visa lodgement:** 187.211 mandatory state/territory licence, registration or professional membership (held or eligible); 187.221 age; 187.222 competent English or the specified exemption. Get the actual regulator rule for the occupation, location and tasks, and evidence effective at lodgement. A later-issued document may prove historical eligibility; obtaining eligibility later is not the same. Cross-check separately against the employer’s earlier nomination licensing snapshot. Do not assume restricted/supervised credentials qualify; verify what the person could lawfully do.
- **At decision:** same declared 187 TRT position and named applicant; nomination approved and not withdrawn; regional and still available; employment matches the nomination; no relevant adverse information about the nominator/associates or a lawful basis for disregarding it (187.212, 187.223). Refresh dated employer confirmation where processing is lengthy or the offer, trading status, duties or site changed. Record and notify relevant changes; respond to official requests within the stated deadline. A skills demonstration may be required in the specified manner under 187.225; it is not a universal up-front skills assessment. Prepare qualifications, licence, references and duty evidence in case it is requested.
- **Integrity, health and character:** match each person and the applicable PIC/SRC under 187.212A–.214, .224 and .312A–.315, including non-applicants where specified. Screen payment-for-visa conduct in the last three years separately from the lodgement declarations; do not promise disregard. For TRT, track PIC 4007 for the primary applicant and relevant family applicants, and for relevant non-applicants unless requiring an assessment would be unreasonable under the applicable clause. A possible health waiver under PIC 4007 is not automatic. Record police/character and identity evidence, prior removals or exclusions, consent/custody evidence for minors, and do not equate an absent check with clearance. Check the exact clause and person rather than treating every failure as applying to the whole family.

### Age and English alternatives are independent

**Age at visa lodgement:** under 45, or establish a specified class under 187.221(b) and the applicable age instrument. Review only plausible alternatives:

| Alternative | What makes the claim supportable |
|---|---|
| Academic / science | For academic: Australian university as nominator, actual academic level A–E and University Lecturer (242111) or Faculty Head (134411). An affiliated provider or non-academic position is not enough. For science: actual scientist, researcher or technical specialist, ANZSCO skill level 1 or 2, and nomination by a Commonwealth/state/territory science agency or Australian university. Obtain employer status, level/classification and duties, not a title alone. |
| Regional medical practitioner | Prove the nominated position is in a designated regional area, and, in the three years before **visa** lodgement, prove at least two years employed as a medical practitioner at places designated at the relevant times (periods can be non-consecutive) and the qualifying two-year visa holding limb. Record the clinical roles, actual postcode and dates; apply historical postcode deeming where relevant. For the instrument version applying to both nomination and visa applications on or after 7 December 2024, test 457/TSS/SID and the instrument’s specified bridging alternative in this **age** ledger. Do not move that definition into nomination eligibility. For saved versions, check their narrower visa-holding rules. |
| 457/482 high-earnings worker | Under the version applicable when **both** applications were made on or after 7 December 2024, prove at least two years in the three years before **visa** lodgement in position(s) related to approved s 140GB nominations, each of those years at or above the employee Fair Work High Income Threshold applying at the **end of that year**, plus at least two years holding 457/TSS/SID or the instrument’s prescribed bridging visas in the same lookback. For the bridge, check both last substantive visa and grant basis as specified in the instrument. Earlier versions can require the nominating employer and nominated occupation; do not use the expanded version for an earlier nomination. Build two year-by-year pay reconciliations with approved nominations, contracts, payslips, income statements and agreed non-cash values. Exclude indeterminate payments, expense reimbursements and compulsory employer super from the earnings analysis. Where leave or continuity is disputed, show both totals and verify the applicable treatment; do not borrow the nomination work result or substitute one tax-year total, an average or a salary offer. |
| Transitional 457 worker under 50 | Prove the worker had not turned 50 at visa lodgement and **on 18 April 2017** held a 457 or was an applicant for a 457 subsequently granted. Get the dated status/grant chain and date of birth. Holding a 457 for the first time after 18 April 2017 may establish the general cohort but does not by itself establish this exemption. |

For the two work-based age classes under the current definitions, the bridge must have been granted on the basis of an application for 457, TSS, SID, 186 or 187, with 457, TSS or SID as the last substantive visa held. Record the actual grant basis and qualifying intervals; a bridge alone does not prove employment or earnings.

**Competent English** is a primary grant criterion, not something a second charge can buy out. Verify qualifying passport/citizenship or specified result for all required test components taken within the three years before visa lodgement; check permitted test format, date and any saved pre-7 August 2025 evidence under the applicable instrument. Alternatively, prove that by visa lodgement the person had completed at least five years of full-time secondary/higher education taught in English for the 187 exemption. Build a dated transcript and teaching-language record from each institution, including load/mode and gaps; secondary and higher study can be combined. For tertiary or vocational study verify the provider, recognised qualification level, course load and actual instruction language rather than counting any course offered in English. An English-test preparation course is not proof of the required schooling. **Functional English** is a separate second-instalment check: for a person aged 18 or older at application assessed as not having it, check the applicable primary or secondary amount before grant, unless a nil category applies (including qualifying minister-of-religion/family cases or 187.311(3)–(5)). Verify prescribed functional evidence and its own timing; do not reuse the competent-test timetable.

## 5. Work each family member’s branch separately

- **Ordinary combined application:** list accompanying and non-accompanying family; prove the applicable family-unit link, combined application and, under 187.311(2)/187.312, primary grant and inclusion in the approved nomination (check the record; seek written confirmation from the nominator for an omission). Check ordinary family unit rules in regulation 1.12(2) and check the separate 1.12(5) Class RN item 6 possibility for a person included in the new application who at that time holds the listed earlier visa granted on a family basis (457, TSS or **SID** in the current table). Collect grant relationship and current status, age, dependency and custody/consent evidence as relevant. The family rule does not make SID acceptable for the primary nominee under 5.19(5A)(a).
- **After lodgement, before decision:** do not advise a standalone 187 subsequent-entry application as the ordinary route. Regulation 2.08 can deem a newborn to have applied in combination; obtain birth/parentage evidence and notify safely. If born in Australia, separately verify citizenship under Citizenship Act s 12 based on either parent’s status **at birth**, and any deemed visa under Migration Act s 78; a pending 187 is not a grant. For a spouse/de facto partner or dependent child added under 2.08A, verify a qualifying written request received before decision, the relationship claim, applicable charge and whereabouts; record the later of receipt/payment as the deemed lodgement and check remaining statutory bars and secondary criteria. Do not presume that adding a person to one file updates the other or that a pending request suffices.
- **Relationship cessation/family violence:** use a safe contact method and a separate confidential record; do not expose material to an employer, alleged perpetrator, shared account or unsafe authorised recipient. Check Division 1.5 and 187.311(3) and (4): spouse/de facto at application, relationship ceased, violence by the primary applicant against the applicant or a specified family member/dependent child, and in Australia at application or entry after it. Subclause (3) requires the primary 187 grant; (4) requires a primary refusal for reasons including family violence, not just any refusal and not necessarily the same incident. Under (5) a qualifying member of the secondary applicant’s family can rely on a combined application with the appropriate applicant and the secondary applicant’s 187 grant. Ordinary nomination inclusion under 187.312 is not the test for these alternatives. Check the applicable transition, remaining PIC/SRC and safety process; do not adjudicate a violence claim or promise a grant.

## 6. Close the review with dated actions

Give separate findings for **cohort**, **nomination requirements/status**, **visa validity**, **visa grant criteria**, and **each family branch**. State what the records show, what is inconsistent, the exact fact or law still needed, who can obtain it, and what it blocks. If both applications already exist, verify the earliest notice/deadline, recipient and current review status before discussing withdrawal or review. Where relevant, check the claimed nomination or first-instalment refund against 5.37A or 2.12F(3B) on the specific ground, dates and sequence; neither is an automatic consequence of withdrawing. A saved pre-16 November 2019 file needs its own in-force law and Schedule 13 clause 8101 (including linked visa lodgement, stream/cohort and nomination decision or deemed withdrawal); do not apply the new branch retrospectively. For older TRT files, check clause 12302 for the 25 November 2023 changes to older 5.19 nominations (including qualifying not-finally-determined cases lodged on or after 18 March 2018) and the relevant dated instruments for the former COVID reduced-work, unpaid-leave or age rules. Use 5.19(5A) as introduced for nominations made on or after 7 December 2024, but check the separate date tests for the visa age instrument. For family violence check clauses 14101 and 14701 for changes from 15 October and 17 December 2024 respectively, including pending cases; then check later pay, charges and English changes. Do not apply expired or ENS-only concessions as current 187 rules.

Grant is possible inside or outside Australia but not in immigration clearance (187.411); check offshore first-entry requirement and any secondary condition 8515 in the actual grant. Keep the **post-grant** six-month work commencement clock separate from the approval-to-visa-lodgement clock: under regulation 2.50AA the former runs from grant if in Australia at grant, or first entry as holder if outside at grant. Record start date and evidence of genuine efforts if commencement is delayed or employment ends within two years of starting; section 137Q provides a cancellation power, not automatic cancellation.
