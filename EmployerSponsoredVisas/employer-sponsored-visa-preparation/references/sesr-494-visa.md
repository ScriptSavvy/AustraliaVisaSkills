# Subclass 494 — visa application preparation

Use for primary Employer Sponsored (ES) and Labour Agreement (LA) applicants and family applying together or later, from initial preparation to grant and holder handoff. The [494 nomination reference](sesr-494-nomination.md) covers the employer's nomination. A nomination, visa application, visa decision and continuing authority to work are four separate checks. This is preparation support, not authority to lodge, travel or start work. Do not apply SID/482 criteria or presume that a 494 holder qualifies for any permanent visa.

## Applicability and date record

Capture separately for each person: role (primary, combined secondary, later secondary or non-applicant family), date of birth, location and immigration clearance at application/planned grant, visa held and conditions, immigration decisions/bars, nomination identifiers, employer and worksite, dates of nomination made/approval/cessation and visa application/decision, relevant assessment, English and employment dates, and family relationships. For LA obtain the actual signed agreement and variations. If reviewing a grant or transfer, add visa grant/first entry and work start/stop dates, later nomination dates, and each person's grant conditions. Verify date-specific law and transitions when facts span a change, including different transitions for nomination and visa. Unknown dates or missing instruments leave only affected conclusions **Needs verification**; don't infer applicability or use later evidence to change an earlier fact.

## Schedule 1 — Class PE validity at application (item 1242)

- **Form / place / method:** use 494V (Internet) under LIN 19/211 and reg 2.07(5), subject to the governing version. Authorised alternative form/method is narrow: verify the operative instrument, case-specific departmental authorisation, current approved form, sender, place, deadline and reference details. Use official technical support for a blocked online application. Don't assume a separate family application or IT issue itself authorises email filing; under the checked instrument an authorised systems problem and the prescribed email and same-day ACT time limit apply. A system receipt does not cure invalidity.
- **Location and status (all applicants):** inside or outside Australia, not in immigration clearance. Each applicant in Australia must hold a substantive visa or BVA 010, BVB 020 or BVC 030 at filing. Check actual status/VEVO and dates including travel, cancellation and refusal; BVE or no visa is not an alternative. Under the checked reg 2.12(u), Class PE is prescribed for s 48; do not treat a prior onshore refusal/cancellation as a blanket s 48 bar on this class, or the prescription as curing item 1242(3)(c) or any separate bar. Flag 8503/8534 or another no-further-stay condition, s 46(1A) and any applicable waiver. Reg 2.05 contains special 8503/8534 waiver routes for genuine 494 intention (for 8534 verify course completion); read reg 2.07AG for limited application effect and timing. A waiver request, screen acceptance or implied automation is not proof of a waiver, validity or a right to stay; check documentary result and pending deadlines. Bridging eligibility and activation are separate (offshore filing while holding a substantive visa may warrant a check); obtain actual grant and travel/work conditions. Don't assume a BVA is a travel visa, a bridging visa has taken effect, or every unrelated bridging grant is condition-free.
- **Charge:** confirm applicable first instalment at filing (base plus combined applicant age components, any applicable subsequent temporary/non-internet charges); standalone subsequent entrants normally incur a new base charge. Check dated item 1242(2) including any valid Pacific-regional passport/category rates and related family branch; don't use generic 482 pricing or the date of an old quote. Check the second instalment independently before grant below. Record lodgement and payment confirmations separately.
- **Nomination (primary, either stream):** identify the specific nomination in which the person is the nominee, match the stream and occupation, and check at filing that the nomination is undecided or approved and not ceased, and the nominator is not barred under Act s 140M. A pending nomination can support validity, not grant; a draft or TRN for a different nominee/stream is not enough.
- **Payment declaration (primary, both streams):** declare **whether or not** the primary or any combined applicant engaged in s 245AS(1) conduct in relation to the primary/combined application. Record actual offers or provision of benefits, dates, demands and recipient, whether or not the sponsorship event occurred. A truthful positive declaration is not an automatic validity failure or a waiver of the three-year Schedule 2 conduct criterion. Do not confuse the visa declaration with the sponsor's s 245AR certification.
- **Skills declaration (ES primary only, item 1242(5)–(6), LIN 19/211 ss 4, 6):** accurately declare the relevant authority already assessed the nominated occupation as suitable and **not** for subclass 485, and provide reference/evidence; or establish the exact academic or 444/461 circumstance that disapplies the declaration. Age exemptions are not skills declaration exemptions. Validate this independently from cl 494.224, including for the previous-visa route: a prior visa by itself is not a declaration or an exemption; reconcile authority, exact occupation and original result before declaring. A booking, pending assessment, late assessment, different occupation, unsupported exemption or erroneous declaration is not a safe filing route; distinguish requirement to make the declaration from truth/evidence and Schedule 2 eligibility. Restrict borderline declaration/late evidence cases; don't promise a post-filing cure or automatically label every skills evidence issue invalid.
- **Family filing:** combined under 1242(3)(d) if claiming family-unit relationship and filed together; for a dependent child added before decision check reg 2.08B, applicable procedure and additional charge (not a new base charge if valid addition). A child born after application and before decision follows reg 2.08 deemed combined process where applicable; notify the Department with birth/parentage evidence, normally no new charge. A non-citizen child born in Australia to a visa holder can have a visa by operation of Act s 78; first establish citizenship at birth, place of birth, parents' actual visas and birth timing, then confirm Department records. Do not add a partner or other family member to a filed application by uploading evidence alone: resolve a separate secondary application as appropriate, with its own status and validity gates.

## Schedule 2 — common primary decision gates (both streams)

Assess at decision, with earlier facts tested at the date expressly required. **494.213:** the very nomination identified in the lodged visa application must be approved under s 140GB, sponsor was approved at nomination approval, and approval must not have ceased under 2.75B. If refused, withdrawn or ceased, do not switch TRN: obtain the notices, relevant dates, any withdrawal election and request/reply deadline. Check whether the Department has given effect to a withdrawal election, whether the case remains pending or faces refusal, and whether a fresh visa filing with a new nomination is valid. Preserve any opportunity to respond; do not assume s 57 notice is always owed or unnecessary. Assess both position genuineness and the person's intention to perform the actual role; corroborate qualifications, duties, relocation/roster and employer/applicant accounts. Approval is evidence, not irrebuttable proof if facts change.

**494.214–215:** no adverse information known about nominator or associated persons unless reasonably disregarded; reassess new information even after nomination approval. Separately screen the primary for conduct within the previous three years under ss 245AR/AS/AT/AU (asking, receiving, offering or providing a benefit for sponsorship-related events, including relevant third-party conduct). A decision to disregard is not automatic. Keep contemporaneous demands, payments, communications and disclosure/corrections; prioritise protection and deadlines if coercion or exploitation is raised. Also apply the PIC/SRC checks below.

## Employer Sponsored — primary decision gates

### Employment and age

**494.222:** unless an applicable 2.72C(14) instrument occupation exception, the applicant is employed in the nominated occupation in the sponsor's business or a real associated entity. Verify who hires/dismisses, assigns and controls work, supervises, provides equipment/site, pays wages/PAYG/super and is responsible for leave/WHS/insurance. A short assignment on an unrelated client's site may still be employment in the sponsor's business where the sponsor retains real control (and regional location rules are satisfied); client-directed labour hire, sham contractor arrangements or group branding alone don't establish it. Occupational exceptions have a specific scope; don't carry a similar-sounding 482 exception across.

**494.223:** under 45 on application day or an applicable dated age exemption below. Verify passport and intended filing date; a later decision birthday is not the test.

### Skills assessment (494.224) — separate from the Schedule 1 declaration

1. **Recent assessment:** suitable from the relevant assessing authority for the exact six-digit nominated occupation, completed by filing and not for 485. If the assessment specifies a validity period ending no more than three years after assessment, it must not have ended; otherwise no more than three years may have passed at filing. Document operative assessment date and validity, including competing dates or replacements. An assessment done after filing does not satisfy the recent route; evidence supplied later may demonstrate a fact that already existed. Check LIN 19/260 applicable authority and authority's suitable migration program (for trades, relevant TRA migration program or approved OSAP pathway where required, not a temporary graduate result). A same four-digit unit group is not equivalent; in a genuine processing error involving the same authority, seek corrected evidence or dated confirmation relating to the exact nominated occupation and obtain confirmation of the Department's treatment before relying on it, not a substitute or retrospectively created result.
2. **Specific prior-visa assessment route:** at application held 457, 482 TSS or 482 SID where covered by the applicable law, for the **same** occupation, and had to demonstrate skills under the specified prior clause for that grant (former 457.223(2)(d)/(4)(e), former 482.212(4), or 482.221(3)/482.231(3) as applicable), which included a suitable non-485 assessment by the specified 494 authority. Obtain the previous grant, nomination and application/assessment record; merely holding a 457/482 or submitting a voluntary assessment is not enough. Do not apply the recent route's three-year cut-off to this distinct route or count an earlier visa that was not held at filing. Check any SID transitional question against dated regulations, never import SID eligibility.
3. **Exemption:** LIN 19/216 s 8(2) identifies academic and 444/461 circumstances. Confirm the separate Schedule 1 instrument too.

For either assessment route, where based on a qualification obtained in Australia while holding a student visa, prove the qualification resulted from study in a registered course (transcript, course registration, credit/RPL/RCC basis and visa dates); an RPL/RCC credential alone is not proof of such study. Check registration/licensing separately for the role and 8608. Where full professional registration is from/recognised by the same specified assessing authority, obtain evidence it is accepted as a suitable assessment (e.g. relevant Medical Board/Ahpra registration subject to exact occupation/authority); occasional/limited registration or a licence from a different authority (e.g. nursing/dentistry where the relevant authority is separate) cannot be assumed to substitute. Document the precise authority basis and dates; don't use missing records as a waiver.

### Employment experience and competent English

**494.225:** at least three years of full-time work at the nominated occupation's skill level by application, unless the applicable academic/444–461 employment exemption. Build a dated ledger with dates, duties, analogous occupation and responsibilities, paid hours, contracts/references, tax/pay records and absences; exclude unemployment, unrelated/lower-skilled employment, unpaid leave, volunteering and non-full-time casual work, and avoid double counting overlaps. Periods can be non-continuous and need not immediately precede filing. Typical full-time is 38 hours; investigate 32–45 hours under applicable award/agreement and NES or unusual industry patterns. Where relying on part-time equivalence or closely related work, document full-time equivalence, recency, comparable duties and same skill/responsibility, and flag for case-specific acceptance (job title alone fails). Formal medical internships/clinical experience, relevant research higher-degree work, formal study placements/Professional Year component, performing arts and niche transferable work can warrant examination; prove industry-specific arrangement, duties and relevant skill level, not study dates alone. Do not treat these as automatic credited years.

**494.226 / 1.15C:** competent English at filing (or actual specified circumstances; do not assume an ES exemption). Evidence is a qualifying valid passport satisfying the applicable citizenship/passport rule or a qualifying test and minimum scores in the correct dated instrument, ordinarily within the preceding three years. Record test type, date, test mode, scores in each component and result authentication. Check LIN 25/016 and saved rules for tests before **7 August 2025**, any allowed retake and test-specific expiry; don't convert an old scale or reuse functional English rules. Paying a second instalment cannot cure this grant criterion.

### ES exemption evidence — use the versions applicable to both dates

For current-form LIN 19/216, **age only**: regional doctor, science, 457/482 worker; **age, skills and employment**: academic and 444/461. Check the specific criterion and separate declaration, never give a general exemption. LIN 19/216 s 13 changes the definitions for visas **and associated nominations both made on or after 7 December 2024**. The 25 November 2023 compilation already uses two years in the three-year window for the relevant regional-medical and 457/482 tenure branches, but limits those qualifying visas to 457/TSS; its 457/482 worker employment branch requires the nominating employer and the nominated occupation. For mixed/older dates, select the applicable saved text before relying on either set of rules, and do not default to a three-year earnings test.

| Circumstance | Proof and limits |
|---|---|
| Academic | An Australian university (not an affiliated college/TAFE), Level A–E academic appointment and university lecturer 242111 or faculty head 134411. Obtain university and classification documents and research/teaching duties. |
| Regional medical practitioner | Nominated regional position, qualifying minor group 253 work, at least two years of medical work in area(s) designated at time worked, and at least two years of qualifying visa periods within the three years immediately before application in the amended instrument. Verify worksite/period, visa histories (457/TSS/SID; only defined bridging cases, including conditions on last substantive visa and linked application) and any rule for pre-16 November 2019 areas. Separate cumulative work from visa tenure; locum/multi-site evidence and actual addresses matter. |
| Science | Researcher, scientist or technical specialist in a skill level 1 or 2 nominated role and nominated by government science agency (Commonwealth/state/territory) or Australian university. Show institutional identity and scientific duties, research outputs, publications, awards or professional endorsement where relevant. A routine corporate job in a science organisation, local government or subcontractor alone is not this exemption. |
| 444/461 | Almost all of the three years immediately before filing held qualifying 444/461 status, ≥2 years working in the same nominated occupation for the nominating employer in that window; periods may be non-consecutive. Exclude unpaid leave, unemployment and other occupations/employers; verify paid-entitlement leave, part-time equivalence and visa gaps, rather than counting bridging visas as 444/461. For overseas training/work record sponsor role, full pay and training costs or duties and continuous employment; accept no borderline claim without verifying actual coverage. Support with grant/entry history, payslips/tax, employer statement and leave/travel records. |
| 457/482 worker | In the amended instrument, at least two qualifying employment years within the three-year pre-filing period in positions related to approved s 140GB nominations, each year's earnings at/above Fair Work High Income Threshold applying at that year's end, and at least two years of defined visas (457/TSS/SID or specifically qualifying bridging status) in that three-year period. Reconcile actual periods, nomination approvals, occupations, employers, payroll and dated threshold for each year, not fiscal-year summaries, today's rate, TSMIT or a current annual salary. Identify guaranteed earnings under 2.57A, not statutory super, reimbursements or contingencies. If split, leave, associated/successor employer or older overseas-to-Australian employment is material, retain the actual entity, contractual continuity, periods and full payroll and resolve against the applicable older/newer definition; don't import old same-employer rules or assume all periods can be aggregated. |

## Labour Agreement — primary decision gates

Apply **494.231** health route below. For **494.232–235**, obtain the executed occupation/subclass schedules and signed variations, not a DAMA endorsement or sponsor summary. The amended agreement requirements took effect **14 December 2024** and apply to new and then-not-finally-determined cases; an agreement entered earlier and still in effect just before commencement can continue under reg 2.75C (F2024L01653, Sch 13 item 14902). Verify savings and the signed terms for the actual case. Separately record agreement occupation and 494 authority; applicant's age under 45 at filing or below the agreement's occupation/subclass age cap; the language tests and demonstration method (if any) specified in the agreement; required skills, qualifications and employment background (if any); and ≥3 years of work in the occupation or related field, or the period (if any) specified in the agreement for occupation and visa. Check the specified method proving necessary skills; if it requires suitable assessment, use the relevant assessing authority or (if none) the specified agreement body, with the shorter stated validity up to three years / otherwise three-year rule in 494.235(4), tested at decision under the checked text. Do not apply the ES Schedule 1 declaration or recent-assessment-at-filing rule to LA by analogy; check any additional agreement timing. Do not assume a free-standing English waiver, experience discretion, generic concessions or equivalence to ES; the operative conditions and applicable law control. If agreement contents/transition are missing, give only the independently supported preparation checks and identify each blocked conclusion. Keep age, English, skills/experience, nomination cap and charge evidence separate.

## Family, health, character, identity and return criteria

- **Secondary criteria (494.311–316):** family member of the person who satisfied primary criteria and holds 494 at decision, including for a subsequent entrant. Apply reg 1.12 and dependency/partner definitions: spouse/de facto, eligible unpartnered/unengaged child or stepchild under 18, dependent adult child under 23, older child dependent due to qualifying incapacity, or qualifying dependent child of such a child. Obtain civil/relationship and financial dependency evidence and age/relationship timeline; test custody and lawful relocation consent for a child. For de facto check 2.03A's usual 12-month pre-filing term and exceptions (e.g. qualifying registration or compassionate/compelling circumstances as applicable); don't assume a certificate alone proves the relationship. For each person verify listing on the nomination identified in the primary application, or written consent to secondary sponsorship from the current/former approved work sponsor with the most recent approved nomination (494.314). Check adverse information about the original nominator/associated persons under 494.315 separately; and each secondary's own previous-three-year ss 245AR/AS/AT/AU conduct under 494.316. Document changes of sponsor, newborn, relationship breakdown or aging out, and restrict any special family continuation or protection conclusion until dated law and records are checked.
- **Common PIC/SRC:** under 494.211–212 and .312–313 check PICs 4001, 4002, 4003, **4003B**, 4004, 4010, 4020, 4021 for primary and secondary; 4019 where 16+ at filing. For the primary's **applicant** family, 494.211(3)–(5) add family-level checks for the specified PICs (including 4015/4016 for under-18 applicants); for **non-applicant** family, 494.211(6) covers 4001/4002/4003/4003B/4004. Secondary minors have 4017/4018 as well. SRCs 5001, 5002 and 5010 apply to primary, applicant family and secondary criteria, not presumed away by a new sponsor. Check character/security and prior visa history; settlement where relevant, values statement, passport and identity, integrity/false or misleading evidence, and custody/best interests. Identify affected people and statutory cross-effects before suggesting withdrawal; removing someone from a combined application does not resolve criteria that extend to non-applicant family.
- **Health:** primary ES and its family under 494.221 use PIC **4007** (possible waiver by Department if the statutory conditions are met, not presumed); LA under 494.231 uses **4005**, not the ES waiver route. Secondary .312(4)–(5) follows the primary's stream. Primary grant criteria extend to applicant and non-applicant family; the latter may be excepted if it is unreasonable to require assessment, not simply because they will not travel. Keep health requests and deadlines for each affected person; get the official health findings before characterising a refusal or waiver question.
- **Identity/evidence:** use matching passport/identity and name changes for all family members, appropriately translated and verifiable records. Prepare biometrics-quality facial images (Department process generally age 5+); follow current biometric country rules, exemptions and the actual requesting letter for fingerprints/image and appointments. Keep overseas work/study references independently verifiable; the Department can verify relevant overseas claims. Do not upload sensitive identifying, health or police records outside the official or agreed secure process.

## Grant, conditions and work mobility

**Grant (494.411, .511–.512, .611–.613):** inside or outside Australia but not immigration clearance; family can be in different places if each meets grant requirements. Primary generally has multiple entry/stay for five years from grant; secondary normally runs to the primary grant-based end date, not another five years from the secondary grant. Check the historical eight-year exception in .511(2)/.512(2) for travel/visa in-effect dates from **1 February 2020 to 14 December 2021** and **18 February 2022**, including the secondary visa's grant/in-effect timing; verify actual notices and VEVO rather than calculating expiry from the subclass name. Offshore grant carries first-entry date specified by the Minister (use the notice; not an assumed 12 months) and potential 8515 for secondary. Check actual conditions. Verify functional English under 5.17 separately for applicants 18+ at application; where applicable pay second VAC before grant. Item 1242(2)(b) has distinct primary/secondary amounts and Minister of Religion basis/family nil branch. Not a cure for missing ES competent English or LA requirements.

**Regional conditions (primary and secondary):**

- **8578:** notify Immigration within 14 days of changes to residential address, email, phone, passport details, employer address or work-position location address. Keep acknowledgement. The sponsor may have a separate reporting obligation.
- **8579:** while in Australia, live, work and study only in areas designated at the time the **relevant nomination was made**, not at grant or by today's map. For the first nomination period start at grant if holder was onshore or first entry as holder if offshore. When a later relevant nomination for that visa is approved and the primary actually starts the position, use the later nomination's made date from the new start day (previous period ends the day before); nomination filing or approval alone does not shift the map. Maintain a timeline for every holder including subsequent entrants and multiple locations. Check actual physical location for remote work/study, living and client-site visits; mixed facts, brief visits, temporary work or unclear map/catchment must not be assumed compliant or noncompliant from a postal address.
- **8580 / 8581:** comply with written requests: evidence of residential, employer, position and education addresses within 28 days after the request under 8580, and interview at stipulated place/manner/time under 8581. Keep residence, employment, study and travel records.

**8608 (primary only):** normally work only in the nominated occupation linked to the most recent 494, in the sponsor/associated business for ES or for the nominator for LA. Check the specified occupation and industrial-relations notice exceptions, which concern the employer restriction, and the more recent cessation flexibility separately. Start within 90 days from onshore grant or offshore grantee's arrival; check mandatory licensing/registration/membership within the applicable 90 days, ongoing validity and compliance, and promptly notify Immigration in writing of refusal, cessation, revocation or cancellation. Verify the actual dates and any different other visa conditions before work.

Under the changes from **1 July 2024** (F2024L00776; Sch 13 item 13201), an actual cessation of compliant sponsored work can allow time to find a new sponsor/seek another visa/leave; the limit is **180 consecutive days** in a period and **365 total days** during the visa period, disregarding cessation time before commencement for earlier visas under the transition. Home Affairs describes temporary scope for other work after stopping sponsored work; it is not permission to work outside the regional condition, take unsponsored second work while continuing sponsored employment, ignore authorisation/licensing or assume a permanent transfer. Record last compliant day, whether employment really ceased, visa/stream/conditions, work for each employer/occupation, 180/365 ledger, sponsorship notices, actual site dates and restart. For continuing sponsored work in the same occupation, check an approved new nomination as required (unless specific occupational exception); for a changed nominated occupation normally new nomination **and visa grant** before relying on the new sponsored role. No automatic cross-stream switch, expiry extension or permission to return to a ceased arrangement. Near limits, contested cessation, unrelated placement, altered occupation or site, or conflicts in grant/official sources warrant urgent individual verification before work or travel.

## Linked-case decisions and future pathway handoff

- **Refusal, withdrawal, invalidity, review and cancellation:** retrieve full notices, the nominated applicant's location and sponsor/nomination/review status at the required times. Check applicable Act and reg 4.02/ART transitional jurisdiction, whether primary onshore/offshore, live nomination or pending upstream review, secondary review branch and who has standing; file deadlines are not inferred from usual durations. Invalidity, withdrawal and refusal differ. Visa first-instalment refund under 2.12F(3C) is discretionary for prescribed withdrawals, e.g. mistaken occupation/stream, specified linked nomination withdrawals, sponsor action under s 140K or Australian business ceasing lawful operation; check exact sequence and grounds, not a general refund upon refusal. SAF refunds belong to the sponsor workflow. For compliance notices/cancellation or a deadline, help organise chronology and evidence promptly without predicting a discretionary outcome or advising abandonment of a matter.
- **Subclass 191:** send an evidence-only handoff to [regional-191-handoff.md](regional-191-handoff.md). Preserve grant/VEVO, holding periods, nomination and employment transitions, regional residence/work/study and notification/compliance records and tax notices of assessment. A three-year holding milestone is an intake prompt, not a grant or full 191 assessment. Do not apply a numeric taxable-income floor or substitute payslips for assessment notices. Parent regional reference owns the 191 handoff. For other visa pathways verify their specific validity/grant restrictions and exceptions (e.g. onshore partner application versus subclass-specific permanent grant gates) against the applicable text and existing/grant history; don't infer a universal bar or transplant a historical list.
