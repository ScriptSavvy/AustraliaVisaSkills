# Subclass 186 ENS: visa application preparation

Use the [employer nomination reference](ens-186-nomination.md) only for the employer side, and the shared [evidence and procedure rules](evidence-and-terms.md). This reference covers the primary and family preparation branches, not application submission, certification of validity, immigration status or an outcome. Choose the actual stream and issue first. The following findings compare the November 2026 dated Regulations, identified amendments and the preparation guidance; verify the applicable provision, instrument and any transition for the dates in the case.

## Before lodgement: independent application requirements

- **Class EN, Schedule 1 item 1114B:** confirm prescribed form, place and manner; actual application date and acknowledgement; visa charge and applicable applicant, age, nationality or other concession; and each person's location and evidence of status at application. Application may be onshore or offshore, but not in immigration clearance. Onshore requires a substantive or actually held BVA, BVB or BVC under item 1114B(3); do not confuse this with the narrower TRT qualifying history or assume an unactivated grant suffices for the fact under review. Check statutory bars, Schedule 3 only when triggered, and authorised form and channel for unusual cases. Narrative evidence that forms were started or fees paid is not lodgement.
- **Nomination linkage:** the primary applicant declares the position was nominated under reg 5.19 and supplies the correct nomination reference. Match the person, stream, legal entity and role to the actual nomination. Home Affairs' TRT guidance describes an already approved nomination within the six months before application. The dated Schedule 1 declaration and the Schedule 2 approval, withdrawal, availability, timing and adverse-information gates are separate tests. Do not derive a permission to lodge before approval just from the declaration wording, or infer invalidity just from a generic guidance summary. Verify the actual permitted lodgement pathway, form, chronology, notice and applicable criteria; never advise deliberate early filing or link a different nomination by assumption.
- **Payments:** the Schedule 1 declaration concerns specified s 245AS(1) conduct by the primary or combined applicants in relation to those applications; it is distinct from nomination certification and the later wider integrity tests under cls 186.212A and 186.312A. If relevant, record dates, exact conduct and evidence, and use [worker protection](worker-protection.md). Do not promise the decision-maker will disregard facts. Charge instalments, employer nomination charges and prohibited cost shifting are separate.
- **Family at application:** test each person’s combined application and actual family category rather than importing 482 subsequent-entrant rules. For a newborn during a pending application, check regulation 2.08 and the actual pending application, parentage, notification and any citizenship issue before recommending a document route. For later additions of a partner/dependent child while undecided, verify the conditions and procedure under reg 2.08A and the specific family basis; do not assume an independent route after the primary grant. Check actual notices and case status before any suggestion to add, vary or withdraw an application.

## Across streams: common criteria and decision evidence

- **Licence, registration or membership (186.211):** if mandatory for the actual tasks and jurisdiction, identify the kind required and evidence of holding or eligibility at application. Distinguish provisional/supervised, conditional and full registration by permitted tasks and available workplace supervision; a regulator confirmation can identify which prerequisites have already been met. A skills assessment, letter of intention or overseas qualification alone does not establish local licence eligibility. Record any renewal or change and verify the question it affects; no assumed waiver.
- **The offered employment (186.212):** compare contract, nominated role, actual availability, entity, duties and worksite. Establish that the position will actually provide the nominated employment. For TRT/DE use the identified nomination, approval and withdrawal history and fresh position evidence; the six-month condition is keyed to nomination **approval** (186.223(5), 186.233(6)), not a fixed interval measured from lodgement or visa grant. Do not impose those express clauses on LA, for which 186.242 supplies a separate test. For approved-but-changed or pending nominations, prepare the actual dated records and withhold a readiness finding until the applicable link and timing are established.
- **Health, character, integrity and return criteria:** organise genuine identity documents, declarations, relevant history, actual health/character notices and any request concerning a non-applying family member. Check common public interest criteria and Special Return Criteria against the actual applicant role, age, date and stream. For health distinguish the 4007 TRT and 4005 DE/LA branches and the particular unreasonable-to-require qualification for assessment of some non-applicants; it is not a universal waiver. Don't decide a character waiver or danger, buy examinations from an unauthorised channel, predict exclusion, or declare records genuine merely because readable.

## Temporary Residence Transition: count the two histories separately

| Check | Apply at the relevant visa application date; prepare distinct proof |
|---|---|
| Age and English | Under 45 and competent English at application under 186.221/.222 unless the appropriate **separate** instrument class is satisfied. Use actual birth and test/passport evidence; distinguish competent English from functional English for a possible second charge. |
| Visa holding under 186.226 | Ordinary comparison: cumulative two years within the three years immediately before application. Record each 457/TSS/SID grant, commencement and cessation, including visa changes, and the bridging grant basis and period actually held. On the bridging limb, verify the last substantive visa was a qualifying 457/482 and the bridging grant relates to the listed 457/482/186/187 application. A bridging period linked to the first sponsored application after an unrelated substantive visa is not the same branch. A grant date, later activation and visa expiry are separate facts; check the official status record and actual transition rather than estimating. |
| Employment under 186.227 | Ordinary comparison: full-time work undertaken in Australia for a cumulative two years within the immediately preceding three years, in the occupation for which the relevant 457/482 was granted, excluding unpaid leave. A listed occupation under the reg 2.72(13) instrument has a distinct limb; do not import the general full-time/location wording into that limb, but retain the occupation, period, sponsored-employment and unpaid-leave checks. Look for any applicable instrument changing the period separately for each clause. |
| Verified sponsor and change of employer | For applications made on or after **29 November 2025**, the [2025 amending Regulations](https://www.legislation.gov.au/F2025L01389/asmade/2025-11-17/text/original/epub/OEBPS/document_1/document_1.html) add the approved-work-sponsor requirement to both employment limbs (Schedule 1 item 6, Schedule 2/new Schedule 13 cl 15502(4)). Do not apply that amendment indiscriminately to applications before that date. Work with more than one employer may be relevant; check each employer, role and approval history. [Home Affairs TRT guidance](https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/visas/getting-a-visa/visa-listing/employer-nomination-scheme-186/temporary-residence-transition-stream) describes some work before nomination approval counting from lodgement where that nomination was ultimately approved. Do not generalise to any pending/rejected nomination or unsponsored work; obtain both nomination dates, approval, actual start, duties, visa/work dates, approval basis, and verify the provision/policy before counting. |
| Paid absences, group entities, review periods | Separate paid annual/parental/sick leave from unpaid leave. Stored policy suggests considering genuinely agreed paid parental leave at reduced pay on its facts; document contract/award, paid leave proof and prior full-time engagement rather than pro-rating or automatically counting. Payroll at a related company, bridging during review, work overseas or while occupation changed, and an earlier sponsor with adverse information require individual actor, status, authority and date analysis. Do not import a separate age-exemption employment calculation. |

Draw both ledgers with date ranges, visa/legal employer, occupation, actual location, hours, leave, and verified/unresolved status; use dated grants, application and decision notices, employer letters, contracts, payslips, payroll and work/travel records, and confirm approved sponsor/nomination evidence for each work period. Clip to the correct window, deduplicate overlapping spans, and use a date tool. Do not treat time waiting or merely holding a visa as work, count unknown time as eligible, or treat the final nominator’s status as proof of every past period. Compare with separate [TRT nomination checks](ens-186-nomination.md) and preserve a visa-holding result apart from employment and total readiness.

**Dated transitions:** For a pre-7 December 2024 186 visa application, verify saved nomination and visa provisions rather than using the new ledgers as controlling criteria. For an earlier nomination paired with a visa application on/after 7 December 2024, the [2024 amendment](https://www.legislation.gov.au/F2024L01585/asmade) includes specific Schedule 13 application/saving provisions; compare the actual dates and former predicates with the relevant saved text. Don't extend concessions after their cohorts. Use 186.225 if skill demonstration is requested: a skills assessment may be requested as specified, but don't turn DE's lodgement-stage assessment into a blanket TRT requirement. Read the actual request and occupation/licensing connection.

## Direct Entry: age, English, prior regional visa and skills

- Under 45 and competent English at application unless the matching separate instrument class applies (186.231/.232). A concession for another stream or competent English for a professional regulator is not automatically the visa requirement. If the applicant then held a regional provisional visa or it was their last substantive visa, 186.232A requires the three-year holding check unless specified circumstances apply. Read exact visa history and applicable instrument; don't apply this clause to TRT or confuse it with the [191 handoff](regional-191-handoff.md).
- Under 186.234, either the correct instrument class applies or **both** a suitable non-485-purpose assessment from the prescribed assessor and at least three years' full-time work at the required skill level in the occupation must be evidenced at application. The assessment must remain valid: apply its express period if it ends no more than three years after issue, otherwise verify the three-year outer comparison. Check type, date, assessment authority, code and occupational fit using the [186 occupation and assessing authority instrument](https://www.legislation.gov.au/F2024L01618/latest). A provisional or pending result, unrelated occupation or later course completion does not establish the application-time fact. Examine recognised trade/RTO arrangements, specialised occupations and reclassification only with actual evidence of equivalence and recognised authority; do not assume all RTO documents qualify.
- Prepare genuine work statements (tasks, locations, dates, hours, level, periods of unpaid leave and paid proof), assessment letter and prerequisites. Separate relevant skilled work from training, volunteer work, a role below the occupational skill level, overlapping roles and time not worked. A new title or qualification date may not show when duties reached the required level. Verify alternative exemptions before omitting the assessment/experience package. Cross-check employer/nomination match under 186.233.

## Labour Agreement: read the operative ENS terms

1. Inspect the executed agreement, schedules/deeds, scope, actual occupation and class of role, nomination and position availability, employer party and applicable date. Use [the agreement reference](labour-agreements.md). Under 186.241 test conventional age or the operative agreement's specified age separately. The prior-regional-visa test in 186.241A has a potential **agreement-specified shorter period** and an instrument-based alternative; neither follows from a template, endorsement or temporary visa concession.
2. Apply 186.242's exact position, nominated individual, approved/not withdrawn nomination, available role and relevant adverse-information alternative. Compare offered terms (pay and other conditions) with the Australian-equivalent workplace/location terms. Check both the agreement and ordinary mandatory licensing and labour obligations; do not apply a blanket concession to employer responsibilities.
3. Under 186.243 match qualifications, history, other attributes, any English test or mode of evidence and demonstration of skills to both the occupation and the ENS visa terms. If the agreement requires skills assessment, verify the stipulated employer/occupation scope, prescribed or agreement-designated authority and express validity/three-year alternative; don't rely on an older assessment with a different occupation or assume every agreement requires one at application. The prior preparation benchmark for years worked is not a substitute for the operative 186 wording/actual agreement. Don't import either DE's blanket three-year experience or SID's minimum as an ENS LA rule where the terms differ or are missing. Obtain the complete countersigned provisions or leave the affected finding open.

## Exemptions: confirm each class separately

Consult [LIN 19/216](https://www.legislation.gov.au/F2019L01404/latest) in the legally applicable version. In the inspected compilation section 6(1) specifies TRT **age** classes; section 6(2) specifies DE **age and skills/experience** classes. Neither set automatically supplies an English exemption. Verify any separate English instrument independently and keep the functional-English charge question separate.

| Instrument class | Prepare the actual distinguishing evidence |
|---|---|
| Academic or science (possible TRT age or DE age/skills) | Academic: match the nominating Australian university, classification level and specified academic occupation to the actual appointment and code. Science: prove the employer is a qualifying Commonwealth, State or Territory science agency or Australian university and that the post is researcher/scientist/technical specialist at the specified ANZSCO skill level. A teaching or technical title alone is not enough. |
| Regional medical practitioner (TRT age) | Check that the nominated position is in a designated regional area and identify two distinct 2-in-3 histories: actual practitioner employment in locations that were regional when worked, and actual qualifying visa holding. Record licence, statements of service, worksite dates and applicable historic postcode instruments. Do not count generic travel or an address at head office as regional work. |
| Qualifying 457/482 high-income worker (TRT age) | Reconcile the 2-in-3 qualifying visa limb with at least two years in position(s) related to approvals under s 140GB. Test actual earnings separately for each qualifying year against the Fair Work High Income Threshold applicable at that year's end; not average pay, an annualised pay offer or today's threshold used retrospectively. Use payslips, payroll and dated threshold history; distinguish salary, excluded amounts and verified annual earnings. Resolve related entities, overseas periods and leave under this class, not the ordinary 186.227 calculation. |
| Historical 457 alternatives (TRT age) | Do not merge legacy and transitional-under-50 classes. The inspected legacy class includes a 457-held condition on/after 18 April 2017, the defined 12 months in Australia within 1 February 2020–14 December 2021, and a visa application by 30 June 2024. It is historical, not a new-application shortcut. The separate transition class requires the dated 18 April 2017 grant/application branch and being under 50 at application; get the actual historic grant/application and outcome, date of birth and any applicable saving provisions. Do not rely on apparent shorthand or abbreviated source wording to decide the historical limb. |
| Qualifying subclass 444/461 employment (DE age/skills) | Prepare two years of work with the actual nominating employer and in the nominated occupation within the three years before application, plus subclass 444/461 holding at almost all times in that three-year period. Keep work and visa histories separate; same corporate group, overseas service, unpaid leave, or unverified bridging periods must not be counted by default. |

Request only the missing fact that changes the class being considered. If the exact instrument or date is unclear, leave the affected exemption unresolved and keep the ordinary branch and independent preparation visible.

## Secondary applicants and later events

- Confirm every actual combined application and relationship/dependency/age evidence separately. Div 186.3 ordinary family path depends on the combined application and primary grant. Don't use the repealed 186.312 as a freestanding later 186 route. If facts involve family separation or violence, 186.311(3)–(5) has narrowly different grant/refusal and dependent-member branches; record former relationship, application/entry location and dates, actual grant/refusal and its reasons, affected person and case chronology only as necessary and safe. Do not request unsafe contact, notify an alleged perpetrator or infer automatic loss/eligibility because a relationship has ended. Keep Division 1.5 evidence and legal assessment distinct.
- Check actual health and integrity requirements for each applicant (including 186.313/.315 where relevant), Special Return Criteria, any responsibility/consent evidence for a child, and functional English or applicable second instalment for adult applicants. A second instalment does not satisfy the primary competent-English requirement; exceptions exist for specified religious/family-violence cases under item 1114B. Do not quote stored fees. Verify lodgement and decision location, actual grant notice, any first-entry condition and travel facility separately; no grant date or work-start prediction. For an adverse decision, prioritise the actual recipient, time of notification and dated authority for any review, not informal processing estimates.
