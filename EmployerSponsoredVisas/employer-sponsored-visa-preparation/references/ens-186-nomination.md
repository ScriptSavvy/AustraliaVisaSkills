# Subclass 186 Employer Nomination Scheme (ENS): employer nomination

Use for the employer’s nomination under regulation 5.19. Choose Temporary Residence Transition (TRT), Direct Entry (DE) or Labour Agreement (LA) by actual pathway; do not assume a prior 482 application determines the 186 stream. This is not a separate Standard Business Sponsorship application, the worker’s visa application, or permission to lodge. Use [186 visa preparation](ens-186-visa.md) for the applicant’s independently timed criteria; the [shared workflow](integrated-workflow.md) governs evidence, dates and uncertainty.

## Prepare the nomination common to all streams

| Check | What to prepare and what not to infer |
|---|---|
| Nominator and named position | Identify the precise legal person that will nominate and employ: company, individual/sole trader, partnership, or unincorporated association as applicable. For a trust, examine the trustee and the authority and capacity in which it acts, not merely the trust ABN. Match registration, contracts, payroll, actual operation, business/trading name and authority of signatory; registration alone does not demonstrate trading. Reconcile a restructure, sale, franchise or related entity before treating earlier approval, operation or employment as belonging to the new party. Record occupation and classification version, location, position, stream and identified person separately. |
| Prescribed process and payments | Check reg 5.19(2), the dated nomination form/place/manner instrument and any genuinely authorised alternative. A draft, receipt for payment or system acceptance is not a decision. Check the fee under reg 5.37 (stream and qualifying regional location may matter) and the separate SAF nomination training contribution under the Charges Regulations, including turnover definition, applicable date, exemption and correct legal payer. Calculate from supported records, not estimates or today's fee. Collect acknowledgement, actual nomination reference, payment record and later notices. |
| Truthful written certification | Reg 5.19(2)(g) concerns the nominator’s own asking-for/receiving-a-benefit conduct under Act s 245AR(1) in relation to this nomination. Separate the visa declaration, s 245AS issues, forbidden sponsor costs and actual payments. Keep correspondence and use [worker protection](worker-protection.md) if any charge, offer or threat is raised. Never supply a fabricated negative answer. |
| Adverse information, employment and SAF debt | Identify any relevant known adverse information about the nominator or associated person (and the basis of association), applicable employment-law record in the Commonwealth and relevant jurisdictions, and unpaid SAF debt under Act s 140ZO. Keep notices, findings, payment/remediation and genuine response evidence. Do not make an undisclosed matter disappear or treat any allegation as established; the reasonable-disregard alternative for adverse information is not a waiver of an outstanding SAF debt. |
| Licensing at the nomination application date | Determine whether compulsory state/territory licensing, registration or professional membership is required for the specific tasks at the actual worksite. Show the identified person held it or was eligible to obtain it at that date. Obtain the regulator’s confirmation or exact rule and evidence of completed prerequisites. In-principle approval, supervised/provisional registration or a skills assessment may answer different questions; check scope and outstanding prerequisites, not just the document title. Do not presume discretion to waive a mandatory credential. |

## TRT and DE: business, role and pay evidence

- **Active, lawful operation in Australia:** identify the actual operating entity; use appropriate registration/licences together with genuine trading records (signed customer work, invoices/payment, BAS, accounts, bank and payroll records). For newer businesses or acquisitions, document real activity, financial capacity and continuity; being incorporated or part of an established group is not enough. Record confidentiality and request no more comparator or finance records than needed.
- **Need and direct control:** explain how the role fits actual work, referral/customer demand, staffing, contracts and current/projected workload. Show who directs duties, pay, hours, leave, performance and hiring/termination. For an associated host or client site, compare the employment contract and service agreement: independent service delivery, control of how the work is done and payment for a deliverable can differ from an arrangement to supply labour. Neither a shared brand nor a customer address decides the issue. Separate evidence of a real position from the claim that this nominee is needed and from the worker’s competence.
- **Duration and capacity:** normally prepare evidence of genuine full-time work for at least two years, no express exclusion of extension, capacity to employ and to pay the annual market salary rate (AMSR) each year, and applicable award or enterprise terms. Reconcile term, hours, start timing, probation and any early-termination language with the employer’s actual capacity and intention; a probation clause is not alone proof of a sham or a guaranteed two-year offer. Check award/agreement hours rather than assume one fixed definition. Under reg 5.19(7), the specified-occupation TRT exception removes only the listed paragraphs (5)(j)–(l); do not carry it to DE or dispense with the remaining requirements. Verify the applicable reg 2.72(13) instrument and exact occupation first.
- **Earnings:** analyse genuine Australian comparator doing equivalent work at the same site, award or agreement classification, and the applicable instrument’s evidence method when there is no usable comparator. Record hours, cash guaranteed earnings, variable allowances/bonuses, superannuation and non-monetary benefits separately; distinguish market rate, the nominee’s actual offer and each applicable threshold. Apply reg 2.72(15) and (16) only **as modified by reg 5.19** for the stream. The inspected TRT and DE modifications use the Core Skills Income Threshold for the specified AMSR and nominee earnings comparisons even though this is ENS; verify the dated threshold, market-method and discretion before any finding. Do not import SID Specialist Skills thresholds or apply them merely because the worker’s previous visa was Specialist Skills. Distinguish lesser non-pay conditions, a supported discretionary response and employment-law compliance. A viable role is not proven by a salary offer alone.

## Stream switches and separate gates

### Temporary Residence Transition (TRT)

1. At nomination application, inspect the worker’s actual most recent 457, TSS 482 or SID 482 or the qualifying bridging-visa route under reg 5.19(5)(a). Identify the bridging grant basis, preceding substantive visa and whether the bridging visa was actually held for the relevant check; a grant letter or a pending application is not enough. Keep this snapshot separate from the worker’s visa-stage historical holding ledger.
2. Confirm the nominator is the SBS or agreement party that **last identified this person in a nomination approved under s 140GB**, and independently establish active lawful operation. Use approval notices and chronology; being the most recent payroll employer, submitting a nomination or sharing owners with the approved entity does not independently establish this. The fact that another employer’s work might count at visa stage is not evidence the other employer can nominate.
3. Compare the intended ANZSCO occupation/duties with the one attached to the most recently held relevant grant. The amended reg 5.19(5)(b) specifies the same occupation, not a freestanding rule granting acceptance for shared four-digit code. Exact duties, classification editions, historical code mapping and actual evidence of performing the tasks matter; policy flexibility or reclassification is not an entitlement. Where historical or alternate code treatment is material, verify the date, applicable provision and any written basis for the treatment. A legacy agreement code or position title alone does not establish a match.
4. Read the two actual application dates. For nominations before **7 December 2024**, consult the saved version of reg 5.19 and its amendments; the employment-history requirements cannot simply be relocated onto that old case. For later cases keep the new visa-stage tests in [186 visa](ens-186-visa.md) separate. Do not replace a genuine legacy criteria check with the current checklist.

### Direct Entry (DE)

1. The DE occupation instrument under reg 5.19(11) controls the position, not the rules for an earlier temporary occupation. Use the dated [subclass 186 occupation and assessing authority instrument](https://www.legislation.gov.au/F2024L01618/latest) and verify the version in force at nomination. Screen all caveats that may apply to the occupation or person (role/duties, business, worksite and any expressly applicable earnings or contextual exclusion). Do not assume matching job titles or shared unit-group tasks make a code suitable. A specialised role can match without performing every generic unit-group task; compare actual material duties and occupational level.
2. For a labour-hire business, determine whether the position is in its own operations and **not** for supplying labour to an unrelated business. Inspect the actual service and control arrangements; identify the party that hires, supervises and pays. Do not replace this DE gate with the TRT specified-occupation exception. Separately reconcile the nomination against the worker’s DE assessment and experience points of evidence; track mismatch without deciding worker eligibility here.

### Labour Agreement (LA)

Use the [executed agreement and variations workflow](labour-agreements.md). Under reg 5.19(14) verify the nominator is the legal party, an agreement is in effect and specifies the position occupation for the purpose of this regulation, all triggered party requirements are met, and the count of approvals under reg 5.19 on that nominator’s applications is below the agreement’s annual permitted approvals. Check scope by visa, occupation, period and worksite, exact usage rules and authoritative records. A proposed increase, regional endorsement or templated concession is not operative. Do not substitute the 482 yearly-count rule, treat pending nominations as approvals, or import 482 applicant concessions into ENS. Prepare the actual conditions/certifications, nomination requirements and evidence of any agreement-specific salary/recruitment obligations. A new request is not the approval basis for an ENS nomination.

## Official notices, decision and refund preparation

- Capture acknowledgement, approval/refusal notice, actual dates, linked visa and position availability. Respond to a fairness/request issue by distinguishing officer concerns, evidence provided and missing documents without speculating about withheld information or contacting anyone. A sponsorship approval, payment or draft does not establish nomination approval or visa readiness. Do not assume a replacement nomination can be linked to an existing visa or an apparent error can be corrected by resubmitting.
- For review or refund, prepare the actual notice, role/standing, recipient, delivery date, payer, linked-visa history and exact trigger; verify applicable review jurisdiction and time limit before advising. Reg 5.37A contains separate discretionary refund branches for Department error, specified LA occupation/ceiling or pre-agreement withdrawal, incorrect SAF information, wrong TRT occupation, wrong stream, finally determined health/character refusal and failure to commence. Timing, withdrawal reason and whether only a particular component should be refunded under policy matter. Do not withdraw, assert eligibility, promise repayment or treat every employer charge as refundable.
- Give the employer the supported nomination finding, the concrete missing record/action, and only the visa dependency that actually affects it. Use [the shared report](../templates/report.md) for broad reviews. Verify reg 5.19 and 5.37–5.37A in [the Regulations](https://www.legislation.gov.au/F1996B03551/2026-11-03/2026-11-03/text/original/epub/OEBPS/document_1/document_1.html), relevant instruments and commenced amendments for the event. The inspected compilation dated 3 November 2026 does not itself establish a subsequent date. See the [2024 transitional amendment](https://www.legislation.gov.au/F2024L01585/asmade) and [Home Affairs ENS guidance](https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/visas/getting-a-visa/visa-listing/employer-nomination-scheme-186) for process; retain any material source conflict rather than assuming a blanket rule.
