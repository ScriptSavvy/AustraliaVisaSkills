# Integrated employer-sponsored preparation workflow

## Separate preparation tracks, one consistency review

Do not collapse approvals or assume all stages occur in a fixed order. Establish the case-specific authority, scope, event dates and validity, approval and decision gates independently. See [source verification and limits](verified-updates.md).

| Track | Main owner | Preparation output | Connection |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Standard business sponsorship](standard-business-sponsorship.md) | Eligible employer/legal entity | First application, renewal or accreditation evidence, actual approval and scope | Check the relevant operating entity, status and dates; a renewal receipt is not a renewed approval |
| [Labour agreement](labour-agreements.md) | Requesting or executing party | New request, endorsement if applicable, executed terms/schedules, variation evidence | Distinguish requested terms, template eligibility, endorsement, executed authority, effective terms and available ceiling |
| Nomination | Employer/sponsor | Role, occupation/stream/caveats, genuine/full-time or permitted part-time position, contract/entity, salary/AMSR/capacity, LMT or specific alternative/exemption, fees/SAF and agreement conditions | Identifies nominee, role, period and application reference used by visa stage |
| Visa | Primary applicant and included family | Identity, location/status, skills/experience/assessment, English/exemption, insurance, family, health/character/request evidence | Uses correct nomination and stream; sponsor certifications do not prove every applicant criterion |
| [Sponsor obligations](sponsor-obligations.md) | Sponsor or party to a work agreement | Duties, start/end dates, records, changes, notifications, notices and costs | Tie each duty to the correct role and event; do not treat approval expiry as ending every duty |
| [Worker protection and costs](worker-protection.md) | Worker or sponsor, with separate authority and privacy | Fee allocation, evidence and voluntary help options | Distinguish prohibited cost shifting and a requested benefit for a sponsorship event; do not assume offence, visa immunity or authority to report |
| Shared consistency | Both, separately authorised | Matching table and dated event ledger | State each side's gap and who can supply/correct it; never contact either party automatically |

An adviser label identifies a review responsibility, not a claim the agent is licensed. The agent may organise evidence and explain sourced preparation rules; regulatory/professional obligations need appropriate review before personalised assistance.

## Select the pathway before applying any rule

| Pathway | Full or bounded preparation | Main distinctions to keep visible |
|---|---|---|
| 482 SID | [Nomination](nomination.md) and [visa](visa-application.md) | Core Skills, Specialist Skills and Labour Agreement have different occupation/pay or agreement gates; sponsor approval, nomination and visa are separate. |
| 186 ENS | [Nomination](ens-186-nomination.md) and [visa](ens-186-visa.md) | TRT, Direct Entry and Labour Agreement each have their own position/nomination and applicant criteria. Do not require a separate SBS application as a universal step. |
| 494 SESR | [Nomination](sesr-494-nomination.md) and [visa](sesr-494-visa.md) | Employer Sponsored and Labour Agreement have different authority and certification requirements. Check actual regional locations, applicable certifying-body evidence and distinct visa conditions. |
| 187 RSMS | [Transitional TRT only](rsms-187-transition.md) | Establish a historical cohort, then identify the actual nomination and individual visa gates. A 482 label does not distinguish TSS from SID. Do not revive Direct Entry. |
| 494 → 191 | [Evidence handoff only](regional-191-handoff.md) | Preserve visa history, income-year notices and condition evidence independently. No predicted grant, work rights, full application validity or automatic transition. |

### 482 or 457 → 186 TRT record handoff

1. Use the 482 record as evidence, not as a conclusion for 186. Prepare a **visa-status ledger**: actual grant dates and the earlier visa basis; any cessation or later visa; each bridging visa type, basis and in-effect dates; and the planned 186 visa-application date. Apply only the qualifying periods and denominator in the target-date 186 provision.
2. Separately prepare an **employment ledger**: one row per employer/entity and materially different position/period; actual duties, the occupation of the relevant visa grant, dates, Australia/overseas work, hours, paid/unpaid leave and evidence of sponsor status for each period. Use statements of service, payslips, verified contracts/payroll or other corroborating records. Do not double count overlap, treat a completed visa year as work performed or assume all employers qualify—or fail because they differ from the final nominator. Reconcile associated entities rather than using the trading name as proof.
3. Apply the date-specific 186 TRT visa and 186 nomination tests separately, including historic timing provisions and any relevant exceptions; see [ENS nomination](ens-186-nomination.md) and [ENS visa](ens-186-visa.md). Identify each unverified segment and the record that would resolve it rather than treating the whole history as eligible/ineligible. No estimated approval prospects.

## Minimal branching

Use known facts first. Ask one numbered material question only if necessary. For a broad request with no facts, determine which preparation or evidence result the user needs; if already known, do not ask. Next identify the earliest unknown that changes the immediate task (for example stream or an actual request). Keep role/authority, stage, dates and evidence inventory internal. Do not request a complete personal or business history by default.

- **Pre-lodgement:** assess relevant preparation gates at the actual/proposed nomination and visa dates. Distinguish documents useful now from those required at lodgement or decision.
- **Already lodged:** use actual acknowledgement/reference/status; do not pretend a proposed correction can relink an existing application. Read actual Department requests and deadlines.
- **Nomination approved:** inspect approval details and cessation events; approval alone does not establish visa readiness or employer work permission.
- **Existing holder changes:** first identify current grant, nominated occupation/employer and proposed change. Nomination-only versus new visa, exempt occupations, interim work and agreement transitions need their own checks.
- **Family/subsequent entrant:** establish the specific route, primary status, relationship and sponsor inclusion/agreement; distinguish unborn/newborn mechanisms from adding another family member after lodgement.
- **Labour Agreement:** inspect the actual executed agreement, applicable schedule, occupation, ceilings, LMT, salary/skills/experience/English concessions and dates. An agreement possibility or negotiation is not an executed concession.

## Shared evidence matching matrix

For each relevant field record the evidence in each implicated stage, match status, uncertainty and next action. Do not request upstream or downstream records unless they could change this finding.

| Field | Check |
|---|---|
| Nominee identity | Compare inspected passport/identity with nomination/visa records; record explainable name variants without alleging fraud |
| Sponsor and employing legal entity | Distinguish business/trading name, legal person, associated entity, employer and worksite; an unrelated worksite alone does not disprove direct employment |
| Stream and legal route | Match the actual subclass, stream and dated cohort; do not substitute a similarly named stream in another subclass |
| Occupation/code/duties | Match exact classification/version and core tasks, applicant skills and position; title alone is not occupational proof |
| Earnings and period | Compare contract, nomination, monetary/non-monetary breakdown, hours and proposed period; different monetary tests need separate rows |
| LMT and recruitment | Track sponsor-authorised advertisements/submissions/exemption evidence and nomination-date relevance; applicant English exemptions are unrelated |
| Skills and licensing | Sponsor's role certification does not establish applicant competence; separate assessment-at-lodgement and suitability-at-decision, registration and work-start rules |
| Agreement concessions | Check exact agreement terms and whether each concession applies to nomination, applicant, both, or neither |
| Family coverage | Family eligibility and sponsor inclusion/written agreement are distinct gates; an omitted dependant is not automatically added later |
| Nomination reference and status | Confirm identified reference, pending/approved/withdrawn/refused/ceased events and sponsor status; status is not inferred from a filename |

## Event ledger

Record only material events and evidence: sponsor application/approval/expiry/renewal and any lapse; agreement request/endorsement/execution/commencement/expiry and executed variations; agreement capacity/usage; obligation triggers, receipt of notices, notifications and cessation; LMT start/end/redundancy dates; nomination lodgement/approval/cessation; visa lodgement/request/decision; assessment commencement/result; English test/re-sit/evidence dates; current grant expiry; employer/occupation changes; family birth/status events. Use source-specific date anchors. Record date unknown rather than estimating. Calendar-month, year and day periods are not interchangeable; use deterministic tools and state boundary assumptions. Do not invent full-time hours or double-count concurrent experience/advertisements.

Before suggesting a nomination correction, withdrawal or new application, flag the effect on any already lodged visa. The skill does not execute these actions. Nomination refusal, withdrawal, cessation and review are not equivalent events. A new nomination cannot be assumed to replace the one linked to a pending visa; actual legal route needs verification.

## Outputs

For narrow questions, after notices give a direct answer, specific finding, practical action, material uncertainty and brief source note. Do not dump unrelated stage checklists.

For broad reviews use the [report template](../templates/report.md):
1. Main supported findings and priority actions.
2. Separate sponsor/authority, nomination, visa or compliance findings as requested, each limited to what was actually checked.
3. One shared matching table, not repeated findings in multiple places.
4. Evidence-dimension rows and case-relevant uncertainties with source/date and resolution action.

For an unrequested stage, record **Not assessed — not requested** as the scope note; do not assign a readiness status or use Not applicable as a substitute. Different stages can contain independent Ready document-quality findings while an overall readiness conclusion remains unavailable. Do not create a combined percentage score. A user can choose to prepare records without an eligibility conclusion.
