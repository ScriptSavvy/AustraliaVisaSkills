# Employer labour agreements: request, use and maintenance

Use this reference for the employer's agreement request, review of an existing agreement, or preparation of a variation. Keep four findings separate: **agreement request evidence**, **authority under the operative agreement**, **nomination**, and **visa**. This is preparation support, not legal advice, a decision, or authority to lodge, sign, pay, withdraw, send documents, contact a third party or access an account. Apply the skill's intake and privacy rules; ask for the next material fact, not the entire checklist. Use redacted comparator and workforce records where possible.

## Agreement or ordinary sponsorship?

Start with the legal employer, actual worksite(s), role/duties, intended visa and whether the employer already has an agreement. For an existing agreement, go directly to [Agreement status and scope](#agreement-status-and-scope). For a new request, establish a genuine Australian labour need and identify the best available route before drafting any concession request.

| Route | What to establish first |
|---|---|
| **Standard skilled program** | Check the occupation, duties, employer, location, stream, pay and ordinary requirements for the case date. If the need can be met through the standard program, use [standard business sponsorship](standard-business-sponsorship.md) for ordinary SID sponsorship. Do not switch to an agreement solely because a preferred worker cannot meet standard criteria. Document why any apparently available standard pathway does not meet the evidenced need. |
| **Industry** | Match the business activity and role to a listed industry and the template actually offered. The common industry settings are generally fixed, not a starting offer for bespoke concessions. Obtain any branch-specific endorsement or union agreement first. Use the [industry branches](#industry-branches) below. |
| **Designated Area Migration Agreement (DAMA)** | Match the **worksite**, not just headquarters, to a current regional framework. Check the Designated Area Representative's (DAR's) published coverage, intake, occupation, visa, ceiling and concessions for that site. Get written employer endorsement **before** an employer request; match its scope and validity to the request. The head agreement sets regional terms; it is not the employer's agreement or nomination authority. This workflow does not negotiate or establish a head agreement. Do not use DAMA to bypass a covering industry template; resolve overlap against the DAR's and Home Affairs' actual coverage, not the code alone. |
| **Company-specific** | Document what the ordinary, industry, regional and project routes do not cover. Show why the shortage or niche need cannot be met locally, with diverse recruitment and detailed role evidence. The normal occupation range is ANZSCO skill levels 1–4; for level 5 do not treat the exceptional business-case route as generally available. Public guidance limits its stated possibility to Category 3; verify location, current approach and objective shortage evidence before relying on it. A role removed from a skilled list is not in itself a shortage case. |
| **Project** | For a resource or infrastructure construction-phase shortage, verify the project's Department of Foreign Affairs and Trade endorsement under the China–Australia Investment Facilitation Arrangement and the process in Home Affairs' company-specific guidance. Prepare project scope, dates, workforce, roles and shortage evidence. A qualifying project may be processed on a company-specific basis; size alone does not qualify. For other large projects test available DAMA/industry coverage first. A short specialist project evidence case is not necessarily a Project Agreement. |
| **Skilled Refugee Labour Agreement Pilot** | Check actual intake dates, remaining access, occupation list, and Talent Beyond Boundaries (TBB) endorsement of **both** employer and candidate. Check the actual template for available subclasses, recruitment evidence, employer skills validation, registration/licensing, pay, English and settlement support. Home Affairs' November 2026 guidance reports extension to **31 December 2026** and a successor expected in early 2027; neither establishes an open place or a commenced successor. Do not treat endorsement as a statutory LMT exemption or calculate the pay floor from conflicting summaries. |

Global Talent Employer Sponsored agreements are not a new-request route here. If an employer relies on an older agreement mentioning TSS, subclass 457 or Global Talent, obtain the actual terms, current status and operative saving/transition provisions; a legacy label is neither a current offer nor automatic authority for a new SID nomination.

**Official lookup:** Start at Home Affairs' [types of labour agreements](https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/visas/employing-and-sponsoring-someone/labour-agreements/types-of-labour-agreements) and [step-by-step process](https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/employer-subsite/Pages/la-step-by-step.aspx). For DAMA use the linked DAR's current occupation list and endorsement material. For industry, use the named section and its current template. For the pilot or a successor, use the specific Home Affairs program and occupation pages and confirm intake/endorsement with the authorised program channel. A list of agreements or an archived template is not evidence of current intake or an individual agreement's authority.

## Industry branches

The rows identify terms to check, not concessions that apply across industries. For each relevant branch retrieve the effective template, then the employer's executed schedules when assessing use. Resolve differences in the occupation code, worksite category, assessment method, recruitment timing, salary and available visa before relying on a branch.

| Branch | Distinct checks |
|---|---|
| Minister of Religion | Verify qualifying religious organisation and charitable/tax status; distinguish senior religious leadership from religious assistant duties or ordinary support work at a faith-affiliated entity. Check the two named occupations' agreement-entry recruitment exception separately from any nomination levy treatment. Check applicable vow/non-cash or reduced salary terms, English alternatives, and transitional versus exceptional senior-role permanent provisions. Do not apply a Minister's vow concession to a Religious Assistant by analogy. |
| Dairy | Distinguish Senior Dairy Cattle Farm Worker from Dairy Cattle Farm Operator, even when a common special code is used. Match tasks, occupational and visa experience, regional recruitment, pay and English settings; check any annualised pay reconciliation. |
| Fishing | Distinguish Deck/Fishing Hands, senior fishing/vessel roles and regional Seafood Process Worker coverage. Check the vessel and applicable sea-time, first-aid, safety or registration evidence. Deck/Fishing Hand English concessions can carry interpreter, translated safety and training duties. Catch-share pay requires comparator and top-up checks; use the occupation schedule, not a count of roles in the overview. |
| On-hire | For third-party placement, the on-hire party remains the direct employer and payer; obtain its signed worker and host/client contracts. Ordinary hiring into the firm's own business is a different route. Check eligible SID occupations, skill limits, employer allocation, experience and the monetary pay uplift plus local-equivalent pay. Some pages use different threshold labels; confirm the operative basis. This template is SID only; it does not offer an ENS pathway. |
| Meat | Primary processing, not retail butchery. Check Skilled Meat Worker duties, acceptable processing establishment and authorised industry skills verification, experience alternatives, and regional pay/LMT and top-up terms. For additional ENS places obtain the industry-specific ceiling arrangement; do not infer an ordinary variation will supply them. |
| Pork | Check Senior Stockperson (Piggery), actual supervisory/production duties, evidence of work at the appropriate commercial scale and skills/experience alternatives. Use the location- and subclass-specific schedule for recruitment, pay, age and English; do not borrow the dairy terms. |
| Restaurant (Premium Dining) | The operating employer and restaurant must meet the premium-dining settings; ordinary cafés, bars and pubs are not enough. Document business model, revenue and specialised service. Distinguish Chef, Cook, Café or Restaurant Manager and Trade Waiter duties and credentials; verify occupation-specific transition and regional provisions. |
| Advertising | Check the specified occupations and annual expression-of-interest/endorsement process through the Media Federation of Australia and Advertising Council Australia. Written endorsement is separate from the industry-wide annual allocation and the employer's own ceiling. Check guaranteed pay, market rate and experience, not simply the job title. |
| Horticulture | Confirm the approved occupation, mapping of legacy/current codes and any bespoke role description. Check whether standard skills evidence or a specified assessment (such as VETASSESS) applies, along with regional terms and the relevant pay floor. A shared special code is not permission to substitute occupations. |
| Aged care | Check direct care in the aged care sector, not disability-sector access by job title alone. Obtain and maintain a current MoU with the relevant union(s) for the occupation/site. Check its recruitment-evidence role; retain local shortage evidence if requested. Distinguish standard and established culturally and linguistically diverse (CALD) services, target-language ability, qualification versus experience alternatives, and when occupation-specific skills assessment is required. Treat any ENS service requirement as a separate dependency. |

## Prepare the employer's request

1. **Legal entity, lawful operation and viability.** Reconcile legal and trading names, company/business registration, operating history, proposed employer, payroll and worksite. Prepare evidence of the ordinary expectation of at least 12 months' lawful, active Australian operations and a chartered or certified practising accountant's evidence that the employer can support the proposed workforce. Use dated accounts, activity statements, bank/trading records, leases, work orders, contracts and payroll as appropriate. For a trust identify the trustee as the possible legal party; reconcile the deed, authority and any use of the trust's ABN. For a purchase or new entity document continuity, workforce transfer, previous business performance and buyer finances; seek confirmation of any exception rather than assuming eligibility. If an associated entity supplies payroll or contracts, evidence the relationship and actual control of duties, hours and conditions; related branding does not make a host placement direct employment.
2. **Good standing.** Disclose relevant contraventions, investigations, proceedings, warnings, insolvency and false or misleading information, including relevant associated persons/entities. Distinguish allegation from finding; identify dates, underlying notices/outcomes, remediation and evidence supporting any request that material be disregarded. Do not decide that the Department will disregard it or rely on receiving a further-information request before decline.
3. **Role, need and Australian recruitment.** Provide each actual role, occupation or special title/code, skill level, duties in business context, worksite, requested numbers and proposed subclass/period by agreement year. Document recent genuine efforts to recruit Australian citizens/permanent residents for each occupation and location: ads and application windows, channels and reach, responses and specific lawful reasons positions remained unfilled. Add training/retention efforts and relevant local/industry evidence (e.g. Jobs and Skills Australia data, job fairs, contracts, unfilled orders or reliable support letters). A national shortage list is not proof for a particular position or locality. For a small specialist project or setup phase, show why ordinary recruitment evidence is inadequate, the limited number/duration, equipment or process expertise, Australian economic benefit, domestic staffing and skills transfer. Alternative evidence is a case to be assessed, not an automatic exemption.
4. **Keep labour-market stages separate.** A labour agreement under reg 2.75C can set occupations, locations, numbers and other terms. In the inspected version, a statutory work agreement under reg 2.76 is a labour agreement **in effect** authorising the relevant recruitment, employment or services for SID, TSS or 494. For entry into a work agreement, verify that the reg 2.76A test of recent and genuine Australian recruitment for the occupations and locations is addressed; the Minister of Religion and Religious Assistant exceptions are occupation-specific. Do not confuse that test with the broader employer business case, evidence of industry or regional need, or the later nomination's LMT requirements. For 482, 494 or 186, check the executed clause and applicable subclass nomination rules separately on timing, channels, recent genuine recruitment and alternatives; do not import ordinary-program trade exemptions or another subclass rule. If a pilot or template says no advertising is required, identify how the entry requirement is addressed (including whether endorsement supports the required finding), and verify the nomination position separately. For an ENS-only proposal, verify the separate agreement and ENS rules; neither the statutory work-agreement definition nor later LMT settings carry over by analogy.
5. **Workforce plan.** Supply current and projected site-by-site counts by employment and visa category; distinguish Australians/permanent residents, employer-sponsored temporary skilled workers and other temporary holders. Reconcile contractors and group entities to the applicable counting basis. Explain requested ceilings, hires, retention, redundancies, apprenticeships/training, skills transfer and a measurable reduction in reliance. For a repeat agreement compare earlier commitments with actual outcomes. Published company-specific guidance states a one-third workforce limit; regional policy benchmarks can differ. For a case that depends on one of those measures, identify the correct denominator, scope, date and route-specific rule before confirming it; even a regional benchmark without a numerical limit does not remove the Australian-first assessment.
6. **Stakeholder evidence before request.** For a company-specific request document the relevant industry body, union(s) even if there are no union members on staff, and affected community bodies where relevant. Provide the role/site/count, pay and basis, proposed concessions and Australian training plan. Check current response and follow-up periods; the company-specific page specifies 10 working days and a further 5 after follow-up for non-response. Save contacts, dates, responses or genuine follow-up attempts, objections and mitigations. Silence is not endorsement, and an objection does not decide the outcome. Other industry and DAMA requests generally do not require the same employer consultation exercise; keep the aged-care MoU, advertising endorsement and DAMA employer endorsement as distinct gates. If a form suggests consultation is compulsory for all industry requests, check the current Home Affairs form notice and branch requirements rather than inventing consultation or omitting actual endorsement.
7. **Conditions and concession case.** Show proposed pay, hours and conditions, award/enterprise agreement and classification, with Australian-equivalent wage and conditions at the same location. Compare cash, benefits, applicable threshold and market rate separately using contracts, payslips or independent evidence. Keep lawful workplace minima and occupational licence/registration requirements independent of migration concessions. For each requested change record the usual requirement, exact requested alternative, subclass/occupation/site, evidence of need, worker safeguards and residual criteria. For English, record test equivalence or alternative claimed and safe induction, translated materials or other risk controls; for skills, assessment, qualifications and experience, distinguish occupational competence from visa experience and support both. Do not turn an agreement-office skills benchmark into a universal SID visa criterion. SID has no general age limit; for a 186/494 age request demonstrate why the seniority or specialised capability is needed, not just why a preferred person is over the usual limit. A permanent pathway request needs evidence of ongoing need, efforts to develop Australian workers and skills transfer, plus the proposed qualifying role/service—not just a wish to retain someone.
8. **Request and decision.** Check the current Labour Agreement Request form in ImmiAccount and the official checklist. Prepare readable, labelled attachments, applicable prior endorsements and consultation proof. A thin, repeated or unsupported request, missing mandatory evidence, or unexplained alternative route can lead to return without assessment. A request is distinct from charged nomination/visa stages; check current charges before quoting them. Record the receipt, nominated contact and any actual information request/deadline; never assume a further request or extension will be given. The Department decides case by case. A favourable decision or request to sign is not evidence that the agreement is in force.

## Agreement status and scope

Collect the full countersigned agreement, schedules and deeds, the confirmation that it took effect, and subsequent notices. Do not rely on a template, draft, approval email, employer-only signature or endorsement. Keep the requested, offered, signed and operative terms separate. Record the legal parties, agreement reference, signature and effective dates, end date, any suspension, restriction, lifting or termination (event date and scope), and the operative version **at the relevant event**. Agreements often run for five years; the actual terms control. For expiry/renewal check the current new-request process; a fresh receipt does not extend the existing agreement. If a notice is incomplete, obtain the full notice, relevant clause and effective dates before finding that authority continues or a pending matter is unaffected.

Extract a clause-to-evidence matrix: nominated visa/stream, occupation title/code and real duties, approved site or area, employment entity, hours, pay and work conditions, recruitment, period of stay, any assessment, experience, English, age or pathway terms, ceiling dimensions, and reporting/monitoring. For each row record the actual schedule/clause, date/stage, effect, party responsible and proof. A concession must be allowed by the applicable law and written into the operative terms covering this case; still verify the worker's proof at the visa stage. Keep agreement conditions separate from the holder's visa conditions. See [sponsor obligations](sponsor-obligations.md) for legislated duties, notices and post-cessation checks; track extra agreement duties, deadlines, source records and any audits or site visits separately.

## Ceilings and variations

- **Find the right allocation.** Identify the agreement year boundaries and total, occupation, subclass, site or other sublimits. Check whether the signed text grants annual places, a bulk allocation (reported for the NT DAMA), or employer places constrained by a separate industry or regional cap. If the terms use a bulk ceiling, verify how the applicable yearly nomination approval rule is satisfied; do not assume the bulk balance is a statutory yearly allocation. Do not assume unused places roll over, can be moved across occupations/subclasses, or can be reclaimed after an employee leaves.
- **Reconcile usage.** Record the employer's nominations, stream/visa, occupation, dates made and approved, current status, and reason and evidence for each inclusion or exclusion. Use the made-and-approved nominations in the statutory classes for the SID yearly comparison (check reg 2.72(19)(c), including relevant 457 and TSS approvals), plus any separate agreement limits. Track pending nominations as forecast demand, not approvals. Do not substitute employees, visa grants, submissions or a system balance for approved nominations. Confirm count, scope and potential adjustments with the authoritative records if disputed. System acceptance or industry-wide availability alone does not establish a place for this employer.
- **Requesting is not varying.** First identify the actual agreement type and current official variation pathway. For DAMA obtain DAR endorsement for changes available under the head terms and confirm the DAR submission channel. Otherwise check the relevant company/industry/on-hire form and Home Affairs channel. The general Home Affairs step-by-step process says that from 2026 it supports only one variation per agreement year, does not accept requests in the first six months after signing, and asks for all recruitment activity in the immediate six months. For **ceiling increases**, a separate policy account allows for possible exceptional timing, calls for LMT evidence from the preceding **12 months**, and flags a distinct Meat Industry ENS ceiling process. Do not merge the periods, assume an exception will be accepted or treat either policy description as a legal entitlement. Confirm the timing and required evidence for the actual branch with Home Affairs or the DAR; until resolved, prepare the relevant recruitment records for both periods. Record current versus requested terms, agreement reference, reason, ceilings by year/occupation, updated workforce plan, Australian comparator and actual overseas pay, redundancies, breaches and other confirmed evidence.
- **Use only operative authority.** Require a written deed executed by all necessary parties and verified as effective. A pending request, support letter, endorsement or draft adds no places and changes no term. Do not progress a nomination dependent on increased places before execution; if an application already exists, or the employer wants to rely solely on remaining old places while an increase is pending, verify the effect of the actual request/status and applicable administration before giving a readiness finding.

## Nomination and visa handoffs — choose the actual subclass

- **Ordinary sequence:** use the operative agreement matrix and ceiling reconciliation in [482 nomination](nomination.md), especially N03, N13, N17, N49, N67–N71 and N82/N85. Prepare the separate employer nomination, including agreement-specific duties, recruitment, remuneration and approvals. Once a nomination is actually lodged, the employer can provide the nomination transaction reference (TRN) for linkage. A TRN is not nomination approval and does not prove a valid or grantable visa application.
- **Exceptional pending-new-agreement branch (not a ceiling variation):** Do not route a pending variation of an existing agreement to this exception. Where the existing ceiling is exhausted or an increase is not evidenced by an operative deed, do not advise early lodgement based on the pending-new-agreement branch, including where the worker's visa is nearing expiry. Keep urgent visa-status advice separate. For a genuinely new agreement only: a nomination has sometimes been facilitated during genuine negotiations when the nominee is already in Australia and their current visa is nearing expiry. Record negotiation status and correspondence, actual onshore status, visa expiry/conditions and deadlines. Verify the applicable nomination lodgement provision, form/channel and any case-specific Department instructions. This is a reported possibility, **not Ready or authority to lodge**. An operative agreement authorising the role is still needed for agreement-stream nomination approval. Check visa validity, lawful stay/bridging and decision dependencies separately and promptly; a negotiation or an imminent expiry confers no visa or work rights. If negotiations stall, fail or the agreement ceases, review impact on both linked stages before any withdrawal, refund or new application; nothing is withdrawn automatically by this workflow.
- **Worker's application:** use [482 visa application](visa-application.md), especially V02–V05, V07, V10, V15–V21 and V24–V26, to reconcile the stream, agreement, nominee, employer, actual role/site, term and nomination status. Gather individual skills, experience and English proof and evaluate family, status/location, health, character and other criteria independently. For 186 or 494, this reference establishes only the agreement dependency. Carry the verified authorised visa, occupation, location, pathway conditions and operative places to the [186 nomination](ens-186-nomination.md) and [visa](ens-186-visa.md) or [494 nomination](sesr-494-nomination.md) and [visa](sesr-494-visa.md) references for the separate subclass tests. Do not transfer SID nomination cessation, visa linkage, ceiling formulas or early-lodgement exceptions to those pathways.

## When a finding is still open

Report the issue, missing evidence, stage affected and next action. Do not mark a mandatory issue Ready while it is unresolved. If no applicable law for the case date is verified, retrieve the Federal Register text and commenced amendments/savings for that date; a compilation displayed for **3–11 November 2026** does not establish coverage afterward. If the template or public page differs from another applicable source, identify the competing readings and obtain the current branch terms, executed schedules and any written clarification needed. If site, occupation, endorsement, allocation or usage is unclear, obtain the exact worksite/duties, current DAR or industry settings, scope of endorsement and authoritative nomination history. If status or terms are unknown, obtain the signed agreement, deeds and operative notices—not another copy of the proposal. In the meantime continue the supported evidence work, without extrapolating permission, eligibility or a visa outcome.
