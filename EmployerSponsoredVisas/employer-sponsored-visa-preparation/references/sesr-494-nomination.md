# Subclass 494 — nomination preparation

This reference is for the employer/nominator, either alone or alongside [the applicant's 494 visa preparation](sesr-494-visa.md). It covers Employer Sponsored (ES) and Labour Agreement (LA) nominations for a proposed applicant, applicant or existing Skilled Employer Sponsored Regional (Provisional) (SESR) holder. It does not confer approval, work permission or authority to lodge. Keep sponsor/agreement, nomination and visa findings separate. Do not use the SID/482 nomination checklist, occupation list, income thresholds, LMT exemptions or levy formula for 494.

## Route and chronology

- **ES:** match the legal nominator to an in-force standard business sponsorship (SBS) that is **not** an overseas business sponsor. A current approval for the same Australian sponsor need not be obtained again just for 494. Check the legal entity and history at approval or most recent variation, not just an ABN, current branch address, related company's approval or accreditation. An overseas business sponsor is expressly excluded from ES by 2.72C(5)(a); an overseas approval does not itself make the entity eligible under LA either.
- **LA:** match the actual party and an executed work agreement in effect that authorises recruitment, employment or engagement of a 494 holder in the occupation and location (2.72C(5)(b), (21), 2.75C–2.76A). Identify the parties, agreement/variation dates, term, signed schedules, subclass, location, occupation, caps and conditions; a DAMA head agreement, designated area representative endorsement, request or unsigned variation is not the employer's agreement. Do not route an overseas business through LA as a workaround; establish the Australian party and its own authority.
- Under Migration Act s 140GB and regs 2.72C(1)–(2), 2.73B, an approved sponsor, SBS applicant or party to agreement negotiations may make the relevant nomination when the applicable form/process permits. **Filing is not approval**: ES needs eligible SBS and LA needs an operative agreement by nomination approval; a linked visa can generally be filed while nomination is pending, but must have the same linked nomination approved and live at grant. Track pending, approved, barred, cancelled, ceased, refused and withdrawn separately. If authority falls away or negotiations fail, check actual notices, elections and dates before acting on the nomination or visa.

## Prescribed process and common nomination gates — both streams

1. **Form, identity and certifications (2.72C(3); 2.73B; LIN 19/215).** Prepare 494N (Internet) in ImmiAccount; check the applicable instrument for fee (distinct from SAF) and place/manner. Do not email a form as a substitute: use authorised alternative lodgement only if the instrument applies and the Department has given case-specific directions (through official technical support). Match travel-document name, DOB, sex/gender field and visa/application identifiers; record exact occupation title and 6-digit code, actual work postcode, turnover and the existing 494 grant date if relevant. Check sponsor's truthful s 245AR certification (asking for or receiving a sponsorship-related benefit; the event need not happen) and Australian employment-law/NES certification where required. For ES certify substantial task alignment, commensurate qualifications/experience and employment in own or associated business unless the instrument exception applies; for LA apply the agreement/proposed agreement requirements where relevant, including agreed tasks and worker qualifications/experience. Preserve submitted copy, receipts and corrections, rather than assuming system acceptance proves accuracy.
2. **Adverse information (2.72C(4); 1.13A/B).** Check the nominator and associated persons for relevant compliance issues, sanctions/bars, unlawful or exploitative work, payment allegations or false information. Keep dates, parties, notices, findings, remediation and contradictory evidence separate. Disregard is a Department decision, not a checklist pass. Check s 140M status independently for visa lodgement.
3. **Actual designated regional worksite (2.72C(6); 1.15M).** Record street address, postcode, state/territory, actual work pattern and **nomination-made date**; match the instrument applicable to that date (e.g. LIN 22/022 for its covered period). Do not use head office, payroll address, marketing descriptions or the visa grant date. For regular multiple sites record each and the majority-time site; for a mobile job with sites not yet known record the actual base and proposed coverage. Use roster, contract, site assignment, lease/client records and travel/site logs. Do not infer all work is regional from a regional base; flag non-regional, remote/hybrid, cross-border or unknown sites, interpreting holder condition 8579 separately. If the worksite changes, establish dates, retain old/new evidence, recheck eligibility and RCB jurisdiction and use the official sponsorship change channel; don't assume a new postcode can fix an original defect or that no further action is required.
4. **Occupation and applicability (2.72C(10)–(11)).** ES: verify exact title/code against LIN 19/219 (including the applicable ANZSCO edition and list) in force when made; check any applicable task/applicability provisions. LA: verify exact code and authorisation under the agreement and any caveats/applicability conditions; deal with any instrument/agreement-defined non-ANZSCO occupation using its prescribed code and descriptors. Test listing and applicability separately from genuine duties, employment and the applicant's assessment; don't rely on dropdown acceptance or a neighbouring code.
5. **Genuine, full-time, likely to exist ≥5 years (2.72C(12)).** Show a real position doing what it purports to do, not an abstract commercial need or a title chosen for a migration outcome. Map actual tasks, percent time, skill/responsibility and team/reporting lines to the occupation; allow genuine specialisation and explain technology or unit-group tasks that don't fit the specific occupation. Document headcount and incumbents, operating hours/rosters, prior vacancy/recruitment, output, contracts/orders and evidenced expansion or unusual business diversification. Explain family/personal links, a role apparently created for the nominee, copied duties, mostly lower-skilled work, staffing/pay cuts or retrenchments, low-end managerial pay, or a workforce pattern inconsistent with genuine recruitment. No single risk signal settles the issue. Typical full-time benchmark is 38 hours; explain award/agreement and NES support for an alternative schedule (e.g. 32–45 hours where applicable), irregular industry patterns or overtime. Show the position's five-year likelihood through an ongoing business case, budgets/contracts and staffing plan; don't equate it with a compulsory five-year fixed-term employment contract or the worker's current visa expiry date.
6. **Existing 494 holder and family (2.72C(8)–(9)).** Identify primary holder, grant and expiry, last nomination and each linked secondary 494 holder (minimum names and DOBs; use secure channels). Reconcile actual family composition and any omission privately; explain with evidence, including residence or dependency changes. The Department may disregard an omission if reasonable; do not assume an omitted person is covered, or that an omission or separation automatically cancels a visa. Check each person's sponsorship, grant and conditions independently.

## Employer Sponsored — employment and regional market pay

**Employee and terms (2.72C(13)–(14), (17); LIN 19/212; visa cl 494.222).** Unless the applicable occupation instrument provides the specific exception, the worker must be employed by the sponsor or a genuine associated entity (reg 1.03 / Corporations Act s 50AAA). Document association and which entity hires, pays, supervises, carries employment liabilities and can dismiss; shared branding, a client site or a services contract does not establish association. Independent contractor/ABN engagement, sham employment, unrelated labour hire or a sole trader contracting with themself cannot be assumed to meet the rule; screen partnership and self/related-party arrangements for actual legal employment and role genuineness. Provide contract signed by both parties including ordinary hours, pay, duties, worksite and no express exclusion of possible extension. Ongoing employment is the straightforward route; fixed-term lawful contracting and possible extension are separate checks (check dated Fair Work rules/exceptions). A transfer/assignment letter alone is not the relevant employment contract. For an award/agreement engagement letter and acceptance, retain company letterhead, employee statement, applicable agreement/award and identifier/classification, ordinary hours, pay and dated employer/nominee acceptance; keep the verifiable email acceptance chain or position/date record of online acceptance. Show conditions other than pay are not less favourable than for an equivalent Australian worker at the same location (leave, hours, deductions, etc.); retain fair recruitment evidence, including lawful reasons for any citizenship/immigration-status restriction. Discrimination and non-pay conditions are not resolved by a salary pass.

**AMSR calculation and comparison (2.72C(15)–(16); regs 1.03, 2.57A; IMMI 18/033 ss 5, 7–8).** Calculate what an Australian citizen or permanent resident earns or would earn annually for equivalent full-time work at the same workplace/location. Maintain four numbers: comparator total, comparator monetary-only, nominee total and nominee monetary-only. Show guaranteed wages and other qualifying earnings separately from agreed reasonably valued non-cash benefits; do not count compulsory super, reimbursements or contingent bonuses/commissions/overtime that cannot be determined in advance. Reconcile award/enterprise classification and actual guarantee; assess separately under workplace law.

- **Actual equivalent worker:** show real equivalence in duties, skill, complexity, experience, location and hours; title alone is not enough. Use the applicable Fair Work/state/transitional instrument and classification, or relevant employment documents (contract, pay records) showing no less than the applicable industrial rate; if no such instrument use the equivalent worker's employment documents. Generic market surveys are not a substitute for an actual equivalent worker.
- **No actual equivalent:** use applicable industrial instrument and level or relevant evidence supporting a rate at least that figure; without an instrument use relevant information to substantiate a single local market figure. Cross-check locally comparable recent job ads, reputable remuneration data, Jobs and Skills Australia information, or registered union/employer-body advice (prefer two sources where no instrument). Explain any regular shift arrangements, market premium, range or data discrepancy. Do not default to the bottom of a national range or use materially different advertised conditions.
- If the high-earnings trigger is not established (IMMI 18/033 s 5 specifies AUD 250,000 for 2.72C(15)(b) in the checked version), check correct AMSR method, **comparator cash ≥ TSMIT**, **nominee total ≥ AMSR**, **nominee cash ≥ TSMIT**, and no unresolved contrary labour-market information. Paragraph (16)(a) permits disregarding the comparator cash criterion only if total AMSR is at least TSMIT and it is reasonable (e.g. credible comparable package, not inflated benefits); (16)(b) separately requires reasonableness for nominee cash (document equivalence of terms and practical cash adequacy). These are discretionary, not automatic; no discretion in (16) lowers the applicable market-pay comparison in (15)(e). High earnings change the (15) test, **not** RCB, lawful pay, non-pay parity or genuineness requirements.
- **TSMIT is selected by nomination application date.** For **1 July 2025–30 June 2026**, use AUD **76,515** under former IMMI 18/033 s 6, not the later amount because a decision is pending. For nominations made **on or after 1 July 2026**, F2026L00874 Sch 2 and Sch 13 Part 164 move the authority into reg 1.03 (AUD **79,423** initially), remove the old instrument references in (15)(d), (15)(f) and (16)(a)(i), and provide for indexation under 5.42A starting no earlier than **1 July 2027**. F2026L00877 Sch 2 repeals the spent instrument amount (not the AMSR methodology); note the separate LIN 26/038 methodology transition, including nominations undecided on its commencement. For earlier/future dates or any missing date, retrieve applicable law and transition before finding compliance; no automatic update of a previously lodged threshold and no 482 CSIT/SSIT substitution.

### Regional Certifying Body advice — ES only

Under 2.72C(18)–(20) the Department must receive advice **about whether** the nominee would be paid at least AMSR from the specified body, located in the same state/territory and responsible for the actual work region. Check LIN 19/214 plus the Department directory for the appropriate body's catchment and current local form, timing, evidence and any fee. No generic state endorsement, DAMA endorsement, sponsor self-assessment or favourable advice for a different site/entity satisfies this check. For overlapping/multiple sites or an unclear successor/catchment, resolve which body covers the actual nominated position before treating advice as usable.

- Give the RCB the legal entity name/ABN, exact occupation/code/title and position description, hours, sites and postcode, contract/pay breakdown and AMSR evidence including local comparisons and relevant award/agreement classification. Retain application, correspondence, date and any reference number. Obtain dated advice identifying the body/representative, entity/ABN, code, position and nominated salary, its finding about pay at least AMSR and reasons (Form 1404 or approved alternative; reasons may be separate). Confirm the advice and reasons have reached the Department; keep sponsor copy and upload in the nomination channel as directed. Reconcile changes in pay, duties, workplace or employer with the RCB and Department.
- Operational timing: aim to obtain before filing, no more than three months before nomination lodgement under policy; advice can be obtained after lodgement but must be received for decision. If stale, obtain fresh advice; don't treat this policy timing as a separate legislative lodgement gate. Record requested/pending/received/negative/mismatched separately. Advice may be positive or negative; the RCB does not approve the nomination. Home Affairs weighs the advice and independently assesses pay; answer discrepancies or adverse advice with local comparator and payroll/contract evidence and respond to any procedural fairness request by its deadline.

### Labour market testing — ES statutory route

Use Migration Act ss 140GBA–140GBC, reg 2.72AA and dated LIN 18/036. Do not import a 482 international-trade exemption; Home Affairs says those ITO exemptions do not apply to 494. Test a claimed statutory disaster or skill/occupation exemption only against an actual applicable written authority; do not infer one. A **select position** is an alternative evidence route, not a statutory exemption. For standard LMT:

- Build a four-month timeline ending on the nomination date, with sponsor/associated-entity retrenchments or redundancies and evidence advertising occurred after any statutory trigger; disclose relevant events with the nomination. Check duration and dates for each ad/application window; collect two valid English ads in Australia with national reach on an eligible recruitment platform, print or radio, or on the sponsor's website where accredited. Retain evidence of at least four continuous weeks of acceptance; under LIN 18/036 s 8(5)–(7) qualifying overlapping advertisements may cover that continuous period (show the actual overlap and count overlapping days once), but disjoint or merely back-to-back ads cannot be added. Workforce Australia is not a compulsory third ad under the regime from 11 December 2023; check its transition for earlier/pending cases. Social media posts, restricted profile ads and general classifieds (as distinct from a qualifying jobs section) are not substitutes.
- Verify role/title or description, skills/experience, sponsor or authorised recruiter and advertised salary if intended annual earnings are below the instrument's AUD 96,400 amount in the checked version. Retain publication/start/end and acceptance proof, ad copies/screenshots (not links alone), authorisation/invoices and recruitment results at filing, not merely evidence of having placed ads. Match actual site, shifts, skills and conditions to nomination; document why each otherwise suitable Australian citizen, permanent resident or eligible temporary visa holder was not readily available. A contract signed before LMT, or an ad differing from the offer, needs a genuine recruitment explanation, not an automatic pass/fail.
- Check exact select position definitions and their dates: where the position requires an internationally recognised record of exceptional/outstanding achievement (e.g. sport, academia/research, top-talent chef), qualifying overseas intra-group transfer to an Australian associated SBS, narrowly defined existing-holder unchanged-position re-nomination for earnings or restructure, high earnings at the instrument amount, specified medical practitioners (not 253111 or 253999) or ambulance officers/paramedics (4111). Provide the written submission with why suitable domestic/eligible workers are not readily available and proof of the specific category (e.g. external awards, peer recognition, transfer and association records, old/new position and pay). Do not confuse a select position with an authority to use an overseas sponsor or an RCB or pay exception.

## Labour Agreement — requirements, pay and recruitment

Under 2.72C(21) prove the signed agreement/variations permit this occupation and nomination, meet conditions and have headroom in the relevant agreement year. Reconcile approved nominations against total and occupation-specific ceilings, accounting for pending nominations as competition for capacity, not as approved places. Obtain a dated sponsor ledger and agreement schedule; don't promise a place before checking.

Build an agreement-clause/evidence table for scope, actual site, duties/employment arrangement, award/market pay, any signed pay or non-cash concession (amount, guarantee and conditions), skills and recruitment. Apply common 2.72C gates above. Do not copy ES-only contract, RCB or threshold tests into LA as automatic rules, or infer a concession from a DAMA or from use of the LA stream. Without operative terms, limit the review to common gates/evidence and **Needs verification** for LA terms, caps and concessions.

Reg 2.76A requires recent genuine recruitment before entry into the work agreement, with Minister of Religion/Religious Assistant exception; nomination-stage LMT is separately governed by the executed agreement and applicable official instructions. Government guidance uses two valid ads and up to 12 months for Category 2 in its specified regime without compulsory Workforce Australia; existing agreements are not automatically varied. Some Category 3 arrangements recognise national plus local advertising. Do not assume either pattern replaces the signed terms. Match advertising dates, category, evidence reuse, channels and results to the actual agreement/directions; a recruitment effort at agreement or endorsement stage counts at nomination only if still applicable and evidenced. Do not use an ES statutory exemption to erase an LA obligation.

## SAF and lodgement reconciliation — both streams

Reg 2.73B and s 140GB require any payable nomination training contribution charge at filing; separately check paid-in-full charge debt/late penalties under 2.72C(7) and Act s 140ZO. Use Migration (Skilling Australians Fund) Charges Regulations 2018 ss 4 and 5A: latest income year ending before nomination, total ordinary income for Australian operations (reconcile return, accounts or documented good-faith estimate if not yet filed). Base AUD 3,000 if turnover **less than** AUD 10 million; AUD 5,000 otherwise. For an existing 494 holder use base × (5 − whole years from grant to nomination day) ÷ 5; near anniversaries check the actual dates, and refer any extended-duration or otherwise anomalous result rather than improvising a floor or using a 482 formula. LA Minister of Religion or Religious Assistant: nil under s 5A(4). Keep the payer/receipt and sponsor's evidence; do not shift the charge or prohibited sponsorship/recruitment costs to the nominee.

Investigate any material turnover/grant-date mismatch or underpayment before advising action. Don't assume an editable form, partial refund, automatic top-up or that lodging again transfers the visa: obtain the Department's recorded correction/payment route or confirm whether timely withdrawal/re-lodgement and separate refund requests are required. A paid receipt alone does not validate the basis or clear a debt.

## Decision, change and visa handoff

- **Sponsor reporting after grant or transfer:** keep agreed start, actual start, cessation/expected cessation, work duties, entity and location records. Check and make the applicable employer reports under reg 2.84 and the prescribed channel/time limit (including the 28-day reporting period for reportable employment events in the checked version). Reconcile holder notifications separately; a report is not new nomination approval or a variation of a visa condition.
- **Record and hand off:** sponsor owns nomination facts, signed disclosures and agreement/RCB/LMT/SAF evidence; applicant owns declarations and individual eligibility evidence. Share nomination TRN/ID via secure channel, exact stream/occupation/code, sponsoring/employing entity, work site and nomination date, pay/contract, family inclusion, approval/expiry/withdrawal notices and bar/status, and any unresolved exceptions. Reconcile identifiers and dates before visa filing, after any change and again for decision. No substitution of another nominee or relinking a new nomination to a lodged 494 visa application.
- **Life of approval (2.74; 2.75B):** keep the Department's written decision and any reasons. Calculate the earliest cessation event: receipt of written withdrawal; 12 months from approval unless a visa application based on it is then not finally determined (then final determination/withdrawal after the 12 months); linked 494 grant; for ES the sponsorship cessation-related nomination end day (three months after sponsorship end, subject to sponsor already approved again or a pre-expiry application still pending; then refusal date if that application is refused), or SBS cancellation under s 140M(1); for LA the agreement ending. A visa refusal does not independently end nomination approval before the 12 months; other triggers and any onshore restrictions still apply. Written withdrawal of a pending nomination and cessation of an approved nomination are distinct; check receipt, elections, effect on the linked visa and dates before making a decision to withdraw. A transfer nomination for an existing holder has no automatic grant-related end event merely because the worker transfers; verify actual live status for any later visa filing.
- **Change of sponsor/occupation:** for a continuing sponsored role in the same occupation, normally obtain new nomination approval before starting under that sponsorship; no automatic replacement 494 solely for that change during a valid visa. An applicable LIN 19/212 occupation exception can permit multiple employers or changing employer in the same occupation without a fresh nomination. For a change of nominated occupation obtain a new nomination and a new 494 grant before relying on the new sponsored role. Distinguish temporary mobility after genuine cessation (condition 8608) from an ongoing approved transfer, and do not infer how a cross-stream change affects the actual grant/conditions. Preserve cease/start, prior/new approval, site and family records and review the holder rules in the [visa reference](sesr-494-visa.md#grant-conditions-and-work-mobility).
- **Review and refunds:** retrieve the notice, receipt/service date, standing, date-specific review statute/reg 4.02, and deadline promptly; no merits-review representation or guarantee. Nomination fee/SAF refunds under 2.73C are discretionary and distinct from visa VAC, payable to the payer. Check grounds and chronology: Department error; pre-decision withdrawal for LA occupation/ceiling, mistaken occupation/stream/SAF inputs, withdrawn/refused SBS or agreement not entered; or approved nomination followed by finally determined linked refusal on specified character/health/integrity grounds (check the full list), grant with failure to start, or cessation within one year of starting (partial formula under 2.73C(9)). Nomination refusal itself, duplicate or change of mind is not an ordinary refund ground. Retain invoices, withdrawal/decision, employment/start/cease and sponsor-notification records, and review before acting on deadlines or creating a new case.
