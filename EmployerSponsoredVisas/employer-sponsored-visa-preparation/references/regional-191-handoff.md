# 494 → 191: Regional Provisional evidence handoff

Use this for record preparation for a 494 holder considering the **Regional Provisional Visas stream** of subclass 191. This is not a full 191 assessment, a new employer nomination, a 491 policy module, the Hong Kong (Regional) stream, or advice to allow a visa to expire. Do not assume an automatic grant, a visa extension, a bridging visa, or continued work rights.

## Keep three questions separate

| Question | Evidence to prepare | Important boundary |
|---|---|---|
| **Visa held on intended application date** | Actual 494 grant, any replacement or cessation notices, current status/conditions and dated visa history. Record the proposed application date and calculate elapsed time from the supported visa history using a date tool. | Schedule 1 item 1139(3)(c) requires the primary Regional Provisional applicant to hold a regional provisional visa, and to have held **that visa** for at least three years. Regulations define the class to include 491 and 494. Do not assume that separate grants can be added to satisfy the hold-time rule, that a bridging visa counts as the held 494, or that meeting the anniversary proves valid lodgement. Get the exact grants and applicable rule where dates, replacements or interruptions are unclear. |
| **Three distinct relevant income years** | For each year record its end date, dated overlap with an eligible regional provisional grant, evidence of the notice of assessment and any amended notice, and the identity match. | Under clauses 191.111 and 191.222, the year must end before application and there must be a regional provisional visa overlap for all **or part** of the year. Do not count a year that has not ended, a pre-visa year with no overlap, or the amendment as an extra year. Do not confuse this test with the Schedule 1 hold-time rule. |
| **Compliance with actual conditions** | Keep each person's grant and conditions, dated home/work/study addresses and postcodes, employer and worksite dates, leases and bills, payslips, school/transcript records, departures/returns, change notifications, receipt evidence and responses to Department requests where relevant. | Clause 191.221 distinguishes substantial compliance with other applicable conditions (including relevant subsequent bridging visas) from compliance with 8579 on the regional provisional visa held at application, subject to any applicable instrument class. Secondary applicants have their own test under clause 191.314. Check dated regional designations and actual locations, not just an employer's registered office. Do not assume any excursion or change conclusively establishes breach, exemption, or satisfaction. |

## Notices, not a salary threshold

- The [Home Affairs Regional Provisional 191 page](https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/visas/getting-a-visa/visa-listing/skilled-regional-191/regional-provisional) says there is **no minimum income requirement** and calls for ATO assessment notices for three income years. The [Regulations, Schedule 2](https://www.legislation.gov.au/F1996B03551/2026-11-03/2026-11-03/text/original/epub/OEBPS/document_2/document_2.html) still contain a power in 191.222(3) to specify an amount. Do not treat the enabling clause, a generic overview, a 494 nomination salary threshold, a contract or a historical policy summary as a current dollar requirement. If anyone asserts a threshold, ask for the operative instrument, applicant class and applicable period; verify it before applying an amount. Do not represent a targeted source check as clearance for every date.
- Missing or uninspected notices remain a separate evidence gap. Payslips, income statements and a lodged tax return can explain the record but are not substitutes for the ATO-issued notices in this check. A notice of amendment belongs with the corresponding year. A copy may omit the tax file number under 191.222(4); redact it and unrelated sensitive fields for preparation. The applicant may get copies themselves through the [ATO's notice-of-assessment guidance](https://www.ato.gov.au/individuals-and-families/your-tax-return/your-notice-of-assessment); the skill never accesses ATO or myGov accounts.
- For family members, identify their intended role, individual grant/conditions and whether a relationship or combined-application issue needs a separate 191 review. Do not extend the primary person's tax-evidence test to all family members. For a material family-violence or separation issue, support safe, minimal evidence preparation without asking for confrontation or deciding status.

## Handoff output

Give a short dated summary: hold-time evidence (or exact missing grant/date), separate year ledger for the notices, actual conditions/locations and unresolved discrepancies, and next useful document action. Do not substitute a full visa checklist for a narrow question. When current sources cannot be checked, distinguish the stored Home Affairs guidance from verified applicability and keep the legal conclusion open. Verify dated requirements using [Schedule 1 item 1139](https://www.legislation.gov.au/F1996B03551/2026-11-03/2026-11-03/text/original/epub/OEBPS/document_1/document_1.html), [Schedule 2 subclass 191](https://www.legislation.gov.au/F1996B03551/2026-11-03/2026-11-03/text/original/epub/OEBPS/document_2/document_2.html), relevant condition and postcode instruments, and any applicable amendments. The 3 November 2026 text is a dated comparison, not proof for a later proposed date. Use [494 visa preparation](sesr-494-visa.md) for the underlying provisional pathway and [source verification](verified-updates.md) for evidence and law limits.
