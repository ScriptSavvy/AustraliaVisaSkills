## Purpose and scope
One self-contained preparation skill for **Standard Business Sponsorship, labour agreement requests and variations, ongoing work-sponsor obligations, paying-for-sponsorship concerns, and nomination and visa preparation for 482 Skills in Demand (SID), 186 Employer Nomination Scheme (ENS), 494 Skilled Employer Sponsored Regional (SESR), and transitional 187 Regional Sponsored Migration Scheme (RSMS) TRT**. The 494 → 191 Regional Provisional pathway is an evidence handoff only, not a full 191 assessment. Use it for a focused question, evidence review, checklist or a cross-stage preparation plan. Keep employer, sponsor, worker and family actions separate.

A standard business sponsorship and a work agreement are distinct forms of authority, not universally consecutive approvals. Confirm the authority and chronology required for the actual stream and stage. Do not treat a request, approval for another entity, endorsement, draft or proposed variation as authority already in force. Route by subclass, actual stream and historical cohort. Do not impose standard business sponsorship on a pathway whose nomination has its own authority test. Prior TSS/457 facts can be relevant to a dated SID, ENS or 187 branch; full historical TSS/457 applications, closed 187 Direct Entry and full subclass 191 are out of scope. Negotiating a regional head agreement, determining enforcement outcomes, and exercising officer powers are not covered.

No submission, payment, withdrawal, account access, employer/Department contact, representation, review filing, visa-work permission determination or guaranteed outcomes. Refusal/review/refund/change-of-employer material supports evidence organisation and identifying questions, not an automatic legal strategy. Personalised immigration assistance may trigger professional/regulatory obligations; a disclaimer does not resolve them. Escalate the specific consequential interpretation, with documents and dates, to an appropriately qualified professional.

## Workflow and decision rules
1. **Define the preparation check.** Identify requested stage(s), user role, stream and material dates. Keep sponsorship lodgement/approval/expiry, agreement request/execution/variation, nomination lodgement/approval/cessation, visa lodgement/decision, and obligations and post-grant events separate. Establish what each person is authorised to share. Unknown nomination or stream produces a conditional check, not assumed compliance.
2. **Refresh applicable requirements.** Map the complete legal pathway for the question rather than stopping at one schedule or instrument: check Schedule 1 application validity, the relevant Schedule 2 application-time and decision-time criteria, any triggered Schedule 3 criteria, the enabling Act, applicable instruments, and transitional/savings provisions. Identify each criterion's express time anchor; do not assume a decision-stage heading erases a separately stated application-date condition, or that a Schedule 1 validity rule captures all substantive requirements. Then inspect actual official bodies and relevant effective/savings provisions. Record title, URL, section, version/effective dates, date checked and effect on the stored rule. Check volatile thresholds, tests, fees, forms, assessment classes and deadlines at use. A retrieval shell, search snippet or stored check date does not verify the law. Preserve exceptions/discretion and unresolved issues; continue independent work.
3. **Build stage-specific evidence rows.** Use mandatory, conditional (with explicit trigger), useful supporting evidence or practical suggestion. Keep sponsor certifications separate from applicant proof. Distinguish examples from requirements. Load the applicable agreement and concessions instead of assuming standard-stream rules apply.
4. **Inspect actual records.** Assign neutral document IDs/pages. Check contents, not filenames or statements alone. Separate presence, readability, completeness, consistency, currency and compliance with a verified applicable requirement. Name uncertainty from missing pages or extraction; do not invent obscured contents or authenticity findings.
5. **Reconcile the stages.** Compare nominee identity, employer/sponsor/nominator legal entity and approval basis, occupation/code/duties, stream, location, nominated period, contract/earnings, executed agreement version and applicable limits/concessions, family coverage, nomination reference and event status. A pending nomination may differ from a grant-ready nomination. A new nomination must not be assumed capable of replacing the one linked to an existing visa application. Track cross-stage risks explicitly.
6. **Give the answer first.** Apply the focused-answer, certainty and consistency rules above. Broad reports lead with concise findings/actions, then separate requested stage results and any relevant handoff table; retain each detailed issue in its canonical row. Do not substitute all stage checklists for a narrow answer or expose untriggered research branches.
7. **Final audit.** Check that no evidence item or legal rule was carried across subclasses without testing the target criterion, role, instrument and date independently. Every material finding has inspected evidence or a declared gap, requirement type, actor, stage/date, source/currentness and next action. No unexplained readiness score or approval probability. Unknown mandatory applicability/compliance blocks a whole-stage Ready finding, but not independently supported document-quality findings. State exactly what was checked and what was not.
